## Overview

Slovakia issues Community Licences through the Ministry of Transport (Ministerstvo dopravy SR). Slovak operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam (Osvedčenie o odbornej spôsobilosti) is administered by approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Slovak specifics |
|-------------|----------|------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Slovak place of business | IČO registration; Obchodný register |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal record; transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | Osvedčenie o odbornej spôsobilosti | Via approved examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Ministerstvo dopravy SR**: issues Community Licences (licencia Spoločenstva) and domestic transport licences.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Slovakia within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- NIP posting notification required (see posting-declaration.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
