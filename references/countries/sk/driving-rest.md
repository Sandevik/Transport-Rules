## Overview

Slovakia applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without national deviations. Enforcement is shared between the **Traffic Police** (Dopravná polícia) and the **Road Transport Inspectorate**. Slovakia is a transit country on the Vienna–Budapest corridor and the main SE corridor.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
