## Overview

Slovakia applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The Ministry of Transport (Ministerstvo dopravy SR) coordinates emission policy. Slovakia has no national LEZ programme for HGVs. The e-TOLL Slovakia electronic tolling system applies to vehicles >3.5t on motorways and expressways.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Slovak national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- Slovak government provides EU co-funded support for ZEV commercial vehicles through SIEA (Slovak Innovation and Energy Agency)
- Reduced annual vehicle tax for ZEV trucks
