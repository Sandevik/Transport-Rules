# Slovakia — Road Tolls (e-MYTO)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.emyto.sk/en — e-MYTO: Slovak electronic toll system

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Slovakia operates the **e-MYTO** electronic toll system for heavy vehicles (>3.5t) on motorways and expressways. Rates are CO₂-differentiated since July 2025, aligned with EU Eurovignette Directive requirements. The system uses OBU devices and is operated by SkyToll.

---

## Which Vehicles Must Pay

Vehicles with GVW **exceeding 3.5 tonnes** on motorways and expressways.

---

## Rate Structure

Rates depend on:
- Vehicle weight category
- CO₂ emission class (five classes, introduced July 2025)
- Road type

---

## OBU and Registration

OBU device required. Register at https://www.emyto.sk/en/

EETS-compatible transponders are also accepted.
