## Overview

Slovakia implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through zákon č. 351/2015 Z. z. (Act on Posted Workers). The NIP declaration portal is covered in posting-declaration.md. This file covers minimum pay, equal treatment, and enforcement. The NIP (Národný inšpektorát práce / National Labour Inspectorate) and SOI enforce compliance.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Slovakia | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in SK + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Slovakia only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Minimálna mzda**: approximately €750/month (2025; updated annually by government regulation)
- **Sector collective agreements**: road transport sector collective agreements (ČESMAD Slovakia sector) may set higher rates
- Posted drivers must receive at least Slovak minimum wage or applicable sector rate

---

## Driver Documentation

- NIP notification proof
- Employment contract showing Slovak-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Slovak authorities may demand security deposits.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | €1,000–€100,000 | Zákon č. 351/2015 |
| Failure to notify posting | €500–€50,000 | Zákon č. 351/2015 |
