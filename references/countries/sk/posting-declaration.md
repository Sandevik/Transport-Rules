## Overview

Slovakia implements EU Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Národný inšpektorát práce (NIP — National Labour Inspectorate). Foreign carriers performing cabotage in Slovakia or posting drivers for work in Slovakia must submit a declaration via the IMI system or the Slovak online portal. Slovakia's minimum wage applies to posted workers for the Slovak portion of their work.

## Who Must Declare

| Operation | Declaration required |
|---|---|
| Cabotage in Slovakia | **Yes** — from day 1 |
| Bilateral SK ↔ home country transport | **No** |
| Transit through Slovakia (no loading/unloading) | **No** |
| Third-country transit | **No** |

## Declaration Procedure

**Portal:** [https://www.ip.gov.sk/vyslanie-pracovnikov/](https://www.ip.gov.sk/vyslanie-pracovnikov/)

| Required information | Details |
|---|---|
| Employer details | Company name, address, home state, registration |
| Driver identity | Name, date of birth |
| Posting period | Start and end dates |
| Type of work in Slovakia | Cabotage description; route |
| Minimum wage commitment | Slovak minimum wage (minimálna mzda) |

**Documents in vehicle:**
- Declaration confirmation from NIP portal
- Employment contract or equivalent
- Evidence of wages paid covering Slovak posting period
- Accessible tachograph records

## Slovak Minimum Wage

Slovakia's minimum wage (minimálna mzda) is set annually by Government Regulation. For 2026, check the current rate at [MPSVR SR](https://www.mpsvr.sk/sk/praca-zamestnanost/minimalna-mzda/).

Additional sectoral minimum wages may apply under collective agreements (kolektívne zmluvy).

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| No posting declaration submitted | €1,000–€30,000 | Zákon 351/2015 §4 |
| Missing documents in vehicle | €300–€2,000 | Zákon 351/2015 |
| Underpayment of Slovak minimum wage | €1,000–€5,000 per driver | Zákon 311/2001 (Labour Code) |
| Obstruction of inspection | €1,000–€5,000 | Zákon 125/2006 (Labour Inspection Act) |
