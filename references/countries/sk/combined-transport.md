## Overview

Slovakia implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Slovakia's position between Czech Republic, Austria, Hungary, and Poland makes it an important transit country. Key intermodal nodes include Bratislava (Danube port) and Košice (Eastern Slovakia industrial hub). Slovakia has no general HGV driving bans.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Terminals

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Bratislava (DP World) | Bratislava | Danube river; rail; Austria border |
| Žilina | Northwest | Rail; Czech/Polish border |
| Košice | East Slovakia | Rail; Hungary/Ukraine border |

**Danube waterway**: River barge transport from Bratislava to Vienna or Budapest qualifies as the non-road leg for combined transport.

---

## Documentation Required

- Terminal booking/confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC.
