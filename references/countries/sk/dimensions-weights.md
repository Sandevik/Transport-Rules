# Slovakia — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**
- https://www.mindop.sk/ — Ministry of Transport
- https://www.nds.sk/ — NDS: National Motorway Company

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Slovakia applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the zákon č. 106/2018 Z. z. o prevádzke vozidiel v cestnej premávke and related regulations. No special large vehicle programme. The e-TOLL Slovakia system applies to vehicles >3.5t on motorways and expressways.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits issued by the Slovak Road Administration (Slovenská správa ciest) or regional road authority for oversize/overweight loads.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
