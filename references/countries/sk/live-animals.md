---

## Overview

Slovakia applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the ŠVPS SR (Štátna veterinárna a potravinová správa SR / State Veterinary and Food Administration). Enforcement is by regional veterinary administrations (Regionálne veterinárne a potravinové správy — RVPS).

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | RVPS (regional) |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | RVPS (regional) |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Veterinárne osvedčenie (veterinary certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | €500–€5,000 | Zákon o veterinárnej starostlivosti |
| Welfare violations | €500–€5,000 | Zákon na ochranu zvierat |
| Failure to maintain journey log | €200–€2,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
