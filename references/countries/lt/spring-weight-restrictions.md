# Lithuania — Spring Weight Restrictions (Pavasaro keliu apkrovu apribojimai)

**EU Baseline:** references/eu/spring-weight-restrictions.md — national implementation under EU Directive 96/53/EC Art. 4(4).

**Sources:**
- https://lakd.lrv.lt/ — Lietuvos automobiliu keliu direkcija (LAKD — Lithuanian Road Administration)
- https://www.e-tar.lt/ — E-TAR (Lithuanian Official Gazette — legislation)

**Last verified: 2026-06-02**

---

## Overview

Lithuania applies seasonal spring weight restrictions (**pavasaro keliu apkrovu apribojimai** — spring road load restrictions) on secondary and local roads during the spring thaw period. Restrictions are imposed when frost-susceptible road subbase layers thaw and temporarily lose bearing capacity.

The restrictions are administered by **LAKD (Lietuvos automobiliu keliu direkcija)** for national roads. Municipal authorities manage restrictions on local roads.

---

## Timing

| Region | Typical restriction period | Notes |
|--------|--------------------------|-------|
| Western Lithuania (Klaipeda region) | February–March | Earlier thaw; proximity to Baltic Sea |
| Central Lithuania | March–April | Standard spring thaw window |
| Eastern Lithuania (Aukstaitija) | March–May in upland areas | Longer period; more continental climate; heavier frost penetration |

Exact dates vary annually with weather. LAKD announces restrictions each year as conditions develop.

---

## Weight Reductions

During restriction periods, reduced axle load and GVW limits apply on designated roads:

| Road category | Typical restriction |
|---|---|
| National main roads (magistraliniai keliai) | Generally not restricted; individual weak sections may be restricted |
| National regional roads (krasto keliai) | Reduced axle loads applied; specific limits vary by section |
| National local roads (rajoniniai keliai) | More significant reductions; sometimes prohibition on vehicles above threshold |
| Municipal and forest roads | Set by local authorities; often stricter than national roads |

Specific weight limits per road section are published by LAKD and signed on-site with standard weight restriction signs.

---

## Checking Restrictions

- **LAKD website:** https://lakd.lrv.lt/ — spring restriction announcements and current restriction maps
- **On-site signing:** restricted roads carry posted signs showing the permitted maximum axle load or GVW
- Operators planning routes in the March–April period should check LAKD restriction maps before departure

---

## Penalties

Violations are treated as standard overload violations under Lithuanian road traffic law. See `references/countries/lt/penalties.md` if available.
