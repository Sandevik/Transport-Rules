# Lithuania — Road Tolls (ViaLietuva / Kelių mokestis)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://vialietuva.lt/en/user-charge-vignettes — ViaLietuva: Road user charge
- https://www.keliumokestis.lt/ — Kelių mokestis: Official Lithuanian toll portal

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Lithuania requires an **e-vignette (kelių mokesčio vinjetas)** for buses (M2–M3) and goods vehicles (N1–N3 categories) on toll roads. The system is administered by **ViaLietuva**. Since 2018, Lithuania has been expanding toll road coverage.

---

## Which Vehicles Must Pay

- Buses (categories M2, M3)
- Goods vehicles (categories N1, N2, N3)

The vignette must be purchased **before entering toll roads**.

---

## Vignette Durations and Rates

Rates depend on vehicle category and weight. Available periods typically: 7 days, 1 month, 1 year.

Full rates and purchase: https://www.keliumokestis.lt/

---

## Roads Covered

Applies to toll roads in Lithuania — a designated network of main and regional roads. Check https://www.keliumokestis.lt/ for current map.

---

## Payment

Online at ViaLietuva / keliumokestis.lt, at petrol stations, and at authorised resellers.
