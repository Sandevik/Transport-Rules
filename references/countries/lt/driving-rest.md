## Overview

Lithuania applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **LTSA** (Lietuvos transporto saugos administracija — Lithuanian Road Transport Safety Administration) and the police. Lithuania is part of the main Baltic corridor (SE-corridor) connecting the Baltics to Western Europe.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
