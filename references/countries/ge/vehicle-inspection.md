## Overview

Georgia has its own national vehicle roadworthiness inspection regime. EU Directive 2014/45/EU does not apply directly. Periodic technical inspections are required for commercial vehicles operating in Georgia. Foreign-registered vehicles must comply with their home country inspection requirements and must be roadworthy under applicable AETR rules.

---

## Inspection Intervals (Commercial Vehicles)

| Vehicle Type | Inspection Frequency |
|---|---|
| Goods vehicles >3.5t | Annual |
| Passenger vehicles >8 seats | Annual |
| Trailers >3.5t | Annual |
