## Overview

Cyprus applies EU Regulation 561/2006 directly as an EU member state. There are no significant national deviations from the EU baseline. Enforcement is carried out by the Department of Road Transport (Τμήμα Οδικών Μεταφορών) under the Ministry of Communications and Works. As an island, most commercial transport is domestic; AETR does not apply for Cyprus–mainland EU routes (sea crossing breaks the continuous road journey).

## Deviations from Baseline

No significant deviations. EU Regulation 561/2006 applies in full.

**Island-specific note:** International road journeys from Cyprus require a sea crossing to reach mainland Europe. The sea crossing interrupts driving time recording per EU 561/2006 Art. 9 (ferry/train exception). Drivers must take their normal rest during the ferry leg; the 1-hour pre/post-ferry activity allowance applies.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Driving time exceeded (up to 2h) | €85–€170 | Cyprus Road Transport Law |
| Insufficient rest | €85–€170 | Cyprus Road Transport Law |
| Tachograph not used | €170 | Cyprus Road Transport Law |
| Tachograph manipulation | €1,700+ | Criminal prosecution |

*Note: Exact penalty amounts subject to annual revision — confirm with Department of Road Transport.*
