## Overview

Cyprus is an EU member state and applies all EU transport regulations including EU Regulation 561/2006, Directive 96/53/EC, and all other applicable EU transport law. The **RTD** (Department of Road Transport) under the Ministry of Transport is the primary authority.

Cyprus has no motorway toll or HGV vignette. As an island, international road transport to Cyprus is limited (no land border with other EU states); most international freight arrives by sea or air.
