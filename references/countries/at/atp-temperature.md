## Overview

Austria is an ATP signatory and applies the **ATP Agreement** (Agreement on the International Carriage of Perishable Foodstuffs) for international refrigerated transport. The EU/UNECE ATP equipment class system (FNA/FRB/FRC/FDA/FDB, etc.) applies fully.  The national competent authority for ATP certification and inspection is **BMLFUW / AGES (Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety)**.

See `references/eu/atp-temperature.md` for the full equipment class table, temperature requirements, inspection intervals, and documentation requirements.

## National Implementation

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Competent authority | BMLFUW / AGES (Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety) |
| ATP certificate validity | 3 years (new equipment); 3 years (subsequent) |
| Testing stations | Designated by national authority — contact BMLFUW / AGES (Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety) |
| eCMR/CMR reference | ATP certificate number must appear on transport document |

## Equipment Most Commonly Used in Austria

| Class | Use case |
|---|---|
| FRC (Frozen) | Ice cream, deep-frozen fish, frozen meat |
| FRA (Chilled) | Fresh meat, dairy, chilled fish, fresh produce |
| FDA (Deep frozen) | Quick-frozen foods (≤ −18°C) |
| FNA (Insulated) | Short-haul chilled transport, cut flowers |

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| BMLFUW / AGES (Austrian Agency for Health and Food Safety) | ATP certification, inspection, market supervision |
| Police / DVSA equivalent | Roadside document checks |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Transporting perishables without valid ATP certificate | Fine + prohibition from continuing journey | ATP Agreement; national food safety law |
| Expired ATP certificate | Same as above | ATP Agreement |
| Temperature exceedance without remediation | Product seizure; carrier liability | National food safety law |
