## Overview

Austria has a multi-layered system of HGV driving restrictions covering night driving bans (Nachtfahrverbot), Sunday/holiday bans, a sectoral driving ban on the A12 Inntal motorway, and seasonal mountain road restrictions. Enforcement is by the **Landespolizeidirektion** and **Straßenaufsicht**. Austria's driving ban system is particularly complex due to the transit pressure through Alpine corridors.

---

## Night Driving Ban (Nachtfahrverbot)

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles ≥ 7.5t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| All nights, year-round | **22:00–05:00** |

**Scope:** Applies on all roads. Goods vehicles ≥ 7.5t GVW may not drive during these hours.

**Exemptions from night ban:**
- Perishable foodstuffs (milk, fresh food, live animals)
- Newspapers and mail
- Combined transport rail/sea road legs
- Fuel deliveries for service stations
- Empty vehicles returning after exempt-load delivery
- Emergency/breakdown services

---

## Weekend and Holiday Ban

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles ≥ 7.5t GVW

| Period | Hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sundays and national holidays | **00:00–22:00** | All roads |
| Holiday eve (Saturday before holiday) | **15:00–24:00** | All roads |

---

## Sectoral Driving Ban — A12 Inntal Motorway (Sektorales Fahrverbot)

The A12 Inntal motorway (Innsbruck → Kufstein/German border) carries the heaviest Alpine transit freight volume. Austria's **Sektorales Fahrverbot** prohibits certain **goods categories** from using the A12 by road, forcing them onto rail (the Brenner railway parallel corridor):

**Banned goods on A12 (by road):**
- Waste and scrap metal
- Automobiles and automotive components
- Stones, earth, sand, gravel
- Construction materials
- Food products for human and animal consumption
- Other goods subject to periodic extension by Austrian authorities

**Purpose:** To incentivise modal shift to rail (Brenner Rail Freight Corridor) for high-volume/low-value goods.

**Applies to:** Heavy goods vehicles on the A12 Inntal motorway (both directions)

---

## Night Driving Ban on Inntal — A12 "Nacht-Fahrverbot"

The A12 Inntal additionally has a strict night driving ban for certain vehicles:
- Heavy goods vehicles > 7.5t: 22:00–05:00
- Enforcement by Landespolizei Tirol with radar/weight checks

---

## Additional Mountain Road Restrictions

Several mountain passes and high Alpine roads in Austria have weight and dimension restrictions, particularly:
- **Brenner Autobahn (A13):** Special section toll (Brenner Maut €11.50/one way for 5-axle truck); periodic convoy restrictions during peak traffic
- **Arlberg pass (route 316 / A14 tunnel):** Tunnel height restriction 4.5m; dangerous goods restrictions
- **Tauern Autobahn (A10):** Special section toll + peak period restrictions

---

## Summer Holiday Traffic Management

During peak summer weeks (late July and August), Austria coordinates with Germany and Switzerland on traffic management. Additional restrictions or convoy systems may be activated for the **Inntal, Tauern,** and **Phyrn** corridors.

## Enforcement and Fines

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|---|---|
| Night driving ban violation | €100–€726 |
| Sunday/holiday ban violation | €100–€1,453 |
| Sectoral driving ban violation (A12) | €726–€2,180 |
| Repeat offence | Up to €5,000 + vehicle immobilised |

Foreign operators: Security deposits (Sicherheitsleistung) may be demanded on the spot.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Night driving ban (Nachtfahrverbot) | €100–€726 | §43 StVO; KFG |
| Weekend/holiday ban | €100–€1,453 | §43 StVO |
| A12 sectoral ban violation | €726–€2,180 | Bundesstraßengesetz; Sektoral-Fahrverbotverordnung |
