## Overview

Austria has a **mandatory winter tyre requirement** during the period 1 November to 15 April, with specific rules for goods vehicles exceeding 3.5 tonnes GVW that are stricter than the rules for passenger cars. The legal basis is the **Kraftfahrgesetz (KFG)** and associated Verordnungen. Austria's alpine geography means enforcement is frequent, particularly on mountain passes and motorway checkpoints. The BMK (Bundesministerium für Klimaschutz, Umwelt, Energie, Mobilität, Innovation und Technologie — reorganised as BMIMI) and the Bundespolizei enforce compliance.

---

## Mandatory Period

**1 November – 15 April** (inclusive) for all winter equipment requirements.

---

## Requirements by Vehicle Category

### Goods Vehicles Over 3.5 Tonnes GVW (Heavy Vehicles)

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Mandatory period** | 1 November – 15 April, **regardless of weather/road conditions** |
| **Tyre requirement** | Winter tyres on **at least one drive axle** |
| **Minimum tread depth (radial tyres)** | **5 mm** |
| **Minimum tread depth (cross-ply/bias tyres)** | **6 mm** |
| **Chain carry requirement** | Must carry **suitable snow chains for at least two drive wheels** during 1 Nov – 15 Apr, unless the vehicle cannot be fitted with chains or is used exclusively on snow-free roads |

The requirement for vehicles over 3.5 tonnes is a **fixed-date, unconditional obligation** during the winter period — it applies even on dry roads.

### Passenger Cars and Goods Vehicles Up to 3.5 Tonnes GVW

The requirement is **situational**: winter tyres are mandatory only when **snow, slush, or ice** is present on the roads, during the 1 November – 15 April period. If roads are dry and clear, summer tyres are technically permitted.

---

## Tyre Symbol Acceptance

| Symbol | Accepted? |
|--------|----------|
| **M+S** (Mud and Snow) | Yes — accepted as winter tyre in Austria |
| **3PMSF** (snowflake ❄) | Yes — accepted |

Austria accepts M+S-marked tyres. There is no current legal requirement for the 3PMSF symbol, though it is increasingly recommended and industry guidance suggests 3PMSF tyres are superior to M+S-only tyres.

---

## Snow Chains

### Carry Requirement (HGV)

Heavy vehicles (> 3.5 t) must carry snow chains during 1 November – 15 April. Minimum:
- **Chains for at least two drive wheels**

### Mandatory Chain Use Sections

On certain **mountain passes and alpine roads**, chain use is mandatory regardless of tyre type. These sections are signed with the chain symbol (Schneekettenpflicht). Key examples include:
- **Brenner motorway (A13)** and connecting alpine roads during heavy snowfall
- **Tauern motorway (A10)**
- Various Landesstraßen on alpine passes

Chain-mandatory sections are activated by road operators and signalled in real time on motorway variable message signs and the ASFINAG traffic information system.

### Prohibited Use

Snow chains must not be used on **snow-free roads** (risk of road surface damage). Operators driving with chains on dry tarmac face potential liability for road damage.

---

## Drive Axle Clarification

The requirement for "at least one drive axle" means:
- A truck with a single powered drive axle must fit winter tyres on that axle.
- A truck with two or more drive axles (e.g., 6×4 or 8×4 configuration) must fit winter tyres on **at least one** drive axle, but fitting on all drive axles is strongly recommended and often operationally required for road grip.
- **Steering axle (front axle)**: Winter tyres are not legally mandatory on the steering axle, but ÖAMTC and road safety organisations strongly recommend winter tyres on all axles.

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Bundespolizei** | Roadside checks on all road categories |
| **ASFINAG** | Spot checks at motorway checkpoints; can direct vehicles to pull over |

Foreign operators are subject to the same requirements. A **security deposit (Sicherheitsleistung)** may be demanded at the roadside for violations before the vehicle is permitted to continue. The deposit covers the anticipated fine.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Basic fine (operating without required winter equipment) | **€35** |
| If other road users are endangered | **Up to €5,000** |
| Failure to carry required chains (HGV, Nov–Apr) | €35–€218 |
| Tread depth below minimum | €35–€218 |

**Civil liability note:** Drivers/operators involved in road accidents while using non-compliant tyres face a **reverse burden of proof** — they may be deemed partially at fault. This is a significant practical risk for HGV operators on alpine routes.
