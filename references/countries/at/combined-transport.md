## Overview

Austria implements Directive 92/106/EEC and is a critical country for combined transport due to its position on major Alpine transit corridors. The Rollende Landstrasse (RoLa — Rolling Road) is Austria's flagship rail-road solution, transiting entire trucks by rail through the Alps. Austria's night and weekend HGV bans make the combined transport driving ban exemption essential for logistics operations.

---

## National Driving Bans (Why Combined Transport Exemption Matters)

Austria has extensive HGV driving restrictions:

| Period | Ban applies to |
|--------|---------------|
| Night (22:00–05:00) | Vehicles >7.5t laden on ALL roads (not just motorways) |
| Sundays all day | Vehicles >7.5t |
| Public holidays all day | Vehicles >7.5t |
| Saturdays 15:00–24:00 (summer: 13:00–24:00) | Vehicles >7.5t on certain major routes |

**Combined transport exemption:** Road legs of qualifying combined journeys are exempt from these bans under §42 StVO + national implementing regulation. Documentation must be in the vehicle.

---

## Weight Allowance

44 tonnes for 5+ axle combinations operating as part of qualifying combined transport journeys.

---

## RoLa — Rollende Landstrasse (Rolling Road)

Austria's RoLa service allows entire trucks (tractor + trailer) to travel by rail through Alpine passes, avoiding the Brenner, Tauern, and Pyhrn mountain routes for HGVs:

| Route | Terminals | Distance |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Brenner RoLa | Wörgl (AT) ↔ Brennersee (AT) | Brenner axis |
| Tauern RoLa | Villach (AT) ↔ Salzburg (AT) | Tauern axis |
| Pyhrn RoLa | Linz (AT) ↔ Graz (AT) | North-South |

The RoLa qualifies under Directive 92/106/EEC — the rail leg is the non-road portion.

## Documentation Required

- Rail or waterway booking confirmation (RoLa booking, intermodal terminal confirmation)
- CMR waybill for road legs
- Vehicle registration confirming axle count ≥5 (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from the EU baseline. Austria strongly promotes combined transport through infrastructure investment and RoLa subsidies.
