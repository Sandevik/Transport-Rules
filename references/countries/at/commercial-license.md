## Overview

Austria issues EU Community Licences and domestic transport licences through the BMK (Bundesministerium für Klimaschutz) and the Bezirksverwaltungsbehörden (district administrative authorities). Austrian transport operators must meet the standard EU four requirements: establishment, good repute, financial standing, and professional competence.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Austrian specifics |
|-------------|----------|--------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine place of business in Austria | Registered Austrian address; effective management |
| Good repute | No serious criminal/transport offences | Checked via Strafregister and transport register |
| Financial standing (international) | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual balance sheet or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC examination or recognised qualification | Fachkundenachweis — exam via WKO |

---

## National Licensing Authority

| Level | Authority | Role |
|-------|-----------|------|
| National policy | BMK | Licensing framework and regulations |
| Issuing authority | Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde | Issues Community Licences and domestic licences |
| Trade association | WKO Transport (Wirtschaftskammer) | CPC training and professional competence exams |

---

## Community Licence

The EU Community Licence for international road haulage is issued by the Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde on behalf of the BMK. Certified true copies are issued to each vehicle. The Community Licence entitles Austrian carriers to operate internationally in all EU/EEA member states.

---

## Cabotage Rules

Under EU 1072/2009 (as amended by Mobility Package 2020/1054):
- Max **3 cabotage operations** within 7 days following an international delivery in Austria
- **4-day cooling-off** period before another series of cabotage operations in Austria
- Each cabotage operation in Austria must comply with Austrian wage and working conditions laws

---

## Driver Attestation

For drivers who are nationals of non-EU/EEA countries, the Austrian Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde issues a **Driver Attestation** (Fahrernachweis) certifying the driver is legally employed by the Austrian carrier.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009. Austria applies the EU framework directly.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | €363–€5,000 | GütbefG §23 |
| Exceeding cabotage operations | €363–€5,000 | GütbefG §23 |
