# Austria — Collective Agreement for Road Transport (Kollektivvertrag Güterbeförderung)

**Sources:**
- https://www.wko.at/branchen/transport-verkehr/ — WKO: Wirtschaftskammer Österreich (employer side)
- https://www.vida.at/ — Gewerkschaft vida (union side)
- https://www.kollektivvertrag.at/ — Austrian collective agreement register

**Last verified: 2026-06-02**

---

## Overview

Austria has a national collective agreement for workers in the **Güterbeförderungsgewerbe** (road freight transport industry): the **Kollektivvertrag für Arbeiterinnen und Arbeiter im Güterbeförderungsgewerbe**. It is negotiated between the **Wirtschaftskammer Österreich (WKO)** on the employer side and the **Gewerkschaft vida** on the union side.

The agreement is **automatically generally binding** in Austria. Under Austrian law, WKO membership is **compulsory** for all registered businesses, and WKO has the exclusive right to represent employers in collective bargaining. This means the Kollektivvertrag applies to **every employer** in the sector — there is no opt-out. There is no separate "allgemeinverbindlich" declaration required.

---

## Scope

Covers all workers employed by Austrian enterprises carrying out:
- Professional road freight transport (gewerbliche Güterbeförderung)
- Removal transport (Umzugsunternehmen — separate annex may apply)
- Related logistics activities at transport enterprises

Does not cover: bus/coach drivers (separate KV), taxi (separate KV), own-account transport.

---

## Framework

The Kollektivvertrag is renegotiated **annually** (Herbstlohnrunde — autumn wage round), effective typically from 1 January. It covers:

| Topic | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Minimum wages (Mindestlöhne) | Wage scales by driver category — C, CE licence, vehicle GVW, local/long-haul |
| Overtime (Überstunden) | Premium rates above standard 40-hour week |
| Night work (Nachtarbeit) | Supplement for work between 20:00–06:00 |
| Weekend premiums | Saturday afternoon and Sunday supplements |
| Public holiday work (Feiertagszuschlag) | 100% supplement on holiday work |
| Travel allowances (Reisegebühren) | Per diem and overnight allowances for drivers away from home |
| Annual leave (Urlaub) | Minimum 5 weeks (25 days) after 25 years service — statutory Urlaubsgesetz baseline |
| Sick pay (Entgeltfortzahlung) | Per ANGG: employer pays during illness; scales by years of service |
| Pension | BUAK (Bauarbeiter-Urlaubs- und Abfertigungskasse) is NOT relevant here; vida sectoral pension schemes apply |

> **Note:** Specific current wage rates change annually. Verify current rates via WKO (wko.at) or Gewerkschaft vida (vida.at).

### Annual Leave

Under the Austrian **Urlaubsgesetz**:
- 5 weeks (25 working days) per year for employees with less than 25 years of service
- 6 weeks (30 working days) after 25 years of service

The Kollektivvertrag cannot provide less than this statutory minimum.

### Sick Pay (Entgeltfortzahlung)

Austrian sick pay under the **ANGG (Angestelltengesetz)** and **AngG** / **ArbeiterInnen equivalent**:

| Illness duration | Employer pays |
|---|---|
| Up to 6 weeks (if service < 5 years) | 100% salary |
| 6–12 weeks | 50% salary |
| Longer term | Krankengeld from ÖGK (Austrian Health Insurance Fund) — approx. 60% of daily earnings |

Years of service increase the fully-paid period.

### Travel Allowances (Reisegebühren / Diäten)

Austrian tax law sets the following tax-free allowances (Taggeld):

| Situation | Tax-free daily amount |
|---|---|
| Travel within Austria (over 3 hours from home) | EUR 26.40 per day (full day) |
| International travel | Higher rates per country (set by Kollektivvertrag and tax authority) |
| Overnight stay away from home | EUR 15.00 Nächtigungsgeld (if no employer-paid accommodation) |

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| Arbeitsinspektorat | Working conditions and labour law enforcement |
| Finanzpolizei | Minimum wage enforcement (lohnabgaben); posted workers |
| Arbeits- und Sozialgericht (ASG) | Labour court for collective agreement disputes |
| Gewerkschaft vida | Union enforcement via civil law |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below Kollektivvertrag minimum wage | Back pay; Arbeitsinspektorat report | AVRAG (Lohn- und Sozialdumping-Bekämpfungsgesetz) |
| Social dumping (posted workers underpaid) | Fines up to EUR 50,000 per worker; cumulative caps | LSDB-G (Lohn- und Sozialdumping-Bekämpfungsgesetz) |
| Not paying overtime or weekend premiums | Civil liability via ASG | Kollektivvertrag |
