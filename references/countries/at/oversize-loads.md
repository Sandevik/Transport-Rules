# Austria — Oversize and Exceptional Loads (Sondertransporte)

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/oversize-loads.md — no harmonised EU permit system; Austria operates its own regime]

**Sources:**

## Overview

In Austria, exceptional transport is regulated as **Sondertransport** under §79 StVO and §104 KFG. The competent permitting authority depends on which roads are traversed:

- **Federal motorways (Autobahnen):** Landeshauptmann of the relevant province (or ASFINAG for certain processes)
- **Federal roads (Bundesstraßen):** Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde (district authority)
- **Provincial roads (Landesstraßen):** Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde

For multi-province routes, applications must be filed with each relevant authority, or a lead authority may coordinate. Austria is working toward a centralised portal but as of 2026 the system remains fragmented compared to Germany's VEMAGS.

## Standard Limits (§104 KFG)

| Parameter | Standard Austrian limit |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Gross vehicle weight | 40 t (road train) |
| Single axle load | 10 t (non-drive) / 11.5 t (drive) |
| Tandem axle group | 18 t |
| Width | 2.55 m |
| Height | 4.00 m |
| Length (articulated) | 16.50 m |
| Length (road train) | 18.75 m |

## Thresholds Triggering a Permit

A Sondertransportgenehmigung (§79 StVO) is required when any standard limit is exceeded. Additional conditions by dimension range:

| Width range | Condition |
|-------------|-----------|
| 2.55–3.0 m | Permit required, no mandatory escort |
| 3.0–4.0 m | Permit + 1 escort vehicle (Begleitfahrzeug) |
| 4.0–5.0 m | Permit + 2 escort vehicles (front and rear) |
| > 5.0 m | Permit + police escort (Polizeibegleitung) mandatory |

| Weight / length range | Condition |
|----------------------|-----------|
| > 40 t total | Permit required |
| > 100 t total | Likely police escort; individual bridge assessments mandatory |
| > 18.75 m length | Permit required |
| > 30 m convoy | 1–2 escort vehicles |

## Permit Application

### Where to apply

- Determine all roads on planned route
- For motorway segments: contact ASFINAG (coordinates with Landeshauptmann)
- For district/provincial roads: Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde in each affected district
- Multi-province transport: file separately per province, or use a permit service

### Required documentation

1. Vehicle and combination technical data (axle loads, tyre specifications, turning radius)
2. Load specifications (dimensions, weight, indivisibility declaration)
3. Planned route with road numbers
4. Escort vehicle details
5. Route survey report (for very heavy loads affecting bridges)

### Processing time

- Standard applications: **5–10 working days**
- Complex loads (bridges, tunnels, multiple provinces): **3–6 weeks**
- Urgent processing: possible but at premium fee (Eilgenehmigung)

---

## Permit Types

### Einzelgenehmigung (Single-trip permit)
- Issued for one specific journey on a defined route
- Contains exact route, date/time window, speed limits, escort conditions

### Dauergenehmigung (Standing/annual permit)
- For regular transports with uniform parameters
- Typically up to 12 months
- Usually limited to moderate dimensions (width ≤ 3.5 m, weight ≤ 60 t)
- Driver must carry current permit and comply with all stated conditions

## Mountain Road Specifics

Austria has many Alpine roads where exceptional transport faces additional constraints:

### Brenner Pass / A13 Brenner Autobahn
- Heavy/wide loads: ASFINAG permit required; escort mandatory for width > 3.0 m
- Night ban applies; extreme weather may trigger emergency stops
- Brenner corridor is heavily trafficked; time-slot coordination required

### Arlberg Pass / A14
- Width restrictions due to tunnel sections (Arlberg Tunnel 13.97 km)
- See Arlberg Tunnel section below

### Karawanken Tunnel (A11, AT–SI border)
- Height clearance: 4.5 m
- Width of carriageway restricts abnormal loads; permit required and ASFINAG coordination with Slovenian authorities for cross-border moves

### Alpine road bends (Kehren)
- Very long loads (> 25 m) may not be physically able to negotiate Alpine switchbacks
- Route survey mandatory; alternative routes (rail flatcar) may be required

### Height clearances on Alpine roads
- Overhead power lines on mountain passes: check clearance (generally ≥ 5.5 m required for abnormal height loads)
- GPS-only routing is insufficient; physical route survey essential

## Tunnel Restrictions

### Arlberg Tunnel (13.97 km on A14, ADR category C)
- Max speed: 80 km/h
- Minimum following distance: 150 m for trucks
- ADR dangerous goods: Category C applies (certain explosives and high-danger goods prohibited)
- Exceptional loads: ASFINAG permit required; convoy system applies during peak hours
- Temperature monitoring at tunnel entrance for overheated brakes/exhausts

### Karawanken Tunnel (7.86 km on A11, ADR category C)
- Max speed: 80 km/h
- ADR dangerous goods: Category C restrictions
- Exceptional loads: bilateral AT–SI authority coordination required

### Tauern Tunnel (6.4 km on A10)
- Exceptional loads: ASFINAG coordination required
- Height clearance: 4.5 m
- One-way convoy operation during maintenance periods

## Speed Limits

| Load category | Speed limit |
|---------------|------------|
| Standard exceptional load (≤ 3.5 m wide) | 70–80 km/h or posted limit |
| Wide load 3.5–4.5 m | 60 km/h |
| Wide load > 4.5 m | 40–50 km/h |
| Very heavy loads > 100 t | 40–60 km/h (route-specific) |

## Time Restrictions

| Period | Restriction |
|--------|------------|
| Sunday all day | Exceptional transport prohibited (HGV driving ban §42 StVO applies) |
| Public holidays | Same as Sunday |
| Night (22:00–05:00) | Prohibited for most exceptional loads; permit may specify daytime only |
| Saturday 15:00–24:00 (summer peak) | Restrictions on Brenner and main Alpine corridors |
| Special events / ski season | Additional restrictions on Alpine corridors by Landeshauptmann decree |

## Escort Vehicle Requirements

### Escort vehicle (Begleitfahrzeug) specifications
- Amber rotating or flashing beacon (Rundumlicht) minimum 360° visibility
- "SCHWERTRANSPORT" / "SONDERTRANSPORT" sign boards (approved size: 1,000 × 400 mm front and rear)
- Radio communication capability
- Escort driver certification: required per Sondertransportverordnung; course through ÖAMTC or equivalent

### Pilot car positioning
- Front escort: approaches intersections, roundabouts, railway crossings first
- Rear escort: controls following traffic, alerts of hazards
- Additional escorts for very long or wide loads as specified in permit

## Fees

Austrian permit fees are set by regulation and vary by:
- Route length
- Load dimensions
- Number of administrative jurisdictions

| Indicative fee type | Range |
|--------------------|-------|
| Standard single-trip permit (motorway) | €50–€200 |
| Multi-province single-trip permit | €100–€500 |
| Annual standing permit | €200–€800 |
| Bridge load assessment | €200–€600 per structure |

ASFINAG charges a **supplemental toll** on motorways for exceptional transport movements over and above the standard GO Toll.

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Consequences |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Operating without Sondertransportgenehmigung | Up to €5,000 | Vehicle immobilisation |
| Exceeding permit conditions (route, time, speed) | €365–€2,180 | — |
| Exceeding width without permit | Up to €2,180 | Immobilisation |
| Exceeding weight limits (§134 KFG) | Up to €5,000 | Immobilisation; bridge damage liability |
| Driving during prohibited hours (Sunday ban) | Up to €726 | — |
| No valid escort when required | Up to €726 | — |
| Bridge damage from overloaded vehicle | Unlimited civil liability | Criminal prosecution possible |

Penalties are imposed by the Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde. Foreign carriers must pay a security deposit (Sicherheitsleistung) at the roadside to guarantee payment.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Bundespolizei | Roadside checks, escort for police-escorted loads |
| BALM (German equivalent = ASFINAG in AT for motorways) | On-motorway enforcement coordination |
