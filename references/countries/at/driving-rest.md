## Overview

Austria applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without national deviations. Enforcement is by the BMK (Bundesministerium für Klimaschutz) and the police (Bundespolizei). Austria is a major transit country for Alpine routes and enforcement is frequent, especially on the A12 Inntal motorway (Brenner corridor), A10 Tauern motorway, and at the Karawanken tunnel.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Austria fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

---

## Alpine-Specific Rules

Austria has **sector driving bans (Sektorales Fahrverbot)** on certain Alpine motorways restricting the transit of HGVs carrying specific goods (e.g., certain waste, steel, construction materials) — these are separate from driving/rest time rules and relate to environmental transit restrictions. See BMK for current list.

**Night driving bans:** Austria prohibits HGV driving on certain Alpine routes at night (22:00–05:00) and on weekends. These are **traffic restriction rules**, not driving/rest rules.
