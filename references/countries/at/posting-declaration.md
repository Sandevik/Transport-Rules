## Overview

Austria implements EU Directive 2020/1057/EU through the EFZR (Errichtungsgesetz zur zentralen Koordinationsstelle des Bundesministeriums für Finanzen für die Kontrolle illegaler Beschäftigung — ZKO). Foreign carriers performing cabotage or posting drivers to Austria must submit a ZKO declaration via the online portal before the posting begins. Austria enforces minimum wage requirements and social security contributions for posted workers.

## Who Must Declare

| Operation | Declaration required | Portal |
|---|---|---|
| Cabotage operations in Austria | **Yes** — from day 1 | ZKO (zka.at) |
| International transit (no loading/unloading in AT) | **No** | — |
| Bilateral AT ↔ home country | **No** | — |
| Third-country transit through Austria | **No** | — |

## ZKO Declaration Requirements

**Portal:** [https://www.zka.at](https://www.zka.at) (Zentrale Koordinationsstelle — ZKO4 for road transport)

| Field required | Details |
|---|---|
| Employer details | Company name, address, country, registration number |
| Driver identity | Name, date of birth, nationality |
| Posting period | Start and end dates |
| Austrian work location | Route/region of operation |
| Austrian minimum wage confirmation | Commitment to pay AT minimum wage (KV Transportgewerbe) |

**Documents in vehicle (during posting):**
- Copy of ZKO declaration (confirmation from portal)
- Employment contract or equivalent document
- Payslips covering the posting period (or evidence of wage calculation)
- Tachograph data accessible

## Austrian Minimum Wage for Transport

Austria's minimum wage for road transport drivers is set by the **Kollektivvertrag für das Güterbeförderungsgewerbe** (collective agreement for freight transport), updated annually. Foreign carriers must pay at least this rate for the Austrian portion of the work.

- Current collective agreement rates: [Wirtschaftskammer Österreich (WKO)](https://www.wko.at)
- Rate applies only for days/hours worked in Austria

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| No ZKO declaration | €500–€5,000 (first offence); up to €10,000 (repeat) | LSD-BG §26 |
| Missing documents in vehicle | €500–€2,000 | LSD-BG |
| Underpayment of Austrian minimum wage | €500–€5,000 per driver | AVRAG |
| Obstruction of inspection | €1,000–€5,000 | LSD-BG |
