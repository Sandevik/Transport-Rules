## Overview

Austria applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Kraftfahrgesetz 1967 (KFG). Austria has no special large vehicle programme analogous to the Nordic exception or German Lang-Lkw. The key national features are strict Brenner corridor restrictions on HGV transit and a comprehensive night/weekend driving ban system.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit | Legal basis |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** | §4 KFG 1967 |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** | Directive 92/106/EEC |
| Max width | **2.55 m** | §4 KFG 1967 |
| Max width (refrigerated) | **2.60 m** | §4 KFG 1967 |
| Max height | **4.00 m** | §4 KFG 1967 |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** | §4 KFG 1967 |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** | §4 KFG 1967 |

## Axle Weight Limits

| Axle type | Max load |
|-----------|----------|
| Single driving axle | 11.5 t |
| Non-driving single axle | 10.0 t |
| Tandem axle | 18.0 t (19.0 t air suspension) |
| Tridem axle | 24.0 t |

## Brenner Corridor Restrictions

Austria imposes special restrictions on HGV traffic on the Brenner corridor (A13) beyond standard dimension/weight rules:
- **Sectoral driving ban** restricts certain cargo categories (see emissions.md)
- **Night driving ban** (22:00–05:00) applies on the Brenner as on all Austrian roads
- **Dosierungssystem** (dosing system): limits the number of HGVs entering the Brenner per hour during peak periods

---

## Oversize/Overweight Loads

Exceptional transport permits issued by:
- **Bezirksverwaltungsbehörde** (district authority) for local/regional routes
- **Landesstraßenverwaltung** (state road authority) for major routes
- ASFINAG authorization required for motorway sections

Escort vehicles (Begleitfahrzeuge) required for loads >3.5m wide or >30m long.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant weight/dimension deviations. Austria applies EU standard limits strictly.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding GVW by up to 10% | €363–€726 | §134 KFG 1967 |
| Exceeding GVW by >20% | €726–€5,000 | §134 KFG 1967 |
| Exceeding width/length without permit | €363–€5,000 | §134 KFG 1967 |
