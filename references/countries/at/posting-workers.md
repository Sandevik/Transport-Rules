## Overview

Austria implements Directive 2020/1057/EU via the AVRAG (Arbeitsvertragsrechts-Anpassungsgesetz) and LSD-BG (Lohn- und Sozialdumping-Bekämpfungsgesetz). The declaration portal EFZR is covered in posting-declaration.md. This file covers minimum pay obligations, equal treatment rights, and enforcement. The Finanzpolizei (Finance Police) is the primary enforcement body for wage and working condition inspections.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Declaration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Austria | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in AT + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Austria only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in Austria) | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay Obligations

Austria has one of the most complex minimum pay systems in the EU due to its extensive collective agreement (Kollektivvertrag) structure:

**Sector collective agreement for road transport:**
- **Kollektivvertrag für das Güterbeförderungsgewerbe** sets minimum wages for freight drivers
- Rate depends on vehicle weight category and activity type
- Published annually by the Wirtschaftskammer Österreich (WKO)
- Current rates: check https://www.wko.at/branchen/transport-verkehr

Drivers from other member states performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the Austrian Kollektivvertrag rate for road transport.

**Anti-Social Dumping Law (LSD-BG):**
Austria has strict anti-social dumping rules (Lohn- und Sozialdumping-Bekämpfungsgesetz). Employers who underpay posted workers face severe penalties, and liability extends up in the subcontracting chain.

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry and present on request:
- Proof of EFZR declaration (see posting-declaration.md)
- Employment contract showing at least Austrian Kollektivvertrag wages
- Pay slips for the preceding period
- A1 social security certificate

---

## Equal Treatment (After 12 Months)

After 12 continuous months of posting, Austrian labour law applies in full, including:
- Austrian holiday law (Urlaubsgesetz)
- Austrian working time law (AZG)
- Austrian occupational safety standards

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wage (per employee) | €1,000–€50,000 | LSD-BG §26 |
| Failure to maintain pay records | €1,000–€10,000 | LSD-BG §28 |
| Failure to register posting (EFZR) | €1,000–€10,000 | AVRAG §7i |
| Repeat violations | Doubled penalty + business prohibition | LSD-BG |
