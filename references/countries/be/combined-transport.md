## Overview

Belgium is a major European logistics hub with an advanced combined transport network. The ports of Antwerp and Ghent are key intermodal gateways, and Belgium has an extensive rail freight network operated primarily by Lineas (formerly B Logistics). Belgium implemented Directive 92/106/EEC via national transport law. While Belgium does not have general HGV weekend driving bans, the combined transport weight allowance and documentation requirements are the primary operational benefits.

---

## Weight Allowance

Vehicles operating as part of a qualifying combined transport journey with 5+ axles may operate at **44 tonnes** (vs. standard 40t). This is particularly relevant for container transport to/from Antwerp port.

---

## Driving Conditions

Belgium does not operate general HGV weekend or night driving bans (unlike Germany, France, or Italy), so the driving ban exemption under 92/106/EEC is less critical here than in neighbouring countries. However, the weight allowance and documentation are operationally important.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail/waterway terminal confirmation confirming non-road leg booking/completion
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC. Belgium fully implements the directive including the 44t weight allowance and driving ban exemption provisions.
