## Overview

Belgium implements posting of workers via the **LIMOSA** system (Landenoverschrijdend Informatiesysteem Ten behoeve van MigratieOnderzoek bij de Sociale Administratie). Foreign employers, agents, and self-employed persons must submit a declaration **before work commences** in Belgium.

---

## When Declaration Is Required

| Operation | Required? |
|-----------|----------|
| Cabotage in Belgium | **YES** |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in Belgium + third country) | **YES** |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Belgium only) | **NO** |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in Belgium) | **NO** |

---

## How to Submit

1. Access LIMOSA via the Belgian Social Security portal: https://www.socialsecurity.be/
2. Declaration must be submitted **before work starts**
3. Required information: employer/company details, worker details, vehicle, period, type of transport

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry (and present on request):
- Proof of LIMOSA declaration
- CMR waybill
- Evidence of applicable Belgian minimum pay/conditions

---

## Minimum Pay

Belgium has sector-specific minimum wages for transport. Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least Belgian sector minimum pay (Joint Committee 140 — Road Transport).
