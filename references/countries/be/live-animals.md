## Overview

Belgium applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the FASFC (Federal Agency for the Safety of the Food Chain / Federaal Agentschap voor de Veiligheid van de Voedselketen — FAVV / Agence fédérale pour la Sécurité de la Chaîne alimentaire — AFSCA). Belgium is a major transit country for live cattle and pigs, particularly in the Antwerp port region for third-country imports and exports.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | FASFC |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | FASFC |

Applications via FASFC: https://www.favv-afsca.be/

---

## Border Inspection Posts (BIPs)

Belgium has major BIPs at Antwerp port for third-country live animal imports:
- **Antwerp (Port)**: BIP for bovines, pigs, and other species from third countries
- Zeebrugge: ro-ro BIP for live animals

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy in vehicle
- Driver Certificate of Competence (CoC)
- Journey log (for journeys >8 hours)
- Veterinary health certificate
- TRACES veterinary movement documentation

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005. Belgium fully applies the EU standards.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without Type 2 authorisation | Up to €10,000 | Belgian animal welfare law |
| Failure to maintain journey log | Up to €5,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
| Exceeding maximum journey times | Up to €10,000 | Belgian animal welfare law |
