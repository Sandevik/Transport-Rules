## Overview

Belgium enforces EU transport regulations through the Direction Générale Transport Routier (SPF Mobilité). Fines are issued by police (Federal and Local) and transport inspectors. Belgium operates the Viapass toll system; toll evasion carries separate penalties. Roadside immediate payment of fines is **NOT required** — fixed penalty notices (PV) are issued payable within 30 days.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded ≤ 1h | €100–€250 | Loi relative au transport terrestre |
| Daily driving time exceeded > 1h | €250–€1,000 | Loi relative au transport terrestre |
| Insufficient daily rest ≤ 1h | €100–€250 | Belgian transport enforcement law |
| Insufficient daily rest > 1h | €250–€1,000 | Belgian transport enforcement law |
| Insufficient weekly rest | €500–€2,000 | Belgian transport enforcement law |
| Break not taken | €100–€500 | Belgian transport enforcement law |
| Tachograph manipulation | €5,500–€11,000 + prosecution | Penal Code |

## Overload / Dimensions

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Overload ≤ 5% | €500 | Code de la route |
| Overload 5–25% | €500–€2,000 | Code de la route |
| Overload > 25% | €2,000–€5,000 | Code de la route |
| No exceptional transport permit | €1,000–€5,000 | Arrêté royal du 2 juin 2010 |

## Toll Evasion (Viapass)

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Missing OBU or not registered | €1,000 + unpaid toll | Flemish/Walloon/Brussels toll law |
| OBU tampered | €5,000 | Regional toll legislation |
