## Overview

Belgium applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The SPF Mobilité et Transports handles transport emission policy coordination across Belgium's three regions (Brussels Capital, Flanders, Wallonia), each of which operates its own Low Emission Zone (LEZ) systems. Belgium has among the most developed LEZ networks in the EU, affecting HGV access to all three major cities.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Belgian national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

## Regional Low Emission Zones (LEZs)

Belgium's three main LEZs are operated by the respective regional authorities:

### Brussels Capital Region (LEZ Brussels)
- Area: All of Brussels Capital Region (19 communes)
- Applicable to: All vehicles including HGVs and vans
- HGV requirement: **Euro VI** required for access (diesel HGVs >3.5t)
- System: Automatic camera enforcement (ANPR); no physical sticker required — registration checked automatically
- Foreign vehicles: Must register at https://www.lezbrussels.be/ before entering

### Flanders — Antwerp LEZ
- Area: Antwerp city LEZ
- HGV requirement: Euro V (older vehicles require day passes or are banned)
- System: Camera enforcement; registration via https://www.lez.be/

### Flanders — Ghent LEZ
- Area: Ghent LEZ (inner city)
- Similar regime to Antwerp; see https://stad.gent/ for current rules

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Ecologiepremie** (Flanders): subsidy for zero-emission commercial vehicles
- **Brussels ZEV support**: Brussels Region has EV fleet transition programmes
- Federal fiscal deductions available for ZEV trucks (100% tax deductibility)
- Belgium has an **employer mobility budget** system that may support ZEV fleet costs

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. Belgium adds three regional LEZ networks above the EU minimum.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Entering Brussels LEZ with non-compliant HGV | €350 (first offence) | Brussels LEZ Regulation |
| Entering Antwerp LEZ with non-compliant vehicle | €150–€350 | Flemish LEZ Regulation |
| Type-approval non-compliance | Up to €2,500 | Belgian vehicle code |
