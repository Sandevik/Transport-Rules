## Overview

Belgium has a national sector-wide collective agreement for road transport governed by **Paritair Comité 140** (PC 140 in Dutch, Commission Paritaire 140 / CP 140 in French) — the Joint Committee for Road Transport and Logistics. This is the Belgian equivalent of Sweden's Transportavtalet.

Belgian collective agreements concluded in a Joint Committee and registered with the CNT/NAR are **automatically generally binding by Royal Decree** (arrêté royal / koninklijk besluit) when extended. This means PC 140 minimum conditions apply to **all employers in the sector**, not only those affiliated with Febetra.

---

## Scope

PC 140 covers:
- All **blue-collar workers** (arbeiders/ouvriers) employed in road transport and logistics — truck drivers, distribution drivers, warehouse workers at transport companies
- All Belgian-registered employers active in road transport and logistics
- **White-collar workers** (bedienden/employés) in the same sector fall under a separate committee (PC 226 for transport logistics white-collar staff)

---

## Framework

Agreements concluded under PC 140 cover:

| Topic | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Minimum wages (baremieke lonen / barèmes salariaux) | Wage scales by driver category (CE, C, B licence; short-haul/long-haul) |
| Night premiums (nachtpremies / primes de nuit) | Supplement for work during night hours |
| Weekend and public holiday premiums | OB-equivalent premiums for unsocial hours |
| Overtime | Rates above standard working hours |
| Meal vouchers (maaltijdcheques) | Sector agreement typically includes meal voucher obligation |
| End-of-year premium (eindejaarspremie / prime de fin d'année) | 13th-month equivalent; often 92% or more of a monthly wage |
| Pension (sectoraal aanvullend pensioen / pension sectoriel complémentaire) | Sector supplementary pension fund |
| Sector training fund | Contributions for driver training and CPC periodic training |

> **Note:** Specific current wage rates and allowance amounts are renegotiated periodically (typically annually via interprofessionele loonakkoorden and sector-specific rounds). Verify current rates via Febetra (febetra.be) or ACV BTB (acvbtb.be).

### End-of-Year Premium (Eindejaarspremie)

This is a significant benefit in the Belgian transport sector — drivers receive an additional payment equivalent to approximately 92% of a monthly gross wage, paid in December. Some sector agreements have moved this to 100%.

### Meal Vouchers (Maaltijdcheques)

Belgian transport collective agreements typically include **maaltijdcheques** of EUR 8.00 per working day (employer contribution: max EUR 6.91 tax-exempt; employee contribution: min EUR 1.09). These are tax-advantaged.

## Generally Binding Effect

When the Belgian government extends PC 140 agreements by Royal Decree, they bind **all employers** in the sector including:
- Non-Febetra member companies
- Foreign operators with drivers working in Belgium (combined with the Limosa posting declaration — see `references/countries/be/posting-declaration.md`)

This is directly relevant for Swedish, Dutch, Polish, and other operators whose drivers regularly work in Belgium: they must comply with PC 140 minimum wages.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| Inspectie Welzijn op het Werk / Inspection du Bien-être au Travail (IBW) | Working conditions enforcement; collective agreement compliance |
| Rijksdienst voor Sociale Zekerheid (RSZ) / ONSS | Social security contributions |
| ACV BTB / FGTB | Union enforcement via civil law and labour tribunal |
| Arbeidsrechtbank / Tribunal du Travail | Labour court for disputes |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below PC 140 minimum wage | Back pay + administrative fine | Wet 5 december 1968 (collectieve arbeidsovereenkomsten); Royal Decree extension |
| Not paying eindejaarspremie | Civil liability; back payment | PC 140 collective agreement |
| Not issuing meal vouchers | Back pay; RSZ implications | Sector collective agreement |
| Breach of sector pension obligations | Back premiums + interest | Wet aanvullende pensioenen (WAP) |
