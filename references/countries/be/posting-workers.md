## Overview

Belgium implements Directive 2020/1057/EU via the Wet betreffende de terbeschikkingstelling van werknemers. The LIMOSA declaration portal is covered in posting-declaration.md. This file focuses on minimum pay obligations, equal treatment rights, and enforcement. The social inspection bodies (SIOD / Social Information and Investigation Service) enforce wage compliance.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Declaration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Belgium | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in BE + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Belgium only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in Belgium) | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay — Joint Committee 140 (Road Transport)

Belgium's transport sector minimum wages are set by **Joint Committee (Paritair Comité) 140 — Road Transport and Logistics**:

- Gross hourly minimum rates vary by driver category (heavy goods, van, etc.)
- Published annually by Federal Employment (https://werk.belgie.be/)
- PC 140 rates are generally higher than the Belgian statutory minimum wage
- Current rates: check the official PC 140 collective agreement via www.werk.belgie.be/pc-140

Drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade in Belgium must receive at least the PC 140 rate for the hours worked in Belgium.

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry and present on request:
- LIMOSA declaration proof (see posting-declaration.md)
- Employment contract or payslips showing at least PC 140 minimum wages
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

---

## Equal Treatment After 12 Months

After 12 months of posting (extendable to 18 with justification), Belgian labour law applies in full, including:
- Full Belgian holiday entitlements
- Belgian working time law
- Belgian occupational health and safety requirements

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Belgian inspectors can demand security deposits for wage violations.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay PC 140 minimum wages | €800–€8,000 per worker | Wet 5/3/2002 |
| Failure to submit LIMOSA declaration | €500–€5,000 | LIMOSA regulation |
| Repeated wage violation | Doubled penalty + blacklisting | Social penal code |
