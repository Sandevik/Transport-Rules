## Overview

Belgium issues EU Community Licences through the SPF Mobilité et Transports (Direction des licences de transport). Belgian operators must meet the standard EU four requirements under Regulation (EC) 1071/2009. The professional competence exam (CPC) for transport managers is administered through approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Belgian specifics |
|-------------|----------|--------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Belgian place of business | Belgian address; effective management; registered with Crossroads Bank for Enterprises (CBE) |
| Good repute | No serious criminal/transport offences | Checked via criminal record extract and national transport register |
| Financial standing (international) | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts, bank statement, or guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC examination | ECTS-accredited exam via approved Belgian centres |

## Community Licence

The Community Licence (Communautaire vergunning / Licence communautaire) for international road haulage is issued by SPF Mobilité. Certified true copies are required in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

Under EU 1072/2009 + Mobility Package:
- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Belgium within 7 days following an international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period before next cabotage series in Belgium
- Belgian PC 140 minimum wages apply to cabotage drivers (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Driver Attestation

Belgian attestation (Fahrernachweis / Attestation de conducteur) for non-EU/EEA national drivers issued by SPF Mobilité.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009. Belgium fully implements the EU framework.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | €1,000–€5,000 | Belgian transport code |
| Exceeding cabotage operations | €1,000–€5,000 | Belgian transport code |
| Missing driver attestation | €500–€2,500 | Belgian transport code |
