## Overview

Belgium applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without national deviations. Enforcement is by the SPF Mobilité et Transports (Federal Public Service for Mobility and Transport) and the Belgian police. Belgium is strategically located as a major transit country and enforcement is regular at border crossings and on motorways.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Belgium fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

## Penalties

Full penalty list: https://mobilit.belgium.be/fr/route/conduire/code-de-la-route-violations-et-sanctions/

| Violation category | Consequence |
|-------------------|------------|
| Driving/rest time violations | Fines by category of seriousness (catégories d'infractions) |
| Tachograph violations | Fines + potential driving prohibition |
| Cabotage violations | Fines up to €4,000 per violation |
