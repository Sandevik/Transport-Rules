## Overview

Belgium applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Arrêté royal relatif aux conditions techniques auxquelles doivent répondre les véhicules automobiles (Royal Decree on technical requirements for motor vehicles). Belgium has no special large vehicle programme. The Viapass electronic tolling system applies to vehicles >3.5t on all public roads (Brussels + Flanders) or motorways (Wallonia).

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Oversize/overweight loads require permits from:
- **Federal Road Administration** for roads under federal competence
- **Regional road authorities** (Flanders/Wallonia/Brussels) for regional roads
- Advance notice required for motorway sections managed by SOFICO (Wallonia) or AWV (Flanders)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations. Belgium applies EU standard limits.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding max GVW (up to 5%) | €200–€500 | Belgian traffic code |
| Exceeding max GVW (>20%) | €1,000–€5,000 + possible immobilisation | Belgian traffic code |
| Operating oversize without permit | €500–€2,500 | Belgian traffic code |
