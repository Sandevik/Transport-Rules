## Overview

Montenegro has national professional driver competence requirements. EU Directive 2003/59/EC (Driver CPC) does not apply directly. Drivers from EU/EEA countries with a valid Driver CPC generally operate under their home country CPC for international operations. Bilateral recognition agreements may apply.
