## Overview

Montenegro is an **AETR signatory** and applies the AETR Agreement for international road transport driving and rest time. EU Regulation 561/2006 does not apply directly. AETR rules are substantively identical to EU 561/2006 limits. Enforcement is by the national transport ministry and traffic police.

---

## Core Rules (AETR — identical to EU 561/2006)

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Daily driving (normal) | 9 hours |
| Daily driving (extended, max 2×/week) | 10 hours |
| Weekly driving | 56 hours |
| Two-week driving | 90 hours |
| Break after 4.5 h driving | 45 min (or 15+30 split) |
| Regular daily rest | 11 consecutive hours |
| Reduced daily rest | 9 consecutive hours (max 3×/week) |
| Weekly rest (normal) | 45 consecutive hours |
| Weekly rest (reduced) | 24 consecutive hours |

See `references/aetr/driving-rest-time.md` for full AETR rule text.

---

## Key AETR Rule: No Advance Payment at Roadside

**AETR Art. 22ter:** Advance cash payment (deposit) at roadside is **PROHIBITED** for AETR violations.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Exceeding daily driving limit | Fine — national enforcement law | AETR Art. 19 |
| Insufficient rest period | Fine + possible immobilisation | AETR Art. 19 |
