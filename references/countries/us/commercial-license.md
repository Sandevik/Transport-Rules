## Overview

US commercial transport licensing has two components: (1) **CDL** for individual drivers, and (2) **Operating Authority** (MC number) + **USDOT Number** for carriers.

---

## Commercial Driver's License (CDL)

Required for drivers operating CMVs:
- ≥26,001 lbs GVW/GVWR, OR
- Transporting 16+ passengers (including driver), OR
- Transporting hazardous materials requiring placarding

**CDL Classes:**
| Class | Vehicles |
|-------|---------|
| Class A | Combination vehicles ≥26,001 lbs, towed unit >10,000 lbs |
| Class B | Single vehicle ≥26,001 lbs, towed unit ≤10,000 lbs |
| Class C | Vehicles <26,001 lbs carrying hazmat or 16+ passengers |

**Endorsements** (add-ons): T (doubles/triples), N (tank vehicles), H (hazmat), X (tank + hazmat), P (passengers), S (school bus).

---

## USDOT Number

All carriers operating CMVs in interstate commerce must register with FMCSA and obtain a **USDOT Number** (US Department of Transportation identification number).

Register at: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/

---

## Motor Carrier (MC) Number / Operating Authority

Carriers transporting regulated commodities or passengers for hire in interstate commerce must also obtain **Operating Authority** (MC number):
- Required for: for-hire passenger carriers; household goods carriers; certain freight brokers and freight forwarders

Register at: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/get-mc-number-authority

---

## Insurance Requirements

- Minimum **$750,000** public liability insurance for general freight CMVs
- **$1,000,000** for HazMat (non-bulk)
- **$5,000,000** for HazMat (bulk)
