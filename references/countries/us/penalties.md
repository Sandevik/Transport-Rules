## Overview

US transport penalties operate at two levels: **federal** (FMCSA civil penalties for interstate commerce) and **state** (each state sets its own fines for state road violations). FMCSA enforces Hours of Service, tachograph (ELD), and safety regulations through civil penalties and safety ratings. State police enforce weight, dimension, and state-specific rules with immediate citation or roadside inspection orders. Federal penalties are assessed through formal proceedings; state fines are typically paid through state courts or magistrates. The US does not use criminal prosecution for typical HOS violations (unlike some EU jurisdictions).

## FMCSA Federal Civil Penalties (Interstate Commerce)

| Violation | Penalty (USD) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| HOS violation (single) | $1,000–$16,000 | 49 USC 521(b); 49 CFR Part 395 |
| ELD non-compliance | $1,000–$16,000 per day | 49 CFR Part 395.8 |
| Falsification of records (HOS) | Up to $16,000 per violation | 49 USC 521(b)(2)(B) |
| Operating OOS vehicle (driver) | $1,000–$16,000 | 49 CFR Part 396 |
| Unsatisfactory safety rating — continued operation | Up to $32,000/day | 49 USC 521(b)(2)(A) |
| Pattern of HOS violations (carrier) | $10,000–$500,000 (settlement) | 49 USC 521 |
| Imminent hazard order violation | Up to $32,000/day | 49 USC 521 |

## Overweight — Federal (Interstate)

| Violation | Penalty (USD) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Interstate GVW > 80,000 lbs | State law applies; no federal fine scale | 23 USC 127 |
| Bridge formula violation | Civil liability for bridge damage | 23 USC 127; common law |

*Note: Federal law does not set specific dollar amounts for interstate overweight — states impose fines on their roads and enforce federal limits on Interstate.*

## CVSA Out-of-Service (Roadside)

CVSA inspectors can place vehicles or drivers **Out of Service (OOS)** immediately for:
- HOS violations (driver OOS — may not drive until sufficient rest)
- Critical vehicle defects (vehicle OOS — may not move until repaired)
- Missing/invalid ELD records

Operating a vehicle under OOS order: up to $16,000 federal fine + state penalties.

## Safety Rating Consequences

Carriers with **"Unsatisfactory" safety rating** or **conditional** rating under FMCSA's Safety Fitness Determination:
- Cannot operate in interstate commerce if Unsatisfactory
- May be required to demonstrate corrective action
- Ratings affect insurance premiums and shipper contracts
