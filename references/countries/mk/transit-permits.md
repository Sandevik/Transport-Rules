# North Macedonia — Transit Permits and International Road Transport

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national rules. North Macedonia is an EU accession candidate (candidate status since 2005; accession negotiations opened 2022 under Prespa Agreement framework).

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

**Sources:**
- https://mtc.gov.mk/ — Ministry of Transport and Communications — international road transport permits

## Overview

International road freight to, from, and through North Macedonia requires either a **bilateral road transport permit** or an **ITF/CEMT multilateral permit**. EU Community Licences do not provide automatic market access.

North Macedonia is a **full member of the International Transport Forum (ITF, formerly CEMT)** — ITF/CEMT multilateral permits are the primary mechanism for EU hauliers. North Macedonia is also a **NATO member** (since 2020).

North Macedonia is a strategically important transit country on the **E-75 corridor** (Corridor X: Salzburg–Ljubljana–Zagreb–Belgrade–Niš–Skopje–Thessaloniki), the primary north–south road freight route between Central Europe and Greece. It lies between Serbia (north) and Greece (south), making it a critical transit link.

The national currency is the **MKD (Macedonian denar)**.

---

## ITF/CEMT Multilateral Permits

North Macedonia is a **full ITF member**. ITF/CEMT permits are valid for bilateral transport and transit:

| Permit Type | Validity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Annual ITF permit | Calendar year | Minimum Euro III standard |
| Short-term ITF permit (30-day) | 30 consecutive days | |

Cabotage within North Macedonia is not permitted under ITF permits.

---

## Bilateral Road Transport Permits

North Macedonia has bilateral road transport agreements with EU member states and neighbouring countries.

| Permit Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Bilateral permit | For direct transport between North Macedonia and partner country |
| Transit permit | For vehicles transiting North Macedonia without loading/unloading |
| Third-country permit | For transport involving a third country |

Key bilateral partners: Greece, Bulgaria, Serbia, Kosovo, Albania, all EU member states.

### EU Member State Relationships

All EU member states have bilateral road transport agreements with North Macedonia. Greece and Bulgaria, as neighbouring EU member states, have particularly significant freight relationships. Quotas are exchanged annually.

---

## TIR Convention

North Macedonia is a **contracting party to the TIR Convention**. TIR Carnets are used for international transit, particularly for traffic between EU, Turkey, and the Middle East using the E-75/Corridor X route.

---

## Corridor X — E-75 Transit Through North Macedonia

The E-75 European motorway/road is the backbone of north–south transit:

- **Entry from Serbia:** Tabanovce/Preševo border crossing (A1)
- **Transit:** Kumanovo → Skopje → Veles → Gevgelija
- **Exit to Greece:** Bogorodica/Evzoni border crossing (A1) — major EU entry/exit point

This crossing is one of the busiest for EU–Turkey road freight (EU → North Macedonia → Greece → Turkey via Kapikule).

### Key Border Crossings

| Crossing | Partner Country | Route | Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------|-------|
| Tabanovce | Serbia | A1/E-75 | Main north freight |
| Bogorodica | Greece | A1/E-75 | Main south freight; EU border |
| Deve Bair | Bulgaria | E-871 | |
| Blace | Kosovo | M25 | |
| Kafasan | Albania | — | |
| Stenje | Albania | Lake Ohrid area | Secondary |

**Key note:** The Bogorodica–Evzoni crossing is on the main Turkey–EU freight corridor; significant traffic volumes, particularly Turkish-registered vehicles. Delays can be significant at peak periods.

## Penalties

Penalties under North Macedonian road transport law. Amounts in MKD (Macedonian denar).

| Violation | Penalty (MKD) | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Operating without required permit | 30,000–80,000 MKD | Vehicle detention possible |
| Permit conditions exceeded | 15,000–50,000 MKD | |
| AETR tachograph violation | 10,000–40,000 MKD | |
| Overloaded vehicle | Variable per tonne excess | Road damage levy may apply |
| Cabotage violation | 50,000–100,000 MKD | |

*Note: MKD amounts are indicative; verify current rates with the Ministry of Transport and Communications.*
