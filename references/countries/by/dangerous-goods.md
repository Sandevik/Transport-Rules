## Overview

Belarus is a contracting party to the ADR Convention (Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road). ADR 2025 applies to international dangerous goods transport. National implementing legislation may impose additional requirements. Verify current ADR signatory status at UNECE.

---

## ADR Scope

ADR applies to road transport of dangerous goods exceeding exemption thresholds. Key requirements:
- Vehicle approval (ADR certificate)
- Driver training (ADR certificate — valid 5 years)
- Proper packaging, labelling and documentation
- Emergency information (TREMCARD)

## Driver ADR Training

| Class | Training Required | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| All classes | Basic ADR training | 5 years |
| Tanker operations | Additional tank training | 5 years |
| Class 1 (explosives) | Explosives module | 5 years |
| Class 7 (radioactive) | Radioactive module | 5 years |
