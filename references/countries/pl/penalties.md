## Overview

Poland enforces EU transport regulations through GITD (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego — Road Transport Inspectorate) and Policja. Poland has one of the most comprehensive penalty catalogues in the EU with detailed graduated fines. Penalties are expressed in PLN. GITD inspectors can impose fines at roadside — immediate payment is possible; non-payment can lead to vehicle immobilisation. Poland applies both driver and operator liability.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations

| Violation | Fine — Driver (PLN) | Fine — Operator (PLN) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded 1–2h | PLN 100–200 | PLN 2,000 | Załącznik nr 1 do ustawy o transporcie drogowym |
| Daily driving time exceeded 2–3h | PLN 200–500 | PLN 4,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Daily driving time exceeded > 3h | PLN 500–2,000 | PLN 8,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Insufficient daily rest ≤ 1h | PLN 100–200 | PLN 2,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Insufficient daily rest 1–3h | PLN 200–500 | PLN 4,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Insufficient daily rest > 3h | PLN 500–2,000 | PLN 8,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Weekly rest too short | PLN 200–3,000 | PLN 8,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Break not taken | PLN 100–500 | PLN 2,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Tachograph not used | PLN 500–2,000 | PLN 8,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Tachograph manipulation (driver) | PLN 3,000 | — | Załącznik nr 1 |
| Tachograph manipulation (operator) | — | PLN 10,000 | Załącznik nr 1 |

*Maximum operator fine per GITD inspection: PLN 15,000 (single inspection); can accumulate per violation.*
