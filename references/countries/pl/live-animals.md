## Overview

Poland applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the GIW (Główny Lekarz Weterynarii / Chief Veterinary Inspectorate) at national level, with enforcement by Powiatowe Inspektoraty Weterynarii (County Veterinary Inspectorates) at local level. Poland is one of the EU's largest livestock producers and a major source of live cattle, pigs, and poultry exports.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | Powiatowy Lekarz Weterynarii |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | Powiatowy Lekarz Weterynarii |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Świadectwo zdrowia (veterinary health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | PLN 500–PLN 5,000 | Ustawa o ochronie zdrowia zwierząt |
| Welfare violations | PLN 500–PLN 10,000 | Ustawa o ochronie zwierząt |
| Failure to maintain journey log | PLN 500–PLN 2,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
