**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Poland applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Prawo o ruchu drogowym (Road Traffic Act) and implementing regulations. Poland has no special large vehicle programme. Poland's extensive road network, including developing expressways (S-roads) and motorways (A-roads), generally accommodates EU-standard HGVs.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Road Network Classification

Poland classifies roads by maximum axle weights:
- **Category A**: motorways and expressways — standard EU axle limits
- **Category B**: national and regional roads — lower axle limits apply on some sections
- E-TOLL system applies to vehicles >3.5t on motorways and expressways (electronic toll)

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits issued by GDDKiA (Generalna Dyrekcja Dróg Krajowych i Autostrad) for national roads. Application required in advance. Heavy convoy (transport nienormatywny) rules apply.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding max GVW (up to 5%) | PLN 500–PLN 5,000 | Prawo o ruchu drogowym |
| Exceeding max GVW (>10%) | PLN 5,000–PLN 15,000 + vehicle immobilisation | Prawo o ruchu drogowym |
| Operating oversize without permit | PLN 5,000–PLN 15,000 | Prawo o ruchu drogowym |
