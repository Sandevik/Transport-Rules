## Overview

Poland's exceptional transport system is governed by the **Ustawa o ruchu drogowym** (Road Traffic Act) and managed by **GDDKiA** for national roads and **Marshal's offices** (Urzędy Marszałkowskie) for regional roads. Poland is a significant transit country for exceptional industrial loads (wind energy components, transformers, construction equipment) moving between Eastern European manufacturing and Western European installation sites.

## Categories and Thresholds

| Category | Width | Length | Height | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Category I | 2.56–3.0 m | 15.01–20.0 m | Up to 4.5 m | Up to 40t |
| Category II | 3.01–3.6 m | 20.01–30.0 m | 4.5–4.8 m | 40–60t |
| Category III | 3.61–4.6 m | 30.01–40.0 m | 4.8–5.5 m | 60–100t |
| Category IV | > 4.6 m | > 40.0 m | > 5.5 m | > 100t |

---

## Permit Procedure

Applications submitted to:
- **GDDKiA** for national roads (S-roads, A-roads, express roads)
- **Marshal's office** for voivodeship (regional) roads
- Municipal authority for local roads

**Lead times:**
- Category I–II: 3–7 working days
- Category III–IV: 14–30 working days (route inspection required)

---

## Escort Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Category I | Pilot vehicle not required but recommended for width >3.0 m |
| Category II | 1 pilot vehicle required |
| Category III | 2 pilot vehicles + notification to police |
| Category IV | Police escort (Policja) mandatory |

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement by **Inspekcja Transportu Drogowego (ITD/GITD)** — Road Transport Inspectorate.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without permit | PLN 10,000–20,000 | Art. 140 Ustawa o ruchu drogowym |
| Exceeding permit dimensions | PLN 5,000–15,000 | Art. 140 |
| Missing required escort | PLN 2,000–5,000 | Art. 140 |
