## Overview

Poland applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations. Enforcement is by **GITD** (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego — Chief Inspectorate of Road Transport) whose inspectors (ITD — Inspekcja Transportu Drogowego) conduct roadside checks and company inspections. Poland is a major transit country with very active enforcement. Advance payment (zabezpieczenie) is routinely collected from foreign carriers.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Poland fully applies Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role | Website |
|-----------|------|---------|
| GITD (ITD) | Road Transport Inspectorate — main HGV enforcement body | https://gitd.gov.pl/ |
| Policja | Police — roadside checks | — |

**Advance payment at roadside (zabezpieczenie):** YES — GITD inspectors can require immediate security deposits from foreign carriers for serious violations. Vehicle is immobilised until payment is made. This is one of the most consistently applied advance payment systems in the EU.

**Lookback period:** 56 days (aligned with EU Mobility Package).

## Penalties

Penalties are administered by GITD via the CANARD system (eBOK CANARD portal):

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) |
|-----------|--------------|
| Exceeding daily driving limit | PLN 100–5,000 (per violation, per driver) |
| Insufficient daily rest | PLN 100–3,000 |
| Tachograph card violations | PLN 500–5,000 |
| Missing/invalid tachograph record | PLN 500–3,000 |
| Cabotage violation | Up to PLN 12,000 |
| Operating without transport licence | PLN 8,000 |

Full penalty catalogue: https://ebok.canard.gitd.gov.pl/
