## Overview

Poland implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Poland has a growing intermodal rail and port network. The Baltic Sea ports (Gdańsk, Gdynia) are major EU gateways, and Poland is increasingly central to the Trans-European Transport Network (TEN-T) corridor connecting Western Europe to the East. Poland has no general HGV weekend driving bans at national level, so the driving ban exemption is primarily relevant when Polish trucks operate in Germany or other ban-applying countries.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Terminals

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Gdańsk DCT (Deep Container Terminal) | Gdańsk | Baltic Sea hub; one of Europe's fastest-growing container ports |
| Gdynia port | Gdynia | Container + ro-ro |
| Warsaw (Praga terminal) | Warsaw | Central distribution |
| Łódź (euroterminal) | Łódź | Silk Road (China–Europe) rail connection |
| Gliwice intermodal | Silesia | Industrial hub |
| Małaszewicze | Eastern Poland (Belarusian border) | Gauge-change hub for China–Europe rail (1520mm → 1435mm) |

**Łódź significance**: Łódź/Małaszewicze is a key interchange on the New Silk Road (New Eurasian Land Bridge) from China to Western Europe.

---

## Documentation Required

- Port/rail terminal confirmation document
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
