**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Poland implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the **biznes.gov.pl** portal. Foreign operators sending drivers to perform cabotage or cross-trade transport in Poland must submit a posting declaration. GITD (ITD) inspectors check for posting compliance at roadside.

---

## When Declaration Is Required

| Operation | Required? |
|-----------|----------|
| Cabotage in Poland | **YES** |
| Cross-trade (loading/unloading in Poland + third country) | **YES** |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Poland only) | **NO** |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in Poland) | **NO** |

---

## How to Submit

1. Access the **State Labour Inspectorate declaration portal** via biznes.gov.pl: https://www.biznes.gov.pl/en/portal/02176
2. Submit declaration before the start of the posting period
3. Required: company details, driver details, vehicle, type of transport, dates

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry:
- Proof of posting declaration
- CMR waybill
- Pay evidence (Polish minimum wage or sector minimum applies)
- Working time records (last 28+ days)

---

## Minimum Pay

Posted drivers must receive at least the Polish **minimum wage (minimalne wynagrodzenie)** for work performed in Poland. Current rates: https://www.gov.pl/
