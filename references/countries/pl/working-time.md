## Overview

Poland implements EU Directive 2002/15/EC through the **Ustawa o czasie pracy kierowców** (Act on the Working Time of Drivers, SFS-equivalent: Dz. U. 2004 Nr 92 poz. 879 as amended). Enforcement is primarily by **GITD** (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego — Chief Road Transport Inspectorate) and the **Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy** (PIP — State Labour Inspectorate).

---

## Key Working Time Limits

| Parameter | EU baseline | PL implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Average weekly working time | 48 hours (over 4-month reference period) | 48 hours |
| Maximum weekly working time | 60 hours | 60 hours |
| Night work max | 10 hours per 24h containing night period | 10 hours |
| Night period definition | 00:00–04:00 (national choice) | **00:00–04:00** |
| Rest breaks | 30 min after 6h, 45 min after 9h | Same as EU |
| Reference period | 4 months | 4 months (standard) |

---

## Polish-Specific Rules

### Ustawa o czasie pracy kierowców
The Polish Act on Working Time of Drivers closely follows the EU Directive with few significant deviations. Key Polish-specific provisions:
- **Night period:** 00:00–04:00 (standard EU choice)
- **Self-employed drivers:** Directive 2002/15/EC applies to all self-employed mobile workers since 2009
- **Record keeping:** Employers must keep working time records for at least **3 years**
- **Periodic rest:** Same as EU baseline (daily rest = 11h; weekly = 45h normal, 24h reduced)

### Interaction with Driving/Rest Time
Polish law tightly integrates working time rules with EU 561/2006 driving/rest time rules. GITD inspectors check both simultaneously during roadside inspections and company audits.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Driver exceeding maximum working time | PLN 500–2,000 | Ustawa o czasie pracy kierowców Art. 24 |
| Operator — no working time records | PLN 2,000–10,000 | Ustawa o czasie pracy kierowców |
| Employer — systematic violation | PLN 10,000–15,000 + possible prosecution | Labour Code; Ustawa o czasie pracy kierowców |
