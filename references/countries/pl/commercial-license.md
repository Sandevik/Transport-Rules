## Overview

Poland issues Community Licences through the GITD (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego / Chief Road Transport Inspectorate). Polish operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence examination (Certyfikat Kompetencji Zawodowych — CKZ) is administered by approved Polish examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Polish specifics |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Polish place of business | KRS or CEIDG registration |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Checked via national transport register + criminal records |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CKZ exam | Via approved centres; GITD supervises |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**GITD**: issues Community Licences and domestic transport licences (Licencja na wykonywanie transportu drogowego).

Website: https://www.gitd.gov.pl/

---

## Community Licence

Issued by GITD; certified true copies in each vehicle. Poland is one of the EU's largest HGV fleets.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Poland within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- biznes.gov.pl posting notification required (see posting-declaration.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
