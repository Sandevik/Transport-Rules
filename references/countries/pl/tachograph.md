## Overview

EU Regulation 165/2014 applies directly as Poland is an EU/EEA member state. All commercial vehicles >3.5t GVW in scope must be fitted with a smart tachograph v2 from August 2025 (retrofit deadlines per Regulation 2021/1228). Calibration and workshop approval is managed by GITD (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego).

## Calibration Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration interval | Every 2 years | Reg. 165/2014 Art. 23 |
| Seal check | Every 6 years | Reg. 165/2014 Art. 23 |
| After repair | Immediate recalibration required | Reg. 165/2014 |
| Workshop type | Approved tachograph workshop only | Reg. 165/2014 Art. 24 |

Approved workshop list: [GITD (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego)](https://www.gitd.gov.pl/)

## Smart Tachograph v2 — Retrofit Deadlines

| Vehicle type | Deadline |
|---|---|
| New type approvals | 21 August 2023 |
| All new N3/M3 vehicles | 21 August 2024 |
| Retrofit smart v1 vehicles (international) | 21 August 2025 |
| Retrofit pre-smart vehicles (domestic) | 21 August 2026 |

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|---|---|---|
| GITD (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego) | National tachograph oversight | https://www.gitd.gov.pl/ |

Roadside immobilisation: permitted for vehicles with uncalibrated or tampered tachographs.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Tachograph not calibrated | Medium fine — see references/countries/pl/penalties.md | Reg. 165/2014; national enforcement law |
| Tachograph manipulation | Serious fine + prosecution | Reg. 165/2014 + national criminal law |
| Card not used correctly | Fine — see penalties file | National enforcement law |
