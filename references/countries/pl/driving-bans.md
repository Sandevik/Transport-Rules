## Overview

Poland imposes a **year-round Sunday driving ban** and a **summer Saturday/weekend restriction** for heavy goods vehicles. The restrictions are designed to ease congestion during holiday periods and Sunday family travel. Enforcement is by **GITD** (Road Transport Inspectorate) and the national Police (Policja). Violations by foreign operators result in on-the-spot fines payable immediately.

---

## Ban Schedule

### Sunday and Holiday Ban (Year-Round)

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 12t GVW (solo or with trailer)

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| Sundays (all year) | **08:00–22:00** |
| National public holidays (all year) | **08:00–22:00** |

### Summer Saturday/Weekend Ban

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 12t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| **July and August — Friday evenings** | **18:00–22:00** |
| **July and August — Saturdays** | **00:00–22:00** (effectively full Saturday) |

The summer ban in July/August creates a near-continuous restriction from Friday 18:00 to Sunday 22:00 — a de facto 52-hour weekend exclusion during peak summer.

---

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Goods vehicles > 12t GVW | **YES** |
| Goods vehicles ≤ 12t GVW | NO — lower threshold than DE/AT/CH |
| Passenger cars, vans | NO |

Poland uses a **12t threshold** rather than the 7.5t threshold used by Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

---

## Exemptions (§34 UDR — Ustawa o Drogach Ruchu)

The following are exempt from Polish HGV driving bans:

| Exemption | Notes |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh meat, dairy, fish, fresh fruit/vegetables, live animals |
| Press/newspapers | Time-critical daily press distribution |
| Fuel and energy | Petrol, diesel, LPG, heating oil |
| International transit (some) | Vehicles in transit through Poland — verify current rules |
| ADR dangerous goods | Classes exempt from ban (varies by class; Class 1 explosives always restricted) |
| Emergency services | Police, fire, ambulance |
| Military | Polish armed forces; NATO forces with Polish government permission |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal road legs with documentation |
| Agricultural harvest | Grain, sugar beet, potatoes — specific seasonal periods |
| Empty vehicles returning | After delivering exempt goods |

## Public Holidays (National)

Polish national holidays triggering the ban:
- New Year's Day (1 Jan)
- Epiphany (6 Jan) — Święto Trzech Króli
- Easter Sunday + Monday
- Labour Day (1 May)
- Constitution Day (3 May)
- Corpus Christi
- Assumption of Mary (15 Aug)
- All Saints' Day (1 Nov)
- Independence Day (11 Nov)
- Christmas Day (25 Dec) + Boxing Day (26 Dec)

## Enforcement and Fines

**GITD** (Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego) is the primary enforcement body, with dedicated mobile units on major roads and at border crossings. Police (Policja) also enforces.

| Violation | Fine (PLN) |
|---|---|
| HGV driving during ban without exemption | PLN **500–2,000** (driver) + possible operator fine |
| Operator permitting violation | PLN **2,000–15,000** |
| Forged exemption documentation | Criminal prosecution |
| Foreign operator | Fine payable immediately; vehicle may be immobilised until fine paid |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Driving during Sunday ban | PLN 500–2,000 | Art. 60 §4 Ustawa o ruchu drogowym; Rozporządzenie MiR |
| Driving during summer Saturday ban | PLN 500–2,000 | Art. 60 §4 |
| Operator fine | PLN 2,000–15,000 | GITD administrative penalty |
