## Overview

Poland implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through amendments to the ustawa o delegowaniu pracowników (Act on Posting Workers). The posting notification portal (biznes.gov.pl) is covered in posting-declaration.md. This file focuses on minimum pay obligations, equal treatment rights, and enforcement. The PIP (Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy / State Labour Inspectorate) enforces compliance.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Poland | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in PL + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Poland only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay — Polish Labour Law

**Minimum wage (minimalne wynagrodzenie za pracę)**: approximately PLN 4,300/month (2025; updated annually by Decree).

**Transport sector collective agreement**: road transport sector rates may be set by sectoral agreements; where applicable, these apply to posted workers.

Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade in Poland must receive at least the Polish minimum wage for hours worked in Poland.

---

## Driver Documentation

- biznes.gov.pl notification proof (see posting-declaration.md)
- Employment contract showing Polish-level minimum pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

---

## Equal Treatment

After 12 months of posting (extendable to 18), Polish labour law applies in full.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| PIP | Państwowa Inspekcja Pracy (State Labour Inspectorate) | https://www.pip.gov.pl/ |
| GITD | Główny Inspektorat Transportu Drogowego | https://www.gitd.gov.pl/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Poland demands advance kaucja from foreign carriers for violations.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (PLN) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | PLN 1,000–PLN 30,000 per worker | Kodeks pracy |
| Failure to notify posting | PLN 1,000–PLN 30,000 | Ustawa o delegowaniu pracowników |
| Failure to maintain pay documentation | PLN 1,000–PLN 30,000 | Kodeks pracy |
