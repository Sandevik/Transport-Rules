## Overview

Greece implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national law. Greece's geography — a peninsula with numerous islands and bordered by the Balkans — means sea transport plays a major role. The Port of Piraeus (one of Europe's largest container ports, operated by COSCO) is the primary intermodal gateway. Greece has limited domestic rail freight infrastructure.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Type |
|----------|----------|------|
| Piraeus port (PCT, PPA) | Athens area | Europe's 4th largest container port; Mediterranean hub |
| Thessaloniki port | Thessaloniki | Northern Greece; Balkan rail connections |
| Igoumenitsa | Epirus | Adriatic ro-ro ferry to Italy |
| Patras | Peloponnese | Adriatic ro-ro ferry to Italy |

**Ferry routes**: Patras–Ancona, Patras–Bari, Igoumenitsa–Venice/Ancona are major combined transport routes — ferry legs qualify as the non-road (maritime) leg under 92/106/EEC.

---

## Documentation Required

- Port/ferry booking confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC. Greece fully implements the directive.
