## Overview

Greece applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the Ministry of Rural Development and Food (Υπουργείο Αγροτικής Ανάπτυξης και Τροφίμων — ΥΠΑΑΤ), with enforcement by regional veterinary authorities (Κτηνιατρικές Υπηρεσίες). Greece is a significant transit country for livestock from Bulgaria/Romania to Western Europe, and a destination for live animals (particularly cattle from Northern Europe).

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | Regional veterinary service |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | Regional veterinary service |

---

## BIPs

- **Piraeus port**: BIP for third-country live animal imports (sea route)
- **Thessaloniki port**: BIP for sea arrivals from Turkey/other third countries
- Land BIPs at the Turkish and North Macedonian borders

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation in vehicle
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Veterinary health certificate
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.
