## Overview

Greece issues Community Licences through the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (YME) and its regional transport offices. Greek operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam for transport managers is administered by approved Greek examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Greek specifics |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Greek place of business | ΓΕΜΗ (General Commercial Registry) registration |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal record check; transport registry |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC exam (Πιστοποιητικό Επαγγελματικής Ικανότητας) | Via approved centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**YME** (Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport) and its **Περιφερειακές Υπηρεσίες Μεταφορών** (Regional Transport Services):
- Issue Community Licences and national licences
- Maintain the national transport register

---

## Community Licence

Issued by regional transport offices; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Greece within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- ERGANI posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
