# Greece — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Greece applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Greek Road Traffic Code (Κώδικας Οδικής Κυκλοφορίας / KOK) and implementing Presidential Decrees. Greece has no special large vehicle programme. Mountain terrain limits HGV operations on some roads, and many island routes use ro-ro ferry vessels with vehicle weight restrictions set by the ferry operator.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits for oversize/overweight loads issued by the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport / regional road authorities (ΔΔΠΕ — Regional Departments of Infrastructure and Transport). Advance application required.

---

## Island/Ferry Considerations

HGVs travelling to Greek islands use ro-ro ferry services. Ferry operators set their own maximum vehicle weight restrictions (typically aligned with vessel capacity), which may be lower than the national road limits. Always check ferry operator restrictions for island routes.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.
