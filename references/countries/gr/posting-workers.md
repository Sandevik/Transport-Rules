**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Greece implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through Law 4052/2012 (as amended). Foreign carriers must submit posting declarations via the **ERGANI** system (Σύστημα ΕΡΓΑΝΗ) before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. The SEPE (Σώμα Επιθεώρησης Εργασίας / Labour Inspectorate Body) enforces compliance.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Greece | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in GR + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Greece only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification — ERGANI System

- Portal: https://www.yleap.gr/ (ΕΡΓΑΝΗ)
- Pre-notification required before work commences in Greece
- Employer must register company and provide driver/vehicle details

---

## Minimum Pay

- **National minimum wage (Κατώτατος μισθός)**: approximately €830/month (2025; updated by Ministerial Decision)
- **Sector collective agreements** for road transport (GSEVEE/sector agreements) may set higher rates
- Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the applicable rate

---

## Driver Documentation

- ERGANI notification confirmation
- Employment contract showing Greek-level pay
- A1 certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to notify via ERGANI | €1,000–€5,000 | Law 4052/2012 |
| Failure to pay minimum wages | €2,000–€50,000 | Labour law |
