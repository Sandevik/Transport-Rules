# Greece — Road Tolls

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.aodos.gr/en/ — Attiki Odos: main Attica motorway operator

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Greece charges tolls at motorway plazas. The network is concession-operated by multiple companies. HGVs pay rates based on axle count and vehicle class. No national GNSS distance charge.

---

## Key Operators

| Route | Operator |
|-------|---------|
| Attiki Odos (Athens ring) | Attiki Odos S.A. |
| Egnatia Odos (E90, northern Greece) | Egnatia Odos A.E. |
| Athens–Thessaloniki (A1) | NEA ODOS, CENTRAL GREECE MOTORWAY |
| Olympia Odos (A8, Athens–Patras) | Olympia Odos |

## Payment

- **e-pass** transponder (Via Verde / EETS-compatible) — recommended
- Cash at toll plazas
- Credit/debit card at automated lanes
