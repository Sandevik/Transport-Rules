## Overview

Greece applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport** and the **Hellenic Police** (traffic department). Greece is a key entry/exit point for SE Europe and Turkey-bound traffic.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
