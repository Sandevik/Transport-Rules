## Overview

Greece applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport (Υπουργείο Υποδομών και Μεταφορών — YME) and the Ministry of Environment and Energy (YPEN) jointly handle transport emission policy. Greece does not have a national LEZ programme comparable to other EU countries, though Athens has restrictions on vehicle access in the centre based on odd/even registration plates and some emission-based rules.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Greek national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

---

## Athens Traffic Restrictions

**Athens Δακτύλιος (Ring/Daktylios):**
- Athens operates an odd/even licence plate system in the city centre
- Applies to all vehicles except ZEV and certain special categories
- Separate from EU emission class requirements
- HGVs also subject to time-based access restrictions in the Attica basin

No formal national LEZ with Euro-class based access as of 2026, but municipalities are planning emission-based zones.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- Greek government has offered periodic EV purchase subsidies for commercial vehicles
- ΤΑΙΠΕΔ / Recovery Fund: EU RRF-funded incentives for green logistics
- Reduced circulation tax for ZEV commercial vehicles
