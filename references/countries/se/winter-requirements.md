## Overview

Sweden requires winter tyres on motor vehicles (including trucks) during the **winter period (1 December – 31 March)** when there are winter road conditions. For heavy vehicles (trucks), the mandatory requirement applies specifically to the **drive axle(s)**. Studded tyres are permitted from 1 October to 30 April (in northern Sweden, 1 October to 15 May). The minimum tread depth for winter tyres on trucks is stricter than for passenger cars.

---

## Winter Tyre Requirement

### Mandatory Period

**Motor vehicles ≤ 3.5t GVW:** Winter tyres required December 1 – March 31 when winter road conditions prevail.

**Motor vehicles > 3.5t GVW (trucks, buses):** Winter tyres required on the **drive axle(s)** December 1 – March 31 when winter road conditions prevail. Other axles: no mandatory winter tyre but must be safe.

**Trigger condition:** "Winter road conditions" (vinterväglag) = snow, slush, ice, or frost on the road surface. The requirement is **condition-triggered**, not purely calendar-based.

---

## Winter Tyre Specifications

| Requirement | Passenger cars ≤ 3.5t | Heavy vehicles > 3.5t |
|---|---|---|
| Tread depth minimum | **3 mm** | **5 mm (drive axle)**, 3 mm other axles |
| Symbol requirement | M+S or snowflake (three-peak mountain + snowflake) | M+S or snowflake |
| Studded tyres permitted | Yes — 1 Oct to 30 April (northern Sweden: 1 Oct – 15 May) | Yes — same periods |

**Note on heavy vehicles:** The 5 mm minimum tread depth on drive axles is a Swedish-specific requirement stricter than the general EU minimum. This ensures grip under heavy braking loads.

---

## Studded Tyres (Dubbdäck)

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Permitted period (general Sweden) | **1 October – 30 April** |
| Permitted period (northern Sweden — north of Luleå/Sundsvall latitude) | **1 October – 15 May** |
| Mandatory to use on all axles if used | If studded on drive axle, all axles of the motor vehicle should have studs for stability |
| Nordic Ecolabel | Preferred but not mandatory |

---

## Chains

Chains are **not mandatory** in Sweden as a legal requirement, but are **recommended** and commonly used in northern Sweden during severe winter conditions. Certain mountain roads (Riksväg 95, fjällvägar) may be difficult or unsafe without chains during severe weather.

---

## Responsibility and CoR

Under Swedish law, both the **driver** and the **vehicle operator/owner** are responsible for ensuring the vehicle meets winter tyre requirements. Fleet operators must have procedures to ensure vehicles are equipped correctly when deployed in winter months.

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement by **Polisen** (traffic police) and **Transportstyrelsen** inspectors at roadside. Fines issued for:
- Insufficient tread depth
- Non-winter tyres on drive axle during mandatory period in winter conditions

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (SEK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| No winter tyres during mandatory period (>3.5t — drive axle) | **1,500 SEK** (sanktionsavgift) | TSFS (Transportstyrelsen regulations); Trafikförordningen §3:12 |
| Tread depth below minimum (drive axle) | **1,500 SEK** | TSFS |
| Passenger car — no winter tyres | **1,500 SEK** | Same |
