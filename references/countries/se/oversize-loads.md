## Overview

Sweden has a comprehensive system for exceptional (oversize/overmass) transport known as **Transportdispens**. Loads exceeding Sweden's standard dimension or weight limits — including BK-class limits — require a permit from Transportstyrelsen. Sweden's high standard limits (up to 74t on BK1) mean that truly exceptional loads are fewer than in many EU countries, but the system is well-developed for heavy industrial, wind energy, and construction transport.

See also: `references/countries/se/transport-dispensation.md` (existing file covering the full Swedish dispensation system including Trix L/XL/XXL classification).

## Thresholds Triggering a Permit

Permits are needed when exceeding any of the following:

| Parameter | Standard limit on BK1 roads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 25.25 m (EMS) or 18.75 m (standard) | Depends on combination type |
| Width | 2.60 m (refrigerated) / 2.55 m (standard) | |
| Height | 4.50 m (transport height maximum) | |
| Weight | See BK class limits | BK1 allows up to 74t for EMS |
| Axle load | Exceeding BK class axle limits | BK1: single axle 11.5t |

---

## Trix Classification System

Sweden uses the **Trix system** for classifying exceptional transport permits:

| Class | Description | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| **Trix L** (Long) | Vehicles/combinations up to 30 m / exceeding standard lengths | Extended agricultural machinery, boats |
| **Trix XL** | Combinations up to 50 m or widths up to 4.0 m | Industrial equipment, transformers |
| **Trix XXL** | Combinations > 50 m or widths > 4.0 m | Bridge beams, large turbine components |

---

## Notification Lead Times

| Trix class | Required notice |
|---|---|
| Trix L | 2 working days |
| Trix XL | **5 working days** |
| Trix XXL | **10 working days** (+ police coordination) |

---

## Escort and Pilot Vehicle Requirements

| Load size | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Width 3.0–4.0 m | 1 pilot vehicle (varningsbil) |
| Width > 4.0 m | 2 pilot vehicles + police escort on some roads |
| Length > 30 m | Pilot vehicle required on turns and narrow roads |
| Night transport | Specific lighting requirements + notification |

---

## Speed Limits for Exceptional Loads

| Transport size | Speed limit |
|---|---|
| All exceptional loads on public roads | **40–60 km/h** (per permit conditions) |
| Motorways | Up to 80 km/h if permitted |
| Through urban areas | 30 km/h typically |

---

## Application Process

Applications for transportdispens are submitted to **Transportstyrelsen** online at transportstyrelsen.se. Applications require:
- Vehicle and combination specifications (axle layout, weights, dimensions)
- Route description
- Time/date of transport
- Contact person for the transport

For Trix XL/XXL, route inspections by Trafikverket or local road authorities may be required.

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| Transportstyrelsen | Permit issuance |
| Polisen (Trafikpolisen) | Escort, roadside checks |
| Trafikverket | Route approvals, bridge load assessments |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (SEK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without required permit | Sanktionsavgift: **15,000–40,000 SEK** | SFS 2001:559; TSFS regulations |
| Exceeding permit dimensions/weight | Sanktionsavgift + overload charges | SFS |
| Operating without required escort | Sanktionsavgift | SFS |
