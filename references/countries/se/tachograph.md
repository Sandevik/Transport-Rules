## Overview

Sweden applies EU Regulation 165/2014 on tachographs in road transport. Transportstyrelsen is the competent authority for tachograph oversight and approved workshop designation. Smart tachograph v2 (ITS-G5/GNSS) is required for new vehicles from 2023 onwards, with retrofitting deadlines for existing vehicles.

---

## Calibration Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration interval | Every 2 years | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| Seal check | Every 6 years | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| After repair | Immediate recalibration required | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| Workshop type | Approved tachograph workshop only (verkstad) | Art. 24 Regulation 165/2014 |

Approved workshop list: Transportstyrelsen — https://www.transportstyrelsen.se

## Smart Tachograph v2 — Retrofit Deadlines

| Vehicle type | Deadline |
|---|---|
| New type approvals (from) | 21 August 2023 |
| All new N3/M3 vehicles | 21 August 2024 |
| Retrofit smart v1 vehicles in international ops | 21 August 2025 |
| Retrofit pre-smart vehicles in international ops | 31 December 2024 |

---

## Driver Card and Records

- Drivers must use their driver card in all vehicles fitted with a digital/smart tachograph
- Records must be available for the current day and the preceding 28 calendar days
- Manual entries required for any period the tachograph was not recording (ferry, illness, etc.)

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Transportstyrelsen | National tachograph oversight, workshop approval | https://www.transportstyrelsen.se |
| Polisen (Trafikpolisen) | Roadside tachograph checks | — |

Roadside immobilisation: permitted for vehicles with uncalibrated or tampered tachographs under the Swedish road transport enforcement legislation.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (SEK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Tachograph not calibrated | Sanktionsavgift — see Transportstyrelsen rates | Regulation 165/2014; SFS |
| Tachograph manipulation | Serious fine + criminal prosecution | Regulation 165/2014; Brottsbalken |
| Driver card not used | Sanktionsavgift | Regulation 165/2014; national enforcement |
| Records incomplete | Sanktionsavgift | Regulation 165/2014 |

See `references/countries/se/penalties.md` for full Swedish sanktionsavgift schedule.
