## Overview

Sweden's road network is maintained by **Trafikverket** (national roads) and **municipal authorities** (local roads). Operators of heavy vehicles that cause damage to roads — typically through overloading, overloading during spring thaw, or operating outside permit conditions — may be held liable for the cost of road repair. This liability (väghållaransvar / road damage liability) is separate from traffic sanctions and can result in significant civil claims.

## Who Is the Road Holder (Väghållare)?

| Road type | Road holder | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| National motorways and main roads | **Trafikverket** | trafikverket.se |
| County roads (länsvägar) | **Länsstyrelsen** or Trafikverket (delegated) | varies |
| Municipal streets | **Municipality (kommunen)** | local authority |
| Private roads | **Road association (vägsamfällighet)** | local |

## When Road Damage Liability Arises

Road damage liability can arise when:
1. A vehicle **exceeds the weight limits** for the road class (BK1/BK2/BK3) or the specific road
2. A vehicle operates on a road **during tjällossning (spring thaw) restriction** when weight limits are reduced
3. A vehicle operates **without or outside the terms of a transport dispensation** (Trix permit)
4. A vehicle causes **physical damage** to road surface, bridges, or road furniture

---

## Claim Procedure by Trafikverket

If Trafikverket determines that a vehicle has damaged a road:
1. Trafikverket documents the damage (photos, weight measurements)
2. Trafikverket sends a damage claim (skadeståndskrav) to the vehicle operator
3. Operator has right to contest the claim; dispute resolved through civil courts
4. Trafikverket calculates repair cost based on actual damage + overhead

**Scale of claims:** Minor surface damage: SEK 10,000–50,000. Bridge or structural damage: SEK 100,000–several million.

---

## Insurance Coverage

Standard **third-party motor liability insurance** (trafikförsäkring) covers **bodily injury and property damage to third parties** but typically does NOT cover road damage caused by overloading — this is considered operator fault/breach of permit conditions.

**What to check with insurer:**
- Whether your commercial fleet policy includes "road damage liability" coverage
- Maximum coverage limit for civil road damage claims
- Whether operating outside permit conditions voids coverage

Specialist transport operators (particularly oversize/overweight loads) should ensure their insurance policy explicitly covers road damage liability.

---

## Interaction with Sanktionsavgifter

Note: **Sanktionsavgifter** (administrative fines from Transportstyrelsen) and **road damage claims** from Trafikverket are separate:
- Sanktionsavgift: administrative fine paid to the state (Transportstyrelsen) for the overloading violation
- Road damage claim: civil compensation paid to the road holder (Trafikverket/municipality) for actual repair costs
- An operator can face both simultaneously for the same incident

---

## Spring Thaw Period — Increased Risk

During **tjällossning** (spring thaw restrictions — typically March–May), road bearing capacity drops significantly. Operating at summer weights during tjällossning not only triggers sanktionsavgift but also dramatically increases the risk of causing road damage and associated civil liability claims.

See: `references/countries/se/spring-weight-restrictions.md` for tjällossning details.

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Overloading causing road damage | Civil damage claim (no fixed amount — actual cost of repairs) | Skadeståndslagen; Väglagen |
| Operating outside Trix permit conditions causing damage | Civil claim + sanktionsavgift | TSFS; Väglagen |
| Contesting legitimate damage claim | Civil court proceedings | Swedish Civil Procedure Code |
