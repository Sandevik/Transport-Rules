## Overview

**Transportavtalet** is the central collective agreement governing wages, working conditions, and employment terms for employees in road goods transport in Sweden. It is concluded between **Sveriges Åkeriföretag** (the Swedish Haulage Companies association, representing employers) and **Svenska Transportarbetareförbundet** (the Transport Workers' Union, representing employees).

The agreement covers drivers and other operational employees engaged in goods transport by road, including long-distance HGV drivers, regional distribution drivers, courier drivers, and local delivery drivers. Separate agreements apply to bus/coach drivers (Bussavtalet) and taxi drivers.

Transportavtalet sets minimum terms — individual contracts and local company agreements may provide more favourable conditions, but cannot fall below the collective agreement floor.

## Coverage and Scope

| Category | Covered by Transportavtalet |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Long-distance HGV drivers (tung lastbil) | Yes |
| Regional distribution drivers | Yes |
| Local delivery / city logistics drivers | Yes |
| Courier and parcel drivers (light vehicles) | Generally yes (separate light vehicle annex) |
| Forklift/terminal operators employed at transport company | Yes |
| Bus/coach drivers | No — Bussavtalet applies |
| Taxi drivers | No — Taxiavtalet applies |

## Wage Structure

### Base Salary

Drivers are paid a **monthly fixed base salary** (månadslön). Wage levels are set per category and renegotiated at the start of each agreement period (typically 2–3 year terms).

Wage levels are differentiated by:
- Vehicle type and licence category (C, CE, D)
- Route type (local, regional, long-distance international)
- Years of service / seniority

### Variable Wage Components

In addition to the base monthly salary, drivers typically receive:

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Distanspremie (distance premium) | Per-km or per-hour supplement for long-distance driving above a threshold |
| Nattillägg (night supplement) | Additional payment for driving between 22:00–06:00 |
| OB-tillägg (unsocial hours supplement) | Supplement for work during evenings, weekends, and public holidays (see below) |
| Söndagstillägg | Additional OB rate for Sunday work |
| Helgtillägg | Additional OB rate for public holidays |
| Lördagstillägg | Saturday supplement |

---

## OB-Tillägg (Unsocial Hours Supplements)

OB-tillägg (obekväm arbetstid — "inconvenient working hours") is a percentage supplement on the applicable hourly rate:

| Time period | Typical OB rate |
|-------------|----------------|
| Weekday evenings (18:00–22:00 or 19:00–22:00) | 40% |
| Weekday nights (22:00–06:00) | 50–60% |
| Saturday (from 12:00 or 13:00) | 40–50% |
| Sunday (all day) | 60–70% |
| Public holidays (röda dagar) | 80–100% |
| Day before public holiday (after 13:00–18:00) | 40–50% |

*Exact rates depend on the specific annex and the current agreement period. Rates above are representative of the 2024–2026 agreement.*

---

## Overtime

Overtime (övertid) is work beyond the normal agreed working hours (typically 40 hours/week or an average over a reference period):

| Overtime level | Supplement on hourly rate |
|---------------|--------------------------|
| First 2 overtime hours per day | +50% |
| Beyond 2 overtime hours per day | +100% |
| Overtime on rest days | +100% (plus OB if applicable) |

Compensatory rest (kompledighet) may be taken instead of overtime pay where agreed locally.

## Traktamente (Subsistence Allowances)

**Traktamente** are tax-free daily allowances paid to drivers who travel away from their home base (tjänsteresa) and are unable to return home for an overnight stay. They compensate for additional living costs during travel.

### Domestic Overnight Allowance (Inrikes)

Per Skatteverket's tax-free rates (2025):

| Situation | Tax-free amount |
|-----------|----------------|
| Overnight stay away from home (Sweden) | SEK 241 per night |
| Day allowance (dagtraktamente, no overnight) | SEK 120 per day (if away more than 15km from home and normal workplace) |

Allowances **above** these Skatteverket levels are subject to income tax and social security contributions.

### International Traktamente

For international transport, Skatteverket publishes an annual table of tax-free allowances by country (utlandstraktamente). Rates vary significantly by country:

| Country (representative examples, 2025 rates) | Tax-free daily rate (SEK) |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Germany (DE) | ~670 SEK/day |
| Norway (NO) | ~700 SEK/day |
| France (FR) | ~640 SEK/day |
| Netherlands (NL) | ~590 SEK/day |
| Poland (PL) | ~340 SEK/day |
| United Kingdom (GB) | ~590 SEK/day |

*Verify current rates at: https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/skatterochavdrag/traktamente*

### Meal Allowances (Måltidsavdrag)

If the employer pays for or provides meals during travel, the tax-free traktamente is reduced:
- Breakfast provided: deduct 20%
- Lunch provided: deduct 40%
- Dinner provided: deduct 40%

## Notice Periods (Uppsägningstider)

Under Transportavtalet and the underlying Swedish employment law (LAS — Lag om anställningsskydd):

| Employee's length of service | Minimum notice period (employer → employee) |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Less than 2 years | 1 month |
| 2–4 years | 2 months |
| 4–6 years | 3 months |
| 6–8 years | 4 months |
| 8–10 years | 5 months |
| More than 10 years | 6 months |

Employee notice to employer: typically 1 month regardless of seniority (may be extended by collective agreement).

## Annual Leave (Semester)

Under Swedish law (**Semesterlagen 1977:480 § 4**), all employees are entitled to a **minimum of 25 days paid annual leave** per year. (Verified against riksdagen.se, 2026-06-02.)

Under Transportavtalet and many company-level agreements:
- Employees with longer service may receive **additional leave days** (5–6 extra days in some agreements for 5+ years service)
- Leave pay: **semesterlön** calculated as **12% of annual earned wages** (§ 16b — percentage rule) or the same-wage method (§ 16a — monthly salary + 0.43% supplement per vacation day)
- Minimum **4 weeks** must be taken during June–August (§ 12)
- Saved days (over 20) may be carried forward up to **5 years** (§ 18)
- Leave year: 1 April–31 March (or calendar year by agreement)

## Sick Pay (Sjuklön)

Sweden's sick pay system for employed drivers:

| Day | Coverage |
|-----|---------|
| Day 1 (karensdag) | No pay (employee bears full income loss for first sick day) |
| Days 2–14 | **Employer pays sjuklön** — 80% of normal salary |
| Day 15 onwards | **Försäkringskassan (FK)** pays sjukpenning — approximately 77–80% of normal income (up to SGI ceiling) |

**Note:** The karensdag (waiting day) is a key feature of the Swedish system — drivers with genuinely high absence rates face higher personal cost than in some other Nordic countries.

Collective agreements may supplement FK's payment to bring total sick pay to higher levels during extended absence.

## Pension (Tjänstepension)

Drivers covered by Transportavtalet receive occupational pension via:

**Collectum / ITP-avtalet** (for tjänstemän) — or for blue-collar workers under Transportavtalet, the **SAF-LO collective pension agreement** (Avtalspension SAF-LO) administered through Fora:

- **Contribution:** Employer pays a percentage of gross salary (typically 4.5% up to the social security ceiling, 30% above it)
- **Provider:** Employees can select their own fund from an approved list; default fund applies if no choice is made
- **Eligibility:** Begins from age 25 in most cases
- **Defined contribution:** The pension amount depends on contributions and fund performance

Transport operators who are members of Sveriges Åkeriföretag and bound by Transportavtalet are automatically enrolled in the Fora system for pension and insurance contributions.

---

## Insurance Included in Collective Agreement

Being bound by Transportavtalet (via Fora) includes the following mandatory insurances paid by the employer:

| Insurance | Coverage |
|-----------|---------|
| TFA (Trygghetsförsäkring vid Arbetsskada) | Work injury insurance supplementing statutory workers' compensation |
| AGS (Avtalsgruppsjukförsäkring) | Group sickness insurance — supplements FK sjukpenning from day 15 |
| AGB (Avgångsbidrag) | Redundancy supplement for older workers (from age 40) if made redundant |
| TGL (Tjänstegrupplivförsäkring) | Group life insurance — lump sum to survivors on employee's death |

---

## Employer Obligations Under the Agreement

Transport operators bound by Transportavtalet must:
1. Apply the wage scales and variable supplements
2. Report and pay traktamente within Skatteverket's tax-free limits
3. Register with Fora and pay pension/insurance premiums
4. Comply with sick pay rules under LAS and the agreement
5. Apply correct notice periods
6. Grant statutory and agreed leave
7. Contribute to the industry's collective training funds (Transportfacket kompetens)

## Comparison with Statutory Minimums

| Issue | Statutory minimum (LAS/Semesterlagen) | Transportavtalet provision |
|-------|--------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Annual leave | 25 days | 25 days + possible extra for seniority |
| Sick pay days 2–14 | 80% of salary | 80% (agreement may supplement for longer absence) |
| Notice period (10 yr employee) | 6 months | 6 months (same as LAS) |
| Overtime rate | None mandated by statute | +50%/+100% as above |
| OB supplements | Not mandated by statute | Mandatory under agreement |
| Pension | Not mandated by statute | Avtalspension SAF-LO via Fora |

## Penalties

Non-compliance with Transportavtalet obligations may result in:

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|------------|
| Failure to pay correct wages/OB supplements | Employee claim for back-pay; union may file with Arbetsdomstolen (Labour Court) |
| Failure to register with Fora (pension/insurance) | Back-premiums plus interest; Fora can pursue via general courts |
| Failure to apply correct notice period | Compensation equivalent to lost wages during the notice period |
| Failure to grant statutory leave | Semesterlagen: employee entitled to leave payment plus 25% surcharge on unpaid leave |
| Non-compliance with sick pay rules | Employee can claim difference; LO-facken can file complaint with Arbetsgivarverket |
| Unlawfully avoiding collective agreement (fictitious self-employment) | Arbetsdomstolen can award damages; operator licence risk via Transportstyrelsen company control |
