## Overview

Sweden adopts UN-ECE Regulation 55 (R055) through its national vehicle type-approval system administered by Transportstyrelsen. Swedish regulations on coupling devices are set out in the Fordonsförordning (2009:211) and Transportstyrelsen's technical regulations (TSFS series). The EU-level specifications for D-values, kingpin sizes, and air/electrical connections all apply in Sweden. Sweden has some national additions, particularly relating to ball couplings for trailer combinations and the weight thresholds for long vehicle combinations (EMS/LCV up to 34.5 m).

---

## Deviations and National Additions

### Ball Coupling (80 mm Requirement)

Sweden requires an **80 mm ball coupling (ISO 11033 Class G)** for vehicle combinations where the **trailer's technically permissible maximum mass exceeds 3,500 kg**. A 50 mm ball coupling (Class A/B) may only be used where the trailer mass is 3,500 kg or less and the D-value of the coupling is sufficient for the combination.

This rule is stricter than the minimum ISO standard and reflects Sweden's high prevalence of agricultural and forestry trailer combinations that approach or exceed 3,500 kg.

### Fifth-Wheel and Kingpin

Standard 50 mm kingpin (ISO 337) applies for all standard articulated lorry combinations. For heavy-haul abnormal load combinations exceeding the standard mass limits, the 90 mm kingpin is used in accordance with R055 and Transportstyrelsen's provisions on exceptional loads (transportdispens).

### EMS and Long Combinations

For EMS (European Modular System) combinations of up to 34.5 m and up to 90 tonnes GCW (permitted in Sweden under national derogation), coupling devices must be rated for the actual D-value of the specific combination. EMS operators must verify that all couplings — including intermediate couplings between modules — bear the correct type-approval markings and D-value ratings.

### Drawbar Coupling — S-Value

Swedish regulations require that the S-value of the drawbar coupling is not exceeded. For centre-axle trailers (påhängsvagnar med centrumaxel), the drawbar nose load must be within the coupling's rated S-value, and this is checked at annual inspection (kontrollbesiktning) and roadside checks.

---

## Air and Electrical Connections

Sweden follows ISO 1728 (air) and ISO 7638 / ISO 11446 (electrical) as per EU baseline. All vehicles manufactured after 1991 and operating on public roads must have functioning ABS/EBS-compatible 7-pin ISO 7638 connectors where the vehicle is equipped with ABS. Cross-connected air lines are treated as a safety-critical defect resulting in immediate prohibition from operation.

---

## Inspection

Coupling devices are inspected at:
- **Obligatorisk periodisk besiktning (OPB)**: Annual roadworthiness inspection for commercial vehicles. Fifth-wheel jaw wear, kingpin wear (max 10 mm clearance), drawbar coupling condition, and air/electrical connector condition are all assessed.
- **Vägkontroll**: Roadside checks by Polisen (Police) and Transportstyrelsen inspectors. Coupling condition is a standard check item.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| Operating with worn fifth-wheel (jaw clearance >10 mm) | Prohibition to drive (körförbud) |
| Missing or invalid E-mark on coupling device | Prohibition; Transportstyrelsen administrative sanction |
| Ball coupling rated below combination requirement | Prohibition; sanktionsavgift |
| Cross-connected air lines (red/yellow) | Immediate prohibition; safety-critical defect |
| EMS combination with coupling D-value exceeded | Prohibition; potential fine and loss of EMS permit |

Fines (böter) and administrative sanctions (sanktionsavgifter) are set in the Vägtrafikskadelagen and the Yrkestrafiklag. Roadside prohibition orders are issued by the Police or Transportstyrelsen inspectors.
