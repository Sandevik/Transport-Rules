## Overview

Swedish **domestic** road freight (inrikes vägtransport) is not governed by the CMR Convention — CMR applies only to international road transport. Instead, the standard legal framework for domestic Swedish freight contracts is:

1. **ABV 2019** — the industry standard general conditions, contractually incorporated by reference
2. **Sjölagens Chapter 13** — Swedish law applies principles from maritime freight law by analogy to road freight disputes where ABV does not address a specific point
3. **General Swedish contract law** — Avtalslagen (1915:218), general commercial law principles

Unlike CMR, there is no statutory domestic road freight law in Sweden (analogous to Germany's HGB §§407–450). The parties are largely free to contract on their own terms, but ABV fills the gap in most commercial relationships.

## ABV 2019 — Allmänna Bestämmelser för Inrikes Vägtransport

### What ABV Is

ABV (Allmänna Bestämmelser för Inrikes Vägtransport) is the standard general conditions developed jointly by the transport industry — primarily Sveriges Åkeriföretag (SÅ) — and shipper organisations. The current version is ABV 2019 (published and effective from 2019, updating the previous ABV 2007).

ABV applies **by contractual incorporation** — it becomes binding when:
- The freight contract (transport order, rate agreement, or framework contract) refers to ABV by name, or
- It is an established trade usage between the parties that ABV applies

ABV does **not** apply automatically by law — parties should explicitly reference it in their freight agreements.

### Scope of ABV 2019

ABV governs the **entire transport operation** from the carrier's receipt of the goods to delivery, including:
- Carrier's obligations and duties of care
- Sender's obligations (packing, marking, documentation)
- Liability for loss, damage, and delay
- Limitation of liability
- Claims procedures and time limits
- Freight payment and lien
- Dangerous goods handling

ABV **does not** apply to:
- International road transport (CMR applies)
- Postal services
- Removal transport (covered by separate conditions)

## Carrier Liability Under ABV 2019

### Liability Standard

Unlike CMR (where the carrier is presumed liable and must establish a defence), ABV 2019 uses a **fault-based** liability standard:

The carrier is liable for **loss, damage, or delay** caused by the carrier's **negligence** (or that of its employees or agents). This is a significant difference from CMR's stricter liability regime.

The burden of proof under ABV is placed on the **sender/claimant** to show that the carrier was at fault — though in practice, where goods are lost in the carrier's custody, fault is often presumed.

### Liability Limits Under ABV 2019

ABV 2019 sets **higher liability limits** than CMR:

| Damage type | ABV 2019 limit |
|-------------|----------------|
| Loss or damage to goods | **150 SEK per kilogram** of gross weight of goods lost/damaged |
| Delay | **Amount of the freight charge** (carriage charges) |
| Maximum per consignment | Typically agreed in the contract; if not agreed, the per-kg limit applies |

*Note: The 150 SEK/kg limit substantially exceeds CMR's 8.33 SDR/kg (approximately SEK 130–145/kg at current rates), though both are weight-based limits. For high-value, low-weight goods (electronics, pharmaceuticals), neither limit may cover full commercial value.*

### Carrier Defences Under ABV 2019

The carrier is not liable where loss, damage, or delay is caused by:
- Force majeure — circumstances the carrier could not reasonably foresee or prevent
- Fault or contributory negligence of the sender or consignee
- Inherent vice of the goods
- Inadequate packing by the sender (where the carrier could not reasonably be expected to discover the inadequacy)
- Compliance with sender's instructions

### Higher Declared Value

Like CMR Article 24, ABV allows the sender to declare a higher value for the goods (deklarerat värde). If a higher value is declared and agreed (typically requiring an additional freight supplement), the carrier's liability is limited to the declared value rather than the per-kg cap.

## Claims and Time Limits

### Damage Notification

| Situation | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Apparent damage (visible at delivery) | Consignee must note reservation on delivery receipt at time of delivery; failure = acceptance as delivered in good condition |
| Non-apparent damage (not visible at delivery) | Written notice to carrier within **7 days** of delivery |
| Loss (total or partial) | Written notice promptly after discovery |

### Time Bar (Preskription)

Under ABV 2019, claims must be brought within:
- **1 year** from the date of delivery (or from when delivery should have been made) for loss, damage, and delay

This is the same period as CMR but the counting method differs. Note that Swedish general preskriptionslag provides a 10-year limitation period for contractual claims — ABV's shorter period is an agreed contractual shortening.

## Freight and Lien

Under ABV 2019:
- Freight is due on delivery unless otherwise agreed
- The carrier has a **lien** (retentionsrätt) over the goods for unpaid freight — the carrier may refuse to release goods until freight is paid
- The lien extends to all amounts owed by the consignee (or sender) to the carrier under the contract

## NSAB 2015 — Nordic Logistics Conditions

**NSAB 2015** (Nordiskt Speditörförbunds Allmänna Bestämmelser / Nordic Association of Freight Forwarders Standard Trading Conditions) is the standard conditions used by **freight forwarders (speditörer)** in Sweden and the other Nordic countries.

NSAB applies when a **freight forwarder** (as opposed to an actual carrier) is engaged. Key differences from ABV:

| Issue | ABV 2019 (carrier) | NSAB 2015 (freight forwarder) |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Applicable party | Transport operator (carrier) | Freight forwarder (speditor) |
| Liability basis | Fault-based | Fault-based |
| Liability limit | 150 SEK/kg | **SDR 8.33/kg** (same as CMR) for air freight; **SDR 2/kg** for other transport modes |
| Time limit for claims | 1 year | 1 year |
| Lien | Yes | Yes — broad forwarder lien |

NSAB's lower liability limits reflect the forwarder's intermediary role — the forwarder typically has recourse against the actual carrier. However, this can leave shippers significantly underprotected compared to contracting directly with a carrier under ABV.

## Applicable Courts and Dispute Resolution

### Jurisdiction

Under ABV 2019 and Swedish general commercial law:
- Disputes are heard in **Swedish courts** (allmän domstol — tingsrätt at first instance)
- The parties may agree to resolve disputes by **arbitration** (skiljeförfarande)
- The SCC (Stockholm Chamber of Commerce Arbitration Institute) is the most common Swedish arbitration institution for commercial transport disputes

### Arbitration

ABV 2019 includes an **arbitration clause** as an option. If the parties have agreed to ABV arbitration:
- Disputes are referred to an arbitral tribunal under SCC rules
- Arbitration awards are final and binding
- International enforcement via the New York Convention (for foreign parties)

**Practical note:** For smaller domestic disputes (claims below ~100,000 SEK), arbitration costs (often SEK 50,000–200,000+ in fees) make it uneconomic. Parties frequently resolve smaller claims through negotiation or tingsrätt (where costs are lower for simpler cases).

## Comparison: ABV 2019 vs. CMR

| Issue | ABV 2019 (domestic SE) | CMR (international) |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Applicable transport | Domestic Swedish road freight | International road freight (at least 1 CMR country) |
| Incorporation | Contractual — must be agreed | Automatic — applies by law |
| Liability standard | Fault-based (carrier must be negligent) | Strict — carrier presumed liable; must prove defence |
| Liability limit | 150 SEK/kg (~€13/kg) | 8.33 SDR/kg (~€11/kg) |
| Delay limit | Freight charge | Freight charge |
| Notification — apparent damage | At delivery | At delivery |
| Notification — non-apparent | 7 days | 7 days |
| Claims time bar | 1 year | 1 year (3 years for wilful misconduct) |
| Wilful misconduct | No special rule (general contract law) | Art. 29 — unlimited liability; time bar extended to 3 years |
| Governing law | Swedish law | CMR Convention (international treaty) |
| Courts | Swedish courts (or SCC arbitration) | Courts of any CMR country with jurisdiction (Art. 31) |

## Practical Guidance for Transport Operators

1. **Always reference ABV 2019 explicitly** in quotations, order confirmations, and framework agreements for domestic Swedish transport
2. **Issue a freight note** (fraktsedel) for each consignment — while not legally mandatory for domestic transport (unlike CMR), it creates evidence of the contract terms and condition of goods at loading
3. **Check goods condition at loading** and note any reservations on the freight note — this protects the carrier against pre-existing damage claims
4. **Act on defect reports promptly** — carriers with unresolved damage claims risk ABV time limits running while they delay investigation
5. **Understand the lien** — the carrier's lien over goods for unpaid freight is valuable security but must be exercised carefully; releasing goods before payment waives the lien
6. **Cargo insurance advice for shippers:** 150 SEK/kg is higher than CMR but still insufficient for high-value goods — shippers with valuable cargo should take out all-risk cargo insurance (see `references/eu/cargo-insurance.md`)

## Penalties

ABV 2019 is a civil law framework — violations result in civil liability, not regulatory penalties. However:

| Issue | Consequence |
|-------|------------|
| Carrier refuses to deliver without justification (withholds goods beyond lien right) | Shipper can apply for court order; carrier liable for damages |
| Carrier loses goods and disputes ABV liability | Shipper can sue in tingsrätt; carrier faces judgment for up to 150 SEK/kg |
| Failure to note reservations at delivery (consignee) | Loss of right to claim for apparent damage |
| Shipper fails to notify within 7 days (non-apparent damage) | Risk of time bar; carrier may deny liability |
| Fraud (false description of cargo) | Criminal liability under brottsbalken; civil liability beyond ABV limits |
