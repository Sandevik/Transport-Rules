# Bosnia-Herzegovina — Road Tolls and Charges

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national rules (Eurovignette Directive does not apply).

**Sources:**
- https://www.bihamk.ba/ — BH Auto-Moto Club — road information BiH

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Bosnia-Herzegovina operates a national road toll/charge system. As a non-EU/EEA country, EU Eurovignette Directive requirements do not apply directly. Foreign vehicles are subject to the same toll obligations as domestic vehicles. Check current rates before travel.

---

## Toll System

| Road Type | Toll System | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Motorways | Electronic/manual toll collection | Verify current system |
| National roads | May be toll-free | Verify by route |

---

## Payment Methods

- Cash at toll booths
- Electronic payment (transponder/OBU where available)
- Pre-paid vignettes (where applicable)

*Verify current accepted payment methods before travel.*
