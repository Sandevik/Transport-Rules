# Bosnia-Herzegovina — Transit Permits and International Road Transport

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national/international rules. Bosnia-Herzegovina is an EU candidate country (candidate status granted December 2022).

## Overview

International road freight to, from, and through Bosnia-Herzegovina (BiH) requires either a **bilateral road transport permit** or an **ITF/CEMT multilateral permit**. BiH is a candidate country for EU accession but EU Community Licences do not provide automatic market access.

BiH is a **full member of the International Transport Forum (ITF, formerly CEMT)**, which means ITF/CEMT multilateral permits from other ITF member countries are accepted. This is the primary mechanism for EU hauliers operating in BiH.

BiH is located on the **Corridor Vc** (Budapest–Sarajevo–Ploče/Mostar), a priority TEN-T corridor under the EU's connectivity agenda for the Western Balkans.

BiH has a complex internal governance structure (Federation of BiH and Republika Srpska entities, plus Brčko District). Transport legislation applies at the state level, but implementation may vary.

---

## ITF/CEMT Multilateral Permits

BiH is a **full ITF member**. ITF/CEMT permits are the standard mechanism for EU hauliers:

| Permit Type | Validity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Annual ITF permit | Calendar year | Minimum Euro III standard |
| Short-term ITF permit (30-day) | 30 consecutive days | Minimum Euro III standard |

Conditions: vehicle must meet stated Euro emission standard; permit must be carried on vehicle; cabotage not permitted.

## Bilateral Road Transport Permits

BiH has bilateral road transport agreements with EU member states individually (not a single EU-level agreement). Croatia, Slovenia, Hungary, and Austria are key partners given geographic proximity.

| Permit Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Bilateral permit | For direct transport between BiH and partner country |
| Transit permit | For vehicles transiting BiH (e.g. Croatia–Neum corridor — see below) |
| Third-country permit | For transport involving a third country |

### Neum Corridor (Corridor Vc / A1)

A notable transit situation: Bosnia-Herzegovina has a short coastal corridor at **Neum** that divides Croatian territory. Croatian A1 motorway traffic travelling between Split and Dubrovnik historically had to transit through BiH territory. The **Pelješac Bridge** (opened 2022) now allows Croatia to bypass this BiH territory for its internal traffic, but some freight routes still use the Neum corridor.

---

## TIR Convention

BiH is a **contracting party to the TIR Convention**. TIR Carnets are used for international transit operations, particularly on Corridor Vc routes and for traffic to/from Serbia, Montenegro.

## Penalties

Penalties under BiH road transport law. Amounts in BAM (Convertible Mark — Bosnia-Herzegovina).

| Violation | Penalty (BAM) | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Operating without required permit | 3,000–10,000 BAM | Vehicle detention possible |
| Permit conditions exceeded | 1,500–5,000 BAM | |
| AETR tachograph violation | 1,000–4,000 BAM | |
| Overloaded vehicle | Variable per tonne excess | |
| Cabotage violation | 5,000–15,000 BAM | |

*Note: BAM amounts are indicative; verify current rates with Ministry of Communications and Transport.*
