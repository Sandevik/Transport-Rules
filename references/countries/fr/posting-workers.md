## Overview

France implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Loi Savary (Loi 2016-1088) and the Code du travail. The declaration portal SIPSI is covered in posting-declaration.md. This file covers minimum pay obligations, equal treatment rights, and enforcement. DREETS (Directions régionales de l'économie, de l'emploi, du travail et des solidarités) inspectors enforce compliance; they have been particularly active in checking transport operators at rest areas on major corridors.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Declaration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in France | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in FR + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ France only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in France) | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay — CCN Transport Routier

France's transport sector minimum wages are set by the **CCN Transport Routier de Marchandises** (Convention Collective Nationale):
- Rates depend on driver category (Category C/CE, weight group, and activity type)
- Substantially higher than the French SMIC (statutory minimum wage)
- Updated annually; published by Fédération Nationale du Transport Routier (FNTR)
- 2025 CCN Transport rates: contact FNTR or DREETS for current rates

Drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the CCN Transport Routier rate applicable to their activity.

---

## Additional Obligations

France's posting law includes:
- Indemnités de déplacement (travel and accommodation allowances) at French CCN rates when drivers are away from home base in France
- Compulsory employer representative (représentant): a French-based representative must be designated for inspections (may be a freight forwarder or logistics agent)

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry and present on demand:
- SIPSI declaration proof
- Employment contract showing CCN-compliant wages
- Pay slips for preceding 12 months (or period of posting)
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| DREETS | Directions régionales de l'emploi, du travail et des solidarités | https://travail-emploi.gouv.fr/ |
| Police Nationale / Gendarmerie | Roadside transport checks | — |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — France can demand security deposits (consignation) for suspected wage violations.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay CCN minimum wages | Up to €3,000 per worker (repeat: €6,000) | Code du travail L.1264-1 |
| Failure to submit SIPSI declaration | Up to €10,000 | Loi Savary |
| No designated French representative | Up to €10,000 | Code du travail |
| Obstruction of labour inspector | Up to €3,750 | Code du travail |
