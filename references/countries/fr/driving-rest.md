## Overview

France applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations from the core rules. Enforcement is by **DREAL** (Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement) at regional level, combined with the **Gendarmerie** and **Police nationale** for roadside checks. France has specific rules around advance payment (consignation) that foreign carriers must be aware of.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core driving/rest time rules. France fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 updates.

---

## Working Time (Temps de travail — distinct from driving time)

France also implements Directive 2002/15/EC via a decree. French transport sector collective agreements (conventions collectives) add national specifics including:
- **Convention collective nationale des transports routiers** (CCNTR) — governs pay, working conditions
- Working time for transport workers is regulated separately from driving/rest time

| Gendarmerie nationale | Roadside checks on national roads | — |
| Police nationale | Roadside checks in urban areas and motorways | — |
| ANTAI | Issues fines and manages penalty system | https://www.antai.gouv.fr/ |

**Advance payment (consignation):** YES — French authorities can demand immediate security deposit (consignation) from foreign drivers without a French address. Vehicle may be immobilised until paid.

---

## Penalties

Fines are issued by police/gendarmerie with the ANTAI system managing payment.

| Violation | Penalty range |
|-----------|--------------|
| Exceeding daily driving time | €750–€30,000 (driver + employer) |
| Insufficient daily rest | €750–€30,000 |
| Tachograph violations | Up to €15,000 |
| Operating without transport licence | Up to €15,000 |

Full French transport penalty schedule: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/ (Code des transports)
