# France — Posting of Workers Declaration (SIPSI)

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/posting-workers.md — Directive 2020/1057/EU]

**Sources:**
- https://www.sipsi.travail.gouv.fr/ — SIPSI: French posting declaration system

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

France implements Directive 2020/1057/EU via the **SIPSI** system (Système d'Information sur le détachement — Posting Information System). Foreign operators sending drivers to perform cabotage or cross-trade transport in France must submit a declaration via SIPSI. France is known for strict enforcement of posting obligations.

---

## When Declaration Is Required

| Operation | Required? |
|-----------|----------|
| Cabotage in France | **YES** — from first operation |
| Cross-trade (load in FR + deliver to third country, or vice versa) | **YES** |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ France only) | **NO** |
| Transit through France (no loading/unloading) | **NO** |

---

## How to Submit

1. Create an account on **SIPSI**: https://www.sipsi.travail.gouv.fr/
2. Submit a declaration **before** each posting period (each trip/period of cabotage operations)
3. Required information: company details, driver details, vehicle registration, type of transport, start/end dates, representative in France

**Representative in France:** Foreign operators must designate a representative in France who can be contacted by French authorities. This can be the client/consignee or a logistics agent.

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry:
- Copy of the SIPSI declaration (or electronic access link)
- CMR waybill(s)
- Employment contract or equivalent
- Pay slips or proof of wages
- Working time records (last 28+ days)

---

## Minimum Pay

France applies strict minimum pay rules. Posted drivers must receive at least the French **SMIC** (minimum wage) or applicable transport sector minimum under the **Convention Collective Nationale des Transports Routiers (CCNTR)**, whichever is higher.

---

## Enforcement

DREAL (regional transport inspectorates) and the **Labour Inspectorate (Inspection du travail)** jointly enforce posting rules. France is one of the most active EU states for posting compliance checks.

**Penalties:**
| Violation | Fine |
|-----------|------|
| Missing SIPSI declaration | Up to **€4,000** per worker, up to €8,000 for repeat offences |
| Missing representative in France | Up to €4,000 |
| Missing driver documentation | Up to €4,000 |
