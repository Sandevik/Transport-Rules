## Overview

France's HGV driving restrictions are **less comprehensive** than those of Germany, Austria, Switzerland, or Italy. There is no permanent year-round Sunday driving ban in France. Instead, France applies **prefectural (departmental) bans** during peak summer weeks (late July and August) near borders and on key transit routes. National-level restrictions are limited to a few peak summer Saturdays. The **Bison Futé** traffic information service publishes the annual calendar of forecast restrictions.

---

## Summer Restrictions — Annual Peak Period

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW

### National Bans (Arrêté from Ministry of Transport — issued annually)

| Period | Direction | Hours | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certain Saturdays in July/August | Outbound from major cities | **07:00–19:00** | Applies to key motorways and national roads near borders |
| Return Saturdays (end of August) | Inbound to major cities | **07:00–19:00** | Applies to same routes in reverse direction |

The specific dates are published annually by the Transport Ministry, typically in early spring.

### Prefectural Bans

Individual **prefectures** (departments) may issue their own driving restriction orders (arrêtés préfectoraux) covering HGVs on specific roads within the department. These are:
- Published in the **Journal Officiel** and local administrative notices
- Typically apply to roads feeding into or out of border crossings (Spanish border, Italian border, Swiss border, Belgian/German borders)
- Often extend to the A9, A7, A8, A63 motorways during peak summer

**Key routes affected:** A7 (Lyon–Marseille), A8 (Aix–Italian border), A9 (Lyon–Spanish border), A63 (Bordeaux–Spanish border)

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW | **YES** (summer bans) |
| Goods vehicles ≤ 7.5t | NO |
| Vehicles on exemption list | NO |

## Exemptions

| Exemption | Notes |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh food, chilled/frozen goods, live animals, horticultural products |
| Press/newspapers | Daily press |
| Fuel and energy | Petrol, diesel, LPG |
| ADR dangerous goods in transit | Specific classes |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal legs |
| Agricultural vehicles | Harvest-period transport |
| Emergency services | Police, fire, ambulance |
| Empty vehicles | May be exempt on some routes during summer bans |

## Crit'Air Environmental Vignette (Urban Areas)

Separate from driving bans, France operates the **Crit'Air** (Certificat Qualité de l'Air) vignette system in certain cities:
- Paris and Ile-de-France: Low Emission Zone (Zone à Faibles Emissions — ZFE) — certain trucks prohibited by Crit'Air class
- Lyon, Grenoble, Strasbourg, Toulouse, Bordeaux, and others: ZFE with varying rules
- Crit'Air vignettes are colour-coded by Euro emission class; trucks must display the vignette if entering ZFE areas

**Mandatory:** All vehicles circulating in a ZFE must display their Crit'Air sticker. Foreign vehicles included. Apply at: https://www.certificat-air.gouv.fr

---

## No Permanent Sunday Ban

Unlike Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Italy, and Poland, **France does not have a permanent year-round Sunday driving ban**. Sunday driving is generally permitted except where specific prefectural bans apply.

---

## Enforcement and Fines

Enforcement by **Gendarmerie Nationale** (on national roads and motorways outside cities) and **Police Nationale** (urban areas).

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|---|---|
| Driving during summer ban | **€135** (Class 3 fine) |
| Repeat offence | **€375** |
| Operating in ZFE without Crit'Air or with wrong class | **€135** (lorries) |
| No Crit'Air sticker | **€68–€135** |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Summer driving ban violation | €135–€375 | Art. R411-25 Code de la Route; Arrêté préfectoral |
| ZFE/Crit'Air violation | €68–€135 | Ordonnance n° 2015-1737; Arrêté municipal |
