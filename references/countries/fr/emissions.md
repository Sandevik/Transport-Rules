## Overview

France applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The DGITM (Direction Générale des Infrastructures, des Transports et des Mobilités) coordinates emission policy. France has an extensive national Crit'Air (Air Quality Certificate) sticker system and a growing network of ZFE-m (Zones à Faibles Émissions mobilité) in major urban areas — among the most developed LEZ systems in the EU.

---

## Crit'Air Sticker System

All motor vehicles operating in French ZFEs must display a **Crit'Air** sticker (vignette). Stickers are colour-coded by emission class:

| Crit'Air class | Diesel HGV Euro class | Petrol HGV Euro class |
|----------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| **1** | Euro VI | — |
| **2** | Euro V | Euro 5 |
| **3** | Euro IV | Euro 4 |
| **4** | Euro III | — |
| **5** | Euro II | — |
| Non-classé | Euro I and older | — |

HGVs must obtain a Crit'Air sticker at: https://www.certificat-air.gouv.fr/

---

## ZFE-m — Low Emission Zones

From 2022–2025, ZFE-m became mandatory in French agglomerations exceeding air quality thresholds. Key affected areas:

| City | ZFE restriction (HGVs) |
|------|------------------------|
| Paris (Greater Paris) | Crit'Air 3+ required (Crit'Air 5 and Non-classé banned) |
| Lyon | Crit'Air 3+ required |
| Marseille | Crit'Air 3+ required |
| Strasbourg, Grenoble, Toulouse, Bordeaux, Rouen, Reims, Saint-Étienne, Nice | Various ZFE levels |

Paris/Île-de-France has the strictest application: diesel HGVs older than Euro IV are restricted from the ZFE during operating hours.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Suramortissement**: accelerated depreciation for ZEV commercial vehicles
- **Bonus écologique**: available for certain ZEV goods vehicles
- **ADEME** funding for fleet transition projects

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. France adds the Crit'Air system and ZFE-m network above EU minimum.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| DGITM | Direction Générale des Infrastructures, des Transports et des Mobilités | https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/ |
| Police Nationale / Gendarmerie | Roadside enforcement | — |
| Municipal authorities | ZFE enforcement (ANPR cameras) | varies |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — France can demand security deposits (consignation) from foreign carriers.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Circulating in ZFE without required Crit'Air | €135–€750 (HGVs) | LOM 2019 / ZFE décrets |
| No Crit'Air sticker displayed | €68 | Code de la route |
| Type-approval non-compliance | Up to €7,500 | Code de la route |
