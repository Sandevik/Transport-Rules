## Overview

France introduced a **mandatory winter equipment requirement** under **Loi Montagne 2** (implemented by Décret n°2020-1264 of 16 October 2020). The obligation applies from **1 November to 31 March** in designated mountain areas (around 34 departments in mountain zones). Vehicles must be equipped with winter tyres **OR** carry snow chains. The obligation applies to all vehicles, including commercial trucks and coaches operating in the designated areas.

---

## Mandatory Period

**1 November – 31 March** in mountain areas (massifs montagneux).

---

## Geographic Scope — Affected Departments

The obligation applies in specific departments (approximately 34) covering mountain massifs:

| Massif | Departments (examples) |
|---|---|
| Alps (Alpes) | Savoie (73), Haute-Savoie (74), Isère (38), Hautes-Alpes (05), Alpes-Maritimes (06) |
| Pyrenees | Ariège (09), Hautes-Pyrénées (65), Pyrénées-Atlantiques (64), etc. |
| Massif Central | Puy-de-Dôme (63), Cantal (15), Lozère (48), etc. |
| Vosges | Bas-Rhin (67), Haut-Rhin (68), Moselle (57) |
| Jura | Jura (39), Doubs (25), Ain (01) |
| Corsica | Haute-Corse (2B), Corse-du-Sud (2A) |

Full list at: https://www.securite-routiere.gouv.fr

---

## Compliance Options

Vehicles operating in designated mountain areas November 1 – March 31 must have **one of the following**:

| Option | Specification |
|---|---|
| Winter tyres | M+S marking OR snowflake symbol (three-peak mountain + snowflake) — minimum 3 mm tread depth |
| All-season tyres | M+S marking and minimum 3 mm tread depth |
| Snow chains or anti-skid devices | For all motorised wheels; must be compatible with the vehicle |

**For trucks:** All-wheel-drive trucks with M+S winter tyres on drive axles comply. Chains must be accessible and usable on the vehicle.

---

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle type | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Cars, vans | YES — in mountain zone Nov–Mar |
| Trucks >3.5t | YES — in mountain zone Nov–Mar |
| Buses/coaches | YES |
| Agricultural vehicles | Exempt |

---

## Enforcement

By **Gendarmerie Nationale** and **Police Nationale** at roadside in mountain zones.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| No winter tyres/chains in obligatory zone | **€135** | Décret n°2020-1264; Code de la route |
| Repeat offence | €375 | Code de la route |
