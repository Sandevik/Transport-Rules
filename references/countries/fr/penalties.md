## Overview

France enforces transport regulations through DREAL (Directions Régionales de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement) transport inspectors and the Gendarmerie/Police Nationale at roadside. France uses a classified violation system (contravention de 4e ou 5e classe, délit) with fines graduated by severity. Immediate payment at roadside is possible for small fixed-rate contraventions, but not required. Serious violations lead to formal prosecution (amende pénale). France applies strong liability on the transport operator (donneur d'ordre responsibility).

| Insufficient daily rest > 2h | 5e classe | €375–€1,500 | C. de la route |
