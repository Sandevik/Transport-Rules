## Overview

France has a well-structured system for **Convois Exceptionnels** (exceptional transport). Loads exceeding standard dimension or weight limits require an authorisation from the **Préfecture** or the **Service Instructeur des Transports Exceptionnels (SITE)**. France uses a 4-category system based on load dimensions. Police escort is mandatory for the largest loads.

---

## Categories of Exceptional Transport in France

| Category | Dimensions (length / width / height) | Weight | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st category** | L: 20–25m, W: 2.5–3.0m, H: 4.0–4.5m | Up to 72t | Standard authorisation, no escort |
| **2nd category** | L: 25–37m, W: 3.0–4.0m, H: 4.5–5.0m | 72–120t | Authorisation + pilot vehicle (véhicule d'accompagnement) |
| **3rd category** | L: 37–50m, W: 4.0–5.0m, H: 5.0–6.0m | 120–240t | Authorisation + 2 pilot vehicles + police coordination |
| **4th category** | Exceeds 3rd category limits | >240t | Special ministerial authorisation + Gendarmerie escort |

---

## Application Procedure

Applications submitted to the **Préfecture of departure** or to the **DREAL** (Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement):
- Online via: https://www.service-public.fr
- Lead time: **15–30 working days** for standard authorisations; longer for categories 3–4
- Annual blanket permits available for recurring exceptional transport on defined corridors

---

## Operating Restrictions

- Exceptional loads prohibited during **driving ban periods** (weekends, holidays — see driving-bans.md)
- Night restrictions: most exceptional transport limited to **06:00–22:00**
- Some loads restricted to **specific time windows** and specific route segments

---

## Pilot Vehicle Requirements

| Category | Pilot vehicles | Markings |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | None required (but recommended for width >3.0m) | — |
| 2nd | 1 pilot vehicle | Yellow flashing lights, "Convoi Exceptionnel" sign |
| 3rd | 2 pilot vehicles (front + rear) | As above |
| 4th | Police escort (Gendarmerie) | — |

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement by **Gendarmerie Nationale** and **DREAL** inspectors.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without authorisation | €1,500–€15,000 | Code de la route; Arrêté du 4 mai 2006 |
| Exceeding authorised dimensions | €750–€3,750 | Code de la route |
| Operating without required escort | €750 | Code de la route |
