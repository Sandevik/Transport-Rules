## Overview

France implements Directive 2002/15/EC through:

1. **Code du Travail** (Labour Code) — the general statutory framework; Articles L3121-1 et seq. govern working time
2. **Convention Collective Nationale des Transports Routiers et Activités Auxiliaires (CCNTR)** — the national collective agreement for road transport, identified as IDCC 16; this is the primary operational source for French road transport working time rules

The CCNTR is binding on all road freight transport employers in France (whether their drivers are French residents or posted workers performing cabotage). It extends the EU default reference period from 4 months to **12 months**, which is the most significant French deviation from the EU baseline.

---

## Maximum Working Hours

| Rule | Limit | Legal basis |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Average weekly working time | **48 hours/week** | Code du Travail L3121-23; Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 4 |
| Absolute weekly ceiling | **60 hours** | Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 4 |
| Reference period | **12 months** (extended from EU default 4 months via CCNTR collective agreement) | CCNTR; Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 4 |
| Maximum daily working time | **10 hours** | Code du Travail L3121-18 |

The 12-month reference period is a major operational flexibility: a driver can work more than 48 hours/week in busy periods provided the annual average stays within 48 hours/week.

---

## Night Work

| Rule | Requirement | Legal basis |
|------|------------|-------------|
| Night period definition | **21:00–06:00** (Code du Travail general definition; CCNTR may specify narrower window) | Code du Travail L3122-29 |
| Night work maximum | **10 hours** within any 24-hour period containing night work | Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 7 |
| Night worker threshold | Worker classified as night worker if they work ≥3 hours in night period on ≥270 days/year, or if they regularly work ≥half of their annual working time during night period | Code du Travail L3122-31 |
| Night worker entitlements | Compensatory rest or financial compensation; occupational health assessment | CCNTR; Code du Travail L3122-38–43 |

## Breaks

| Cumulative working time | Required break |
|------------------------|----------------|
| After 6 hours | ≥ 30 minutes (may be split into two 15-min periods) |
| After 9 hours | ≥ 45 minutes |

French rules also require a minimum **11 consecutive hours off** per 24-hour period, which aligns with EU 561/2006 daily rest requirements.

---

## Rest Time and Daily Rest

France applies EU 561/2006 driving/rest rules directly (see references/countries/fr/driving-rest.md). Working time rules under the CCNTR add:
- **11 consecutive hours off** as a daily minimum between working periods
- This is stricter than the Code du Travail general minimum of 11 hours but consistent with EU 561/2006 daily rest

## Self-Employed Drivers

Self-employed drivers (travailleurs indépendants) engaged in road transport are covered by Directive 2002/15/EC as implemented in France from August 2020. The Code du Travail provisions generally apply by analogy; enforcement is primarily through DREAL inspections and self-reporting.

---

## Specific French Rules

### Drivers' Working Time Definition (CCNTR)

Under the CCNTR, "working time" for road freight drivers includes:
- Driving time
- Loading and unloading (if driver is required to assist or supervise)
- Administrative work (documentation, etc.)
- Waiting time where the driver is not free to dispose of their time

"Availability time" (known waiting, e.g., ferry crossings, waiting at customer site while free to rest) is **not** working time under the CCNTR, consistent with Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 3(b).

### On-Call Time

On-call time in France follows the Code du Travail general rules: time spent on-call where the worker is at the employer's disposal but not actively working is not working time unless activated. Transport-specific rules in the CCNTR may provide further detail.

## Record-Keeping

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|-------------|--------|-------------|
| Working time records | Must be maintained; tachograph data constitutes records for driving time | Code du Travail L3171-2; Directive 2002/15/EC Art. 9 |
| Retention period | **5 years** (France applies the general Code du Travail retention period, stricter than EU minimum of 2 years) | Code du Travail L3171-4 |
| Format | Paper or electronic | CCNTR |
| Availability to inspectors | Must be available at company headquarters; summary produced on request | DREAL inspection powers |

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **DREAL (Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement)** | Primary transport enforcement; roadside and company inspections; checks driving/rest time and working time |
| **DRIEETS / DREETS (Direction Régionale Interdépartementale de l'Économie, de l'Emploi, du Travail et des Solidarités)** | Labour inspectorate (successor to DIRECCTE); company-level working time audits; posted workers |
| **Gendarmerie Nationale** | Roadside enforcement, particularly on secondary roads |
| **Police Nationale** | Roadside enforcement in urban areas |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine | Legal basis |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| Exceeding maximum working hours | Up to **€1,500** per employee per violation (contravention) | Code du Travail R3124-14 |
| Repeat violation or systematic breach | Up to **€3,000** per employee (récidive) | Code du Travail R3124-14 |
| Failure to maintain working time records | Up to **€1,500** per violation | Code du Travail R3124-14 |
| Failure to provide required breaks | Up to **€1,500** per violation | Code du Travail R3124-14 |
| Cabotage by foreign carrier: failure to apply CCNTR minimum standards | Retroactive wage claims + DREAL fines; see references/countries/fr/posting-workers.md | Code du Travail L1262-4 |

Note: French working time fines are lower per incident than German fines (€1,500 vs €15,000) but repeated violations accumulate and are multiplied by the number of employees concerned.

**For posting violations** (foreign carriers in France), the penalties are significantly higher — see references/countries/fr/posting-declaration.md.

---

## Interaction with EU 561/2006

Same as EU baseline: driving time under EU 561/2006 counts as working time under Directive 2002/15/EC / CCNTR. The more restrictive rule governs when the rules overlap.
