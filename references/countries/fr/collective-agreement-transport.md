## Overview

France has a **national sector-wide collective agreement** for road transport: the **Convention Collective Nationale des Transports Routiers et activités auxiliaires du transport** (CCNTR), IDCC 16, signed 21 December 1950. It is one of France's oldest and largest sectoral agreements, covering an estimated 600,000+ workers across road haulage, courier, bus/coach, and auxiliary transport activities.

The CCNTR is negotiated between the employer federations (FNTR, TLF, UNOSTRA, FNTV) and the representative trade unions (CGT, CFDT, FO, CFTC, CFE-CGC).

---

## Scope

The CCNTR covers:
- All employees in **road freight transport** (conducteurs/drivers, loading staff, logistics workers)
- **Auxiliary transport activities** (freight forwarding, warehousing, courier services)
- **Passenger transport by road** (covered by separate annexes under the same convention)
- **Self-employed occasional drivers** are NOT covered (they fall under commercial law)

---

## Structure — Annexes by Employee Category

| Annex | Category | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Annex I** | Ouvriers (manual workers) | Truck drivers (conducteurs), warehouse operatives |
| **Annex II** | Employés (office/admin workers) | Dispatchers, administrative staff |
| **Annex III** | Techniciens et Agents de Maîtrise (TAM) | Supervisors, team leaders |
| **Annex IV** | Ingénieurs et Cadres | Managers, engineers |

Professional truck drivers (conducteurs routiers) are covered under **Annex I** as ouvriers.

---

## Key Provisions

### Classification for Drivers (Annex I)

Truck drivers are classified by vehicle type and route:
- **Groupe 6:** Short-haul driver, vehicles up to 3.5t
- **Groupe 7:** Regional driver, vehicles 3.5t–19t, radius to ~150 km
- **Groupe 8:** Long-haul driver (grand routier), vehicles >19t, national/international
- Classification determines base pay coefficients

### Frais de Déplacement (Travel/Subsistence Allowances)

The CCNTR defines allowances for drivers away from home base (not wages — tax treatment applies):

| Type | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Petit déplacement | Driver returns home nightly | Meal allowance applies |
| Grand déplacement | Driver away overnight (domestic) | Full daily subsistence + hotel or sleeper cab allowance |
| International long-haul | Away abroad | Higher international rate |

*Specific amounts are negotiated periodically by the Joint Commission (Commission Paritaire Nationale) and updated via avenant (amendment). Check current rates via FNTR or Légifrance — rates change annually.*

### Working Time

The CCNTR aligns with but extends EU Directive 2002/15/EC (confirmed against Légifrance IDCC 16):
- Working time framework: **Article 12** (CCNTR)
- Reference period: **12 months** (extended from EU default 4 months via CCNTR — confirmed via 4 October 2017 accord on working time)
- Night work: governed by **Article 14 bis** and the **14 November 2001 accord** on night work
- Night period: **21:00–06:00** (French definition — different from EU 00:00–04:00 default)
- Additional rest: CCNTR may provide better rest conditions than the legal minimum
- Most recent wage provisions: **2 October 2023 accord** and **4 December 2023 avenant**

### Overtime

Premium rates above standard working hours are set per annex. For drivers (Annex I):
- Hours 36–43/week: increased rate
- Hours above 43/week: higher rate
- Exact percentages set by periodic wage negotiations — verify current rates with FNTR or CFDT Transports

### Notice Periods and Termination

Standard French Labour Code (Code du Travail) applies, with CCNTR potentially providing enhanced notice periods depending on seniority:
- Typically 1 month (under 2 years seniority), 2 months (2+ years)
- Dismissal follows French Labour Code procedures (entretien préalable etc.)

## Comparison with Swedish Transportavtalet

| Feature | France CCNTR | Sweden Transportavtalet |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Légifrance IDCC 16 | Between Sveriges Åkeriföretag and Transport Workers' Union |
| Coverage | ~600,000+ workers (all transport categories) | Road freight drivers specifically |
| Reference period | 12 months | Aligned with ATL |
| Night period | 21:00–06:00 | Per EU baseline |
| Subsistence | Petit/grand déplacement system | Traktamente (tax-free allowances) |
| National or regional | **National** | **National** |

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| DREAL (Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement) | Working time and transport compliance |
| DIRECCTE / DREETS (Direction Régionale de l'Économie, de l'Emploi, du Travail et des Solidarités) | Labour inspectorate — CCNTR compliance |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below CCNTR minimum wage | Back pay + civil liability | Code du Travail; CCNTR |
| Not applying CCNTR to covered employees | Administrative fine; union legal action | Code du Travail Art. L2262-11 |
| Failure to pay frais de déplacement | Civil liability; back payment owed | CCNTR Annex I; Code du Travail |
