## Overview

France issues Community Licences through the DREAL (Directions Régionales de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement) regional offices on behalf of DGITM. French operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. Professional competence (CPC) exams are administered by approved French examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | French specifics |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Establishment | Genuine French place of business | SIREN registration (French business registry) |
| Good repute | No serious transport/criminal offences | Checked via national transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC exam | Via approved examination centres; exam in French |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**DREAL** (Directions Régionales de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement):
- Regional offices issue Community Licences and national transport licences
- National register: https://www.registres-transport.fr/

---

## Community Licence

Issued by DREAL; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in France within 7 days after an international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** before next cabotage series in France
- SIPSI posting declaration required (see posting-declaration.md)
- CCN Transport Routier minimum wages apply to cabotage (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Driver Attestation

French attestation de conducteur for non-EU/EEA national drivers issued by DREAL.

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | Up to €15,000 | Code des transports L.3452-1 |
| Exceeding cabotage operations | Up to €15,000 | Code des transports |
