## Overview

France applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the DGAL (Direction générale de l'alimentation) under the Ministry of Agriculture. Enforcement is conducted by DDPP (Directions Départementales de la Protection des Populations) veterinary inspectors at regional level. France is a major producer and exporter of cattle, sheep, and horses.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | DDPP (departmental) |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | DDPP (departmental) |

---

## National Specifics

- TRACES veterinary movement documents required for all cross-border movements
- France has designated authorised rest points (postes de contrôle agréés) for long-distance journeys
- DDPP inspectors are active at departure points (abattoirs, farms) and at border inspection posts

**Border Inspection Posts (France):**
- Calais / Channel Tunnel (for third-country animal imports arriving via UK)
- Marseille port (Mediterranean imports)
- Paris Roissy airport (air freight live animals)

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy in vehicle
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for journeys >8 hours
- Veterinary health certificate (certificat sanitaire)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations. France fully applies EU 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without Type 2 authorisation | Up to €15,000 | Code rural et de la pêche maritime |
| Welfare violations | Up to €15,000 | Code rural |
| Failure to maintain journey log | Up to €7,500 | Code rural |
