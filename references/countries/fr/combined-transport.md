## Overview

France implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. France has significant HGV driving restrictions (Saturday night/Sunday bans), making the driving ban exemption under combined transport particularly valuable for operators. France has an extensive rail freight network (managed by SNCF Réseau) with numerous intermodal terminals, and the Channel Tunnel provides a key combined transport rail link to the UK and northern Europe.

---

## HGV Driving Bans (Why Combined Transport Exemption Matters)

France operates one of Europe's stricter HGV ban regimes:

| Period | Ban applies to |
|--------|---------------|
| Saturday 22:00 – Sunday 22:00 | >7.5t vehicles, year-round |
| July/August extended ban | >7.5t vehicles, additional Saturday daytime restrictions |
| Public holidays | Specific restrictions vary |

**Combined transport exemption**: Road legs of qualifying combined transport journeys are **exempt** from these bans. The driver must carry documentary evidence of the combined transport booking. This is particularly important for operations involving the Benelux–France corridor on weekends.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Terminals

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Bonneuil-sur-Marne / Rungis | Paris region | Rail + road hub |
| Lyon (Vénissieux, Sogaris) | Lyon | North–South, Spain |
| Marseille (Mourepiane) | Marseille | Mediterranean port |
| Calais | Pas-de-Calais | Channel Tunnel, UK |
| Bordeaux | Southwest | Atlantic corridor |
| Strasbourg | Alsace | Rhine corridor |

**Channel Tunnel**: Eurotunnel / Getlink Le Shuttle Cargo transports trailers and swap-bodies through the tunnel — this qualifies as combined transport under 92/106/EEC.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail, waterway, or Channel Tunnel booking confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations. France applies the directive fully including the driving ban exemption.
