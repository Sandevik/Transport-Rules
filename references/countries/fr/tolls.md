# France — Road Tolls (Péage)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.autoroutes.fr/ — ASFA: French motorway toll operators association

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

France operates an extensive **motorway toll (péage)** network on its autoroutes. Unlike Germany or Belgium, there is no universal distance-based charge for all roads — tolls apply specifically on autoroutes (motorways) with individual toll plazas or open-road gantries. Most autoroutes in France are concession-operated by private companies. HGVs pay significantly more than passenger cars.

---

## Vehicles and Rate Structure

HGV rates are categorized by vehicle class. There is **no emission class differentiation** in French motorway tolls as of 2026.

| Class | Vehicle type | Typical EUR/km | Range |
|-------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| Class 3 | 2-axle truck >3.5t | ~€0.115/km | €0.08–0.17/km |
| Class 4 | 3-axle truck / 3-axle artic | ~€0.165/km | €0.11–0.24/km |
| Class 5 | 4+ axle combination (5-axle artic) | ~€0.200/km | €0.13–0.28/km |

**Key route examples (Class 5 = 5-axle artic):**

| Route | Distance | Approx. total | Avg. €/km |
|-------|---------|--------------|-----------|
| A1 Paris–Lille | 225 km | ~€45 | €0.20/km |
| A4 Paris–Strasbourg | 490 km | ~€75 | €0.15/km |
| A6/A7 Paris–Marseille | 775 km | ~€140 | €0.18/km |
| A10 Paris–Bordeaux | 580 km | ~€95 | €0.16/km |

*Note: Rates are indicative averages across sections. Use operator route calculators for exact amounts.*

## Payment Methods

- **Liber-t transponder (télépéage):** OBU system for automated payment. Accepted at all French autoroutes and interoperable with other European EETS systems.
- **Credit/debit card** at toll booths (unmanned or staffed)
- **Cash** at staffed booths (being phased out on some routes)

**For HGV operators:** Registering a Liber-t account via a toll service provider is strongly recommended for frequent French operations.
