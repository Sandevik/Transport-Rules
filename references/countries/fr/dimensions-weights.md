## Overview

France applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Code de la route and arrêtés relatifs aux conditions techniques d'admission des véhicules. France has no equivalent to the Nordic large vehicle exception and applies EU standard limits. Exceptional transport (convois exceptionnels) requires advance permits and police escorts for large loads.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport (Convois Exceptionnels)

France has a detailed permit system for loads exceeding standard limits:
- **1st category**: up to 3m wide, 25m long, 48t — general permit, 4 days notice
- **2nd category**: up to 4m wide, 50m long, 72t — prefectural permit
- **3rd category**: larger loads — ministerial authorisation required

Applications via MTES/DGITM or CEREMA (Centre d'études et d'expertise sur les risques, l'environnement, la mobilité et l'aménagement).

Police escort required for convois dépassant certain dimensions.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant weight/dimension deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC. France applies EU standard limits.

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — France demands consignation from foreign carriers for overweight violations.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding GVW (up to 5%) | €135–€1,500 | Code de la route R.312-6 |
| Exceeding GVW (>20%) | €1,500–€7,500 + vehicle immobilisation | Code de la route |
| Operating convoi exceptionnel without permit | €1,500–€7,500 | Code de la route |
