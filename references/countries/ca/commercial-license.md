## Overview

Canada has a dual federal-provincial framework for commercial transport licensing. **Transport Canada** regulates extra-provincial (interprovincial + international) carriers under the Motor Vehicle Transport Act (MVTA). **Provincial governments** regulate intra-provincial carriers. The National Safety Code (NSC) Standards — developed by CCMTA — provide a harmonised framework adopted by all provinces. There is no equivalent to the EU Community Licence; instead, carriers register with provincial authorities and operate under NSC fitness standards.

## Carrier Registration and Safety Fitness

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Safety Fitness Certificate | Required for all commercial carriers; issued provincially | NSC Standard 1 |
| CVOR (Ontario) | Commercial Vehicle Operator's Registration | Ontario HTA |
| NSC Safety Certificate | Most provinces require NSC-equivalent safety fitness | NSC Standard 1 |
| IFTA (fuel tax) | International Fuel Tax Agreement — mandatory for interprovincial | IFTA Agreement |
| IRP (apportioned plates) | International Registration Plan — for multi-province/US operations | IRP Agreement |

## Driver Licensing (CDL Equivalent)

Canada uses a **Class 1 / Class A** licence for combination vehicles (equivalent to US CDL-A):

| Class | Vehicles | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Class 1 / Class A | Tractor-trailer combinations; all vehicles | National standard; provinces issue |
| Class 2 / Class B | Buses, large trucks | |
| Class 3 / Class C | Trucks >11t GVW without trailer | |
| Class D / Class E | Various bus and intermediate | |

- Air brake endorsement (Z) required for vehicles with air brakes
- Knowledge + road test required; medical examination required
- Issued by: provincial licensing authorities (DriveTest in ON, ICBC in BC, etc.)

## Commercial Driver's Licence Requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum age | 18 years (interprovincial); some provinces 19+ |
| Medical | Annual or biennial medical required for commercial drivers |
| Knowledge test | Province-specific; covers NSC standards |
| Road test | Class 1 road test with tractor-trailer |
| English/French | Required for road signage comprehension |

## Hours of Service Compliance (Carrier Obligation)

- Carriers must maintain HOS records for all drivers for minimum 6 months
- ELD (Electronic Logging Device) mandatory since January 1, 2023 (NSC Standard 16)
- Random roadside inspections by provincial CVSA-member enforcement officers

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (CAD) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without Safety Fitness Certificate | CAD 500–5,000 | Provincial HTA |
| No ELD / falsified records | CAD 1,000–10,000 | NSC Standard 16; provincial law |
| Operating with expired/suspended CVOR (ON) | CAD 1,000–10,000 | Ontario HTA |
