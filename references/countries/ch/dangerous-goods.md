## Overview

Switzerland applies ADR under its international obligations. Switzerland AETR signatory; ADR applies under bilateral agreement with EU; ASTRA and BAZG are competent authorities. The national competent authority is ASTRA / BAZG. ADR 2025 (biennial revision) is in force.

## Scope and Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| ADR certificate (driver) | Required for all ADR transport | ADR 8.2 |
| ADR certificate renewal | Every 5 years | ADR 8.2.2.8 |
| Certificate categories | Core + Tank + Explosives (Class 1) + Radioactive (Class 7) | ADR 8.2 |
| Vehicle marking | Orange plates (front + rear) + hazard labels | ADR 5.3 |
| Transport document | Mandatory for all ADR shipments | ADR 5.4 |
| ADR Certificate of Approval | Required for tank vehicles + Class 1/7 | ADR Chapter 9.1 |
| Instructions in writing | Tremcard-equivalent in cab (all languages of journey) | ADR 5.4.3 |

## Tunnel Restrictions

ADR tunnel categories apply in Switzerland:
- **Category A:** No restriction
- **Category B:** Class 1 (except 1.4S) restricted
- **Category C:** Classes 1, 7 + large quantities flammable restricted
- **Category D:** Most dangerous goods in large quantities restricted
- **Category E:** All goods requiring orange plates restricted

Check current tunnel categories: [ASTRA / BAZG](https://www.astra.admin.ch/)

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CHF) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| No ADR driver certificate | Significant fine | ADR; national law |
| Missing transport document | Fine | National law |
| No vehicle marking | Fine + vehicle stopped | National law |
| No Certificate of Approval (tanks) | Vehicle prohibited | National law |
