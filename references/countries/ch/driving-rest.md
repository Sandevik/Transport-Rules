## Overview

Switzerland is not an EU or EEA member. For driving and rest time, Switzerland applies the **AETR Agreement** (not EU Regulation 561/2006 directly). Since the AETR rules are substantively aligned with EU 561/2006 since 2010, the practical limits are identical. Enforcement is by **ASTRA** (Federal Roads Office) and cantonal police. Switzerland also has bilateral land transport agreements with the EU.

---

## Core Rules (AETR — identical to EU 561/2006)

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Daily driving (normal) | 9 hours |
| Daily driving (extended, max 2×/week) | 10 hours |
| Weekly driving | 56 hours |
| Two-week driving | 90 hours |
| Break after 4.5h driving | 45 min (or 15+30 split) |
| Regular daily rest | 11 consecutive hours |
| Weekly rest (normal) | 45 consecutive hours |
| Weekly rest (reduced) | 24 consecutive hours |

See `references/aetr/driving-rest-time.md` for full AETR rule text.

---

## Key Differences from EU Operations

| Topic | EU 561/2006 | Switzerland (AETR) |
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation (directly applicable) | AETR Treaty |
| Advance payment at roadside | Permitted (varies) | **PROHIBITED** (AETR Art. 22ter) |
| Smart tachograph v2 mandate | EU retrofit deadlines | AETR Annex 1B — no Smart v2 obligation yet |
| Mobility Package cab prohibition | YES (from Feb 2022) | Not in AETR text |

---

## Swiss Working Time (Additional National Rule)

Switzerland also has the **Chauffeurgesetz (ChG)** — Federal Law on Work in Driver's Transport — which applies to employed drivers in Switzerland beyond AETR rules. This covers working time, breaks, and rest for Swiss-domiciled operations.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role | Website |
|-----------|------|---------|
| ASTRA | Federal Roads Office — rules and permits | https://www.astra.admin.ch/ |
| Kantons-polizei | Cantonal police — roadside checks | varies by canton |

**No advance payment at roadside** — AETR Art. 22ter prohibits cash payment deposits for AETR violations.
