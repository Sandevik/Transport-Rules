## Overview

Switzerland has **no federal law mandating winter tyres** for passenger vehicles. However, under Article 29 of the Road Traffic Act (SVG/LCR), drivers must ensure their vehicle is roadworthy and appropriate for conditions. Courts have interpreted this to mean that driving on summer tyres in icy/snowy conditions constitutes a violation. In practice, **chains are mandatory** at specific mountain passes and on certain roads when signs indicate, and **cantonal police actively fine** drivers for inadequate tyres in winter conditions.

For **trucks and commercial vehicles**, additional requirements apply — see below.

---

## Federal Rule — No Fixed Date Mandate

| Vehicle type | Mandate |
|---|---|
| Passenger vehicles | **No fixed dates** — situational (must be safe for road conditions per SVG Art. 29) |
| Trucks >3.5t GVW | **No fixed federal dates** — same situational rule applies |

Unlike Austria, Germany, Finland, and Norway, Switzerland has no calendar-based mandatory winter tyre period.

---

## In Practice — Mountain Pass Chain Requirements

Many Swiss mountain passes and Alpine roads display **chain signs** (round blue sign with chain symbol) during winter conditions. When the sign is displayed, all vehicles must either have winter tyres providing adequate grip OR fit chains:

| Pass / Road | Typical chain requirement period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gotthard (old road, Route 2) | Nov–Apr when sign displayed | Tunnel alternative available |
| Simplon Pass (Route 9) | Nov–Apr | |
| Great St. Bernard (Route 21) | Oct–May | |
| San Bernardino (Route 13) | Nov–Apr | |
| Julier / Maloja (Graubünden) | Oct–May | |

When chains are required and a driver proceeds without them and becomes stuck, they may block the pass — **fines and towing costs** apply.

---

## Truck-Specific Requirements

For heavy commercial vehicles (>3.5t), cantonal traffic police and ASTRA guidance is that:
- **All-season or winter tyres on drive axle** are expected November–March in mountain areas
- **Chains must be carried** and fitted when conditions or signs require
- Some cantonal roads post specific requirements for trucks in winter

---

## Chain Requirements for Trucks

Trucks must carry and be able to fit chains when crossing Alpine passes in winter. Minimum requirement:
- Chains on drive axle wheels (at least on one pair per drive axle)
- Quick-fit chain systems (Spikes-Spider or similar) acceptable

---

## Enforcement

Cantonal traffic police (Kantonspolizei). If a truck is stuck on a mountain road due to inadequate traction, driver will be fined, the road may be blocked, and recovery costs are at driver's expense.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CHF) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Driving in conditions requiring winter equipment without it | CHF 100–300 (driver) + points | SVG Art. 29; cantonal enforcement |
| Blocking a mountain road due to inadequate tyres | CHF 100–500 + recovery costs | SVG Art. 29 |
| Ignoring chain sign (driving without chains when required) | CHF 100–500 | SVG Art. 29; cantonal ordinance |
