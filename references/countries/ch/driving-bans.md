## Overview

Switzerland enforces both a **night driving ban** and a **Sunday driving ban** for heavy goods vehicles. These bans are Federal law (national level) and apply on all Swiss roads. Additional cantonal restrictions may apply in some urban areas. Switzerland is a critical Alpine transit country — the night and Sunday bans significantly affect scheduling for SE→IT or DE→IT corridor operators who must plan Swiss transits carefully.

---

## Night Driving Ban (Nachtfahrverbot)

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 3.5t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| All nights, year-round | **22:00–05:00** |

---

## Sunday Driving Ban (Sonntagsfahrverbot)

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 3.5t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| Sundays and national public holidays | **00:00–24:00** (full day) |

Switzerland's Sunday ban is stricter than Germany's — it covers the **full 24-hour day** (00:00–24:00), not just 00:00–22:00 as in Germany.

---

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle | Night ban | Sunday ban |
|---|---|---|
| Goods vehicles > 3.5t GVW | YES | YES |
| Goods vehicles ≤ 3.5t GVW | NO | NO |
| Trucks with trailers (any weight) | YES | YES |
| Agricultural vehicles | Exempt | Exempt |

Note: Switzerland uses > 3.5t threshold (lower than Germany/Austria's 7.5t), affecting more vehicle types.

---

## Exemptions

| Exemption | Applies to |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh food, milk, chilled goods, live animals |
| Fuel and energy | Petrol, diesel, heating oil |
| Press/newspapers | Time-critical media |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal road legs (with documentation) |
| Agricultural vehicles | Farm machinery, harvest transport |
| Emergency services | Police, fire, medical, civil defence |
| Breakdown/recovery | Vehicles responding to breakdowns |
| Military | Swiss Armed Forces |
| Transit between two non-Swiss countries | Vehicles merely transiting Switzerland under certain conditions — but must be able to demonstrate exemption |

## Alpine Tunnel and Mountain Pass Considerations

Switzerland's driving bans interact critically with Alpine transit:

### Gotthard Road Tunnel
- Height restriction: **4.80m** (lower with roadworks)
- **Convoy system** on weekends and peak periods: vehicles queued in convoys with police escort
- Bans apply in/through tunnels identically to open roads
- Gotthard Railway (ROLA/rail motorway) available as alternative — consider for Sunday/night transits

### Simplon, San Bernardino, San Gottardo passes
- Mountain passes may close in winter; tunnel routing then mandatory
- Height restrictions vary

### LSVA Distance-Based Charge
Switzerland's LSVA (Leistungsabhängige Schwerverkehrsabgabe) applies to all vehicles > 3.5t at all times — driving bans do not affect LSVA calculation; charges accumulate only during permitted driving hours.

## Enforcement and Fines

Enforcement by **Kantonspolizei** (cantonal police) and **Fedpol** (Federal Police) at border crossings and on roads.

| Violation | Fine (CHF) |
|---|---|
| Night driving ban violation | CHF 300–1,000 |
| Sunday driving ban violation | CHF 500–3,000 |
| Repeat/serious violation | Up to CHF 5,000 + vehicle immobilisation |

Foreign operators: Fines are typically demanded cash-on-the-spot or via immediate bank transfer (no Swiss police will release a vehicle on a promise to pay later).
