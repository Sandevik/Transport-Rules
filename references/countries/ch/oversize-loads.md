# Switzerland — Oversize and Exceptional Loads (Sondertransporte)

**EU Baseline:** N/A — Switzerland is not an EU member. Swiss law applies exclusively.
**AETR:** Driving and rest time only. All other rules are Swiss national law.

**Sources:**

## Overview

Switzerland operates a **two-tier permit system** for exceptional transport: federal (ASTRA) for motorways and nationally significant roads, and cantonal for cantonal and communal roads. The system is governed by the **Ausnahmebewilligungsverordnung (AuBV)** and administered primarily by ASTRA's online permit portal.

Switzerland's standard maximum weights (28 t for a 2-axle vehicle; 40 t for road trains on standard roads) and widths (2.55 m) align broadly with EU norms, but the **40 t total weight limit on standard roads** is strictly enforced. The **transit route restrictions** (particularly the Alpine tunnels) and the **LSVA** (leistungsabhängige Schwerverkehrsabgabe — kilometre-based heavy vehicle tax) make Switzerland a distinctive and more complex transit environment than EU member states.

## Standard Limits (VTS / SVG)

| Parameter | Swiss limit |
|-----------|------------|
| Gross vehicle weight (general) | 40 t |
| Gross vehicle weight (special permit possible) | Up to 44 t with cantonal or ASTRA permit |
| Single axle load | 10 t |
| Drive axle load | 11.5 t |
| Width | 2.55 m |
| Height | 4.00 m |
| Length (articulated) | 16.50 m |
| Length (road train) | 18.75 m |

**Important:** Switzerland does **not** permit the Nordic-style EMS 34.5 m / 74 t combinations at any time, even on designated roads.

---

## Thresholds Triggering a Permit

Any vehicle/load exceeding the above standard limits requires an **Ausnahmebewilligung** (exceptional permit):

| Dimension | Trigger | Authority |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Width > 2.55 m | Mandatory permit | ASTRA (motorways) / Canton (other roads) |
| Height > 4.0 m | Mandatory permit | ASTRA / Canton |
| Length > 18.75 m | Mandatory permit | ASTRA / Canton |
| Weight > 40 t | Mandatory permit | ASTRA / Canton |
| Axle load > standard | Mandatory permit | ASTRA / Canton |

## Permit Types

### ASTRA Ausnahmebewilligung (Federal permit)

Issued by ASTRA for transport on the national motorway network (Nationalstrassen):

- **Online application:** via ASTRA portal (https://www.astra.admin.ch/astra/en/home/themes/commercial-vehicles/special-transport-permits.html)
- Route must be specified with motorway sections
- Load parameters (dimensions, axle loads) entered digitally
- ASTRA checks bridge tables and clearances for the motorway network
- Processing time: typically **5–10 working days**; urgent: **2–3 working days** (fee premium)

### Cantonal Ausnahmebewilligung

For cantonal and communal roads, each affected canton issues a separate permit:
- Application to Kantonale Behörde (cantonal road authority) of each canton traversed
- Different forms and processes per canton
- Processing time: **5–15 working days** per canton
- Some cantons have moved to online systems; others remain paper-based

### Combination permits
For routes crossing both motorways and cantonal roads (most practical routes), operators must hold:
1. An ASTRA permit for motorway sections
2. Cantonal permit(s) for all cantonal road sections

---

## ASTRA Online Permit Portal

- The ASTRA portal supports online applications for federal road network segments
- Route entered via map interface or road number input
- Bridge clearance checks automated for the national network
- Permit issued electronically with conditions (speed, escort, time windows)
- Portal available in German, French, Italian (the three official Swiss transport languages)
- Foreign operators must register; a Swiss address or representative may be required for some processes

## Tunnel Restrictions — National Importance

Switzerland's Alpine tunnels are the critical bottleneck for exceptional transport. Each tunnel has specific rules.

### Gotthard Road Tunnel (16.9 km, A2)
- **ADR tunnel category:** D/E (varies by substance — some Category E restrictions apply for certain explosive and radioactive goods)
- **Max speed:** 80 km/h
- **Max height:** No specific reduced limit but physical clearance is the standard 4.5 m
- **Exceptional loads:** ASTRA permit required; load dimensions must be confirmed against tunnel cross-section
- **Convoy system:** Truck convoy operates at intervals (approximately 60–150 HGV per hour per direction); exceptional loads coordinated within convoy slots
- **Weekend restrictions:** Exceptional load permits typically restrict Saturday afternoon and Sunday movement in the Gotthard corridor (also applies to standard HGVs under the Swiss Sunday ban)
- **Overtaking:** Prohibited throughout the tunnel

### San Bernardino Tunnel (6.6 km, A13)
- Alternative to Gotthard for east Switzerland
- ADR restrictions apply; ASTRA permit required for exceptional loads
- Lower traffic volumes than Gotthard — more flexibility for exceptional load scheduling

### Simplon Road (open pass, not tunnel except rail tunnel)
- Height and width restrictions on pass road in winter
- Recommended alternative route for exceptional loads when available
- Note: Simplon railway tunnel uses Autoverlad (car/truck train) — exceptional loads cannot use the Autoverlad due to loading gauge restrictions; road or other routing required

### Great St Bernard Tunnel (5.8 km, FR–CH border)
- International: permits needed from both Swiss (VS canton) and Italian authorities
- ADR restrictions: comparable to other major Alpine tunnels

### Mont Blanc Tunnel (11.6 km, FR–IT — does not pass through Switzerland)
- Not Swiss territory but relevant for international routing
- See `references/topics/major-tunnel-restrictions.md`

## LSVA (Leistungsabhängige Schwerverkehrsabgabe)

Switzerland's **LSVA** is a distance- and weight-based heavy vehicle charge that **applies to exceptional loads as well as standard HGVs**:

- Applicable to all vehicles > 3.5 t GVW on Swiss roads
- Rate based on: distance driven in Switzerland × emission class × vehicle weight
- **Exceptional loads**: weight used for LSVA calculation is the actual vehicle + load weight
- Measured electronically (OBU — on-board unit) or at manual LSVA declaration points at border
- Foreign vehicles must install a temporary OBU or use the manual declaration system
- No Sunday exemption for LSVA obligation (it applies whenever moving)

LSVA is managed by **BAZG**. Non-payment or under-declaration: significant fines.

## Night and Sunday Restrictions

Switzerland has strict transport bans:

| Period | Restriction |
|--------|------------|
| **Sunday all day** | No HGV transport > 3.5 t (unless exempt for specific purposes) |
| **Night 22:00–05:00** | No HGV transport > 3.5 t on public roads |
| **Public holidays** | Same as Sunday |

**Exceptional loads** are subject to these same bans. A **special exemption** (Nachtfahrtbewilligung / Sonntagsfahrtbewilligung) can be applied for, but is only granted for genuine operational necessity and is uncommon.

**This is stricter than most EU member states**, where permit-holders can sometimes get night/Sunday slots for exceptional loads. In Switzerland the default answer is no, and exemptions are genuinely exceptional.

## Escort and Pilot Vehicle Requirements

| Width range | Requirement |
|-------------|------------|
| 2.55–3.0 m | Permit only; no escort required |
| 3.0–3.5 m | 1 rear escort vehicle |
| 3.5–4.5 m | 1 front + 1 rear escort vehicle |
| > 4.5 m | Police escort (Polizeibegleitung) mandatory |

### Escort vehicle specifications
- Amber flashing beacon visible 360°
- "SCHWERTRANSPORT" / "TRANSPORT EXCEPTIONNEL" / "TRASPORTO ECCEZIONALE" sign boards (language depends on canton)
- Radio communication with load vehicle
- Escort driver must hold appropriate certification

### Police escort
- Required for widths > 4.5 m or by cantonal authority condition
- Requested through cantonal police (Kantonspolizei) in each canton traversed
- Lead time: 2–3 weeks minimum
- Fee: charged to operator (approximately CHF 150–250/hour per police vehicle)

## Fees

Swiss exceptional transport fees are set by AuBV and cantonal ordinances:

| Fee type | Approximate range |
|----------|------------------|
| ASTRA permit (motorway, single trip) | CHF 100–400 |
| ASTRA permit (motorway, annual blanket) | CHF 500–2,000 |
| Cantonal permit (per canton, single trip) | CHF 50–300 |
| Bridge assessment fee | CHF 200–800 per structure |
| Urgent processing premium | +50–100% of standard fee |

These fees are in addition to LSVA charges. For a cross-Switzerland route traversing 4–5 cantons plus motorways, total permit fees of CHF 500–1,500 for a single trip are typical.

## Cross-Border Notes

For loads entering Switzerland from the EU (Germany, Austria, France, Italy):

1. **LSVA starts at the Swiss border** — OBU or manual declaration required
2. **EU permit does not transfer** — Swiss ASTRA/cantonal permits required separately
3. **Sunday ban** applies from midnight Sunday in Switzerland even if no ban in origin country
4. **Tunnel route selection**: Gotthard is the main HGV corridor but exceptional loads should pre-book through ASTRA system
5. **ADR dangerous goods** on exceptional loads: ADR applies throughout Switzerland (Directive 2008/68/EC equivalent via bilateral CH–EU agreement); tunnel ADR categories must be respected

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (CHF) | Consequences |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| No Ausnahmebewilligung | Up to CHF 10,000 | Vehicle immobilisation |
| Exceeding permit conditions (route, time) | CHF 500–5,000 | — |
| Exceeding width/weight without permit | CHF 500–10,000 | Immobilisation |
| Sunday/night driving without exemption | CHF 500–5,000 | — |
| LSVA under-declaration | CHF 500–20,000 | Customs sanctions |
| Bridge damage | Unlimited civil liability | Criminal prosecution possible |

Switzerland uses an **administrative sanction** system (Verwaltungssanktion) as well as criminal penalties for serious violations under SVG.
