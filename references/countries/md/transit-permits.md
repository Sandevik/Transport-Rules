**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national rules. Moldova is an EU candidate country (candidate status granted June 2022; accession negotiations opened June 2024).

## Overview

International road freight to, from, and through Moldova requires either a **bilateral road transport permit** or an **ITF/CEMT multilateral permit**. Moldova is not yet an EU member state; EU Community Licences do not provide automatic access.

Moldova is a **full member of the International Transport Forum (ITF, formerly CEMT)**, so ITF/CEMT multilateral permits are accepted for operations in and through Moldova. Moldova has also concluded a **DCFTA (Deep and Comprehensive Free Trade Area) Agreement** with the EU as part of its Association Agreement (in force 2016), which includes transport regulatory approximation provisions.

The national currency is the **MDL (Moldovan leu)**.

Moldova borders Romania (EU member) to the west and Ukraine to the north, east, and south. The **Transnistria** region (not under Moldovan government control) complicates transit on some east-west routes; see note below.

**EU-Moldova transport liberalisation:** Similar to the EU-Ukraine arrangement, Moldova may benefit from transport facilitation measures under its DCFTA and Association Agreement framework. Check current status of any permit-free arrangements with DG MOVE.

## ITF/CEMT Multilateral Permits

Moldova is a **full ITF member**:

| Permit Type | Validity | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Annual ITF permit | Calendar year | Minimum Euro III standard |
| Short-term ITF permit (30-day) | 30 consecutive days | |

ITF permits authorise bilateral and transit operations; cabotage prohibited.

---

## Bilateral Road Transport Permits

Moldova has bilateral road transport agreements with all EU member states and other countries. Romania and Ukraine are the primary bilateral partners given geographic proximity.

| Permit Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Bilateral permit | For direct transport between Moldova and partner country |
| Transit permit | For vehicles transiting Moldova |
| Third-country permit | For operations involving a third country |

---

## TIR Convention

Moldova is a **contracting party to the TIR Convention**. TIR Carnets are widely used, particularly given Moldova's position as a transit country between Romania/EU and Ukraine.

---

## Transnistria — Note

The Transnistria region (officially the Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic) is not under the control of the Moldovan government. This creates complications for road transport on east-west routes through Moldova:

- **Main E581/M21 route** through Tiraspol (Transnistria's main city) may require additional documentation or crossing formalities
- Most international freight now routes via southern Moldova or northern Moldova (avoiding Transnistria)
- Consult your customs agent or freight forwarder for current conditions

## Penalties

Penalties under Moldovan road transport law. Amounts in MDL (Moldovan leu).

| Violation | Penalty (MDL) | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Operating without required permit | 5,000–20,000 MDL | Vehicle detention possible |
| Permit conditions exceeded | 2,500–10,000 MDL | |
| AETR tachograph violation | 2,000–8,000 MDL | |
| Overloaded vehicle | Variable per tonne excess | |
| Cabotage violation | 10,000–30,000 MDL | |

*Note: MDL amounts are indicative; verify current rates with Ministry of Infrastructure and Regional Development.*
