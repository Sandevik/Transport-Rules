## Overview

Moldova is an AETR signatory. AETR Annex 1B governs tachograph requirements for international road transport operations. Digital tachographs are required for all vehicles subject to AETR scope (>3.5t GVW or >9 passengers). EU-issued driver cards are generally accepted for AETR operations.

## Calibration Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration interval | Every 2 years | AETR Annex 1B |
| After repair | Immediate recalibration required | AETR Annex 1B |
| Workshop type | Approved tachograph workshop only | AETR Annex 1B |
