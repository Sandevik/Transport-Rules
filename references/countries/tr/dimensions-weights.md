# Turkey — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national rules.

**Sources:**
- https://www.ubak.gov.tr/ — T.C. Ulaştırma — araç boyut Turkey

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Turkey has national rules on maximum vehicle dimensions and weights. EU Directive 96/53/EC does not apply directly. International transport vehicles must comply with the lower of: their home country limits and Turkey national limits. Check route-specific restrictions for heavy vehicles.

---

## Maximum Dimensions

| Parameter | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Length (articulated) | 16.50 m | Verify current national limit |
| Length (road train) | 18.75 m | Verify current national limit |
| Width | 2.55 m | 2.60 m for refrigerated |
| Height | 4.00 m | Verify current national limit |

## Special Permits

Vehicles exceeding national standard limits require a special transport permit. Apply to the national road authority.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (TRY) | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Exceeding permitted weight | Fine (proportional to excess) | National road transport law |
| Exceeding permitted dimensions | Fine + possible prohibition | National road transport law |
| Operating without special permit | Fine + prohibition | National road transport law |
