## Overview

Turkey is an AETR signatory and applies the **AETR Agreement** for driving and rest time on international road transport journeys. Turkey is not an EU/EEA member so EU Regulation 561/2006 does not apply directly. AETR rules are substantively identical to EU 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **UAB** (Ulaştırma ve Altyapı Bakanlığı — Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure) and the national traffic police.

---

## Core Rules (AETR — identical to EU 561/2006)

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Daily driving (normal) | 9 hours |
| Daily driving (extended, max 2×/week) | 10 hours |
| Weekly driving | 56 hours |
| Two-week driving | 90 hours |
| Break after 4.5h driving | 45 min (or 15+30 split) |
| Regular daily rest | 11 consecutive hours |
| Weekly rest (normal) | 45 consecutive hours |
| Weekly rest (reduced) | 24 consecutive hours |

See `references/aetr/driving-rest-time.md` for full AETR rule text.

---

## Key AETR Rule: No Advance Payment at Roadside

**AETR Art. 22ter:** Advance cash payment (deposit) at roadside is **PROHIBITED** for AETR violations. This is a critical difference from EU 561/2006 enforcement in many EU member states.

---

## Tachograph

Turkey follows **AETR Annex 1B** tachograph requirements. Digital tachographs are required for vehicles subject to AETR. EU-issued driver cards are valid for AETR operations in Turkey.
