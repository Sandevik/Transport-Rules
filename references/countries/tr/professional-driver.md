# Turkey — Professional Driver Competence (CPC)

**Standalone:** national professional driver competence framework

**Sources:**
- [Ulaştırma Bakanlığı / MYK — SRC belgesi (Mesleki Yeterlilik Belgesi)](https://www.ubak.gov.tr/)

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

**Note:** Turkey has its own SRC (Mesleki Yeterlilik Belgesi) system which fulfils a similar role to CPC but is nationally legislated, not under EU 2003/59/EC.

---

## Overview

National professional driver competence requirements apply in Turkey.  Training is provided by MYK sertifika merkezleri. Ulaştırma Bakanlığı / MYK oversees certification.

## CPC Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial CPC** | 280 hours training OR accelerated course (theory + practical exam) | National legislation |
| **Periodic CPC** | 35 hours every 5 years (7h/year or block) | National legislation |
| **DQC card validity** | 5 years | National legislation |
| **Licence code** | Code 95 printed on driving licence | National equivalent |
| **Minimum age (C/CE)** | 18 with CPC; 21 without | National legislation |

## Training Providers

- **Approved by:** Ulaştırma Bakanlığı / MYK
- **Providers:** MYK sertifika merkezleri
- **Topics include:** Advanced driving and fuel efficiency, cargo securing, emergency procedures, regulations and compliance, road safety, health and safety

## Exemptions

Drivers of the following are exempt from CPC:
- Vehicles with max speed ≤45 km/h
- Armed forces, civil protection, fire service, police vehicles
- Vehicles undergoing road test for repair/maintenance
- Non-commercial (private) carriage of goods or passengers

| Violation | Penalty (TRY) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Driving without valid CPC | Fine — see penalties file | National enforcement law |
| Expired DQC | Fine + prohibition to drive commercially | National enforcement law |
