# Italy — HGV Driving Bans (Divieti di Circolazione)

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/driving-bans.md — No EU baseline; national law Codice della Strada]

**Sources:**
- https://www.mit.gov.it — Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti: MIT transport ministry
- https://www.aiscat.it/en/ — AISCAT: Italian motorway concessioners
- https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/1086 — Polizia Stradale: HGV restrictions

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Italy imposes year-round Saturday afternoon and Sunday driving restrictions for heavy goods vehicles, with significantly extended summer bans covering Friday evenings through Sunday evenings in July and August. Italy also has extensive night speed restrictions and regional restrictions. Enforcement is by the **Polizia Stradale** (State Traffic Police) and the **Carabinieri** on secondary roads.

---

## Ban Schedule

### Year-Round Restrictions

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| Saturdays (year-round) | **08:00–16:00** |
| Sundays (year-round) | **07:00–22:00** |
| National public holidays | **07:00–22:00** |

### Extended Summer Restrictions (July–August)

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW

| Period | Hours |
|---|---|
| Saturdays in July and August | **07:00–22:00** (extended from 08:00–16:00) |
| Sundays in July and August | **07:00–22:00** (same) |
| Some Fridays in July/August peak weeks | **16:00–22:00** (varies by year — MIT issues annual calendar) |

MIT (Italian transport ministry) publishes the **annual driving ban calendar** (calendario dei divieti di circolazione) which specifies exact dates including extended bans around Ferragosto (15 August).

---

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW | **YES** |
| Articulated trucks (any weight >7.5t) | **YES** |
| Goods vehicles ≤ 7.5t | NO |
| Refrigerated vehicles carrying perishables | Partially exempt (see exemptions) |

---

## Exemptions

| Exemption | Notes |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh food, chilled/frozen goods, live animals, flowers |
| Press/newspapers | Daily press requiring early morning delivery |
| Fuel and energy | Petrol, diesel, LPG, agricultural fuel |
| ADR dangerous goods | Specific classes — check annual MIT list |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal road legs |
| Agricultural vehicles | Harvest transport (seasonal) |
| Emergency/breakdown | Recovery vehicles, emergency services |
| Military | Italian armed forces, NATO forces in Italy |
| Vehicles without load | Controversial — empty vehicles may not always qualify |

---

## ZTL (Zona a Traffico Limitato) — Urban Areas

Separate from national driving bans, Italian cities operate **ZTL** (limited traffic zones):
- **Rome:** Central historic area ZTL with camera enforcement; trucks prohibited at various times
- **Milan:** Area C congestion charge zone + heavy vehicle restrictions
- **Florence, Bologna, Naples:** ZTL with varying HGV access rules

ZTL rules are city-specific and change frequently. Check local municipality before entry.

---

## Night Speed Limits on Motorways

Italian motorways impose **reduced speed limits for HGVs at night** (22:00–06:00):
- Maximum speed: **80 km/h** for trucks > 3.5t in adverse weather
- Standard daytime limit: 80 km/h for trucks > 3.5t (not 90 km/h)
- Tuscany/Sicily motorways may have additional restrictions

---

## Enforcement and Fines

Enforcement by **Polizia Stradale** on motorways and **Carabinieri/Polizia Municipale** on secondary roads.

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|---|---|
| Driving during ban | **€84–€335** (base fine) |
| Repeat offence within 2 years | Double fine |
| Foreign operator with no immediate payment ability | Vehicle may be immobilised as security |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday ban violation | €84–€335 | Art. 6 Codice della Strada; DM MIT annual calendar |
| Sunday/holiday ban violation | €84–€335 | Art. 6 CdS |
| ZTL violation | €80–€300 + possible tow | Local authority ordinance |
