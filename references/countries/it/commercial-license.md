## Overview

Italy issues Community Licences through the MIT (Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti) / Motorizzazione Civile. Italian carriers must be inscribed in the **Albo Nazionale degli Autotrasportatori** (National Register of Road Hauliers) and meet the standard EU four requirements. The CPC examination for transport managers is administered by MIMIT-approved centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Italian specifics |
|-------------|----------|-------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Italian place of business | CCIAA (Chamber of Commerce) registration; inscription in Albo |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Checked via Albo and criminal records |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or guarantee |
| Professional competence | Esame di idoneità professionale | Via approved examination centres; conducted by MIT |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**MIT / Motorizzazione Civile**: issues Community Licences
**Albo Nazionale degli Autotrasportatori**: mandatory national register; all Italian carriers must be inscribed

Website: https://www.alboautotrasportatori.gov.it/

---

## Community Licence

Issued by MIT / Motorizzazione Civile; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Italy within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- Posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)
- CCNL Transport minimum wages apply to cabotage (see posting-workers.md)

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | Up to €15,000 | D.Lgs. 285/1992 CdS |
