## Overview

Italy implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the D.Lgs. 136/2016 (implementing the 2018 Directive revision). Foreign carriers must notify via the Ministry of Labour's online system before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. Enforcement is by INL (Ispettorato Nazionale del Lavoro) and Guardia di Finanza for wage/tax compliance.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Italy | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in IT + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Italy only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification

Notification submitted via Ministry of Labour portal (Cliclavoro / servizio online). Must be submitted **before** work commences in Italy.

---

## Minimum Pay

Italy's minimum pay for transport workers is set by:
- **CCNL Trasporto Merci e Logistica** (national collective agreement for goods transport and logistics)
- Negotiated between Conftrasporto and transport unions (Filt-CGIL, Fit-CISL, Uiltrasporti)
- Rates vary by category; updated periodically
- Italy has no statutory national minimum wage law (as of 2026); CCNL is the reference

Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the applicable CCNL rate.

---

## Driver Documentation

- Notification proof from Italian Ministry of Labour system
- Employment contract showing CCNL-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

| Authority | Full name | Website |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Italy may demand security deposits (cauzione) from foreign carriers.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to notify posting | €150–€1,500 per worker | D.Lgs. 136/2016 |
| Failure to pay CCNL minimum wages | Up to €5,000 per worker | D.Lgs. 136/2016 |
| Failure to maintain documentation | €150–€1,500 | D.Lgs. 136/2016 |
