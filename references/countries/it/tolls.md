# Italy — Road Tolls (Autostrade Pedaggi)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.autostrade.it/en/ — Autostrade per l'Italia: main motorway operator
- https://www.telepass.com/ — Telepass: Italian electronic toll payment

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Italy has an extensive **motorway toll (pedaggi)** network. Most Italian motorways (autostrade) are concession-operated, with Autostrade per l'Italia being the largest operator. Tolls are charged at traditional toll plazas and increasingly via free-flow gantries. Italy uses the **Telepass** electronic payment system, widely accepted and compatible with EETS.

---

## Vehicles and Rates

HGV rates are categorized by axle count and height:

| Class | Vehicle type | Rate vs car |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Class 3 | 2-axle truck >1.30m height | ~2.5× |
| Class 4 | 3-axle truck | ~3.5× |
| Class 5 | 4+ axle combination | ~4.5× |

Average HGV rates: approximately **€0.10–€0.25 per km** depending on route and operator.

---

## Payment Methods

- **Telepass transponder** — recommended for regular operators; automatic debit at gantries; accepted on all Italian motorways and compatible with EETS
- **Viacard** — prepaid card for occasional use
- **Credit/debit card** at staffed or automated booths
- **Cash** at staffed booths (being reduced)

---

## Registration for Foreign Operators

Foreign operators should obtain a **Telepass Europe** account or use an EETS-compatible transponder. Telepass Europe allows driving on Italian motorways and other European toll roads with one account.

Website: https://www.telepass.com/en/
