## Overview

Italy applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations. Enforcement is primarily by the **Polizia Stradale** (highway police) and **Carabinieri** at roadside, with the **MIT** (Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti) overseeing compliance at company level. Italy requires advance security payments (cauzione) from foreign operators for serious violations.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Italy fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.
