## Overview

Italy implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Italy is among the countries where the combined transport driving ban exemption is **most operationally critical** due to Italy's extensive HGV weekend and holiday driving bans. Italian Alpine crossings (Brenner, Mont Blanc/Fréjus, Ventimiglia) make intermodal rail a key alternative to road. The port of Trieste and container ports at Genoa, La Spezia, and Naples are major intermodal gateways.

---

## CRITICAL: Italian HGV Driving Bans

Italy has some of the most extensive HGV driving bans in Europe:

| Period | Ban applies to |
|--------|---------------|
| **Saturdays 08:00–22:00** | Vehicles >7.5t, year-round |
| **Sundays all day** | Vehicles >7.5t, year-round |
| Public holidays all day | Vehicles >7.5t |
| August extended bans | Additional Saturday restrictions |

**Combined transport exemption is ESSENTIAL for Italy:** Road legs of qualifying combined transport journeys are exempt from these bans under Italian implementing law. The driver must carry the terminal booking confirmation. Operations running through Italy on weekends must either travel by combined transport or stop for the ban period.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Documentation Required

- Terminal booking/confirmation document (conferma di prenotazione)
- CMR waybill for road legs
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

Without complete documentation, the driving ban exemption is not valid — Italian Polizia Stradale will not accept verbal claims.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC. Italy applies the directive including the 44t allowance and driving ban exemption.
