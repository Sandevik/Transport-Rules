## Overview

Italy applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the Ministero della Salute (Ministry of Health) — Direzione Generale della Sanità Animale e dei Farmaci Veterinari (DGSAF). Enforcement is conducted by veterinary inspectors (Servizi Veterinari delle ASL) at regional level. Italy is a significant importer of live cattle, pigs, and lambs from Eastern Europe.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | ASL Veterinary Service (Servizi Veterinari ASL) |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | ASL Veterinary Service |

---

## Key BIPs

- **Trieste port**: BIP for live animals from third countries (Balkans/Eastern routes)
- **Genoa port**: BIP for sea arrivals
- Land border BIPs at Brenner/Tarvisio for third-country movements via Austria

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Certificato sanitario veterinario
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.
