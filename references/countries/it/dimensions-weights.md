# Italy — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Italy applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Codice della Strada (CdS) and related regulations. Italy has no special large vehicle programme. Italy's Alpine geography means many mountain routes have lower axle weight restrictions, and some road bridges and tunnels impose further limits. The autostrada (motorway) network is generally compliant with EU standard limits.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Alpine Route Restrictions

Mountain passes and tunnels may impose additional restrictions:
- **Mont Blanc Tunnel**: strict weight and hazardous goods limits
- **Fréjus Tunnel**: specific vehicle class limits
- **Brenner motorway**: combined with Austrian restrictions north of the border
- Local bridges and underpasses may have lower height/weight limits — check MIT/ANAS route guidance

---

## Exceptional Transport (Trasporti Eccezionali)

Permits issued by:
- **MIT** (national roads) or **ANAS** (state road authority)
- **Regional authorities** for regional roads
- Application required in advance; police escort for larger loads

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC at national level, but local restrictions on mountain routes and bridges apply.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding GVW (up to 5%) | €422–€1,715 | CdS Art.167 |
| Exceeding GVW (>10%) | €1,715–€8,575 + vehicle immobilisation | CdS Art.167 |
| Oversize without permit | €422–€8,000 | CdS Art.10 |
