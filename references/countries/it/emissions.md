## Overview

Italy applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. Italy has an extensive network of urban restricted traffic zones (ZTL — Zone a Traffico Limitato) and emission-based access restriction areas. Milan's Area B is one of Europe's largest low emission zones for diesel HGVs. Italy's Motorizzazione Civile handles type-approval, while urban authorities operate independent LEZ systems.

---

## National LEZ — Key Cities

**Milan (Area B):**
- Coverage: entire city of Milan (within the outer ring road)
- HGV access: **Euro 5 diesel banned** from entering Area B (in force from 2024; Euro 6 / ZEV required)
- Hours: Monday–Friday 07:30–19:30; Saturday 07:30–18:00
- Enforcement: ANPR cameras at entry points
- Permits available for deliveries with exceptions; check https://www.comune.milano.it/

**Milan (Area C):**
- Inner central Milan (congestion charge zone)
- HGVs subject to additional access restrictions and charges

**Rome (Fascia Verde / ZTL):**
- ZTL covers central Rome; specific restrictions for HGVs by Euro class

**Other cities**: Florence, Bologna, Turin, Naples, Venice all operate local ZTL/LEZ schemes with varying HGV restrictions.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Piano Nazionale di Ripresa e Resilienza (PNRR)**: investments in ZEV logistics and intermodal
- **MISE incentives**: subsidies for ZEV commercial vehicles via industry ministry
- Reduced tolls for ZEV vehicles on motorways (ANAS/autostrade)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. Italy adds extensive city-level ZTL/LEZ systems.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Entering Milan Area B with non-compliant vehicle | €100–€300 | Milan LEZ ordinance |
| Entering Rome ZTL without authorization | €100–€300 | Rome ZTL ordinance |
| Type-approval non-compliance | Up to €6,000 | CdS Art.78 |
