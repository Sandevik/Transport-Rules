## Overview

Portugal implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport legislation. Portugal's intermodal network is centred on the ports of Sines (Europe's fastest-growing container port on the Atlantic) and Lisbon, with rail connections into Spain and on to Europe. Portugal has no general HGV weekend driving bans, so the driving ban exemption is less operationally significant here than in neighbouring Spain or France.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Type/Connections |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| Sines (Terminal de Contentores) | Sines | Atlantic deep-sea container hub; rail to Spain/EU |
| Lisbon (Bobadela, Poceirão) | Lisbon area | Rail freight terminal |
| Porto (Maia, Leixões) | Porto | Container + ro-ro |
| Elvas/Caia | Alentejo (Spanish border) | Rail gauge-change facility (Iberian → UIC gauge) |

**Iberian gauge**: Portugal uses Iberian gauge (1,668mm) on most lines; gauge-changing at Elvas/Caia allows standard UIC connections to France/Europe. This adds complexity but the Port of Sines has become a major European gateway on the Atlantic corridor.

---

## Documentation Required

- Port or rail terminal booking/confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC.
