## Overview

Portugal issues Community Licences through the IMT (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes). Portuguese carriers must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam (Certificado de Aptidão Profissional — CAP) for transport managers is administered by IMT-approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Portuguese specifics |
|-------------|----------|----------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Portuguese place of business | Registration with RNPC (national business registry) |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Checked via transport register and criminal records |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CAP exam | Via IMT-approved centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**IMT** (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes): issues Community Licences and national transport licences.

Website: https://www.imt-ip.pt/

---

## Community Licence

Issued by IMT; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Portugal within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- ACT posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
