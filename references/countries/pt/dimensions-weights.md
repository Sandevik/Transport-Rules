## Overview

Portugal applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Regulamento de Inspecção de Veículos and the Código da Estrada. Portugal has no special large vehicle programme and applies EU standard limits.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Oversize/overweight permits issued by the IMT or regional road authorities (EP — Estradas de Portugal). Advance application required.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding max GVW | €250–€3,740 | Código da Estrada Art.120 |
| Operating oversize without permit | €250–€3,740 | Código da Estrada |
