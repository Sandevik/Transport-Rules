## Overview

Portugal applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by **ANEPC** (Authority for Road Vehicles and Driving), **IMT** (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes), and the GNR (Guarda Nacional Republicana) for roadside checks. Portugal has an extensive motorway network with widespread tolling.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
