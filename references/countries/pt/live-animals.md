## Overview

Portugal applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the DGAV (Direção-Geral de Alimentação e Veterinária — General Directorate for Food and Veterinary Affairs). Portugal is a significant producer and exporter of cattle, pigs, and horses within the EU and to third countries (particularly to North Africa and the Middle East).

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | DGAV regional directorate |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | DGAV regional directorate |

---

## Key BIPs

- **Sines port**: BIP for livestock imports from third countries
- **Lisbon airport**: BIP for air freight live animals
- **Lisbon port**: BIP for certain livestock categories

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Certificado sanitário veterinário
- TRACES document

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.
