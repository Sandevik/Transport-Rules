## Overview

Portugal implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Lei do Destacamento de Trabalhadores (Lei 29/2017 as amended). Foreign carriers must notify via the ACT (Autoridade para as Condições do Trabalho) online portal before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. The ACT is also the enforcement body for labour conditions.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Portugal | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in PT + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Portugal only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification Portal — ACT

- Portal: https://www.act.gov.pt/
- Must notify before work commences in Portugal

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Salário Mínimo Nacional (SMN)**: Portuguese statutory minimum wage — approximately €870/month (2025; updated annually)
- **Sector collective agreement (PERT — Pessoal de Empresas de Transportes Rodoviários)**: higher rates for professional drivers
- Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the applicable sector agreement rate

---

## Driver Documentation

- ACT notification proof
- Employment contract showing Portuguese-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to notify posting | €2,040–€9,690 | Lei 29/2017 |
| Failure to pay SMN/sector wages | €2,040–€9,690 per worker | Código do Trabalho |
