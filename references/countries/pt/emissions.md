## Overview

Portugal applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The IMT (Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes) is the main transport authority, while the APA (Agência Portuguesa do Ambiente) monitors environmental compliance. Portugal is developing LEZ frameworks for Lisbon and Porto, though national implementation is less advanced than other major EU countries.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Portuguese national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

---

## National LEZ Programme

Portugal does not have a nationally mandated LEZ programme as of 2026. Lisbon and Porto are developing Zone de Emissões Reduzidas (ZER) / Zero Emission Zone access controls:
- **Lisbon**: planning ZEV access zones in the historic centre; not yet fully implemented for HGVs
- **Porto**: planning environmental access restrictions for delivery vehicles

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Fundo Ambiental**: environmental fund supporting fleet electrification
- **POSEUR/PT2020 funds**: EU-co-funded incentives for green transport
- Reduced ISV (Imposto sobre Veículos) for ZEV vehicles
