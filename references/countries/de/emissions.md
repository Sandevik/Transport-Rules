## Overview

Germany applies EU emission standards directly as uniform EU law. The Kraftfahrtbundesamt (KBA) handles vehicle type-approval, while the Umweltbundesamt (UBA) monitors fleet emissions. Germany has an extensive network of Umweltzonen (Low Emission Zones) in urban areas that directly affect HGV access.

---

## Euro VII Application

No German national deviations from the EU Euro VII timeline. New type approvals for heavy-duty vehicles from **1 July 2026**; all new registrations from **1 July 2027**.

The CO2 fleet targets (Regulation EU 2024/1610) bind vehicle manufacturers, not transport operators:
- 2025: −15% vs 2019 reference
- 2030: −45%
- 2035: −65%
- 2040: −90%

ZEV mandate: 35% of new heavy trucks must be zero-emission by 2030; 70% by 2035.

---

## National Low Emission Zones (Umweltzonen)

Germany operates the most extensive LEZ network in the EU. Major affected cities:

| City | Zone requirement | HGV impact |
|------|-----------------|------------|
| Berlin | Green badge (grüne Plakette) | Euro 4 diesel minimum to enter zone |
| Munich | Green badge | Euro 4 minimum |
| Hamburg | Green badge | Euro 4 minimum |
| Frankfurt | Green badge | Euro 4 minimum |
| Stuttgart | Blue badge zone (planned) | Euro 6 diesel restrictions in some areas |
| Cologne | Green badge | Euro 4 minimum |

The Umweltplakette (environmental badge) is issued based on Euro emission class:
- **Green (4):** Euro 4, 5, 6 — permitted in all zones
- **Yellow (3):** Euro 3 — limited access
- **Red (2):** Euro 2 — restricted

HGVs entering Umweltzonen without the required badge face fines.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Bundesförderung für Nichtwohngebäude (BEG)** and specific truck programmes: purchase subsidies for battery electric and hydrogen fuel cell trucks available through BAFA (Bundesamt für Wirtschaft und Ausfuhrkontrolle), https://www.bafa.de/
- **De-minimis support** for zero-emission last-mile logistics
- Maut exemption: zero-emission vehicles pay no CO2 component in the LKW-Maut, though they still pay infrastructure and noise components

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. Germany is among the strictest enforcers of type-approval compliance. National Umweltzonen add a layer beyond EU minimum requirements.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Entering Umweltzone without required badge | €80 | 35. BImSchV / StVO |
| Operating vehicle that does not meet type-approval standards | Up to €5,000 | KBA/EG-FGV |
| Failure to display valid Umweltplakette | €80 | StVO |
