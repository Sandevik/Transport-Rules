## Overview

Germany implements EU operator licensing regulation (EC) 1071/2009 and cabotage regulation (EC) 1072/2009 through the Güterkraftverkehrsgesetz (GüKG). BALM (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität) is the national licensing authority. The Community Licence (Gemeinschaftslizenz) is required for all goods transport >3.5t GVW internationally. National operations >3.5t GVW require a domestic Erlaubnis.

## Operator Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Establishment** | Company must be effectively and stably established in Germany | EC 1071/2009 Art. 3 |
| **Good repute** | No serious criminal convictions; clean driving/transport history | EC 1071/2009 Art. 6 |
| **Financial standing** | €9,000 for first vehicle; €5,000 for each additional | EC 1071/2009 Art. 7 |
| **Professional competence** | Transport Manager must hold CPC (Fachkunde) | EC 1071/2009 Art. 8 |
| **Transport Manager** | Named individual; exam via IHK (Industrie- und Handelskammer) | GüKG §3 |

## Community Licence (Gemeinschaftslizenz)

- Required for: international goods transport >3.5t GVW within EU/EEA
- Issued by: BALM
- Validity: 5 years (renewable)
- Certified true copies carried on each vehicle
- Cabotage rules: max 3 operations within 7 days after international delivery (EU 1072/2009)

## Domestic Erlaubnis

- Required for: national goods transport >3.5t GVW
- Issued by: BALM
- Vehicles ≤3.5t GVW: no goods transport licence required

## German-Specific: Berufskraftfahrer (Professional Driver)

- CPC (Berufsqualifikation) required for drivers of N3 vehicles and M3 passenger vehicles
- Initial CPC: 280 hours training OR theory + practical exam
- Periodic CPC: 35 hours every 5 years (Grundqualifikation + Weiterbildung)
- Issued by: Bundesamt für Güterverkehr / TÜV / approved training centres
- Code 95 on driving licence confirms CPC validity

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without Gemeinschaftslizenz | €5,000–€20,000 | GüKG §19 |
| Cabotage violation (> 3 operations/7 days) | €1,000–€5,000 | EU 1072/2009; GüKG |
| No CPC (driver) | €500–€1,500 | FPersV |
| No Transport Manager CPC (operator) | €500–€5,000 | GüKG |
