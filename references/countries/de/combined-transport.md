## Overview

Germany is Europe's largest combined transport market, with extensive intermodal terminal infrastructure. Germany has significant HGV driving bans (Saturday 08:00–22:00 and Sunday all day for >7.5t), making the driving ban exemption under Directive 92/106/EEC particularly valuable for operators. Germany implemented the directive through national law.

---

## HGV Driving Bans (Why Combined Transport Exemption Matters)

Germany operates among the most restrictive HGV driving bans in the EU:

| Period | Ban applies to |
|--------|---------------|
| Saturdays 07:00–20:00 | Vehicles >7.5t laden (July–August, extended to 07:00–20:00) |
| Sundays all day | Vehicles >7.5t |
| Public holidays all day | Vehicles >7.5t |

**Combined transport exemption:** Road legs of qualifying combined transport journeys are **exempt** from these bans under §30 StVO + Kombinierter-Verkehr-Verordnung. The driver must carry documentary proof of the combined transport booking.

---

## Weight Allowance

Vehicles operating as part of a combined transport journey carrying a container or swap-body may operate at **44 tonnes** (vs. standard 40t EU limit) provided the combination has 5 or more axles.

## Documentation Required

For the driving ban exemption and 44t allowance, the driver must carry:
1. **Rail or waterway booking confirmation** (Buchungsbestätigung) from the rail terminal or inland port showing route, dates, and load details
2. **CMR waybill** for the road leg
3. **Vehicle registration** confirming axle count ≥5 (for 44t)

BALM inspectors verify these documents during roadside checks.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

Germany is generally stricter than the EU minimum:
- Detailed documentation requirements enforced at roadside
- Terminal confirmation must explicitly state the non-road leg details

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Driving in banned period without combined transport proof | €600–€1,500 + vehicle immobilisation | §30 StVO |
| Exceeding 40t without combined transport proof | €255–€765 | §34 StVZO |
| Falsifying combined transport documentation | Up to €10,000 | Criminal/administrative law |
