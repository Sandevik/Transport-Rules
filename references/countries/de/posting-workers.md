## Overview

Germany implements Directive 2020/1057/EU for road transport posting. The declaration portal is FMS (Fahrermeldestellen-Portal) — covered in posting-declaration.md. This file focuses on the substantive rights and obligations: minimum pay, equal treatment, and enforcement. The Zoll (German Customs) is the primary enforcement body for minimum wage compliance under the Mindestlohngesetz (MiLoG) and AEntG (Arbeitnehmer-Entsendegesetz).

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Declaration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Germany | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in DE + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Germany only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in Germany) | NO | NO |

---

## Minimum Pay Obligations

Germany has two parallel minimum pay frameworks for posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade:

**1. Statutory Minimum Wage (Mindestlohn)**
- Rate: €12.82/hour (2025 rate; updated annually)
- Legal basis: Mindestlohngesetz (MiLoG)
- Applies to all workers, including posted foreign drivers

**2. Sector Minimum Wage (Mindestentgelt Güterverkehr)**
- Set by Tarifvertrag Mindestlohn Speditions-, Transport- und Logistikgewerbe
- Often higher than statutory minimum wage; check current Bundesministerium für Arbeit (BMAS) schedule
- Legal basis: Arbeitnehmer-Entsendegesetz (AEntG)

The higher of the two rates always applies.

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry and present on request:
- Proof of FMS declaration (see posting-declaration.md)
- CMR waybill
- Employment contract or pay slips demonstrating at least German minimum wage
- Evidence that social security contributions are paid in home country (typically A1 certificate)

---

## Equal Treatment Obligations

Beyond minimum pay, posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade in Germany are entitled to:
- Reimbursement of travel, accommodation, and meals at German employer rates (if away from home base)
- Working conditions under German occupational health and safety law
- German holiday entitlements (pro-rated) if posting exceeds certain durations

After 12 months of posting (extendable to 18 months with justified notification), the full German labour law applies.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Zoll | German Customs — Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit (FKS) | https://www.zoll.de/ |
| BALM | Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität | https://www.balm.bund.de/EN/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Zoll inspectors can demand security deposits for suspected MiLoG violations.

Zoll has extensive powers including roadside payroll document checks, employer audits, and inter-authority data sharing via SOLVIT and EU posting data exchange systems.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wage | Up to €500,000 | MiLoG §21 |
| Failure to maintain pay documentation | Up to €30,000 | MiLoG §17 |
| Failure to register posting (FMS) | Up to €30,000 | AEntG §23 |
| Obstructing Zoll inspection | Up to €30,000 | MiLoG §21 |
