## Overview

Germany applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations. The enforcement authority is BALM (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität), formerly BAG. Germany is known for rigorous roadside enforcement with a network of dedicated control points (Kontrollstellen) across the federal road network. Advance security deposits (Sicherheitsleistung) may be collected at roadside from foreign carriers.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core driving/rest time rules. Germany fully applies the EU standard including all Mobility Package 2022 updates (cab prohibition for regular weekly rest, return home every 4 weeks).

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| BALM | Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität (formerly BAG) | https://www.balm.bund.de/EN/ |
| Polizei | Federal and state police — roadside checks | — |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Sicherheitsleistung (security deposit) can be demanded from foreign carriers for serious violations. Amount reflects the expected fine. Vehicle may be immobilised until paid.

**Roadside check approach:** Germany operates dedicated BALM control service with permanent and mobile checkpoints on federal motorways and trunk roads. Risk-based targeting using ERRU data. Germany is one of the most active enforcement states in the EU.

## Penalties

*Specific EUR amounts are published in the BALM Bußgeldkatalog (Catalogue of Fines).*

| Violation | Penalty range | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding daily driving time | €60–€1,500 depending on severity | EU 561/2006 + national implementing rules |
| Insufficient daily rest | €60–€1,500 | EU 561/2006 |
| Missing or invalid tachograph | €100–€3,000 | EU 165/2014 |
| Operating without Community licence | Up to €5,000 | EU 1072/2009 |
| Cabotage violation | Up to €3,000 | EU 1072/2009 |

Full current Bußgeldkatalog: https://www.balm.bund.de/EN/Service/QuickService/CatalogueOfFines/catalogueoffines_node.html
