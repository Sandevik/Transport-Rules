## Overview

Germany has one of Europe's most structured exceptional transport permit systems. Since 2014, the **VEMAGS** (Verwaltungssystem für Erlaubnisse und Genehmigungen im Schwertransport) online portal coordinates permit applications across all 16 federal states. Enforcement is primarily by **BALM** (the Federal Office for Logistics and Mobility) and the state police.

The legal basis is **§29 Abs. 3 StVO**: road transport that by reason of its nature or dimensions can only be conducted with damage or danger to public roads or traffic requires a permit (Erlaubnis) or exemption (Ausnahmegenehmigung) from the competent authority.

---

## Thresholds Triggering a Permit

A permit under §29 Abs. 3 StVO / §46 StVO is required when the vehicle and/or load exceeds:

| Parameter | Standard limit | Permit threshold |
|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Gross vehicle weight | 40 t (road train) | > 40 t |
| Single drive axle load | 11.5 t | > 11.5 t |
| Single non-drive axle load | 10 t | > 10 t |
| Width | 2.55 m | > 2.55 m |
| Height | 4.00 m | > 4.00 m |
| Length (articulated vehicle) | 16.50 m | > 16.50 m |
| Length (road train) | 18.75 m | > 18.75 m |

**Note:** Germany's standard maximum GVW is 40 t (not 44 t). The 44 t exception applies only to combined transport (rail/ferry leg). Any load above 40 t requires a permit even if within 44 t combined-transport threshold.

## Permit Categories

Germany uses a multi-category permit system based on load dimensions and weight:

### Category A — Erlaubnis (Permit) for loads up to defined thresholds
- Width up to **5.0 m**
- Total weight up to **approximately 80–100 t** (route-specific)
- Height up to **approximately 5.0 m** (route-specific)
- Issued by the issuing authority (Erlaubnisbehörde) of the state where transport originates
- For multi-state transports: **VEMAGS coordinates between all states**

### Category B — Ausnahmegenehmigung (Exemption) for extreme dimensions
- Width > 5.0 m, or total weight > 100 t, or other extreme parameters
- Stricter conditions, longer processing, mandatory police escort in most cases
- May require individual bridge load assessments from Landesbetrieb Straßenwesen

### Dauerausnahmegenehmigung (Blanket/Standing Permit)
- Valid for 12 months for recurring transports with uniform parameters
- Available for loads within moderate thresholds (typically ≤ 3.0 m wide, ≤ 40 t, ≤ 20 m)
- Operator must notify the authority before each individual transport

## VEMAGS System

**VEMAGS** is Germany's national online platform for exceptional transport permits:

- **URL:** https://www.vemags.de/
- **Access:** Registered hauliers and transport companies; registration required
- **Function:** Single application submitted via VEMAGS is automatically routed to all relevant state authorities (Länder) whose roads are on the planned route
- **Route input:** Route must be entered digitally; VEMAGS checks bridge tables and route suitability
- **Processing time:** Typically **5–20 working days** for standard applications; complex loads may take 4–8 weeks
- **Fee calculation:** Automated based on route length and load parameters; fees collected by each state authority separately
- **Output:** Digital permit with route, conditions, time windows

VEMAGS replaced paper-based multi-state applications. For foreign operators transporting through Germany, the same VEMAGS system applies (German-language interface; professional transport organisers typically handle this).

## Escort and Pilot Vehicle Requirements

### Pilot vehicles (Begleitfahrzeuge)

| Width | Escort requirement |
|-------|--------------------|
| 2.55–3.0 m | No escort required (permit only) |
| 3.0–4.0 m | 1 rear escort vehicle |
| 4.0–5.0 m | 1 front + 1 rear escort vehicle |
| > 5.0 m | Police escort + escort vehicles |

### Length-based escort

| Length | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Up to 25 m | No escort (permit only) |
| 25–30 m | Rear escort |
| > 30 m | Front and rear escorts |
| > 60 m convoy length | Police escort required |

### Escort vehicle requirements
- Amber flashing beacon bar (Warnbalken)
- "ÜBERBREITE" / "SCHWERTRANSPORT" sign boards front and rear
- Radio communication with load vehicle
- Pilot vehicle drivers must hold qualification: Fahrzeugführerschein + transport escort certificate (Begleitpersonenschulung per VDI/VDE guidelines)

## Police Escort (Polizeibegleitung)

Police escort is required for:
- Width > 5.0 m
- Total convoy length > 60 m (road train + any additional elements)
- Weight > approximately 150 t (route-dependent — authority's discretion)
- Loads passing through tunnels (case-by-case)
- Complex urban routes with traffic management needs

**Request process:** Police escort must be requested through the Polizei of the relevant federal states separately from the VEMAGS permit application. Lead time: typically **2–4 weeks**. Police set the movement schedule and speed.

**Cost:** Police escort fees are charged to the haulier (typically €70–€120/hour per police vehicle).

## Time Restrictions

| Day/time | Restriction |
|----------|-------------|
| Monday–Friday, daytime | Permitted (most loads) |
| Saturday 00:00–midnight | Wide loads (> 3.0 m): restricted on major roads |
| Saturday 07:00–20:00 | Generally no exceptional transport on motorways for widths > 3.0 m |
| Sunday all day | Exceptional transport banned for loads > standard width/weight |
| Public holidays | Same as Sunday — banned |
| Night (22:00–06:00) | Wide loads (> 3.5 m) restricted; permit may specify daytime only |
| Autobahn at peak times | Additional time restrictions may be imposed by permit |

**Note:** The general Sunday driving ban for HGVs (§ 30 StVO, > 7.5 t) also applies to exceptional loads. Permits can grant exemptions for specific time windows.

## Fees

VEMAGS fees are set per state (Länder) and vary by route length and load parameters. Indicative ranges:

| Route length | Approximate fee range |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Short route (< 50 km), single state | €50–€150 |
| Multi-state route | €100–€500 (cumulative per state) |
| Complex annual blanket permit | €300–€1,000/year |

Fees are payable to the relevant state road authority. Bridge load calculation assessments are charged separately (€200–€1,000+ depending on bridge count).

---

## Speed Limits

Most VEMAGS permits specify speed conditions:

| Load type | Typical permitted speed |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Width 2.55–3.5 m, normal weight | 80 km/h (or posted limit if lower) |
| Width 3.5–4.5 m | 60 km/h |
| Width > 4.5 m | 40–50 km/h |
| Very heavy loads (> 80 t) | 40–60 km/h (route-specific) |
| Police escort | Police-set, typically 40–60 km/h |

## BALM Enforcement

**BALM** (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität) is the federal authority responsible for:
- Roadside weight/dimension checks (mobile weighing stations)
- Verification that permits are being complied with
- Coordination with state police for roadside stops

At roadside checks, drivers must carry:
1. Signed permit (Erlaubnis/Ausnahmegenehmigung) — must match the actual load
2. Route map as specified in the permit
3. Vehicle registration documents
4. Escort vehicle coordination documentation

BALM can immobilise a vehicle at the roadside if dimensions exceed permit parameters or if no valid permit is carried.

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Additional consequences |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Operating without a permit (§29 Abs. 3 StVO) | €500–€1,500 | Immobilisation of vehicle |
| Exceeding width limit without permit | €255–€1,530 | Immobilisation |
| Exceeding weight limit by 5–10% | €135–€395 | — |
| Exceeding weight limit by > 20% | €855 | + possible immobilisation |
| Violating permit conditions (wrong route, wrong time) | €200–€1,000 | Permit revocation risk |
| Causing road or bridge damage | Civil liability (unlimited) | Criminal prosecution possible |
| Repeat violations | — | Operator licence fitness assessment (EU Reg. 1071/2009) |

## Practical Notes for Foreign Carriers

- VEMAGS operates in German. Professional transport organisations (Spediteure) or specialist permit agents are typically used by non-German carriers
- Bilateral exceptional load permits between Germany and some neighbouring states exist (e.g., DE–AT corridor routes), but route-specific permits are still needed
- The **Nordic EMS combination** (SE 34.5 m / 74 t) cannot transit Germany — standard 18.75 m / 40 t limits apply from the German border
- Carriers from Sweden or Finland operating long combinations must arrange re-loading or use standard EU-compliant vehicles at the border
