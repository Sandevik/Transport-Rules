## Overview

Germany enforces transport regulations through BALM (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität) for commercial transport violations, and Polizei (federal and state) for roadside enforcement. Fines (Bußgelder) are governed by the Fahrpersonalgesetz (FPersG) and the associated Bußgeldkatalog. Germany distinguishes between Verwarngeld (warning fine, paid at roadside) and Bußgeld (formal fine, higher amounts requiring formal proceedings). Cash roadside payment is possible for minor violations but not mandatory for serious ones.
