# Germany — Posting of Workers Declaration (FMS)

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/posting-workers.md — Directive 2020/1057/EU]

**Sources:**
- https://www.fms-portal.de/ — FMS: Fahrermeldestellen-Portal (German posting declaration portal)

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Germany implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the **FMS (Fahrermeldestellen) portal**. Foreign operators sending drivers to perform cabotage or cross-trade transport in Germany must submit a posting declaration (Entsendeerklärung) via this portal. Bilateral transport (picking up/delivering directly to/from home country) is NOT posting under the Directive.

---

## When Posting Declaration Is Required

| Operation type | Posting required? |
|---------------|------------------|
| Cabotage in Germany | **YES** — from first operation |
| Cross-trade (load from DE, deliver to third country or vice versa) | **YES** |
| Bilateral transport (load in home country → Germany or vice versa) | **NO** |
| Transit through Germany (no loading/unloading) | **NO** |

---

## How to Submit

1. Register on **FMS Portal**: https://www.fms-portal.de/
2. Submit Entsendeerklärung before or at start of each posting period
3. Declaration covers: company details, driver details, vehicle registration, type of transport, start/end dates

---

## Driver Documentation During Posting

The driver must carry (and present at roadside on request):
- Copy of the posting declaration (or electronic access)
- CMR waybill (consignment note)
- Evidence of applicable minimum pay/wages
- Working time records covering the last 28+ days

---

## Minimum Pay Requirements

Germany applies the **Mindestlohn** (statutory minimum wage) and sector-specific minimum wages to posted drivers during cabotage/cross-trade operations. Current rates: https://www.bmas.de/EN/

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| BALM | Checks posting declarations and driver documentation at roadside |
| Zoll (Customs) | Enforces Mindestlohn and posting obligations (Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit) |

**Penalty for missing declaration or documentation:** Fine per driver, per journey. Can be up to €30,000 for systemic violations.
