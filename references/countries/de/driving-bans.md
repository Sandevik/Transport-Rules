## Overview

Germany has one of Europe's most strictly enforced HGV driving ban systems under **§30 StVO** (Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung). Trucks with a GVW over 7.5 tonnes and trucks with trailers (regardless of weight) are prohibited on Sundays, public holidays, and Saturday afternoons in July and August. The ban applies to all roads — motorways and rural roads alike. Enforcement is by **BALM** (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität) and the state police (Landespolizei).

---

## Ban Schedule

| Period | Ban applies | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Sundays (all year) | All roads | **00:00–22:00** |
| Public holidays (all year) | All roads | **00:00–22:00** |
| Saturdays (Nov–Jun, excl. Jul/Aug peak) | All roads | **00:00–22:00** |
| Saturdays in July and August | All roads | **07:00–20:00** (shorter ban — peak holiday relaxation) |

The Saturday relaxation in July and August was introduced to ease holiday logistics.

---

## Vehicle Scope

| Vehicle | Applies? |
|---|---|
| Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW | **YES** |
| Trucks with trailers, regardless of combined weight | **YES** |
| Goods vehicles ≤ 7.5t GVW | NO |
| Passenger cars and vans ≤ 3.5t | NO |
| Buses and coaches | Generally exempt from this specific ban |

---

## Exemptions (§30(3) StVO)

The following vehicle categories are **exempt** from the German Sunday driving ban:

| Exemption | Details |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh and chilled food, milk, meat, fish, bakery products with short shelf life |
| Livestock | Live animals |
| Press/newspapers | Time-critical newspaper distribution |
| Fuel and heating oil | Petrol, diesel, heating oil, LPG |
| Maritime/air cargo | Connecting cargo for scheduled sea/air services |
| Agricultural produce | During harvest seasons (grain, sugar beet, potatoes) — limited periods |
| Empty returns | Empty vehicles returning after exempt-load delivery |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal road legs (with documentation) |
| Federal/state vehicles | Official government transport |
| Breakdown/recovery | Vehicles responding to or recovering from breakdowns |
| Military | Bundeswehr vehicles |
| Emergency services | Police, fire, ambulance, THW |
| International transit | Foreign operators transiting Germany (controversial — BALM enforces rigorously) |

**Perishable exemption detail:** Goods must be genuinely perishable — BALM inspectors may ask for temperature records and commodity documentation. Pre-packaged goods with long shelf life do not qualify.

## Public Holidays (National + State)

Germany has **3 national public holidays** on which the ban applies nationwide:
- Tag der Deutschen Einheit (3 October)
- Christmas Day (25 December)
- Boxing Day (26 December)

Plus nationwide religious holidays: New Year's Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Labour Day (1 May), Ascension Thursday, Whit Monday.

**Federal states (Länder) have additional holidays** that trigger the ban in that state only:
- Bavaria (BY): All Saints (1 Nov), Epiphany (6 Jan), Corpus Christi
- Baden-Württemberg (BW): Corpus Christi, All Saints
- NRW: Corpus Christi, All Saints
- etc.

Check the BALM calendar for the current year's complete holiday ban list.

## Enforcement and Fines

Enforcement is primarily by **BALM mobile control units** and by **Landespolizei** (state traffic police).

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|---|---|
| HGV driving during ban without exemption | **€500** (driver) + **€500** (operator, per Bußgeldkatalog) |
| Repeat offence | Up to **€2,000** + vehicle immobilisation |
| Operating with invalid/falsified exemption documentation | **€1,000–€3,000** |
| Foreign operator violations | Same fines; security deposit may be demanded at roadside |

Vehicles found violating the ban may be ordered to park until the ban period ends.

---

## Permit for Non-Exempt Operations

Operators needing to move non-exempt goods on a ban day may apply for an **Ausnahmegenehmigung** (exemption permit) from the competent state authority (Regierungspräsidium or equivalent). Applications must be submitted well in advance. Permits are granted in exceptional circumstances only and require justification.
