## Overview

Germany has a **situational (bedarfsbezogene) winter tyre obligation** — there are **no fixed calendar dates**. Under §2(3a) of the Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung (StVO), drivers must use winter tyres whenever **winter road conditions** are present. There is no requirement to fit winter tyres in advance or by a specific date.

This contrasts with the fixed-date systems in Finland, Norway, and Sweden. The German approach places responsibility on the driver to assess road conditions and ensure appropriate tyres are fitted before driving in winter conditions.

---

## Legal Basis

**§2(3a) StVO (Straßenverkehrs-Ordnung, 2013 amendment):**

> "Bei Kraftfahrzeugen ist die Ausrüstung an die Wetterverhältnisse anzupassen; insbesondere sind M+S-Reifen oder Reifen mit dem Schneeflockenzeichen bei Glatteis, Schneeglätte, Schneematsch, Eisglätte oder Reifglätte zu verwenden."

Translation: "Motor vehicle equipment must be adapted to weather conditions; in particular, M+S tyres or tyres with the snowflake symbol must be used in the event of black ice, compacted snow, slush, icy conditions, or frost."

---

## Triggering Conditions ("Winterfahrverhältnisse")

Winter tyre obligation is triggered by any of:
- **Glatteis** — black ice
- **Schneeglätte** — compacted/packed snow
- **Schneematsch** — slush
- **Eisglätte** — icy road surface
- **Reifglätte** — frost/hoarfrost on road surface

**The obligation is weather-driven, not calendar-driven.** A driver arriving at an icy road section without winter tyres is in violation, even if the weather forecast did not indicate winter conditions.

In practice, German operators and drivers typically fit winter tyres from approximately **October/November through March/April** to ensure compliance.

## Tyre Symbol Requirements

Since the 2017 amendment to §36 StVZO (Straßenverkehrs-Zulassungs-Ordnung):

| Symbol | Status |
|--------|--------|
| **M+S only** (no snowflake) | **Transitional**: M+S-only tyres manufactured before 1 January 2018 were accepted until **30 September 2024**; after that date, M+S-only tyres without the 3PMSF are **no longer accepted** for satisfying §2(3a) StVO |
| **3PMSF (❄ snowflake)** | **Fully compliant** — required for all winter tyres from 1 October 2024 onwards |
| **M+S + 3PMSF** | Fully compliant |

**From 1 October 2024**, M+S-only tyres (without the three-peak mountain snowflake symbol) no longer satisfy the German winter tyre obligation. Only tyres bearing the 3PMSF (or both M+S and 3PMSF) are compliant.

## Minimum Tread Depth

| Vehicle category | Minimum tread depth (legal minimum) | Recommended minimum |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------|
| All vehicles (general legal minimum) | **1.6 mm** | — |
| Practical enforcement standard for winter tyres | **4 mm** | 4–6 mm for winter performance |

Germany has no separate legal minimum tread depth specifically for "winter" use, but traffic law enforcement authorities (BALM, Police) and courts have established that tyres below **4 mm** provide insufficient winter grip and a driver with sub-4mm tyres who causes an accident in winter conditions is likely to be found at fault. ADAC and KBA recommend replacing winter tyres when tread reaches 4 mm.

---

## Application to Heavy Goods Vehicles

§2(3a) StVO applies to **all motor vehicles** including:
- Lorries (LKW) of all categories
- Semi-trailer tractors (Sattelzugmaschinen)
- Buses and coaches

For HGV:
- Winter tyres must be fitted on **all axles** where the situational obligation applies (interpretation of Gesamtfahrzeug — the whole vehicle must be appropriately equipped).
- Drive axles that remain in summer tyres while roads are icy constitute a violation even if steering axles have winter tyres.
- BALM enforces compliance during roadside checks; operators with non-compliant vehicles in winter conditions face prohibition from continuing.

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Bundespolizei / Landespolizei** | General roadside checks; winter tyre compliance |
| **BALM (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität)** | HGV-specific roadside checks; tyre and vehicle condition |

BALM operates at fixed check stations (Kontrollstellen) on motorways and major routes. Winter tyre checks are common during cold weather periods. Foreign vehicles (non-German registered) are equally subject to §2(3a) StVO.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Points (Fahreignungsregister) |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Driving with non-compliant tyres when winter conditions present | **€60** | 1 point |
| As above, causing obstruction of other traffic | **€80** | 1 point |
| As above, causing danger to other road users | **€100** | 1 point |
| As above, causing accident | **€120** | 1 point |
| Operator (company) allowing vehicle to be used without compliant tyres | **€135** | — |

Points are recorded in the Fahreignungsregister (FAER) at the Kraftfahrtbundesamt (KBA) in Flensburg. Accumulation of points can lead to licence suspension.

**Foreign vehicle note:** Foreign-registered HGV drivers face the same fines. BALM can require a security deposit (Sicherheitsleistung) before allowing the journey to continue.
