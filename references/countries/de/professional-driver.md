# Germany — Professional Driver Competence (CPC)

## Overview

EU Directive 2003/59/EC applies directly in Germany. All professional drivers of N3 goods vehicles and M3 passenger vehicles must hold a valid Driver Qualification Card (DQC) with Code 95 on the driving licence.  Training is provided by IHK (Industrie- und Handelskammer) approved centres. BALM / IHK oversees certification.

## CPC Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial CPC** | 280 hours training OR accelerated course (theory + practical exam) | Directive 2003/59/EC Art. 3 |
| **Periodic CPC** | 35 hours every 5 years (7h/year or block) | Directive 2003/59/EC Art. 8 |
| **DQC card validity** | 5 years | 2003/59/EC |
| **Licence code** | Code 95 printed on driving licence | EU harmonised code |
| **Minimum age (C/CE)** | 18 with CPC; 21 without | Directive 2006/126/EC |

## Training Providers

- **Approved by:** BALM / IHK
- **Providers:** IHK (Industrie- und Handelskammer) approved centres
- **Topics include:** Advanced driving and fuel efficiency, cargo securing, emergency procedures, regulations and compliance, road safety, health and safety

## Exemptions

Drivers of the following are exempt from CPC:
- Vehicles with max speed ≤45 km/h
- Armed forces, civil protection, fire service, police vehicles
- Vehicles undergoing road test for repair/maintenance
- Non-commercial (private) carriage of goods or passengers
