# Ukraine — Commercial Road Transport Licensing

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — standalone national rules.

**Sources:**
—

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Ukraine has national commercial road transport licensing requirements. EU Regulation 1071/2009 and EU Community Licences do not apply directly. Foreign operators require bilateral transport permits. Operations are governed by national licensing law and bilateral/multilateral transport agreements.

---

## Licence Requirements for Domestic Operators

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Establishment | Operator must be established in Ukraine |
| Good repute | No relevant criminal convictions |
| Financial standing | Proof of financial capacity |
| Professional competence | Qualified transport manager |

---

## Foreign Operator Requirements

| Operator Origin | Requirement |
|---|---|
| EU/EEA operators | Bilateral transport permit or CEMT/ITF multilateral permit |
| Third-country operators | Bilateral permit or TIR carnets (where applicable) |
