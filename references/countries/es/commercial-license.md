## Overview

Spain issues Community Licences through the DGTT (Dirección General de Transporte Terrestre) under MITMA. Spanish operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence examination (Certificado de Aptitud Profesional / CAP de transportista) is administered by MITMA-approved centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Spanish specifics |
|-------------|----------|-------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Spanish place of business | Registered with RETA or RD (business registry) |
| Good repute | No serious offences | National transport register check |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CAP de transportista exam | Via MITMA-approved examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**DGTT** (Dirección General de Transporte Terrestre):
- Issues Community Licences (Licencia comunitaria)
- Maintains the national transport register (Registro de Empresas y Actividades de Transporte — REAT)

Website: https://www.mitma.gob.es/

---

## Community Licence

Issued by DGTT; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Spain within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- Posting declaration required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | €2,001–€20,000 | LOTT Art.141 |
| Exceeding cabotage operations | €2,001–€20,000 | LOTT |
| Missing driver attestation | €1,001–€6,000 | LOTT |
