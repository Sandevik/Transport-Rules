---

## Overview

Spain applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Real Decreto 2822/1998 (Reglamento General de Vehículos) and subsequent amendments. Spain has no special large vehicle programme and applies EU standard limits. Exceptional transport (transportes especiales) requires advance permits.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport (Transportes Especiales)

Permits for oversize/overweight loads issued by:
- **DGT / Ministerio del Interior**: for national road network
- **Comunidades Autónomas**: for regional roads

Categories:
- **Régimen especial**: vehicles up to 3m wide and 20m long — simplified permit
- **Régimen excepcional**: larger dimensions — individual authorisation

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.
