## Overview

Spain implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Ley sobre desplazamiento de trabajadores. Foreign carriers must notify before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade in Spain. Enforcement is by the ITSS (Inspección de Trabajo y Seguridad Social). Spain's minimum wage (SMI — Salario Mínimo Interprofesional) applies; sector collective agreements in road transport (Acuerdo General de Transporte de Mercancías por Carretera) set higher rates.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Spain | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in ES + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Spain only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification

Notification submitted to the MTES (Ministerio de Trabajo y Economía Social) via the online portal: https://www.mites.gob.es/

---

## Minimum Pay

- **SMI (Salario Mínimo Interprofesional)**: Spanish statutory minimum wage — approximately €1,134/month (2025; updated annually by Royal Decree)
- **Sector collective agreement**: Acuerdo General para las Empresas de Transporte de Mercancías por Carretera sets higher rates for professional drivers
- Posted drivers must receive at least the applicable collective agreement rate for hours worked in Spain

---

## Driver Documentation

- Notification proof
- Employment contract showing Spanish-level pay
- A1 certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay Spanish minimum wages | €626–€6,250 per worker | LISOS Art.7 |
| Failure to notify posting | €626–€6,250 | LISOS |
| Serious repeat violations | €6,251–€187,515 | LISOS Art.8 |
