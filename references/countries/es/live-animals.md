## Overview

Spain applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is MAPA (Ministerio de Agricultura, Pesca y Alimentación) at national level, with enforcement by the Comunidades Autónomas (regional governments) through their veterinary services. Spain is a major exporter of live pigs, cattle, and sheep within the EU.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | Comunidad Autónoma |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | Comunidad Autónoma |

---

## Key BIPs (Border Inspection Posts)

- **Algeciras port**: BIP for live animals from Africa/Mediterranean third countries
- **Barcelona port**: BIP for Mediterranean sea arrivals
- Spain also has BIPs at land borders (Portuguese and French) for certain species

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation in vehicle
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for journeys >8 hours
- Certificado sanitario veterinario (health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without Type 2 authorisation | Up to €30,000 | Ley 8/2003 de sanidad animal |
| Welfare violations | €1,001–€30,000 | Ley 8/2003 |
| Failure to maintain journey log | €601–€3,000 | Ley 8/2003 |
