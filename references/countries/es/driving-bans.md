## Overview

Spain's HGV driving restrictions are primarily applied during **summer holiday periods** and around major public holidays, and are less comprehensive than those of France, Germany, or Italy. The restrictions are coordinated by the **DGT** (Dirección General de Tráfico) and enforced by the **Guardia Civil de Tráfico** on national roads and motorways and the **Mossos d'Esquadra** in Catalonia and **Ertzaintza** in the Basque Country.

---

## Summer and Holiday Restrictions

**Applies to:** Goods vehicles > 7.5t GVW

### Key Restriction Periods

| Period | Hours | Roads | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key summer Saturdays and Sundays (Jul–Aug) | **08:00–21:00** | Main national roads (CN) | Particularly Catalan routes towards France (A-2, AP-7, N-II) |
| Key summer Fridays (outbound afternoon) | **14:00–21:00** | Selected routes | DGT publishes annual forecast |
| Key summer Sundays (inbound) | **08:00–21:00** | Selected routes | |
| Christmas and Easter holiday periods | Various | Key routes | Annual DGT calendar |

**DGT annual plan:** Spain publishes a yearly traffic forecasting and restriction plan. Restrictions are published in advance and available via DGT website and the INFOCAR system.

---

## Border Crossing Restrictions

At key border crossings to France, additional restrictions may apply during peak periods:
- **La Junquera / Le Perthus (A-9/AP-7):** Major France–Spain border crossing; summer Saturday bans in both France (Arrêté préfectoral) and Spain (DGT)
- **Irún / Hendaye:** Biscay border; less restricted but some peak-period measures
- **Somport/Canfranc:** Mountain pass; seasonal restrictions

---

## Catalonia-Specific Restrictions

The **Generalitat de Catalunya** may issue additional HGV restrictions on Catalan roads:
- AP-7 (Barcelona–French border) and A-2 (Madrid–Zaragoza) are key concern routes
- Catalonia transport authority (DARP) issues supplementary restrictions during harvest and construction seasons

## Exemptions

| Exemption | Notes |
|---|---|
| Perishable foodstuffs | Fresh food, chilled/frozen, live animals |
| Press/newspapers | Daily press distribution |
| Fuel and energy | Petrol, diesel, LPG, agricultural fuel |
| ADR dangerous goods | Specific classes |
| Agricultural harvest | Seasonal agricultural transport |
| Combined transport | Rail/sea intermodal legs |
| Emergency services | Police, fire, ambulance, civil defence |
| Military | Spanish armed forces, NATO |

---

## Enforcement and Fines

Enforcement by **Guardia Civil de Tráfico** on national roads, **Mossos d'Esquadra** in Catalonia.

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|---|---|
| Driving during restriction without exemption | **€200–€500** |
| Repeat offence | Up to **€1,000** |
