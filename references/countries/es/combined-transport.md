## Overview

Spain implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Spain has a growing intermodal network centered on the Barcelona/Valencia corridor and connections to France via the Pyrenean mountain crossings. Spain operates some weekend and holiday HGV driving restrictions, making the combined transport driving ban exemption relevant for operations on the Iberian Peninsula.

---

## HGV Driving Restrictions

Spain restricts HGV circulation (>7.5t) on certain dates:
- Public holidays and selected Saturdays/Sundays during peak summer (July/August): restrictions on main roads
- The Guardia Civil de Tráfico coordinates seasonal ban schedules

**Combined transport exemption**: Road legs of qualifying combined transport journeys are exempt from applicable driving restrictions.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Barcelona (Puerto, Morrot, Can Tunis, BEST) | Barcelona | Mediterranean hub, France |
| Madrid (Abroñigal) | Madrid | National distribution |

| Valencia (Puerto de Valencia) | Valencia | Mediterranean, North Africa |
| Algeciras | Strait of Gibraltar | Africa, Mediterranean |
| Zaragoza (PLAZA) | Zaragoza | Cross-Iberian logistics hub |

**Iberian gauge consideration**: Spain uses UIC standard gauge on some lines; gauge-changing operations at French border stations (Irún/Hendaye, Portbou/Cerbère) are relevant for combined transport planning.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail/waterway terminal confirmation
- CMR waybill
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC.
