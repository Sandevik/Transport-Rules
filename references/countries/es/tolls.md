# Spain — Road Tolls (Autopistas de Peaje)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.mitma.gob.es/ — MITMA: Ministry of Transport (motorway concessions)

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Spain has a **mixed toll system**: some motorways (autopistas) have tolls, while many others are free-to-use national highways (autovías). Following the expiry of several concessions in recent years, many previously-tolled motorways have become free. The toll network is operated by multiple private concessionaires. There is no universal national HGV distance charge.

Spain has significantly reduced its toll network as concessions expired:

| AP-7 Tarragona–Valencia | Free | 2021 |
| AP-7 Valencia–Alicante | Free | 2021 |
| AP-1 Burgos–Armiñón | Free | 2018 |
| AP-41 Madrid–Toledo | Free | 2020 |
| AP-36 Ocaña–La Roda | Free | 2020 |
| R-2, R-3, R-4, R-5 (Madrid radials) | Free | 2021 |

**Always verify current toll status before planning a route — the toll map has changed substantially.**

## Payment

- **Via-T / Telepeaje:** Electronic transponder — recommended for regular users; compatible with EETS
- **Credit/debit card** at toll booths
- **Cash** at staffed booths
