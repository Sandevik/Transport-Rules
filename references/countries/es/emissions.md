## Overview

Spain applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The MITMA (Ministerio de Transportes, Movilidad y Agenda Urbana) coordinates emission policy for road transport. Spain's DGT (Dirección General de Tráfico) operates a national environmental vehicle sticker scheme, and from 2023 municipalities with over 50,000 inhabitants have been required to implement ZBE (Zonas de Bajas Emisiones).

---

## DGT Environmental Sticker System

Spain's DGT operates a voluntary-to-mandatory vehicle sticker system for urban access:

| Sticker | Vehicle type | Access |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| **0** (blue/green) | Zero-emission (BEV, FCEV) | Best access; all ZBE |
| **ECO** (blue/green) | Plug-in hybrid, CNG/LPG | Good access |
| **C** | Euro 4+ petrol; Euro 6 diesel | Limited ZBE access |
| **B** | Euro 3 petrol; Euro 4/5 diesel | Restricted access |
| No sticker | Older vehicles | Restricted/banned in ZBE |

For HGVs: **Euro VI** diesel → Class **C** sticker (Euro 6); **Euro V** → Class **B** sticker.

---

## ZBE — Zonas de Bajas Emisiones

From January 2023, Spanish law (Ley de Residuos y Suelos Contaminados) required all municipalities >50,000 inhabitants to implement ZBE (low emission zones):
- Applies to all vehicles including HGVs
- Restricts vehicles without appropriate stickers during peak hours
- Madrid (Madrid Central, Madrid 360), Barcelona (ZBE Rondes, district ZBEs), and other major cities are active ZBE cities

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Plan MOVES** (Mobility and Sustainable Energy Plan): subsidies for electric commercial vehicles via IDAE/Comunidades Autónomas
- Reduced circulation tax (IVTM) for ZEV vehicles under some Comunidades
- IDAE grants for fleet electrification

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. Spain adds the DGT sticker system and ZBE network.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Entering ZBE without valid sticker | €100–€500 | Municipal ZBE ordinance |
| No DGT environmental sticker (in ZBE) | €200 | LTS 2022 |
| Type-approval non-compliance | Up to €3,000 | RDL 6/2015 |
