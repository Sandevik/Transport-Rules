## Overview

Spain applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations. Enforcement is by the **Guardia Civil de Tráfico** and **Policía** for roadside checks, with **MITMA** overseeing compliance at company level. Spain has a notable enforcement presence on its extensive motorway network and at the French border crossings.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Spain fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) |
|-----------|--------------|
| Serious driving time violations | €301–€600 |
| Very serious violations (exceeding limits significantly) | €601–€3,005 |
| Tachograph manipulation | Criminal offence + licence revocation |

Full Spanish penalty schedule: Ley de Ordenación del Transporte Terrestre (LOTT) and Ley de Infracciones y Sanciones en el Orden Social.
