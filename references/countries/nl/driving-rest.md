## Overview

The Netherlands applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without significant national deviations. Enforcement is by the ILT (Human Environment and Transport Inspectorate) and the Dutch police. The Netherlands is notable for strictly enforcing the Mobility Package 2022 cab prohibition (regular weekly rest may not be taken in the vehicle cab).

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. The Netherlands fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

**Key Mobility Package requirements enforced by ILT:**
- Regular weekly rest (≥45h) must NOT be taken in the cab — employer must provide suitable accommodation
- Return home / to operational centre at least every 4 weeks
- 56-day lookback period at roadside inspections

---

## Smart Tachograph

From **July 1, 2026**, Smart Tachograph Type 2 (SMT2) becomes mandatory for vehicles ≥2,500 kg in international operations (aligned with EU 561/2006 scope extension).
