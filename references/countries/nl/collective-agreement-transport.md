## Overview

The Netherlands has a **national sector-wide collective labour agreement** for professional goods transport: the **CAO Beroepsgoederenvervoer over de Weg en de Verhuur van Mobiele Kranen** (Collective Labour Agreement for Professional Road Haulage and Mobile Crane Hire). It is negotiated between the employer organisation **TLN** (Transport en Logistiek Nederland) and the trade unions **FNV Transport & Logistiek** and **CNV Vakmensen**.

The CAO is legally binding for all employers and employees covered by it — the Dutch government can and does declare the CAO *algemeen verbindend verklaard* (AVV — generally binding), meaning it applies to all companies in the sector, not just those whose employers are TLN members.

---

## Scope

Covers all employees in:
- Professional road haulage (beroepsgoederenvervoer) — truck drivers, distribution drivers
- Mobile crane hire (verhuur van mobiele kranen)
- Logistics and distribution activities linked to haulage

Does NOT cover: passenger transport (separate CAO), postal/courier sector (separate CAO), own-account transport (own-account shippers with own fleet — partially exempt).

## Key Provisions

### Working Time (notably stricter than EU baseline)

| Parameter | CAO provision |
|---|---|
| Reference period | **52 weeks** (maximum allowed; extended from EU 4-month default via CAO) |
| On-call time | **Fully counts as working time** in NL — stricter than most EU interpretations |
| Break after 5.5 hours | Required — stricter than EU 6-hour rule |

See also: `references/countries/nl/working-time.md`

### Wage Structure

The CAO sets **minimum scale wages** (schaallonen) by job category. Truck drivers fall into scales based on vehicle type (GVW), route type (local/regional/long-haul), and responsibilities.

*Specific current wage amounts are not included here as they are renegotiated periodically (typically annually or every 2 years). Check current rates via TLN (tln.nl) or FNV (fnv.nl/cao).*

### Toeslagen (Allowances)

| Allowance type | Description |
|---|---|
| Onregelmatigheidstoeslag | Unsocial hours premium for evening, night, weekend work |
| Overwerk | Overtime premium (typically 25–50% above base hourly rate) |
| Verblijfsvergoeding | Overnight stay allowance when away from home |
| Reiskosten | Commuting cost reimbursement — TLN advises employers to increase to **€0.25/km** from 1 July 2026 (source: TLN.nl, verified 2026-06-02) |

*Other specific amounts are set in the current CAO text — verify via TLN (tln.nl) or FNV (fnv.nl/cao).*

### Vacation and Leave

- Minimum **25 vacation days** per year (above statutory 20-day minimum)
- Additional leave for long-serving employees in some CAO versions

### Pension

Dutch mandatory pension scheme: employees in road transport sector participate via the **Bedrijfstakpensioenfonds Transport** (pension fund for the transport sector).

## AVV (Algemeen Verbindend Verklaring)

The Dutch Minister of Social Affairs and Employment can declare the CAO *algemeen verbindend* — meaning it applies to ALL companies in the sector, including those not affiliated with TLN. This is standard practice for the transport CAO, making it effectively a sector minimum standard.

Foreign operators with drivers working in the Netherlands must comply with the CAO minimum wage rates (posting of workers — see `references/countries/nl/posting-workers.md`).

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| ILT (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport) | Working time and transport compliance |
| Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie | Wage and employment law compliance |
| FNV / CNV | Union enforcement via civil law claims |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below CAO minimum wage | Back pay + possible prosecution | Wet minimumloon; CAO (AVV declaration) |
| Not applying CAO to covered employees | Civil liability; union claim | Wet op de collectieve arbeidsovereenkomst |
| Overtime without premium | Back payment owed | CAO |
