## Overview

The Netherlands applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The ILT (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport) enforces compliance. The Netherlands has been at the forefront of LEZ policy in the EU and has legislated zero-emission zones (ZEZ) in 30+ municipalities for delivery vehicles and HGVs from 2025 onward.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Dutch national deviations from the EU timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

---

## National Zero Emission Zones (ZEZ)

The Netherlands has passed legislation requiring municipalities over a certain size to implement Zero Emission Zones (ZEZ) for goods transport by 2025:

| City | ZEZ scope | HGV impact |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Amsterdam | ZEZ for goods transport | Zero-emission required in city core for delivery vehicles and HGVs |
| Rotterdam | ZEZ | Zero-emission required for goods vehicles |
| Utrecht | ZEZ | Phased implementation |
| The Hague | ZEZ | Phased implementation |
| 30+ cities | ZEZ from 2025 | Goods vehicles >3.5t must be ZEV for access |

The national ZEZ framework provides for phased access for Euro VI vehicles during the transition period (transition passes for Euro VI until 2025 deadline in some cities).

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Subsidieregeling Emissieloze bedrijfsauto's (SEBA)**: subsidy for zero-emission commercial vehicles via RVO (https://www.rvo.nl/)
- **MIA/Vamil**: tax benefit scheme for environmentally friendly vehicles, including ZEV trucks
- **Walstroom**: shore power infrastructure grants for ports and logistics

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU emission standards. The Netherlands adds ZEZ requirements beyond EU minimum.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| ILT | Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport | https://www.ilent.nl/ |
| RDW | Dienst Wegverkeer (vehicle registration) | https://www.rdw.nl/ |
| Municipal authorities | ZEZ enforcement (ANPR cameras) | varies |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — ILT can demand security deposits from foreign carriers.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Entering ZEZ with non-compliant vehicle | €400 (first offence; increasing for repeats) | Municipal ZEZ ordinance |
| Type-approval non-compliance | Up to €5,000 | Wegenverkeerswet 1994 |
