## Overview

The Netherlands applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the NVWA (Nederlandse Voedsel- en Warenautoriteit — Netherlands Food and Consumer Product Safety Authority). The Netherlands is a major exporter of livestock (pigs, poultry, cattle) and is a key departure country for live animal transport to Southern Europe and third countries. The NVWA actively inspects animal transports at ports and departure points.

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | NVWA |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | NVWA |

---

## Border Inspection Posts (BIPs)

The Netherlands has major BIPs for live animal imports from third countries:
- **Rotterdam port** — primary BIP for sea arrivals
- **Schiphol Airport** — BIP for airfreight live animals

---

## Netherlands-Specific Context

The Netherlands is a major exporting country for live animals, particularly:
- Pigs and piglets (to Germany, Spain, Italy)
- Day-old chicks and poultry (globally)
- Cattle (to various EU countries and beyond)

NVWA is known for rigorous inspection; non-compliant animal transports may be stopped and animals offloaded for rest and care.

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation
- Driver CoC
- Journey log (>8 hours)
- Health/veterinary certificate
- TRACES movement documentation

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations. The Netherlands fully applies EU 1/2005.
