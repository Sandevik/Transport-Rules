# Netherlands — Posting of Workers

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/posting-workers.md — Directive 2020/1057/EU]

**Sources:**

- https://www.inspectieszik.nl/ — Inspectie SZW (now NLA): Labour market enforcement

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

The Netherlands implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Wet arbeidsvoorwaarden gedetacheerde werknemers in de Europese Unie (WagwEU). Foreign carriers must notify via the **DUOS** (Digital Union Online System) portal before deploying posted drivers in the Netherlands for cabotage or cross-trade operations. Enforcement is by the NLA (Netherlands Labour Authority / Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie).

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Netherlands | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in NL + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Netherlands only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in NL) | NO | NO |

---

## Notification Portal — DUOS

- Portal: https://duos.szw.nl/
- Must notify **before** work commences
- Employer must provide company details, driver details, vehicle, type of transport, period

---

## Minimum Pay

The Netherlands has a statutory minimum wage (Wettelijk Minimumloon — WML):
- Rate: updated biannually (January 1 and July 1); check https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/minimumloon
- Sector collective agreements (CAO Transport en Logistiek) set higher rates; applicable to posted drivers performing cabotage in the Netherlands
- Current CAO rates: check FNV Transport / CNV Vakmensen publications

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry:
- Proof of DUOS notification
- Employment contract showing at least Dutch minimum pay
- CMR waybill
- A1 social security certificate

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| NLA | Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie (Netherlands Labour Authority) | https://www.nlarbeidsinspectie.nl/ |
| ILT | Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport | https://www.ilent.nl/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — NLA inspectors may demand security deposits.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to notify via DUOS | Up to €10,000 | WagwEU §16 |
| Failure to pay Dutch minimum wage | Up to €10,000 per worker | WagwEU |
| Failure to maintain pay records | Up to €10,000 | WagwEU |
