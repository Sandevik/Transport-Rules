# Netherlands — Posting of Workers Declaration (DUOS)

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/posting-workers.md — Directive 2020/1057/EU]

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

The Netherlands implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the **DUOS (Digitaal UitzendOmgeving Systeem)** portal. Foreign operators performing cabotage or cross-trade transport in the Netherlands must submit a posting declaration. Bilateral transport is NOT posting under the Directive.

---

## When Declaration Is Required

| Operation | Required? |
|-----------|----------|
| Cabotage in Netherlands | **YES** |
| Cross-trade through Netherlands | **YES** |
| Bilateral (load NL → home country, or vice versa) | **NO** |
| Transit through Netherlands (no loading/unloading) | **NO** |

---

## How to Submit

1. Go to **DUOS portal**: https://www.duos.nl/
2. Submit notification before the start of the posting period
3. Required data: employer details, driver details, vehicle registration, transport type, start/end dates

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry on request:
- Posting declaration or proof of submission
- CMR waybill
- Pay records (evidence of applicable minimum wage)
- Working time records (last 28+ days)

---

## Minimum Pay

No statutory national minimum wage specific to road transport (Netherlands applies the general statutory minimum — Wet minimumloon). Posted drivers must receive at least the applicable Dutch minimum pay.
