## Overview

The Netherlands has a highly efficient exceptional transport permit system managed by the **RDW** (Rijksdienst voor het Wegverkeer) and the **BTB** (Bureau Bijzondere Transporten — Exceptional Transport Bureau). The Netherlands is a major logistics hub — Rotterdam port generates high volumes of exceptional industrial cargo. In addition to standard exceptional loads, the Netherlands permits **LZV** (Lange en Zware Voertuigen — Long and Heavy Vehicles) up to 25.25 m / 60t on designated routes.

---

## Thresholds Triggering a Permit

| Parameter | Standard limit | Permit required above |
|---|---|---|
| Width | 2.55 m | > 3.0 m (permit), > 4.0 m (police escort) |
| Length | 18.75 m | > 22.0 m (permit), > 30 m (police escort) |
| Height | 4.0 m | > 4.2 m (permit) |
| Weight | 40t (5-axle) | > 50t (permit) |

---

## LZV (Lange en Zware Voertuigen)

The Netherlands permits LZV combinations up to **25.25 m / 60t** on designated routes (LZV network). These do not require individual exceptional load permits but the vehicle/combination must be LZV-certified and the route must be on the approved LZV network.

---

## BTB — Exceptional Transport Bureau

The BTB (Bureau Bijzondere Transporten) in Apeldoorn coordinates all exceptional transport permits, route approvals, and escort vehicle arrangements:
- Permit applications submitted online
- Route analysis for bridge load capacity
- Pilot vehicle (begeleidingsvoertuig) coordination

---

## Processing Times

| Category | Lead time |
|---|---|
| Standard width/length permit | 5–10 working days |
| Heavy loads >100t | 2–4 weeks |
| Complex route/police escort | 4–6 weeks |

---

## Enforcement

Enforcement by **Rijkspolitie** and **ILT** (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport).

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without permit | €1,500–€4,500 | Wegenverkeerswet 1994; RVV 1990 |
| Exceeding permit dimensions | €750–€3,000 | RVV 1990 |
| Missing escort vehicle | €750 | RVV 1990 |
