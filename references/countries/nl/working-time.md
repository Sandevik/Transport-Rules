## Overview

The Netherlands implements EU Directive 2002/15/EC on the organisation of working time for mobile road transport workers through the **Arbeidstijdenwet** (ATW — Working Time Act) and the **CAO Beroepsgoederenvervoer** (collective labour agreement for professional goods transport). Dutch implementation is stricter than the EU baseline in some respects — notably the treatment of on-call time. Enforcement is by **ILT** (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport — Authority for the Living Environment and Transport).

---

## Key Working Time Limits

| Parameter | EU baseline | NL implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Average weekly working time | 48 hours (over 4-month reference period) | 48 hours (same) |
| Maximum weekly working time | 60 hours (if averaged) | 60 hours |
| Night work max | 10 hours per 24h containing night period | 10 hours |
| Night period definition | 00:00–04:00 (member state choice: 00:00–07:00 or 22:00–06:00) | 00:00–06:00 |
| Rest breaks | 30 min after 6h, 45 min after 9h | Same as EU |
| Reference period | 4 months (EU standard; extendable to 6 months by collective agreement) | **Extended to 52 weeks** via CAO |

## Dutch-Specific Rules

### On-Call Time (Beschikbaarheidstijd)
The Netherlands treats **on-call time more strictly** than many EU member states:
- On-call time while the driver must remain available at the vehicle (e.g., waiting at a loading dock): **fully counts as working time** in the Netherlands
- This differs from some EU interpretations where "available time" is treated separately

### CAO Beroepsgoederenvervoer
The **CAO Beroepsgoederenvervoer** (Collective Labour Agreement for Goods Transport by Road) is negotiated between TLN and FNV Transport & Logistiek / CNV Vakmensen. Key provisions:
- Reference period extended to 52 weeks (maximum allowed under Directive 2002/15/EC)
- Specific shift length rules
- Night and weekend premium pay rates
- Additional rest requirements beyond the legal minimum

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Exceeding maximum working time | €1,500–€4,500 per violation | ATW; Wet economische delicten |
| No working time records | €1,500–€4,500 | ATW |
| Operator systematic violation | Escalated fines; possible licence suspension | ATW; Wet wegvervoer goederen |
