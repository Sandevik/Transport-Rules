## Overview

The Netherlands issues Community Licences through the ILT (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport). The KvK (Kamer van Koophandel / Chamber of Commerce) handles business registration for the establishment requirement. Dutch transport operators must meet the standard EU four requirements.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Dutch specifics |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Dutch place of business | KvK registration required; effective management in NL |
| Good repute | No serious transport/criminal offences | Checked via KvK and transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC exam | Via SER-approved examination institutes |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**ILT** (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport) issues:
- EU Community Licences (for international haulage >3.5t)
- National licences for domestic goods transport

Website: https://www.ilent.nl/onderwerpen/beroepsvervoer

---

## Community Licence

Issued by ILT; certified true copies in each vehicle. Entitles Dutch carriers to operate internationally across EU/EEA.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in NL within 7 days after an international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period before next cabotage series in NL
- DUOS posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## LZV (EcoCombi) — Additional National Permit

Operators wishing to use Long Heavy Vehicles (LZV, up to 25.25m/60t) require an additional **ontheffing** (exemption/permit) from RWS (Rijkswaterstaat) on the designated LZV road network. This is separate from the Community Licence. See dimensions-weights.md.
