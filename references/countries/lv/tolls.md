# Latvia — Road User Charge (Vignette)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.csdd.lv/en/road-traffic/road-user-charge-vignette/ — CSDD: Road user charge
- https://lvvignette.eu — LV Vignette: Purchase portal

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Latvia requires a **road user charge vignette** for vehicles using main and regional state roads. The charge applies to vehicles above certain weight thresholds.

---

## Which Vehicles Must Pay

- Vehicles exceeding **3,000 kg** GVW
- Vehicle combinations exceeding **3,500 kg** GVW

---

## Roads Covered

Main state roads and regional roads of Latvia. Applies to all public roads in this network — not limited to motorways.

Legal basis: Law On Road User Charge; Cabinet Regulation No. 272 (26.05.2014).

---

## Vignette Durations

Available periods: typically day, week, month, year. Purchase at https://lvvignette.eu

---

## Payment

Online at https://lvvignette.eu or at petrol stations and authorised points of sale.
