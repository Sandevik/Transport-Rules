# Latvia — Spring Weight Restrictions (Pavasara satiksmes ierobežojumi)

**EU Baseline:** references/eu/spring-weight-restrictions.md — national implementation under EU Directive 96/53/EC Art. 4(4).

**Sources:**
- https://lvceli.lv/ — Latvijas Valsts celi (LVC — Latvian State Roads)
- https://www.likumi.lv/ — Latvijas Vestnesis (Latvian Official Gazette — legislation)

**Last verified: 2026-06-02**

---

## Overview

Latvia applies seasonal spring weight restrictions (**pavasara satiksmes ierobežojumi** — spring traffic restrictions) on secondary and local roads during the spring thaw period. These are issued when road subbase layers lose bearing capacity as frost melts upward through the road structure.

The restrictions are published and managed by **Latvijas Valsts celi (LVC)** for state roads. Municipal road authorities may issue additional restrictions on local roads.

---

## Timing

| Region | Typical restriction period | Notes |
|--------|--------------------------|-------|
| Western Latvia (Kurzeme, Zemgale) | February–March | Earlier thaw; maritime influence from Baltic coast |
| Central and eastern Latvia (Vidzeme, Latgale) | March–April | Later and more pronounced thaw; more frost-susceptible soils |

The exact start and end date of restrictions varies each year depending on weather conditions. LVC publishes updated restriction announcements on its website as conditions develop.

---

## Weight Reductions

During restriction periods, reduced axle load and GVW limits apply on designated roads:

| Road category | Typical restriction |
|---|---|
| State main roads (valsts galvenie autoceli) | Generally unrestricted; individual sections may be restricted |
| State regional roads (valsts regjionalie autoceli) | Reduced axle loads — often 5–8 t per axle |
| State local roads (valsts vietejie autoceli) | Significant reductions; sometimes complete prohibition of heavy vehicles |
| Municipal roads | Set by local authorities separately; often stricter |

Specific limits per road section are published by LVC and posted on-site with standard road signs.

---

## Checking Restrictions

- **LVC website:** https://lvceli.lv/ — spring restriction announcements and maps
- **On-site signing:** restricted roads carry posted weight limit signs showing the permitted maximum axle load or GVW
- Operators should consult LVC restriction maps before planning routes in the March–April period

---

## Penalties

Violations are treated as standard overload violations under Latvian road traffic law. See `references/countries/lv/penalties.md` if available.
