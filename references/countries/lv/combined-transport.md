## Overview

Latvia implements EU Directive 92/106/EEC on combined transport. Combined transport operations (initial/final road leg + main rail/sea/IWW haul) benefit from a cabotage exemption — the road legs are exempt from national cabotage restrictions regardless of haulier nationality. Latvia participates in the EU combined transport network.

---

## Combined Transport Definition

| Leg | Description |
|---|---|
| Initial road leg | Shipper to nearest suitable rail/sea/IWW terminal |
| Main haulage | By rail, sea, or inland waterway |
| Final road leg | Terminal to consignee |

Road legs must not exceed 150 km straight-line distance (or national implementation limit).

---

## Cabotage Exemption

Road hauliers performing road legs of a combined transport operation are **exempt from cabotage restrictions** under Directive 92/106/EEC.

Required documentation:
- Proof of combined transport (rail waybill / bill of lading)
- Standard CMR for road legs
- Community licence or bilateral permit
