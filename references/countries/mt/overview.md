## Overview

Malta is an EU member state and applies all EU transport regulations. **Transport Malta** is the national transport authority. As an island nation with no land borders to other EU member states, international road transport to/from Malta is limited; freight primarily moves by ferry (Malta–Sicily).

Malta has no motorway toll or HGV vignette system.
