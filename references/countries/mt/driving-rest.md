## Overview

Malta applies EU Regulation 561/2006 directly as an EU member state. Enforcement is carried out by Transport Malta and the Malta Police Force (Traffic Branch). As a small island archipelago, most commercial transport is domestic and short-distance; however tachograph and HOS rules apply to qualifying vehicles. International routes require sea/air transit — the ferry to Sicily/mainland Italy triggers the ferry exception under Art. 9 EU 561/2006.

## Deviations from Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 561/2006. Malta has exercised the Art. 13 domestic exemption for **certain agricultural and local delivery vehicles** operating within the island — these are exempt from tachograph requirements within a 100km radius under the national exemption provision.
