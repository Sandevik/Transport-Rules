## Overview

Post-Brexit, the UK operates its own emissions framework. The UK retained EU-equivalent type approval standards (Euro VI for HGVs) via the Retained EU Law. UK-specific CO2 regulations for heavy-duty vehicles are set under the Road Vehicle Carbon Dioxide Emission Performance Standards (Heavy Duty Vehicles) Regulations 2021. Clean Air Zones (CAZs) apply in several UK cities.

---

## Euro VI (UK Equivalent)

The UK applies Euro VI-equivalent standards for new heavy commercial vehicles, aligned with EU standards before Brexit. New vehicles must meet UK type approval requirements.

| Standard | Applies to | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Euro VI (UK equivalent) | New HGVs | Retained EU standard |
| ULEZ/CAZ Class | Urban access | City-by-city; check local rules |

---

## Clean Air Zones (CAZs)

Several UK cities operate Clean Air Zones with HGV access restrictions:
- **London** — ULEZ and LEZ for HGVs
- **Birmingham, Manchester, Bath** — various CAZ classes

Class D CAZs charge all vehicle types including HGVs. Check local authority rules before entry.
