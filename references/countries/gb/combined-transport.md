# United Kingdom — Combined Transport

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — UK post-Brexit framework (Directive 92/106/EEC no longer directly applicable).

**Sources:**

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Post-Brexit, EU Directive 92/106/EEC on combined transport no longer applies directly in Great Britain. The UK retains similar provisions under domestic law for GB-domestic combined transport. For international combined transport (GB–EU via Channel Tunnel or ferry), the road legs in EU member states are subject to EU rules; the GB road legs are subject to UK domestic rules. The EU cabotage exemption for combined transport does not apply to GB.

---

## GB Domestic Combined Transport

Within Great Britain, road-rail combined transport on domestic legs may benefit from exemptions to domestic cabotage restrictions under the Road Cabotage (Goods Vehicles) Regulations as retained. Verify current domestic rules under the relevant UK statutory instrument.

---

## International Combined Transport (GB–EU)

| Leg | Rules |
|---|---|
| EU road leg | EU Directive 92/106/EEC (EU member state rules apply) |
| Channel Tunnel / ferry | Tunnel/ferry operator rules |
| GB road leg | UK domestic regulations |

A standard CMR is required for each road leg. For the EU road leg, the combined transport cabotage exemption applies. For the GB road leg, UK domestic rules apply.
