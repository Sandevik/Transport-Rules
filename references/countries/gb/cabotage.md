# United Kingdom — Cabotage Rules (Post-Brexit)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — GB is Tier 3 standalone. UK-EU cabotage is governed by the UK-EU Trade and Cooperation Agreement (TCA), not EU Regulation 1072/2009.

**Sources:**
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/taking-goods-to-the-eu-by-lorry — GOV.UK: Taking goods to the EU by lorry

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Post-Brexit, UK and EU operators have **limited bilateral cabotage rights** under the UK-EU Trade and Cooperation Agreement (TCA). The rules are **different from EU 1072/2009** (the EU 3-in-7 rule). UK hauliers have no cabotage rights in EU member states beyond the TCA provisions, and EU hauliers have limited cabotage rights in the UK.

---

## EU Operators in the UK (Cabotage)

Under the TCA, EU-licensed hauliers who deliver into the UK may perform:
- Up to **2 additional cabotage operations** within **7 days** of the international delivery

This is less permissive than the EU's own 3-in-7 rule (3 operations in 7 days).

---

## UK Operators in the EU (Cabotage)

UK hauliers performing an international delivery into an EU member state may perform:
- Up to **2 cabotage operations** within **7 days** in that member state

Each EU member state retains enforcement authority. Check specific EU country files for enforcement approach and posting of workers obligations that apply.

---

## Community Licence vs UK Licence

- UK hauliers no longer hold EU Community Licences (post-Brexit)
- UK hauliers need a **ECMT/bilateral permit** or rely on TCA provisions for EU operations
- EU hauliers need their EU Community Licence + comply with UK import/export requirements

---

## Posting of Workers (EU → UK)

EU hauliers performing cabotage in the UK do NOT trigger EU Directive 2020/1057/EU posting obligations — the Directive no longer applies in the UK. UK domestic employment law applies.

For UK hauliers performing cabotage in EU member states — EU posting rules DO apply. See the relevant EU country's posting-declaration.md file.
