## Overview

National professional driver competence requirements apply in United Kingdom.  Training is provided by Approved Training Providers (ATP). DVSA oversees certification.

## CPC Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial CPC** | 280 hours training OR accelerated course (theory + practical exam) | National legislation |
| **Periodic CPC** | 35 hours every 5 years (7h/year or block) | National legislation |
| **DQC card validity** | 5 years | National legislation |
| **Licence code** | Code 95 printed on driving licence | National equivalent |
| **Minimum age (C/CE)** | 18 with CPC; 21 without | National legislation |

## Training Providers

- **Approved by:** DVSA
- **Providers:** Approved Training Providers (ATP)
- **Topics include:** Advanced driving and fuel efficiency, cargo securing, emergency procedures, regulations and compliance, road safety, health and safety

## Exemptions

Drivers of the following are exempt from CPC:
- Vehicles with max speed ≤45 km/h
- Armed forces, civil protection, fire service, police vehicles
- Vehicles undergoing road test for repair/maintenance
- Non-commercial (private) carriage of goods or passengers
