# United Kingdom — Abnormal Loads (Special Types)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — UK post-Brexit standalone rules (STGO and ESDAL system)

**Sources:**
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/special-types-of-vehicles-on-public-roads — GOV.UK: Special types of vehicles
- https://www.gov.uk/apply-abnormal-load — GOV.UK: Apply to move an abnormal load
- https://esdal.co.uk — ESDAL (Electronic Service Delivery for Abnormal Loads)

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

The UK uses the **STGO** (Special Types General Order — Road Vehicles (Authorisation of Special Types) General Order 2003) as the primary framework for vehicles exceeding standard legal dimensions or weights. Abnormal loads must be notified (and sometimes approved) via **ESDAL** (Electronic Service Delivery for Abnormal Loads). The rules differ between England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland for certain aspects. Post-Brexit, EU exceptional transport permits are not valid in GB.

---

## STGO Categories

| Category | Max weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Category 1** | Up to 50,000 kg | Vehicles/combinations for engineering plant |
| **Category 2** | Up to 80,000 kg | Vehicles/combinations for engineering plant with notification |
| **Category 3** | Up to 150,000 kg | Largest category — prior notification + potential police escort |

Standard HGV limit: 44,000 kg (44t). STGO allows heavier loads under specific conditions.

---

## Dimension Limits Triggering Notification

| Parameter | Standard limit | Notification triggered |
|---|---|---|
| Width | 2.55 m | > 2.9 m (police notification); > 5.0 m (escort required) |
| Length | 18.75 m | > 18.65 m (notification); > 30 m (escort required) |
| Height | 4.95 m (practical limit) | > 5.03 m (overhead structure checks) |

---

## ESDAL — Notification System

All abnormal load movements must be notified via **ESDAL** (https://esdal.co.uk):
- **2 clear days** notice for loads up to 5.0 m wide
- **5 clear days** notice for loads exceeding 5.0 m
- ESDAL automatically notifies affected highway authorities, utility companies, and emergency services
- Police notification required separately for loads exceeding 2.9 m width

---

## Police Escort Thresholds

| Condition | Police required |
|---|---|
| Width > 5.0 m | YES — CMPG (Central Motorway Police Group) or local |
| Length > 30.0 m | YES |
| Weight > 150,000 kg | YES |

---

## Speed Limits for Abnormal Loads

| Load width | Max speed |
|---|---|
| < 3.5 m | Standard road limits |
| 3.5–5.0 m | 40 mph on restricted roads, 50 mph on motorways |
| > 5.0 m | 20–30 mph with police escort |

---

## Night Restrictions

Most abnormal loads restricted from moving:
- After **23:00** on Sundays
- Between **07:00–09:00 and 16:00–19:00** on weekdays (peak hours in some areas)
- Specific local authority restrictions may apply

---

## Enforcement

DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) and Police enforce STGO compliance. Overloading penalties: fixed penalties or prosecution with unlimited fines.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (GBP) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Moving without ESDAL notification | Fixed penalty £300 / prosecution | Road Traffic Act 1988 |
| Exceeding STGO weight limits | Unlimited fine on prosecution | STGO 2003 |
| Missing required police escort | Fixed penalty + prosecution | STGO 2003 |
