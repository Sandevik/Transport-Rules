## Overview

The UK (post-Brexit) enforces transport regulations through DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency). EU Regulation 561/2006 is retained as domestic law in Great Britain (EU 561/2006 as retained by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018). DVSA issues Fixed Penalty Notices (FPNs) for minor violations, and refers serious cases for prosecution through the Magistrates' Court. Roadside deposit (provisional payment pending court) may be required from non-UK drivers without a UK address. The Traffic Commissioner can suspend or revoke operator licences for systemic violations.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations (Fixed Penalty Notices)

| Violation | Fixed Penalty (GBP) | Court max (GBP) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded (minor) | £100–£300 | £2,500 | Transport Act 1968; EU 561/2006 (retained) |
| Daily driving time exceeded (serious) | £300 | £2,500 | Transport Act 1968 |
| Insufficient daily rest (minor) | £100–£300 | £2,500 | Transport Act 1968 |
| Insufficient weekly rest | £200–£300 | £2,500 | Transport Act 1968 |
| Break not taken | £100–£200 | £2,500 | Transport Act 1968 |
| Tachograph not installed | £300 | £5,000 | Road Traffic Act 1988 §97 |
| Tachograph misuse / not recording | £200–£300 | £2,500 | RTA 1988 |
| Tachograph manipulation | Prosecution only | Unlimited | RTA 1988 §99ZB |

## DVSA Graduated Fixed Penalties (GFP) — Effective from 2013

DVSA uses a Graduated Fixed Penalty scheme where the level depends on severity:

| Severity | Fixed Penalty (GBP) |
|---|---|
| Minor | £100 |
| Serious | £200 |
| Very serious (prosecutable) | £300 + referral to Traffic Commissioner |

## Operator Licence Consequences

- Traffic Commissioners can **call operators to public inquiry**
- Powers include: curtailment, suspension, or revocation of operator licence
- Operators can be disqualified from holding a licence
- Financial standing checks required annually

## Overload / Overweight

| Violation | Fixed Penalty (GBP) | Court max | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Axle overload | £100–£300 | £5,000 | Road Traffic Act 1988 §41B |
| GVW exceeded | £100–£300 | £5,000 | RTA 1988 §41B |
| No STGO authorisation | £200–£300 | £2,500 | STGO Regulations 2003 |
| Bridge damage (liable) | Civil claim | Unlimited | Highways Act 1980 §59 |

## Non-UK Drivers — Deposit

Foreign drivers without a UK address: DVSA may require an **on-the-spot deposit** (up to the court maximum) before allowing the vehicle to continue. If the driver cannot pay the deposit, the vehicle may be immobilised until payment.
