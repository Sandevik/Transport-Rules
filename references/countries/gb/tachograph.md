## Overview

AETR Annex 1B applies for international road transport operations. United Kingdom is an AETR signatory. All commercial vehicles >3.5t GVW in scope must be fitted with a smart tachograph v2 from August 2025 (retrofit deadlines per Regulation 2021/1228). Calibration and workshop approval is managed by DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency).

## Calibration Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration interval | Every 2 years | AETR Annex 1B |
| Seal check | Every 6 years | AETR Annex 1B |
| After repair | Immediate recalibration required | AETR Annex 1B |
| Workshop type | Approved tachograph workshop only | AETR Annex 1B |

Approved workshop list: [DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tachographs-the-rules)

## Smart Tachograph v2 — Retrofit Deadlines

| Vehicle type | Deadline |
|---|---|
| New type approvals | 21 August 2023 |
| All new N3/M3 vehicles | 21 August 2024 |
| Retrofit smart v1 vehicles (international) | 21 August 2025 |
| Retrofit pre-smart vehicles (domestic) | 21 August 2026 |

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|---|---|---|
