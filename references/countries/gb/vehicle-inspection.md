# United Kingdom — Vehicle Inspection (Roadworthiness Test)

**Standalone:** national vehicle inspection framework (broadly aligned with EU Directive 2014/45/EU)

**Sources:**
- [DVSA](https://www.gov.uk/dvsa)
- [HGV Annual Test / MOT](https://www.gov.uk/get-vehicle-inspected)

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

**Note:** UK retained EU Directive 2014/45/EU as domestic law post-Brexit. The HGV Annual Test (DVSA) and MOT serve as the roadworthiness test. DVSA Authorised Testing Facilities conduct the test.

---

## Overview

United Kingdom implements its own legislation for periodic roadworthiness tests of commercial vehicles. The inspection system is operated by DVSA Authorised Testing Facilities (ATF). DVSA is the competent authority for vehicle inspection oversight.

## Periodic Inspection Intervals

National intervals (aligned with EU 2014/45/EU):

| Vehicle category | First test after registration | Subsequent interval |
|---|---|---|
| M2 (bus ≤5t) | 4 years | 2 years |
| M3 (bus >5t / coach) | 1 year | 1 year |
| N2 (truck 3.5–12t) | 4 years | 2 years |
| N3 (truck >12t) | 1 year | 1 year |
| O3 (trailer 3.5–10t) | 4 years | 2 years |
| O4 (trailer >10t) | 2 years | 1 year |

## Inspection System: HGV Annual Test / MOT

- **Operators:** DVSA Authorised Testing Facilities (ATF)
- **Booking:** Through approved inspection station
- **Pass/fail result:** Certificate issued on pass; re-inspection required for failures
- **Defect categories:** Minor (no immediate action), Major (repair within deadline), Dangerous (immobilise immediately)

## Tachograph and Speed Limiter at Inspection

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Tachograph calibration certificate | Must be valid (≤2 years) |
| Tachograph seals | Must be intact |
| Speed limiter | Checked for N3 (≤90 km/h) and M3 (≤100 km/h) |

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (GBP) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without valid inspection certificate | Significant fine / vehicle prohibition | National transport law |
| Dangerous defect — vehicle immobilised | Until repaired | National law |
