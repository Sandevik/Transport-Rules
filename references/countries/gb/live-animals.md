## Overview

Post-Brexit, the UK applies its own animal welfare in transport legislation. In England, the Welfare of Animals (Transport) (England) Order 2006 (as amended) implements similar standards to the former EU Regulation EC 1/2005. Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland have equivalent devolved legislation. From 2024, the UK introduced stricter rules on long-distance live animal exports.

---

## Journey Time Limits (England — WATO 2006)

| Animal Type | Max Journey (without rest) | Rest Period |
|---|---|---|
| Cattle, sheep, goats, pigs | 8 hours | 1 hour water/feed, then may continue |
| Horses | 24 hours (with water/hay access) | 24 hours at rest facility |
| Poultry (with water) | 12 hours | — |
| Day-old chicks | 24 hours | — |

---

## Exports from GB

The UK has introduced a ban on live animal exports for slaughter and fattening. The Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024 prohibits live exports of cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, and horses from Great Britain for slaughter or fattening. Exports for other purposes (e.g. breeding) remain subject to specific conditions.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (GBP) | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Transport without valid authorisation | Fine + prohibition | WATO 2006; national legislation |
| Animal welfare violation | Fine up to £5,000 + possible imprisonment | Animal Welfare Act 2006 |
| Exporting for slaughter (post-ban) | Criminal prosecution | Animal Welfare (Livestock Exports) Act 2024 |
