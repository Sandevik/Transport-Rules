## Overview

Ireland applies EU Regulation EC 1/2005 on the protection of animals during transport. The regulation sets minimum standards for journey times, rest periods, space allowances, and documentation. Enforcement is by the national veterinary/food safety authority.

---

## Journey Time Limits

| Animal Type | Max Journey (without rest) | Rest Period |
|---|---|---|
| Cattle, sheep, goats, pigs | 8 hours | 1 hour water/feed, then may continue |
| Cattle/sheep/goats/pigs (long journey) | 14h + 1h rest, then 14h | 24 hours at rest facility |
| Horses | 24 hours (with water/hay access) | 24 hours at rest facility |
| Poultry (with water access) | 12 hours | — |
| Day-old chicks | 24 hours | — |

---

## Key Documentation

| Document | Required for |
|---|---|
| Animal transport certificate (ATC) | Long journeys (>8 hours) |
| Journey log | Long journeys crossing borders |
| Transporter authorisation (Type 1/2) | All commercial animal transport |
| Certificate of approval (vehicle) | Long journeys |
| Driver/attendant competence certificate | All journeys |

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Transport without valid ATC | Fine + prohibition | Regulation EC 1/2005; national law |
| Animal welfare violation | Fine + possible criminal prosecution | Regulation EC 1/2005 |
| Journey time exceeded | Fine | Regulation EC 1/2005 |
