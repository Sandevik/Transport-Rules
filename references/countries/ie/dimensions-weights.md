## Overview

Ireland applies EU Directive 96/53/EC as an EU member state. Standard EU limits apply for most vehicles. Ireland has some national deviations including a **4.65m height limit** on national roads (higher than the EU 4.0m common standard) and specific provisions for Northern Ireland cross-border operations (Brexit-related). Overweight/oversize permits are administered by Transport Infrastructure Ireland (TII) and local authorities.

## Standard Limits

| Parameter | Limit | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Semi-trailer + tractor | 16.5m | 96/53/EC |
| Road train | 18.75m | 96/53/EC |
| Width (standard) | 2.55m | 96/53/EC |
| Width (refrigerated) | 2.60m | 96/53/EC |
| Height (national roads) | 4.65m | Irish SI 417/2003 |
| 5-axle semi GVW | 40t | 96/53/EC |
| 6-axle road train | 40t | 96/53/EC |

## Irish Deviations from EU Baseline

| Feature | Irish rule | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | 4.65m on national roads | Higher than EU common standard of 4.0m |
| **5-axle articulated** | 44t (combined transport) | EU CT bonus applies |
| **Northern Ireland** | Post-Brexit: NI follows UK rules within NI; GB–IE transport uses EU limits for ROI portion | Check current Windsor Framework provisions |
| **Off-road aggregate haulage** | Special provisions for quarry/agriculture vehicles on designated roads | Road Traffic (Construction Equipment and Use) Regulations |

## Oversize/Overweight Permits (Abnormal Loads)

- **TII Special Permits**: required for loads exceeding standard limits on national roads
- **Local Authority Permits**: required on non-national roads
- Application: [TII online portal](https://www.tii.ie/roads-light-rail/roads-safety/abnormal-loads/)
- Pilot vehicle: required for width > 3.5m or certain heavy combinations
- Fee: based on route length and load category (EUR)
