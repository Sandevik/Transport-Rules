## Overview

Ireland applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **RSA** (Road Safety Authority) and the **Garda Síochána** (police). Ireland has an island geography with significant ro-ro ferry traffic; ferry rest rules are commonly relevant.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.

**Ferry rest interruptions:** The EU rule allowing daily rest to be interrupted twice for ferry/train boarding (max 1h total) is frequently relevant for Ireland-UK and Ireland-France routes.
