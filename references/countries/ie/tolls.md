# Ireland — Road Tolls

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://etoll.ie/toll-rates/ — eToll.ie: Irish toll rates

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Ireland charges tolls at specific motorway points and crossings — there is no general distance-based HGV charge. Tolls are collected electronically (eFlow) and at traditional plazas. Ireland has no national vignette.

---

## HGV Rates at Major Toll Points

### M50 (Dublin ring road — barrier-free)

| HGV category | Tag/registered | Video | Unregistered |
|-------------|----------------|-------|-------------|
| Up to 2,000 kg | €3.60 | €4.20 | €4.90 |
| 2,000–10,000 kg | €5.20 | €5.90 | €6.50 |
| >10,000 kg | €6.50 | €7.20 | €7.80 |

*Note: Dublin Port Tunnel exempts goods vehicles >3,500 kg.*

### East Link Toll Bridge (Dublin)

| Unladen weight | Rate |
|---------------|------|
| Up to 2 tonnes | €3.50 |
| >2t, ≤2 axles | €4.70 |
| >2t, ≤3 axles | €5.90 |
| >2t, 4+ axles | €7.10 |

### Motorways (M1, M3, M4, M7/M8, N25, etc.)

| Vehicle | Rate per section |
|---------|----------------|
| 2–3 axle HGV >3,500 kg | **€5.90** |
| 4+ axle HGV >3,500 kg | **€7.50** |

---

## Payment

- **eFlow tag** (electronic transponder) — recommended for regular Irish operations; registered accounts get lower rates
- **Credit/debit card** at staffed/automated plazas
- Online payment for M50 barrier-free tolling
