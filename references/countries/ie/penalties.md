## Overview

Ireland enforces EU transport regulations through RSA (Road Safety Authority) enforcement officers and An Garda Síochána (police). Fixed Charge Notices (FCNs) are issued for minor violations. Serious violations are prosecuted through the District Court. Operators may face RSA investigation and potential licence sanctions. Roadside immediate payment is not typically required — notices issued with a payment deadline.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations

| Violation | Fixed Charge (EUR) | Court max (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded (minor ≤ 1h) | €80–€160 | €5,000 | SI 68/2012; Road Traffic Act |
| Daily driving time exceeded (serious > 1h) | €160–€500 | €5,000 | SI 68/2012 |
| Insufficient daily rest | €80–€500 | €5,000 | SI 68/2012 |
| Insufficient weekly rest | €200–€500 | €5,000 | SI 68/2012 |
| Break not taken | €80–€200 | €5,000 | SI 68/2012 |
| Tachograph absent / not working | €200–€500 | €5,000 | Road Traffic Act 2004 |
| Tachograph manipulation | Prosecution only | €5,000 + prison | Road Traffic Act 2004 §15 |

## Overload

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Axle or GVW overload | €1,000–€5,000 | Road Traffic Act 1961 (as amended) |
| Repeated overloading | Prosecution + licence review | Road Traffic Act |
