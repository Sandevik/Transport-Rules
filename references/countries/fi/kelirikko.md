# Finland — Kelirikko (Spring Thaw Weight Restrictions)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — this is a Finnish national seasonal restriction regime.

**Sources:**
- https://www.traficom.fi/en/transport/road/road-traffic-act2020 — Traficom: Road Traffic Act 2020
- https://www.finlex.fi/en/laki/kaannokset/2018/en20180267 — Act on Transport Services (Liikennepalvelulaki)
- https://liikennevirasto.fi/ — Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency (Väylävirasto)

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

**Kelirikko** (Finnish: frost damage season / spring thaw) refers to the seasonal period — typically **March through May** — when melting frost causes roads to become soft and lose load-bearing capacity. During kelirikko, Finland imposes **temporary reduced weight limits (painorajoitukset)** on affected road sections to prevent serious road damage.

These restrictions are one of the most operationally significant seasonal constraints for freight operations in Finland, particularly for forest transport and rural deliveries.

---

## When Kelirikko Restrictions Apply

| Parameter | Typical value |
|-----------|--------------|
| Season | Mid-March — late May (varies by year and region) |
| Northern Finland | Later: may extend into June at higher latitudes |
| Southern Finland | Earlier onset and earlier lift |
| Activation trigger | Ground frost melt depth; road surface conditions |

The restrictions are not fixed calendar dates — they are activated and lifted dynamically based on actual road conditions, monitored by the Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency (Väylävirasto) in cooperation with regional ELY centres (Centre for Economic Development, Transport and the Environment).

---

## Weight Limits During Kelirikko

When kelirikko restrictions are in effect on a given road:

| Restriction level | Axle load limit | GVW limit (indicative) |
|-------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Standard restriction | 8 tonnes per axle | ~20–24 t total |
| Severe restriction | 4 tonnes per axle | varies |

These are **significantly lower** than the standard Finnish limits (76t GVW for modular combinations). Even standard 40t EU-class vehicles may be prohibited or subject to reduced axle load limits on restricted roads.

---

## Which Roads Are Affected

- **Lower category roads (paikallistiet and maantiet)**: Most affected. These include many rural delivery roads and forest tracks.
- **National highways (valtatiet) and European roads (E-roads)**: Generally exempt from kelirikko restrictions due to heavier construction.
- **Forest roads (metsätiet)**: May have their own additional restrictions imposed by forest owners or municipalities.

---

## Impact on Forest Transport

Finland's forestry sector is heavily reliant on road transport during kelirikko:

- **76-tonne forest combinations** are typically **prohibited** on kelirikko-restricted roads
- Forest industry and transport operators must plan timber deliveries for the winter frost period (before thaw) or for summer when roads have dried
- Some forest roads are passable only when frozen (winter logging roads — talvitiet)

---

## Exemptions and Special Permits

- Emergency deliveries (e.g., essential food supply, healthcare) may be permitted with **special dispensation from ELY centres**
- Permits for restricted roads must be applied for in advance
- Permit conditions typically include reduced speed, time-of-day restrictions, or load limits

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Operating over kelirikko weight limit | Overload charge (ylikuormamaksu) — €40–€80 per excess tonne | Laki ylikuormamaksusta 51/1982 |
| Failure to comply with posted restriction sign | Fine + overload charge | Road Traffic Act 2018 |
| Road damage caused by overloading | Civil liability for road damage repair | Laki ylikuormamaksusta |
