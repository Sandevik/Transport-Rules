## Overview

Finland applies EU Regulation 561/2006 fully, with no significant national deviations from the core rules. Enforcement is shared between Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency) and the Occupational Safety and Health authorities (Tyosuojelu). Finland has national exemptions for certain agricultural, forestry, and island operations.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core driving/rest time rules. Finland fully applies all EU standard requirements including Mobility Package 2022 updates.

**National exemptions:** Finland has national exemptions from EU 561/2006 for:
- Agricultural, forestry, and fishing vehicles within a limited radius
- Certain island operations
- See Tyosuojelu.fi for current exemption list

## Penalties

Finland applies penalty fees (liikennevirhemaksu) for traffic violations including driving/rest time breaches.

| Violation category | Approach |
|-------------------|---------|
| Driving/rest time violations | Penalty fees issued by police at roadside; employer may also face sanctions |
| Tachograph violations | Fine and potential prohibition of operation |

Full penalty information: https://www.traficom.fi/en/transport/road/traffic-violations-and-traffic-penalty-fees
