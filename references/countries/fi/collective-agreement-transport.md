## Overview

Finland has a national sector collective agreement for road goods transport drivers: the **Kuorma-autoalan työehtosopimus** (Kuorma-autoalan TES — Road Transport Sector Collective Agreement). It is negotiated between the union **AKT (Auto- ja kuljetusalan työntekijäliitto ry)** and the employer association **Autoliikenteen Työnantajaliitto ry** (Road Transport Employers' Association).

**Current agreement period: 26 February 2025 – 31 January 2028** (confirmed via AKT.fi)

The Kuorma-autoalan TES is declared **yleissitova** (generally applicable) under Finnish law. A yleissitova agreement applies to **all employers in the sector**, not just Autoliikenteen Työnantajaliitto members — including foreign operators whose drivers work in Finland. The generally applicable terms set the floor for posting of workers to Finland.

---

## Scope

Covers:
- Truck drivers (kuorma-auton kuljettajat) employed in road freight transport (kuorma-autoliikenne)
- All weight categories — from medium trucks to full heavy combinations
- Local/regional distribution and long-distance (kaukoliikenne)

Does not cover: bus/coach drivers (separate Linja-autohenkilökunnan TES), taxi (separate agreement).

## Framework

The current agreement runs 26 February 2025 – 31 January 2028, with the following agreed wage increases:

| Effective date | Wage increase |
|---|---|
| 1 March 2025 | +2.5% |
| 1 June 2026 | +2.9% |
| 1 June 2027 | +2.4% |

It covers:

| Topic | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Minimum wages (vähimmäispalkat) | Wage scales by driver category; vehicle type and load |
| Overtime (ylityökorvaukset) | Premium rates above standard working hours |
| Evening and night supplements (ilta- ja yölisät) | Supplements for work outside standard daytime hours |
| Weekend and public holiday premiums | Lauantai-, sunnuntai- ja arkipyhälisät |
| Daily allowances (päiväraha) | Subsistence allowances when away from home |
| Annual leave (vuosiloma) | Per Vuosilomalaki; minimum 4–5 weeks by years of service |
| Sick pay (sairausajan palkka) | Employer pays during illness; scales by seniority |
| Pension | TyEL (Työntekijän eläkelaki) — statutory earnings-related pension; employer contributes approximately 17–18% of salary |

> **Note:** Specific current wage rates change with each negotiation round. Verify current rates via AKT (akt.fi). Päiväraha (daily allowance) amounts are confirmed below as of March 1, 2025.

### Annual Leave (Vuosiloma)

Under the Finnish **Vuosilomalaki (162/2005)**:
- Employees accumulate 2 days' holiday credit per month in the first year, rising to 2.5 days/month after 1 year of service
- Maximum: typically 5 weeks (25 days) after full accumulation
- The Kuorma-autoalan TES may provide conditions at least as favourable as this statutory minimum

### Sick Pay (Sairausajan Palkka)

Finnish sick pay:
- Employer pays **full salary** for the duration of illness up to a collective agreement defined period (typically 1 month or more by seniority)
- After that, **Kela (Kansaneläkelaitos / Social Insurance Institution)** pays sairauspäiväraha (sickness allowance) — approximately 70% of earnings
- The first day of illness (omavastuuaika / waiting period) rules depend on the collective agreement — some TES agreements waive the waiting day

### Daily Allowances (Päiväraha / Matkakustannusten Korvaus)

Confirmed amounts from the 2025–2028 TES, effective **1 March 2025** (source: AKT.fi):

| Situation | Daily allowance |
|---|---|
| Domestic — partial day (osapäiväraha) | €19.30 |
| Domestic — full day (kokopäiväraha) | €42.50 |
| International — Europe | €71.80 |
| International — outside Europe | €76.80 |

Standard conditions: **80 hours per 2-week period** (= 40 hours/week standard working time). Maximum continuous driving before mandatory break: **5.5 hours**. Daily rest: minimum **11 consecutive hours**.

## Generally Applicable (Yleissitovuus)

Yleissitovuus means:
1. The agreement is confirmed as generally applicable by the **Sosiaali- ja terveysministeriö (STM)** / the yleissitovuuden vahvistamislautakunta
2. Once confirmed, it applies to ALL employers in kuorma-autoliikenne operating in Finland — including foreign operators posting workers to Finland
3. Foreign operators must ensure their drivers working in Finland receive at least the yleissitova minimum terms (see `references/countries/fi/posting-workers.md`)

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below yleissitova minimum wage | Back pay; administrative fine | Laki lähetetyistä työntekijöistä (447/2016) for posted workers |
| Not paying overtime premiums | Civil liability; AKT claim | Kuorma-autoalan TES |
| Breach of sick pay obligations | Back pay + interest | Työsopimuslaki (55/2001) |
