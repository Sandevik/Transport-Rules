## Overview

Finland enforces EU transport regulations through Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency) and Poliisi (police). Administrative fines (seuraamusmaksut) for driving/rest time violations were significantly reformed by the 2019 Act (1203/2019), introducing a structured penalty scale. Roadside fines are issued as administrative sanctions — no immediate cash payment required. Overload charges (ylikuormamaksu) are civil administrative fees per tonne over limit.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations (Seuraamusmaksut)

| Violation | Fine (EUR) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded ≤ 1h | €100–€200 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Daily driving time exceeded 1–3h | €200–€500 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Daily driving time exceeded > 3h | €500–€1,000 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Insufficient daily rest ≤ 1h | €100–€200 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Insufficient daily rest 1–3h | €200–€500 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Insufficient daily rest > 3h | €500–€1,000 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Insufficient weekly rest | €300–€1,500 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Break not taken | €100–€300 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Tachograph not used | €200–€500 | Laki 1203/2019 |
| Tachograph manipulation | €5,000–€15,000 + prosecution | Rikoslaki (criminal) |
| Operator liability | Up to €10,000 per violation | Laki 1203/2019 |

## Overload Charges (Ylikuormamaksu)

Finland uses an administrative overload charge system per tonne over axle/GVW limit:

| Overload amount | Fee (EUR/tonne over) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Per tonne over axle limit | €40–€80 | Laki ylikuormamaksusta (51/1982) |
| Per tonne over GVW limit | €40–€80 | Laki ylikuormamaksusta (51/1982) |
| Minimum charge | €100 | Laki ylikuormamaksusta |
