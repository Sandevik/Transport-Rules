## Overview

Finland has **mandatory winter tyre requirements** for all motor vehicles. The rules are among the strictest in Europe, reflecting Finland's severe winter conditions with regular sub-zero temperatures, snow, and ice for three to five months per year. The requirements apply to **all motor vehicles** including trucks (lorries) and buses, not only passenger cars. The legal basis is the Finnish Vehicle Act and Traficom's technical regulations.

---

## Mandatory Winter Tyre Period

| Period | Rule |
|--------|------|
| **1 December – last day of February** | Winter tyres **mandatory** for all motor vehicles (including trucks and buses) |
| **1 November – 31 March** | Studded (nastarengas) tyres **permitted** for all regions |
| **Lapland exception** | Studded tyres permitted from **1 November to 30 April** (Lapin maakunta) |

Winter tyres must be fitted when road conditions require them outside the mandatory period as well (situational obligation year-round).

---

## Tyre Type Requirements

| Vehicle category | Acceptable winter tyre types |
|-----------------|------------------------------|
| All motor vehicles (including HGV) | M+S (Mud and Snow) or 3PMSF (Three-Peak Mountain Snowflake) |
| Trailers | Trailers do not have a mandatory winter tyre requirement (though winter tyres are strongly recommended) |

- Studded tyres (nastarengas / piikkirengas) are permitted during the studded tyre season.
- All wheels on the same axle must be fitted with the same type of tyre.
- Mixing studded and non-studded tyres on the same axle is prohibited.

---

## Minimum Tread Depth

| Period / condition | Minimum tread depth |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| Winter tyre mandatory period (Dec – Feb) | **3.0 mm** |
| Year-round minimum | **1.6 mm** (summer tyres outside mandatory period) |

Traficom recommends a minimum of **4 mm** in practice for winter tyres to provide adequate grip on snow and ice.

---

## Application to Heavy Goods Vehicles (Trucks and Buses)

The winter tyre mandate **fully applies** to:
- Heavy goods vehicles (HGV) over 3,500 kg GVW
- Buses and coaches
- All drive axles and steering axles

The mandatory dates (1 December to last day of February) apply regardless of vehicle mass. However, Traficom guidance recommends heavy vehicle operators fit winter tyres already from **1 November** given Finnish road conditions.

For HGV specifically:
- Winter tyres must be fitted on **all drive axles** and **the front steering axle** as a minimum.
- Tag axles (lift axles) should also have winter tyres fitted when lowered.

---

## Studded Tyres

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Permitted season (standard) | 1 November – 31 March |
| Permitted season (Lapland) | 1 November – 30 April |
| Extension if conditions require | Studded tyres may be used beyond the end of the season if road conditions remain wintry (driver's discretion / situational basis) |
| Stud protrusion max | Regulated in Traficom technical regulations |
| Fee for studded use in cities | Some Finnish municipalities have imposed nominal fees; check locally |

---

## Enforcement

Winter tyre compliance is enforced by:
- **Poliisi (Finnish Police)**: Roadside checks, particularly in November–March.
- **Traficom**: Periodic technical inspection (katsastus) — tyres are inspected for condition, tread depth, and age.

Roadside checks include measurement of tread depth. Officers can prohibit continued use of a vehicle with non-compliant tyres.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (EUR) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Operating without winter tyres during mandatory period | **€40–€200** (fixed penalty notice) |
| Tread depth below minimum | €40–€100 |
| Mixing studded and non-studded tyres on same axle | €40–€100 |
| Using studded tyres outside permitted season (without justification) | €40–€100 |

For commercial vehicle operators (trucking companies), repeated violations can affect operating licence assessment. Penalties are escalated for professional transport operators under the Laki kaupallisista tavarankuljetuksista tiellä.
