# Finland — Oversize and Overweight Loads (Erikoiskuljetukset)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — exceptional transport permits are a national matter.

**Sources:**
- https://www.traficom.fi/fi/liikenne/tieliikenne/erikoiskuljetukset — Traficom: Erikoiskuljetukset (special transport)
- https://www.finlex.fi/fi/laki/ajantasa/1981/19811257 — Tieliikennelaki (Road Traffic Act, as amended)
- https://www.vayla.fi/ — Väylävirasto: Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Vehicles or loads exceeding Finland's standard dimension or weight limits require a **special transport permit (erikoiskuljetuslupa)**. In Finland, special transport is regulated by Traficom and the Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency (Väylävirasto). Finland's high standard limits (76t / 34.5m for modular combinations) mean many loads that require permits in other EU states travel within standard limits in Finland.

---

## Standard Thresholds (No Permit Required)

| Parameter | Finnish standard limit |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Max GVW (9-axle modular) | 76 tonnes |
| Max length (modular) | 34.5 metres |
| Max width | 2.60m (standard road) / 3.0m (forestry) |
| Max height | 4.4 metres |

Loads within these limits do **not** require a special transport permit on Finnish public roads.

---

## When a Special Transport Permit Is Required

A permit is needed when the load exceeds:

| Parameter | Permit threshold |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Width | > 3.0 m (or > 2.6m on roads where standard limit is 2.6m) |
| Height | > 4.4 m |
| Length | > 34.5 m |
| GVW | > 76 tonnes (on approved network) |

---

## Permit Types

| Permit type | Description | Issuing authority |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Route permit (reittilupa)** | Single journey on defined route | Väylävirasto (for national/state roads) or municipality |
| **Area permit (aluepäätös)** | Multiple journeys within a defined area | Väylävirasto |
| **Annual permit** | Recurring transport of the same load type | Väylävirasto |

---

## Application Process

1. Apply online through Väylävirasto's transport permit service (kuljetuslupa.fi)
2. Submit: vehicle technical data, route description, load details, escort details
3. Processing time: typically 5–10 working days for standard permits; longer for complex loads
4. Permit specifies allowed route, time of day, speed limits, and escort requirements

---

## Escort Requirements

| Condition | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Width > 3.5 m or length > 30 m | Rear escort vehicle required |
| Width > 5.0 m | Front and rear escort vehicles required |
| Very large loads on complex routes | Police escort may be required (arranged separately with Poliisi) |

Escort vehicles must display appropriate warning lights and signage.

---

## Specific Speed Limits (During Special Transport)

| Width / weight | Max speed |
|----------------|-----------|
| Standard special transport | 80 km/h (or as posted) |
| Very large/heavy loads | As specified in permit (often 50–60 km/h) |

---

## Time Restrictions

Special transports must generally not travel:
- **During darkness** (unless permit specifically allows it)
- On public holidays (unless permitted)
- During peak traffic hours on congested routes (permit may specify allowed hours)

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Operating oversize without permit | €422–€5,000 + possible immobilisation | Road Traffic Act |
| Exceeding permit conditions (width/height) | Administrative fine | Tieliikennelaki |
| Overload charge (ylikuormamaksu) | €40–€80 per tonne over limit | Laki ylikuormamaksusta 51/1982 |
| Failure to use required escort | Fine | Tieliikennelaki |
