## Overview

Finland applies EU Directive 2020/1057/EU on posting of workers in road transport. Drivers must receive Finland's minimum wage and working conditions when posted. The Mobility Package rules (in force from 2 February 2022) define when posting applies based on operation type.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation Type | Posting Applies? |
|---|---|
| Bilateral transport (home ↔ Finland) | NO (from 2 Feb 2022) |
| Transit through Finland | NO |
| Cabotage in Finland | YES — from 1st cabotage leg |
| Cross-trade operations via Finland | YES |

---

## Minimum Wage Obligation

When posting applies, the driver must receive at minimum Finland's statutory minimum wage for the posting period.

---

## Declaration Requirements

Operators must submit a posting declaration via the IMI system or Finland's national declaration platform before the operation begins.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| No posting declaration | Administrative fine | Directive 2020/1057/EU; national implementation |
| Failure to pay minimum wage | Fine (unpaid wages + penalty) | National labour law |
