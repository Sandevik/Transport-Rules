## Overview

Bulgaria issues Community Licences through the ИА "Автомобилна администрация" (Executive Agency Road Administration). Bulgarian operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam (Свидетелство за професионална компетентност) is administered by approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Bulgarian specifics |
|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Bulgarian place of business | Търговски регистър (Commercial Register) |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal record; transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | Свидетелство за проф. компетентност | Via approved examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**ИА "Автомобилна администрация"**: issues Community Licences (лицензи на Общността) and domestic licences.

Website: https://www.rta.government.bg/

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Bulgaria within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- GIT posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
