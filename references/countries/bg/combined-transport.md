## Overview

Bulgaria implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Bulgaria's key combined transport assets are its Black Sea ports (Varna, Burgas) and rail connections to Turkey, Greece, and the western Balkans. The Danube river forms Bulgaria's northern border with Romania, enabling inland waterway connections. Bulgaria has no general HGV driving bans.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Varna port | Black Sea | Container + ro-ro; connections to Ukraine, Georgia, Turkey |
| Burgas port | Black Sea | Container + bulk; Caspian transit |
| Sofia (TIR terminal Илиянци) | Sofia | Road freight hub; Turkey corridor |
| Ruse / Giurgiu crossing | Danube | Connection to Romania via Danube bridge |

**Danube waterway**: From Ruse/Svishtov, river barges connect to Romania, Hungary, and ultimately Rotterdam via the Rhine-Danube route.

---

## Documentation Required

- Port/rail/river terminal confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
