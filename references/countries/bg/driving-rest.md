## Overview

Bulgaria applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **RTA** (Road Transport Administration / Изпълнителна агенция „Автомобилна администрация") and the **Traffic Police**. Bulgaria is a key transit country for SE Europe–Western Europe freight and Turkey-bound traffic.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
