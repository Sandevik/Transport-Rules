## Overview

Bulgaria implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through Закон за командироване и изпращане на работници и служители в рамките на предоставяне на услуги. Foreign carriers must notify via the GIT (Главна инспекция по труда / General Labour Inspectorate) before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade in Bulgaria.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Bulgaria | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in BG + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Bulgaria only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification

Submit via GIT portal: https://www.gli.government.bg/

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Минимална работна заплата (minimum wage)**: approximately BGN 933/month (2025; updated by Government Decree)
- **Sector collective agreements**: where applicable, higher rates may apply
- Posted drivers must receive at least the Bulgarian national minimum wage

---

## Driver Documentation

- GIT notification confirmation
- Employment contract showing Bulgarian-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (BGN) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | BGN 1,500–BGN 15,000 per worker | Кодекс на труда |
| Failure to notify posting | BGN 1,500–BGN 15,000 | Закон за командироване |
