## Overview

Bulgaria applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The ИА "Автомобилна администрация" (Executive Agency Road Administration) coordinates road transport policy. Sofia is developing a Clean Transport Zone (Чиста зона) but implementation for HGVs is at an early stage as of 2026. The BGTOLL system applies to vehicles >3.5t on national roads.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Bulgarian national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**

---

## Sofia Urban Emission Zone

Sofia is planning clean air/emission-based access restrictions for vehicles. Current status: discussions ongoing; no comprehensive HGV LEZ as of 2026.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- EU RRF-funded programmes (НПВУ) for fleet electrification
- Reduced annual vehicle tax for ZEV vehicles
