# Bulgaria — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**
- https://www.rta.government.bg/ — IA Road Administration
- https://www.api.bg/ — АПИ: Road Infrastructure Agency

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Bulgaria applies EU standard weights and dimensions under Закона за движение по пътищата (ЗДвП / Road Traffic Act) and implementing regulations. No special large vehicle programme. The BGTOLL electronic tolling system applies to vehicles >3.5t on the national road network.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Road Network Note

Some Bulgarian national and regional roads have lower axle weight capacities. Motorways (A-roads) and main national roads (I-roads) generally comply with EU standard limits.

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits (извънгабаритен транспорт) issued by АПИ (Агенция Пътна Инфраструктура) for national roads.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.
