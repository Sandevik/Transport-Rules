## Overview

Bulgaria applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is БАБХ (Българска агенция по безопасност на храните / Bulgarian Food Safety Agency). Bulgaria is a transit country for livestock movements from Turkey and Eastern European countries, particularly via the Капитан Андреево (Kapitan Andreevo) border crossing with Turkey.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | ОДБХ (Regional Directorate of Food Safety) |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | ОДБХ (Regional Directorate) |

---

## Key BIPs

- **Kapitan Andreevo** (Turkish border): major BIP for livestock from Turkey
- **Varna port**: BIP for third-country sea arrivals

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Ветеринарно-здравен сертификат (veterinary health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (BGN) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | BGN 1,000–BGN 10,000 | Закон за ветеринарномедицинската дейност |
| Welfare violations | BGN 1,000–BGN 10,000 | Закон за защита на животните |
| Failure to maintain journey log | BGN 200–BGN 2,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
