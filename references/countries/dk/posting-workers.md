## Overview

Denmark implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Udstationeringsloven (Act on Posting of Workers). Foreign carriers must register on the **RUT** (Registret for Udenlandske Tjenesteydere / Register of Foreign Service Providers) before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. Enforcement is by the Danish Working Environment Authority (AT — Arbejdstilsynet) and the tax authority (Skat).

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | RUT registration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Denmark | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in DK + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Denmark only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## RUT Registration

- Portal: https://rut.dk/
- Registration must be completed before work commences in Denmark
- Required information: company, workers, vehicle, period, type of work

---

## Minimum Pay — Danish Collective Agreements

Denmark has no statutory national minimum wage. Minimum pay is set by collective agreements:
- **3F Transportgruppen** and **DI Transport** negotiate the transport sector collective agreement
- The applicable rate depends on the driver's category; typically around DKK 175–225/hour
- Drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the applicable Danish sector agreement rate

---

## Driver Documentation

- RUT registration confirmation
- Employment contract showing Danish collective agreement wage level
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| AT | Arbejdstilsynet (Working Environment Authority) | https://www.at.dk/ |
| Skat | Danish Tax Authority (social contribution compliance) | https://www.skat.dk/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Denmark can demand security deposits from foreign carriers.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (DKK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to register on RUT | DKK 10,000–DKK 100,000 | Udstationeringsloven |
| Failure to pay Danish collective agreement wages | DKK 10,000–DKK 100,000 per worker | Udstationeringsloven |
