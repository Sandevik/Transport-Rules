## Overview

Denmark applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is Fødevarestyrelsen (Danish Veterinary and Food Administration — FVST). Denmark is a major pig producer and exporter, and live animal export (particularly pigs to Germany and Poland) is significant. FVST is known for rigorous inspection.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | Fødevarestyrelsen |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | Fødevarestyrelsen |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Rejsejournalen (journey log) for >8 hours
- Veterinary health certificate
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005. Denmark may apply stricter enforcement in practice.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (DKK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | DKK 10,000–DKK 100,000 | Dyrevelfærdsloven |
| Welfare violations | DKK 10,000–DKK 100,000 | Dyrevelfærdsloven |
| Failure to maintain journey log | DKK 5,000–DKK 50,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
