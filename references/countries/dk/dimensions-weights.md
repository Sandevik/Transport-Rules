## Overview

Denmark applies EU Directive 96/53/EC as an EU member state. Standard EU limits apply for most road transport. Denmark has no general national weight increase above EU limits, but participates in the Nordic EMS exception (25.25m / up to 48t for certain domestic combinations). Notable infrastructure features include the Storebælt (Great Belt) and Øresund bridges, which have their own posted weight and height restrictions that may differ from general road limits.

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline)

| Parameter | Limit | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Semi-trailer length | 16.5m | Directive 96/53/EC |
| Road train length | 18.75m | Directive 96/53/EC |
| Width (standard) | 2.55m | Directive 96/53/EC |
| Width (refrigerated) | 2.60m | Directive 96/53/EC |
| Height | 4.0m | National standard |
| 5-axle semi GVW | 40t | Directive 96/53/EC |
| 6-axle road train GVW | 40t | Directive 96/53/EC |

## Danish National Provisions

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **EMS (Modulvogntog)** | 25.25m combinations allowed on approved routes; max 48t — check Vejdirektoratet approved route list |
| **Long combinations** | 25.25m requires prior route check via Vejdirektoratet's length-allowed road list |
| **Storebælt Bridge** | Posted height restriction 4.0m; weight restrictions for abnormal loads — contact Sund & Bælt |
| **Øresund Bridge** | Height 4.5m clearance; standard weight limits apply; check toll operator |
| **Oversize permit** | Dispensation required for vehicles exceeding standard limits — apply to Vejdirektoratet |

## Axle Loads

| Axle type | Limit | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Single non-driving | 10t | 96/53/EC |
| Single driving | 11.5t | 96/53/EC |
| Bogie (1.0–1.3m spacing) | 16t | 96/53/EC |
| Bogie driving (1.3–1.8m) | 18t | 96/53/EC |
| Triple axle (< 1.3m) | 21t | 96/53/EC |
