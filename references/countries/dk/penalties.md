## Overview

Denmark enforces EU transport regulations through Færdselsstyrelsen (Danish Road Directorate) and Rigspolitiet (national police). Fines are issued as administrative sanctions (administrative bøder) or fixed penalty notices. Serious violations are prosecuted as criminal offences under Færdselsloven. Roadside cash payment is NOT required — fines issued by notice payable within a set period.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations

| Violation | Fine (DKK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded (< 1h) | DKK 1,000–2,000 | Bekendtgørelse om kørsel med vejtransport |
| Daily driving time exceeded (1–3h) | DKK 2,000–5,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Daily driving time exceeded (> 3h) | DKK 5,000–10,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Insufficient rest (< 1h) | DKK 1,000–2,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Insufficient rest (1–3h) | DKK 2,000–5,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Insufficient weekly rest | DKK 3,000–8,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Break not taken | DKK 1,000–3,000 | Bekendtgørelse nr. 1386 |
| Tachograph manipulation | DKK 50,000+ + prosecution | Straffeloven |

## Overload Violations

| Violation | Fine (DKK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Axle overload ≤ 5% | DKK 2,000–4,000 | Færdselsloven §84 |
| Axle overload 5–10% | DKK 4,000–7,000 | Færdselsloven §84 |
| Axle overload 10–20% | DKK 7,000–15,000 | Færdselsloven §84 |
| Axle overload > 20% | DKK 15,000+ | Færdselsloven §84 |
| No permit for oversize load | DKK 3,000–10,000 | Færdselsloven |
