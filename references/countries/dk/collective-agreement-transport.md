## Overview

Denmark has a sector-wide collective agreement for road goods transport: the **Godsoverenskomsten** (Freight Agreement), negotiated between **Dansk Transport og Logistik (DTL)** on the employer side and **3F Transport** (section of the general trade union Fagligt Fælles Forbund — 3F) on the union side.

Danish collective bargaining follows the **Flexicurity model** — relatively easy hiring/firing (flexibility) combined with strong income protection (security via unemployment insurance) and mandatory collective agreement coverage in most sectors.

> **Note:** Specific current wage rates are published in the DTL Lønoversigt — confirmed as updated **23 February 2026** (verified via dtl.eu). Wage categories distinguish: under 9 months tenure, over 9 months tenure, and skilled workers with relevant vocational qualifications (faglig uddannelse). Exact amounts require direct access to the Lønoversigt document at dtl.eu or via 3F.

> Also note: DTL's website has moved from dtl.dk to **dtl.eu** — update bookmarks accordingly.

## Framework

- **Godsoverenskomsten** covers professional goods transport by road — truck drivers of all categories
- Renegotiated every **2 years** as part of the coordinated Danish bargaining round (forligsrunder)
- The agreement applies to DTL member companies; in practice, the terms spread across the industry as a benchmark

### Key Topic Areas Covered

| Topic | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Minimum wages (mindstebetalingssatser) | Hourly rates by driver category and vehicle type |
| Diæter (subsistence allowances) | Per diem allowances when away from home |
| Weekend and holiday premiums | OB-tillæg (unsocial hours premium) |
| Overtime | Premium rates above standard hours |
| Working time | 37 hours/week standard; overtime above this |
| Pension | Bidrag til pensionsordning |
| Sick pay | Employer obligation; state sygepenge (dagpenge) supplement |

### OB-Tillæg (Unsocial Hours Premium)

Danish collective agreements typically include **OB-tillæg** for work outside standard hours (evenings, nights, weekends, public holidays). The specific percentage premiums are set in the Godsoverenskomsten.

### Diæter (Subsistence Allowances)

When drivers are away from their home base overnight, the Godsoverenskomsten sets allowances for meals and accommodation. These interact with the Danish Skat (tax authority) rules on tax-free allowances.
