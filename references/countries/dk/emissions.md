## Overview

Denmark applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The Færdselsstyrelsen (Danish Road Directorate) is the main transport authority. Denmark has no general national HGV LEZ but Copenhagen and other major cities have emission-related access planning. Denmark is among the EU leaders in green transport transition.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Danish national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

---

## Copenhagen Area Emission Restrictions

Copenhagen does not yet operate a formal HGV LEZ, but environmental zones for lorries delivering to the inner city are under discussion. Some delivery zones require booking/permits. Check https://www.kk.dk/ for current inner-city access rules.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **Green Transport Pool** (Grøn transportpulje): Danish state subsidies for ZEV trucks via Energistyrelsen
- Tax rebates (registreringsafgift reduction) for ZEV commercial vehicles
- **Superpuljen**: grants for reducing road transport CO2
