## Overview

Denmark issues Community Licences through Færdselsstyrelsen (Danish Road Directorate). Danish operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam (erhvervsmæssig kompetencebevis) is administered by approved Danish examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Danish specifics |
|-------------|----------|------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Danish place of business | CVR registration (Central Business Register) |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal record; transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or guarantee |
| Professional competence | Kompetencebevis | Via approved examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Færdselsstyrelsen**: issues Community Licences and domestic road haulier licences.

Website: https://www.fdst.dk/

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Denmark within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- RUT registration required for posting (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.

| Violation | Penalty (DKK) | Legal basis |
