## Overview

Romania implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Romania's key intermodal asset is the Port of Constanța — the largest Black Sea port and Europe's 4th largest port overall — providing connections to Ukraine, Turkey, Georgia, and beyond. The Danube river (Romania has the longest Danube coastline of any EU country) enables inland waterway combined transport.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Constanța port | Black Sea | Europe's gateway to Black Sea/Caspian trade |
| Bucharest (Mogoșoaia) | Bucharest | Rail intermodal hub |
| Cluj-Napoca | Transylvania | Rail to Hungary/Western Europe |
| Galați / Brăila | Danube | River port complex |

**Danube waterway**: Romania has the Danube as a major transport artery. Barge services to Vienna, Budapest, or Rhine-Danube canal qualify as combined transport non-road legs.

---

## Documentation Required

- Port/rail/river terminal confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
