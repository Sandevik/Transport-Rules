# Romania — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**
- https://www.arr.ro/ — ARR: Romanian Road Authority
- https://www.cnadnr.ro/ — CNAIR: National Road Infrastructure Company

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Romania applies EU standard weights and dimensions under OUG 195/2002 (Road Traffic Code) and implementing regulations. No special large vehicle programme. The RO-E-VINIETA electronic vignette and DRPCIV (Driver Licence and Vehicle Registration) system manage vehicle access.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Road Network Note

Romania has a developing motorway network; many national (DN) roads have lower axle weight capacities. Check route-specific limits, especially in mountainous regions (Transylvania, Carpathians).

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits (autorizație specială de transport) issued by CNAIR (Compania Națională de Administrare a Infrastructurii Rutiere) for national roads.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.
