## Overview

Romania applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **ARR** (Autoritatea Rutieră Română — Romanian Road Authority) and the **Poliția Rutieră** (Traffic Police). Romania's road network quality varies and enforcement intensity is increasing under EU compliance pressure.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.

## Tolls

Romania operates the **Rovinieta** vignette system for all vehicles on national roads and the **TollRo** distance-based system for HGVs on motorways.

See references/countries/ro/tolls.md for details.
