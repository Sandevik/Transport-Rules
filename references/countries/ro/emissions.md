## Overview

Romania applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The ARR (Autoritatea Rutieră Română) coordinates road transport policy, while the MMAP (Ministerul Mediului, Apelor și Pădurilor) handles environmental compliance. Romania has no national LEZ programme for HGVs as of 2026. Bucharest has plans for emission-based access restrictions.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Romanian national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- EU RRF-funded programmes via PNRR for fleet electrification
- Government grants for ZEV commercial vehicles through MMAP / AFM (Environmental Fund Administration)
