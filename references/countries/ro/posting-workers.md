## Overview

Romania implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through Legea nr. 16/2017 privind detașarea salariaților. Foreign carriers must notify via the Inspecția Muncii (Labour Inspectorate) online portal before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. Enforcement is by Inspecția Muncii.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Romania | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in RO + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Romania only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification

Submit via Inspecția Muncii portal: https://www.inspectmun.ro/ (online posting notification system).

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Salariul minim brut pe țară (national minimum wage)**: approximately RON 3,700/month (2025; updated by Government Decision)
- **Sector collective agreements**: road transport sector agreements may set higher rates
- Posted drivers must receive at least the Romanian national minimum wage

---

## Driver Documentation

- Inspecția Muncii notification proof
- Employment contract showing Romanian-level pay
- A1 certificate
- CMR waybill

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Inspecția Muncii | Labour Inspectorate | https://www.inspectmun.ro/ |
| Poliția Rutieră | Traffic police | — |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — security deposits demanded.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (RON) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | RON 3,000–RON 30,000 per worker | Legea nr. 16/2017 |
| Failure to notify posting | RON 1,000–RON 10,000 | Legea nr. 16/2017 |
