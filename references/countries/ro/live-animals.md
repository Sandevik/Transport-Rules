## Overview

Romania applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is ANSVSA (Autoritatea Națională Sanitară Veterinară și pentru Siguranța Alimentelor / National Sanitary Veterinary and Food Safety Authority). Romania is a major producer of cattle, pigs, and sheep and an important exporter to other EU countries and third countries.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | DSVSA (county veterinary directorate) |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | DSVSA |

---

## Key BIPs

- **Constanța port**: BIP for third-country livestock imports via Black Sea
- Land BIPs at borders with non-EU neighbours (Moldova, Ukraine, Serbia)

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Certificat sanitar-veterinar (veterinary health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (RON) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | RON 2,000–RON 20,000 | Legea sanitar-veterinară |
| Welfare violations | RON 2,000–RON 20,000 | Legea privind protecția animalelor |
| Failure to maintain journey log | RON 500–RON 5,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
