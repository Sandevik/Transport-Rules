# Romania — Road Tolls (Rovinieta + TollRo)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.cnair.ro/ — CNAIR: National motorway company
- https://www.roviniete.ro/ — Rovinieta: vignette purchase portal

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Romania has a **dual toll system**:
1. **Rovinieta** — time-based vignette required for all vehicles using national roads
2. **TollRo** — distance-based charge for HGVs >3.5t on motorways and expressways (newer system, expanding)

---

## Rovinieta (Time-Based Vignette)

Required for all vehicles (including foreign) using Romanian national roads.

| Duration | Approximate cost (cars/HGVs vary) |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| 7 days | RON 3–28 depending on vehicle class |
| 30 days | RON 7–57 |
| 1 year | RON 28–390 |

HGV categories have higher rates. Purchase at: https://www.roviniete.ro/ or border points.

---

## TollRo (Distance-Based for HGVs)

Romania is progressively implementing distance-based tolling for HGVs (>3.5t) on motorways. This supplements the Rovinieta.

OBU-based GNSS system — register at CNAIR: https://www.cnair.ro/
