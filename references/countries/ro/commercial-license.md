## Overview

Romania implements EU operator licensing regulation (EC) 1071/2009 through the Autoritatea Rutieră Română (ARR — Romanian Road Authority). The Community Licence (Licenţă comunitară) is required for international goods transport. Romania is a major transit country connecting Central Europe with the Balkans and Black Sea region; ARR processes a high volume of international freight licences.

## Operator Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Establishment | Effective and stable establishment in Romania | EC 1071/2009 Art. 3 |
| Good repute | No serious criminal/transport convictions | EC 1071/2009 Art. 6 |
| Financial standing | €9,000 first vehicle; €5,000 each additional | EC 1071/2009 Art. 7 |
| Professional competence | Transport Manager with CPC exam | EC 1071/2009 Art. 8 |
| Transport Manager exam | Via ARR or accredited institutions | OG 27/2011 |

## Community Licence (Licenţă comunitară)

- Required for: international goods transport >3.5t GVW in EU/EEA
- Issued by: ARR (Autoritatea Rutieră Română)
- Validity: 5 years (renewable)
- Application: [ARR online portal](https://www.arr.ro)
- Certified copies carried on each vehicle

## National Licence (Licenţă de transport)

- Required for: domestic goods transport >3.5t GVW
- Issued by: ARR
- Validity: 5 years (renewable)

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (RON) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Operating without licence | RON 5,000–10,000 | OG 27/2011 Art. 53 |
| No certified copy in vehicle | RON 500–1,500 | OG 27/2011 |
| Cabotage violation | RON 3,000–6,000 | OG 27/2011 |
| No Transport Manager CPC | RON 2,000–5,000 | OG 27/2011 |
