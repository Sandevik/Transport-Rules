## Overview

Estonia applies seasonal spring weight restrictions (**kevadised teepiirangud** — spring road restrictions, also called **teekaalupiirangud** — road weight restrictions) on secondary and local roads during the spring thaw period. These are imposed when frost-susceptible road subbase layers thaw and lose bearing capacity.

The restrictions are published and managed by **Transpordiamet** (Estonian Transport Administration), which is responsible for national roads (riigiteed). County and municipal roads may have additional restrictions imposed by local authorities.

---

## Timing

| Region | Typical restriction period | Notes |
|--------|--------------------------|-------|
| Northern and eastern Estonia | March–April | Longer restriction period; more frost-susceptible soils |
| Western Estonia and islands | February–March | Earlier thaw due to maritime climate |
| Southern Estonia | March–May in upland areas | Variable depending on elevation and soil type |

Restrictions are announced annually and can be checked via Transpordiamet's road information system and the official road map portal.

---

## Weight Reductions

During restriction periods, standard GVW and axle load limits on affected roads are reduced. Typical reduction levels:

| Road category | Typical restriction |
|---|---|
| Secondary roads (korgema klassiga maanteed) | Reduced axle loads (typically 5–8 t per axle vs normal 10–11.5 t) |
| Local and forest roads | More severe reductions; sometimes total prohibitions on heavy vehicles |
| National main roads (riiklikud põhimaanteed) | Generally not restricted; occasional partial restrictions on weaker sections |

Specific weight limits for each restricted road are published by Transpordiamet. Drivers should consult the restriction map before departing.

---

## Checking Restrictions

- **Transpordiamet website:** https://www.transpordiamet.ee/ — restriction announcements
- **Road information system:** current restrictions are published as press releases and via the integrated road information portal
- On-site signage: restricted roads are signed with weight restriction signs (round red-bordered signs with tonnage limits)

---

## Penalties

Violations of spring weight restrictions are treated as standard overload violations under Estonian law. Penalties are assessed per the normal overload fee schedule. See `references/countries/ee/penalties.md` if available, or the national road traffic legislation.
