## Overview

Estonia applies EU Regulation 1071/2009 on access to the occupation of road transport operator and EU Regulation 1072/2009 on road haulage market access (Community licence). All commercial haulage operators must meet good repute, financial standing, and professional competence requirements. EU Community Licences are directly valid for operations in Estonia.

---

## Operator Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Establishment | Effective establishment in Estonia | Art. 3 Reg. 1071/2009 |
| Good repute | No disqualifying convictions | Art. 6 Reg. 1071/2009 |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Art. 7 Reg. 1071/2009 |
| Professional competence | CPC exam or qualified transport manager | Art. 8 Reg. 1071/2009 |

---

## Community Licence

EU Community Licences are issued by the national competent authority and are valid for all EU/EEA operations. Certified copies must be carried in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage (EU Regulation 1072/2009)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Max operations | 3 cabotage operations within 7 days of an international delivery |
| Cooling-off period | Vehicle must leave Estonia for ≥4 days before next cabotage |

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Operating without Community licence | Fine + prohibition | Regulation 1072/2009 Art. 23 |
| Cabotage violation | Fine | Regulation 1072/2009; national law |
| False declarations | Licence revocation | Regulation 1071/2009 |
