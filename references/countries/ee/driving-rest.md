## Overview

Estonia applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **Transpordiamet** (Estonian Transport Administration) and the Police and Border Guard Board. Estonia has no significant deviations from EU rules and no motorway distance-based toll (no general HGV toll as of 2026).

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Estonia fully applies all Mobility Package 2022 requirements.

## Tolls

Estonia has no general motorway toll for HGVs as of 2026. No vignette or distance-based charge applies.
