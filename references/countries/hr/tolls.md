# Croatia — Road Tolls (HAC)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.hac.hr/en/toll — HAC: Croatian motorway toll
- https://www.hac.hr/en/toll-rates/eets — HAC: EETS compatibility

**Last verified: 2026-05-30**

---

## Overview

Croatia operates **HAC** (Hrvatske autoceste — Croatian Motorways) toll plazas on its motorway network. Tolls apply to all vehicles on Croatian motorways (autoceste), with HGVs charged at higher rates based on axle count and vehicle class.

---

## Rate Structure

HGV rates categorized by:
- Number of axles
- Vehicle height/class

Rates are charged per section at toll plazas. Croatia's motorways are well-developed since the Adriatic expansion.

---

## Payment

- **ETC (Electronic Toll Collection):** Transponder-based automatic payment — **ENC/EETS compatible**
- **Credit/debit card** at booths
- **Cash** at staffed booths

**EETS** is available on Croatian motorways — use existing transponder from other European countries.
