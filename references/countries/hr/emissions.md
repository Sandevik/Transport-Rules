## Overview

Croatia is an EU member state and applies EU emissions standards directly. EU Regulation 2024/1610 sets mandatory CO2 reduction targets for new heavy-duty vehicles; EU Regulation 2024/1257 establishes Euro VII standards for exhaust emissions. National implementation follows the EU baseline with no significant deviations for commercial vehicles.

---

## Euro VII (Regulation 2024/1257)

Euro VII sets stricter limits for NOx, PM, and PN emissions, and introduces durability and on-road conformity requirements. Application dates:
- Heavy trucks/buses: 1 July 2027 (new type approvals)
- Vans: 1 July 2028 (new type approvals)
