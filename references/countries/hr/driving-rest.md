## Overview

Croatia applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **Policija** (Traffic Police) and the **Inspektorat** under the Ministry of Transport. Croatia joined the EU in 2013 and is part of the Adriatic freight corridor.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
