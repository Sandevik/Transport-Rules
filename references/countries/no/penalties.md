## Overview

Norway, as an EEA member, applies EU Regulation 561/2006 directly. Enforcement is carried out by Statens vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration) transport inspectors and Politiet (police). Administrative sanctions (overtredelsesgebyr) are issued for violations; serious offences may be prosecuted under Vegtrafikkloven. Foreign drivers must note that roadside cash payment of fines is possible but not mandatory — formal notices are issued with payment deadline.

**AETR note:** AETR applies for journeys through non-EU/EEA countries (e.g. Russia, Turkey); for purely EEA-territory journeys, EU 561/2006 applies.

## Driving Time and Rest Violations

| Violation | Fine (NOK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Daily driving time exceeded ≤ 1h | NOK 1,500–3,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31; FOR-2007-10-15-1219 |
| Daily driving time exceeded 1–3h | NOK 3,000–8,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Daily driving time exceeded > 3h | NOK 8,000–20,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Insufficient daily rest ≤ 1h | NOK 1,500–3,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Insufficient daily rest > 2h | NOK 5,000–15,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Insufficient weekly rest | NOK 5,000–20,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Break not taken | NOK 1,500–5,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Tachograph not used | NOK 3,000–8,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31b |
| Tachograph manipulation | NOK 20,000+ + prosecution | Straffeloven |
| Operator — systematic violations | Up to NOK 100,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31b |

## Overload

| Violation | Fee (NOK) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Overload < 5% | NOK 1,000–3,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Overload 5–10% | NOK 3,000–7,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
| Overload > 10% | NOK 7,000–25,000 | Vegtrafikkloven §31 |
