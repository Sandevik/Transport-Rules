## Overview

Norway has sector-wide collective agreements for road transport workers. The main agreement for goods transport drivers is negotiated between **NHO Logistikk og Transport** (formerly ATL — Arbeidsgiverforeningen for Transport og Logistikk) on the employer side and **Yrkestrafikkforbundet (YTF)** (affiliated with LO) on the union side.

---

## Framework

Norwegian collective agreements in road transport follow the general Norwegian model:
- **Riksavtale** (national framework agreement): sets minimum conditions applicable to all covered employers
- Typically renegotiated every **2 years** (main revision) with annual wage adjustments in between
- Coupled with the **Allmenngjøringslov** (General Application Act) — minimum wage rates in the agreement can be declared generally applicable to all workers in the sector, including posted workers from other countries

### Key Topic Areas Covered

| Topic | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Minimum wages | Hourly and monthly scales by driver category |
| Overnight allowances (nattillegg) | When driver is away from home overnight |
| Night and weekend premiums | Unsocial hours supplements |
| Overtime rates | Premium rates above standard hours |
| Working time reference period | Set in agreement |
| Pension | Obligatorisk tjenestepensjon (OTP) — mandatory occupational pension |
| Sick pay | Employer pays during first 16 calendar days; NAV from day 17 |

### Allmenngjøring — Posting Workers Minimum Wages

Under the **Allmenngjøringsloven**, the minimum wage rates from the road transport collective agreement are declared generally applicable. This means all drivers working in Norway — including those posted from other EU/EEA countries — must receive at least these minimum rates. Relevant for Swedish, Polish, Lithuanian, and other operators operating in Norway.

**Confirmed allmenngjorte minimum rates for godstransport på vei (road goods transport)** — source: Arbeidstilsynet.no, verified 2026-06-02:

| Vehicle category | Minimum hourly rate | Effective from |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicles > 2.5 tonnes total weight | **NOK 229.00/hour** | 15 June 2025 |

Additional confirmed provisions:
- **Overtime supplement**: minimum **40%** above the hourly rate for hours beyond standard working time (per Arbeidsmiljøloven)
- **Multi-day tours**: no overnight period (døgn) on a multi-day tour may be paid for less than **7.5 hours** of work

Previous rates: NOK 207.00 (15 June 2023–31 October 2024), NOK 222.00 (briefly, 2025 transition).

The Arbeidstilsynet publishes current rates at: https://www.arbeidstilsynet.no/arbeidsforhold/lonn/minstelonn/

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| Arbeidstilsynet | Minimum wage enforcement (allmenngjøring) |
| YTF / LO | Union enforcement via civil claims |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paying below generally applicable minimum wage | Back pay; Arbeidstilsynet enforcement | Allmenngjøringsloven |
| Not applying agreement to covered employees | Civil liability; union claim | Arbeidsmiljøloven; collective agreement |
