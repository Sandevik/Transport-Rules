# Norway — Commercial Operator Licensing

**EEA Note:** Norway applies the operator licensing framework via the EEA Agreement (equivalent to EU 1071/2009 + 1072/2009).

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/commercial-licensing.md — Regulation (EC) 1071/2009 + 1072/2009, applied via EEA Agreement]

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

## Overview

Norway issues Community Licences (equivalent) and national transport licences through **Statens vegvesen** (Norwegian Public Roads Administration). Norwegian operators must meet the same four requirements as EU operators (establishment, good repute, financial standing, professional competence) under the EEA equivalent framework.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Norwegian specifics |
|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Norwegian place of business | Brønnøysundregistrene registration |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal record; transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | Kompetansebevis | Via approved Norwegian examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Statens vegvesen**: issues transport permits and Community Licence equivalents for Norwegian operators.

Website: https://www.vegvesen.no/

---

## Cabotage Rules (EEA equivalent)

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Norway within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- Arbeidstilsynet registration required for posting (see posting-workers.md)
- Norwegian allmenngjøring wages apply to cabotage drivers

---

## International Note

Norwegian Community Licence equivalents are valid for operations within EEA countries. For operations in non-EEA countries, bilateral permits may be required.
