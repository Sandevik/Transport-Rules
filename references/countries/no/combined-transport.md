## Overview

Norway applies the combined transport equivalent framework via the EEA Agreement. Norway's geography — a long mountainous country — makes rail and sea transport particularly valuable. CargoNet (owned by Vy/Bane NOR) operates the Norwegian rail freight network. Norway has no general HGV weekend driving bans.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes** (same as EU baseline; under EEA equivalent).

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Oslo (Alnabru) | Oslo | Norway's largest intermodal terminal; major Nordic hub |
| Stavanger/Ganddal | Stavanger area | Southwest Norway |
| Bergen (Nygårdstangen) | Bergen | West coast hub |
| Trondheim | Trondheim | Central Norway; rail to Sweden |
| Narvik | Far North | Iron ore rail; Arctic links |

**Oslo–Gothenburg railway**: CargoNet operates overnight services connecting Norway to Sweden and Denmark.

**Ferry routes**: Norway–Denmark (Oslo–Copenhagen, Kristiansand–Hirtshals), Norway–Germany (Bergen–Immingham/Stavanger–Esbjerg) qualify as maritime legs.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail or ferry terminal booking/confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)

---

## Deviations from EEA Baseline

No significant deviations from Directive 92/106/EEC as applied via EEA Agreement.
