# Norway — Oversize and Overweight Loads (Unormale transporter / Dispensasjonstransport)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — exceptional transport permits are a national matter.

**Sources:**
- https://www.vegvesen.no/en/vehicles/professional-transport/list-of-roads-and-dimensions/ — Statens vegvesen: Roads and dimensions

---

## Overview

Vehicles or loads exceeding Norway's standard dimension and weight limits require a **transport dispensation (dispensasjonssøknad)** from Statens vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration). Norway's standard limits for general roads are based on EU Directive 96/53/EC. Modular combinations (25.25m / 60t) on the approved network do not require special permits; loads beyond those limits require individual dispensation.

---

## Standard Thresholds (Limits Requiring No Permit on Approved Network)

| Parameter | Standard limit | Modular (approved network) |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Max GVW | 50–56t (5–6 axle semi) | 60t |
| Max length | 18.75m (road train) | 25.25m |
| Max width | 2.55m | 2.55m |
| Max height | 4.0m | 4.0m |

Loads exceeding these limits require a dispensation permit.

---

## When a Dispensation Permit Is Required

| Condition | Permit needed |
|-----------|--------------|
| Width > 2.55 m | Yes |
| Height > 4.0 m | Yes |
| Length > 18.75 m (non-modular) | Yes |
| GVW exceeding axle/bridge load limits | Yes |
| Any combination of the above | Yes |

---

## Application Process

Applications are submitted to **Statens vegvesen** through the online permit portal:
- **Portal:** https://www.vegvesen.no (transport permits section)
- Submit: vehicle details, load description, route, timing
- Processing time: varies — simple standard routes may be processed quickly; complex loads with bridge assessments may take weeks
- Permits are route-specific and time-limited

---

## Bridge Load Limits

Norway has extensive fjord-crossing infrastructure with bridge-specific load limits. Key considerations:

| Infrastructure type | Consideration |
|--------------------|---------------|
| Fjord bridges | Weight and axle load limits specified per bridge; check Statens vegvesen route database |
| Mountain pass roads | Seasonal closures and weight restrictions may apply |
| Ferries (fjord crossings) | Ferry operator vehicle weight limits apply; check NSB/Fjord1/Norled capacity |

For abnormal loads, a **bridge assessment** (brukontroll) is required from Statens vegvesen before the permit is issued.

---

## Escort Requirements

| Load dimension | Escort requirement |
|----------------|--------------------|
| Width > 3.5 m or length > 25 m | Rear escort vehicle required |
| Width > 5.0 m | Front and rear escort vehicles required |
| Large loads through tunnels or on bridges | Police escort may be required |
| Complex urban routing | Police notification and possible police escort |

---

## Time and Speed Restrictions

- Special transport generally not permitted during **darkness** unless specifically authorised
- Speed limits specified in permits — typically 50–80 km/h depending on load
- Prohibition on travel during high-traffic periods may apply (peak hours, public holidays)

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (NOK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating oversize without dispensation | NOK 5,000–50,000 | Vegtrafikkloven |
| Exceeding permit conditions | Fine | Vegtrafikkloven |
| Causing bridge/road damage | Civil damages liability | Vegtrafikkloven / Civil law |
| Overloading | Per-tonne fine + possible prohibition | Vegtrafikkloven |
