# Norway — Cabotage Rules

**EU Baseline:** Norway is an EEA member and applies EU Regulation 1072/2009 (goods) and 1073/2009 (passengers) via the EEA Agreement, with certain national specifics.

**Sources:**
- https://www.vegvesen.no/en/vehicles/professional-transport/international-transport-and-cabotage-by-road/ — Statens vegvesen: International transport and cabotage
- https://www.vegvesen.no/en/vehicles/professional-transport/international-transport-and-cabotage-by-road/international-transport-cabotage-and-penalties/ — Statens vegvesen: Cabotage and penalties

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Norway, as an EEA member, applies EU cabotage rules for road freight under EU Regulation 1072/2009 (incorporated into the EEA Agreement). EEA-licensed carriers may perform up to **3 cabotage operations in Norway within 7 days** after completing an incoming international delivery. A **4-day cooling-off period** applies before re-entry. Non-EEA operators have no cabotage rights.

---

## EEA Operator Cabotage Rights

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Maximum cabotage operations | **3 domestic operations** using the same vehicle |
| Time window | **Within 7 days** of the international unloading in Norway |
| Cooling-off period | **4 days** before the same vehicle may return to cabotage in Norway |
| Licence required | EU/EEA Community Licence (valid for vehicles ≥ 2,500 kg MAM as of 1 November 2022) |
| International delivery | Required before any cabotage — cannot start with cabotage |

---

## Document Requirements

Drivers must carry and present on request:

1. Community Licence (certified copy) or equivalent EEA transport authorisation
2. Driver attestation (for non-EEA national drivers employed by EEA operators)
3. Consignment note (CMR waybill or equivalent) for each cabotage operation
4. Proof of the incoming international delivery (CMR for the delivery into Norway)

---

## Non-EEA Operators

Non-EEA operators (including post-Brexit UK operators) have **no cabotage rights** in Norway unless:
- A bilateral agreement between Norway and the operator's country permits it, **or**
- An **ECMT multilateral permit** is held — but ECMT permits explicitly **prohibit cabotage**

**UK operators post-Brexit:** UK operators need a UK/Norway bilateral permit (or ECMT) for Norwegian operations and have **no cabotage rights** — the UK-EU TCA does not apply to Norway.

---

## Passenger Transport Cabotage

| Type | Rule |
|------|------|
| Occasional services | Maximum 20 consecutive days; max 30 days per calendar year |
| Regular services | Only domestic segments of international routes allowed |

---

## Penalties (Effective 1 March 2024)

| Violation | Penalty (NOK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Missing Community Licence / operator licence document | NOK 60,000 | Yrkestransportloven |
| Missing driver attestation | NOK 60,000 | Yrkestransportloven |
| Undocumented incoming international delivery | Cabotage deemed unlicensed | Yrkestransportloven |
| Exceeding 3 cabotage operations | Fine + possible vehicle immobilisation | Yrkestransportloven |
| Operations after 7-day window | Fine + possible vehicle immobilisation | Yrkestransportloven |
| Incomplete consignment documentation | Administrative fine | Yrkestransportloven |

Norway enforces cabotage at roadside checkpoints operated by Statens vegvesen. Vehicle immobilisation and security deposit demands are used for serious violations.
