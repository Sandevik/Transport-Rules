## Overview

Norway implements Directive 2020/1057/EU equivalent through the Arbeidsmiljøloven (Working Environment Act) and the Allmenngjøringsloven (Act on General Application of Collective Agreements). Foreign carriers deploying drivers for cabotage or cross-trade in Norway must register with **Arbeidstilsynet**. The Norwegian allmenngjøring (general application) system extends collective agreement minimum wages to all workers in Norway, including posted drivers.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply (EEA equivalent)

| Operation | Registration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Norway | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in NO + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Norway only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Registration — Arbeidstilsynet

- Register before commencing work in Norway: https://www.arbeidstilsynet.no/
- Foreign employers must have a representative in Norway (can be the forwarding agent/customer)

---

## Minimum Pay — Allmenngjøring

Norway's **allmenngjøring** (general application of collective agreements) system is critical:
- **Tariff for road transport**: applicable collective agreement minimum wage rates are set by the Norwegian Confederation of Trade Unions (LO) and the Confederation of Norwegian Enterprise (NHO)
- The **minimum tariff rate for professional drivers** is typically significantly higher than Norwegian minimum wage levels
- Current rates: check Arbeidstilsynet website for the latest allmenngjørte tariffer for transport

Norwegian minimum pay rates are among the highest in Europe. Foreign carriers performing cabotage must pay these rates.

---

## Driver Documentation

- Arbeidstilsynet registration confirmation
- Employment contract showing Norwegian allmenngjøring-compliant wages
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (NOK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay allmenngjøring wages | NOK 10,000–NOK 200,000 | Allmenngjøringsloven |
| Failure to register with Arbeidstilsynet | NOK 5,000–NOK 50,000 | Arbeidsmiljøloven |
