## Overview

Norway is an EEA member and implements EU Regulation 561/2006 via the EEA Agreement. Rules are identical to the EU baseline. Enforcement is carried out by **Statens vegvesen** (Norwegian Public Roads Administration) and the police. Violations are punishable regardless of where the offence was committed or the driver's nationality.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules. Norway fully applies all driving time, break, and rest period requirements identical to EU member states.
