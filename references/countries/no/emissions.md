# Norway — Emissions Standards

**EEA Note:** Norway is not an EU member but applies EU regulations via the EEA Agreement. EU emission standards apply in Norway as EEA law.

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/emissions.md — Regulation (EU) 2024/1610 + Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 (Euro VII), applied via EEA Agreement]

**Sources:**
- https://www.vegvesen.no/ — Statens vegvesen

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Norway applies EU emission standards through the EEA Agreement (not as EU law per se). Statens vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration) enforces vehicle compliance. Norway has strong national ZEV incentives — among the strongest in the world for heavy trucks. Oslo has implemented a LEZ (lavutslippssone) for certain commercial vehicles.

---

## Euro VII Application

Norway transposes EU Euro VII equivalent through the EEA Agreement. Expected timeline aligns with EU:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026** (or shortly after EEA transposition)
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

---

## Oslo LEZ (Lavutslippssone)

Oslo has established a low emission zone (lavutslippssone) affecting heavy vehicles:
- Applies to buses and certain commercial/delivery vehicles in central Oslo
- HGV restrictions being expanded; check https://www.oslo.kommune.no/ for current rules
- Euro VI minimum required for HGVs operating in the restricted zone

---

## National ZEV Incentives — Enova

Norway offers world-leading ZEV incentives for heavy commercial vehicles through **Enova** (state enterprise for energy efficiency):
- **Enova ZEV truck grant**: up to NOK 600,000+ per zero-emission HGV (battery electric or hydrogen fuel cell)
- **Charging/refuelling infrastructure grants**: for depot charging and public HGV charging stations
- **Fleet transition support**: for operators converting to ZEV fleets

Apply at: https://www.enova.no/

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU emission standards as applied via EEA Agreement. Norway adds strong national ZEV incentives and the Oslo LEZ.
