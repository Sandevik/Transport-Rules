## Overview

Norway applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 equivalent through the EEA Agreement. The competent authority is **Mattilsynet** (Norwegian Food Safety Authority). Norway is a significant exporter of farmed salmon (by sea — not land transport) and has a domestic livestock sector (sheep, cattle). Mattilsynet enforces animal welfare rules strictly.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | Mattilsynet |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | Mattilsynet |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Veterinary health certificate
- TRACES movement document for EU/EEA movements

---

## Deviations from Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as applied via EEA Agreement.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (NOK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | NOK 5,000–NOK 100,000 | Dyrevelferdsloven |
| Welfare violations | NOK 5,000–NOK 100,000 | Dyrevelferdsloven |
| Failure to maintain journey log | NOK 2,000–NOK 20,000 | VO (EF) 1/2005 (EEA-implemented) |
