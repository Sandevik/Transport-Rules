# Norway — Road Tolls (AutoPASS)

**EU Baseline:** Not applicable — toll systems are national.

**Sources:**
- https://www.autopass.no/en/ — AutoPASS: Norwegian toll collection system
- https://www.autopass.no/en/user/foreign-vehicles-2/ — AutoPASS: Information for foreign vehicles

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

Norway operates the **AutoPASS** electronic toll collection system. All toll stations in Norway are automated. Norway has no flat motorway vignette — tolls are collected at individual toll stations (bomstasjoner) and toll rings (bompengering) around cities. HGVs (>3,500 kg) must use a transponder (AutoPASS tag) and face mandatory toll payment obligations.

---

## HGV Obligations

- Vehicles **heavier than 3,500 kg** must have a toll tag (transponder) — it is **compulsory**, not optional
- Failure to have a tag results in higher charges and potential fines
- Foreign HGV operators must register before or upon entering Norway

---

## Registration for Foreign Operators

**Option 1 — User agreement with toll service provider:**
- Register online with an authorized AutoPASS toll service provider
- Receive a toll tag (transponder) by post
- **20% discount** on each toll transaction
- Recommended for frequent Norway operations

**Option 2 — Epass24:**
- Register vehicle at Epass24 (https://www.epass24.com/)
- Can drive without a physical tag
- No discount applies

**Without registration:** Invoices are sent to the vehicle's registered keeper via national vehicle registry lookup. Lookup fees apply (e.g. €5.10 for German-registered vehicles, €14.00 for Austrian-registered vehicles).

---

## Toll Locations and Rate Structure

AutoPASS collects tolls at individual toll stations (bomstasjoner) — there is **no universal per-km rate**. Rates vary by location, vehicle class, and whether a transponder is used.

**With AutoPASS transponder (tag):** 20% discount on each transaction.
**Without transponder:** Invoice sent to registered keeper; additional lookup fee charged (e.g. €5.10 for German-registered vehicles, €14.00 for Austrian-registered).

### City toll rings (bompengering) — approximate HGV rates

| City | System | Approx. HGV rate per passage |
|------|--------|-------------------------------|
| Oslo | Oslo bomring | NOK 50–100 (varies by emission class + rush hour) |
| Bergen | Bergen bomring | NOK 40–80 |
| Stavanger area | Nord-Jæren | NOK 35–70 |
| Trondheim | Miljøpakke | NOK 35–70 |

City toll rings apply **environmental differentiation**: electric/zero-emission vehicles pay reduced or zero rate; diesel HGVs pay full rate or surcharge.

**Major infrastructure tolls:**
- Ryfast tunnel (Stavanger) — one of world's longest undersea tunnels; HGV rate ~NOK 200–350
- Various fjord crossings, mountain tunnels, and bridges throughout Norway

---

## Penalties

Non-payment or failure to have a required tag results in:
- Additional administrative fees on top of the toll amount
- Potential fines for persistent non-payment
