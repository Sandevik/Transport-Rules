## Overview

Norway has **mandatory winter tyre requirements** for all vehicles, with **stricter rules for heavy vehicles** (over 3,500 kg maximum authorised mass). The rules differentiate between southern Norway and the northern counties (Nordland, Troms, Finnmark), which have a longer mandatory period. Norway's mountain roads and fjord crossings make winter tyre and chain compliance a critical road safety matter. The rules are established in Forskrift om bruk av kjøretøy and enforced by Statens vegvesen (Norwegian Public Roads Administration) and the Police.

---

## Mandatory Winter Tyre Periods

### Heavy Vehicles (GVW > 3,500 kg) — Mandatory Period

| Region | Mandatory period |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Southern Norway** (all counties except Nordland, Troms, Finnmark) | **15 November – 31 March** |
| **Northern Norway** (Nordland, Troms, Finnmark) | **16 October – 30 April** |

Outside these periods, winter tyres remain required when actual road conditions (snow, ice, slush) necessitate them.

### All Vehicles — Tread Depth Minimum (Seasonal)

| Period | Minimum tread depth |
|--------|---------------------|
| 1 November – first Sunday after Easter (southern) | **3 mm** (all vehicles) |
| 16 October – 30 April (northern Norway) | **3 mm** (all vehicles) |
| Year-round | **1.6 mm** (summer tyres) |

### Heavy Vehicles (> 3,500 kg) — Additional Tread Requirement

| Period | Minimum tread depth (heavy vehicles) |
|--------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 November – first Sunday after Easter Monday | **5 mm** |
| 16 October – 30 April (northern) | **5 mm** |

---

## Tyre Type Requirements for Heavy Vehicles

Winter tyres for heavy vehicles (> 3,500 kg) must be marked:
- **M+S** (Mud and Snow) — accepted as winter tyre
- **3PMSF** (Three-Peak Mountain Snowflake, ❄) — accepted as winter tyre

**Drive axle and front steering axle** of vehicles over 3,500 kg must be fitted with **3PMSF (snowflake) tyres** — M+S alone is not sufficient for these positions on heavy vehicles.

All other axles may use M+S or 3PMSF tyres.

---

## Studded Tyres

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Permitted season | During the winter period applicable to the region (as above) |
| Heavy vehicle studs | Permitted under the same seasonal rules |
| Urban areas | Some Norwegian municipalities apply additional restrictions on studded tyres due to road dust concerns; check locally |

## Snow Chain Requirements

Heavy vehicles (> 3,500 kg) must **carry chains** when driving on snowy or icy roads during the winter period. During the tread depth requirement period (5 mm period), chains must be carried regardless of current conditions.

### Minimum Chain Quantity

| Configuration | Minimum chains required |
|--------------|------------------------|
| Single vehicle (>3,500 kg) | **3 chains**: 1 front, 2 drive wheels |
| Vehicle combination (both >3,500 kg) — twin wheels on drive axle | **7 chains** |
| Vehicle combination (both >3,500 kg) — single wheels on drive axle | **5 chains** |

Exception: Motorhomes between 3,500–7,500 kg are exempt from the chain carry requirement.

Chains must be made of metal/steel, suitable for the weather conditions, and capable of withstanding the operating stress.

### Mandatory Chain Use

On designated mountain roads and steep passes, Statens vegvesen operates **chain control checkpoints** (kjettingpåbudsstasjoner) where chain use is compulsory. Signage (blue/white sign with chain symbol) indicates mandatory chain sections.

---

## Enforcement

- **Statens vegvesen**: Roadside checks at fixed and mobile checkpoints; tread depth measurement; chain compliance checks at mountain passes.
- **Politi (Norwegian Police)**: General roadside enforcement.
- Operators found with non-compliant tyres may be prohibited from continuing the journey until compliant tyres are fitted or chains are attached.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine (NOK) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Heavy vehicle without mandatory winter tyres during period | **NOK 3,000–5,000** (driver fine) |
| Tread depth below minimum | NOK 2,000–4,000 |
| Failure to carry required chains during chain-carry period | NOK 1,500–3,000 |
| Driving on mandatory chain section without chains | NOK 3,000–5,000 |

Note: Fine levels are set by the Ministry of Justice and Police and the Road Traffic Act (Vegtrafikkloven). Levels are reviewed periodically. Foreign vehicles are subject to the same fines; a security deposit (depositum) may be required before continuing the journey.
