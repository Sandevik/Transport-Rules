## Overview

Hungary issues Community Licences through the Közlekedési Hatóság (Transport Authority) under the Ministry of Technology and Industry. Hungarian operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. The professional competence exam is administered by approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Hungarian specifics |
|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Hungarian place of business | Cégbejegyzés (company registration); Cegjegyzék |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal extract; transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or guarantee |
| Professional competence | Szakmai alkalmasság igazolása | Via approved centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Közlekedési Hatóság**: issues Community Licences (közösségi engedély) and domestic licences.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Hungary within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- Munkahivatal posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
