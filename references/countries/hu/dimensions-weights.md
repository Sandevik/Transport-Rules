## Overview

Hungary applies EU Directive 96/53/EC as an EU member state. Standard EU weight and dimension limits apply for normal transport. Hungary requires **route authorisation** for vehicles exceeding standard EU limits (oversize/overweight transport), administered through the Közlekedési Hatóság (Transport Authority). Hungary's toll system (HU-GO / e-VIGNETTE) applies to vehicles >3.5t on motorways, expressways, and main roads.

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline)

| Parameter | Limit | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Semi-trailer + tractor | 16.5m | 96/53/EC |
| Road train | 18.75m | 96/53/EC |
| Width (standard) | 2.55m | 96/53/EC |
| Width (refrigerated) | 2.60m | 96/53/EC |
| Height | 4.0m | National |
| 5-axle semi GVW | 40t | 96/53/EC |
| 6-axle road train | 40t | 96/53/EC |

## Oversize/Overweight Transport (Méretet Meghaladó Szállítás)

Vehicles exceeding standard limits require route authorisation from the Közlekedési Hatóság:

| Category | Limit exceeded | Permit type |
|---|---|---|
| Category I | Up to 30t, up to 3.0m wide | Simplified permit |
| Category II | Up to 60t, up to 4.0m wide | Standard permit + route survey |
| Category III | Over 60t or over 4.0m | Full special permit + escort |
| Category IV | Exceptional loads | Ministry-level authorisation + police escort |

- Application portal: [Közlekedési Hatóság](https://kh.gov.hu)
- Fee: Based on vehicle category and route length (HUF-denominated)
- Pilot vehicle (kísérő jármű): required for Category III and IV

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (HUF) | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Overload ≤ 5% | HUF 50,000–200,000 | KRESZ; Közúti közlekedési törvény |
| Overload 5–25% | HUF 200,000–500,000 | Közúti közlekedési törvény |
| Overload > 25% | HUF 500,000–1,500,000 | Közúti közlekedési törvény |
| No oversize permit | HUF 300,000–800,000 | Közúti közlekedési törvény |
