## Overview

Hungary applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is NÉBIH (Nemzeti Élelmiszerlánc-biztonsági Hivatal / National Food Chain Safety Office). Hungary is a significant pig and cattle producer and a transit country for livestock movements from Eastern Europe.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | NÉBIH regional office |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | NÉBIH regional office |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Állategészségügyi bizonyítvány (veterinary health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (HUF) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | HUF 50,000–HUF 500,000 | Állategészségügyi törvény |
| Welfare violations | HUF 50,000–HUF 500,000 | Állatvédelmi törvény |
| Failure to maintain journey log | HUF 20,000–HUF 100,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
