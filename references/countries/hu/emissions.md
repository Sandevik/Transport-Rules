## Overview

Hungary applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. Transport emission policy is coordinated by the Ministry of Technology and Industry. Hungary has no national LEZ programme for HGVs as of 2026. Budapest is evaluating emission-based access restrictions for delivery vehicles. The HU-GO electronic toll system applies to vehicles >3.5t on national roads.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Hungarian national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- EU RRF-funded programmes for fleet electrification
- Hungarian government grants for ZEV commercial vehicles through NKFIH or ministry programmes
