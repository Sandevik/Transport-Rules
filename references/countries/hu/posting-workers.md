**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Hungary implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the munkaerő-kölcsönzésről szóló törvény amendments. Foreign carriers must notify before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. Enforcement is by the labour inspectorate (Munkahivatal / National Labour Inspectorate system).

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Hungary | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in HU + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Hungary only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification

Submit via the Hungarian online portal through Munkahivatal: https://www.munka.hu/

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Minimálbér (minimum wage)**: approximately HUF 266,800/month (2025; updated annually)
- **Guaranteed minimum wage (garantált bérminimum)**: HUF 326,000/month for professional drivers with relevant qualifications
- Posted drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade must receive at least the Hungarian minimálbér (or guaranteed rate if applicable)

---

## Driver Documentation

- Notification confirmation
- Employment contract showing Hungarian-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (HUF) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | HUF 500,000–HUF 10,000,000 | Munka Törvénykönyve |
| Failure to notify posting | HUF 100,000–HUF 1,000,000 | Posting law |
