## Overview

Hungary implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. Hungary's position at the crossroads of Central Europe makes it an important combined transport country. The Danube river (Budapest is split by the Danube), the BILK logistics hub, and rail connections to Western Europe and the Balkans are the key infrastructure. Hungary has no general HGV weekend driving bans.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Infrastructure

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Budapest (BILK Logistics Hub) | Budapest | Major Central European intermodal hub |
| Budapest-Soroksár | Budapest south | Rail + road |
| Győr | Northwest | Austria/Germany corridor |
| Záhony | Northeast | Ukraine border; gauge-change facility |
| MAHART | Danube river | Barge transport Budapest–Rotterdam |

**Danube waterway**: Hungary's MAHART operates barge services on the Danube from Budapest to Vienna, Bratislava, and Rotterdam — qualifying as combined transport non-road legs.

---

## Documentation Required

- Terminal/barge confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
