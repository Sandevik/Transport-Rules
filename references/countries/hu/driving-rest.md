## Overview

Hungary applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without national deviations. Enforcement is by the **NKH** (Nemzeti Közlekedési Hatóság — National Transport Authority) and the police. Hungary is a major transit country for Balkan-Western Europe freight.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
