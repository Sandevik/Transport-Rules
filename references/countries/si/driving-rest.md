## Overview

Slovenia applies EU Regulation 561/2006. Enforcement is by the **Policija** (Traffic Police) and transport inspectors. Slovenia is on the main freight route between Central Europe and the Balkans/Adriatic.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
