## Overview

Slovenia applies EU Directive 96/53/EC on maximum vehicle dimensions and weights. No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC standard limits for international operations.

---

## EU Standard Maximum Dimensions

| Parameter | EU Standard Limit |
|---|---|
| Length (solo vehicle) | 12.00 m |
| Length (articulated) | 16.50 m |
| Length (road train) | 18.75 m |
| Width (standard) | 2.55 m |
| Width (refrigerated) | 2.60 m |
| Height | 4.00 m |

---

## EU Standard Maximum Weights

| Configuration | GVW Limit |
|---|---|
| 2-axle vehicle | 18 t |
| 3-axle articulated | 36 t |
| 4-axle articulated | 38 t |
| 5-axle articulated | 40 t |
| Alternative fuel bonus | +1 t or +2 t (zero-emission) |

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

None — EU Directive 96/53/EC standard limits apply without modification.
