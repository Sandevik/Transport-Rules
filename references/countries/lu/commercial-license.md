## Overview

Luxembourg issues Community Licences and domestic transport licences through the Administration des Transports. Luxembourg-based operators must meet the standard EU four requirements. Luxembourg's strategic location at the crossroads of France, Germany, and Belgium makes it home to many international road transport operators.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Luxembourg specifics |
|-------------|----------|----------------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Luxembourg place of business | Business registration with RCSL (Registre de Commerce) |
| Good repute | No serious criminal/transport offences | Checked via criminal record and transport register |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | CPC exam | Via INFPC or approved centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Administration des Transports** issues:
- EU Community Licences for international road haulage (>3.5t)
- National licences (autorisation de transport)

Application: https://at.public.lu/fr/transports-terrestres/transport-marchandises/acces-profession.html

---

## Community Licence

Issued by Administration des Transports; certified true copies in each vehicle.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Luxembourg within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** before next cabotage series
- ITM posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.
