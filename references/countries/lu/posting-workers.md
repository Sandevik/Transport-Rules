## Overview

Luxembourg implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the Loi concernant le détachement de travailleurs. The competent authority for posting declarations and enforcement is the ITM (Inspection du Travail et des Mines). Luxembourg's minimum wage (SSM — Salaire Social Minimum) is one of the highest in the EU and applies to all workers, including posted transport workers performing cabotage or cross-trade.

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Declaration required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Luxembourg | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in LU + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Luxembourg only) | NO | NO |
| Transit (no loading/unloading in LU) | NO | NO |

---

## Notification Portal — ITM

Declaration must be submitted to the ITM before work commences:
- Portal: https://itm.public.lu/fr/travailleurs-detaches.html
- Required information: employer details, worker details, vehicle, period, type of transport

---

## Minimum Pay — Salaire Social Minimum (SSM)

Luxembourg's statutory minimum wage is one of the highest in the EU:
- **SSM (unskilled)**: approximately €2,500/month (2025 rate; updated annually)
- **SSM (skilled)**: approximately €3,000/month
- Transport drivers with recognised qualifications (CPC) typically qualify for the skilled rate

Drivers performing cabotage or cross-trade in Luxembourg must receive at least the SSM for hours worked in Luxembourg.

---

## Driver Documentation

The driver must carry:
- Proof of ITM declaration
- Employment contract showing at least Luxembourg SSM wages
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Luxembourg can demand security deposits for wage violations.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to submit ITM declaration | €1,000–€10,000 | Luxembourg posting law |
| Failure to pay SSM wages | €1,000–€50,000 | Code du Travail |
| Failure to maintain pay records | €1,000–€10,000 | Luxembourg law |
