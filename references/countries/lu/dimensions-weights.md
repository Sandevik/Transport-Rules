# Luxembourg — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**
- https://at.public.lu/ — Administration des Transports
- https://police.public.lu/ — Police Grand-Ducale

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Luxembourg applies EU standard weights and dimensions under the Code de la Route luxembourgeois and implementing regulations. Luxembourg has no special large vehicle programme and follows EU standard limits strictly.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Oversize/overweight permits issued by the Administration des Transports. Application required before transport for loads exceeding standard limits.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC. Luxembourg applies EU standard limits.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Administration des Transports | Transport authority | https://at.public.lu/ |
| Police Grand-Ducale | Roadside checks | https://police.public.lu/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Luxembourg may demand security deposits from foreign carriers for overweight violations.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding max GVW | €250–€2,500 (depending on excess) | Code de la Route |
| Operating oversize without permit | €500–€2,500 | Code de la Route |
