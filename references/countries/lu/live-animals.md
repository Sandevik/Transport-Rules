## Overview

Luxembourg applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the Administration des services vétérinaires (ASV), under the Ministry of Agriculture. Luxembourg is a small country with limited domestic livestock production but is a significant transit country for live animals moving between France, Germany, and Belgium.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Authorisation | Issuing authority |
|--------------|---------------|-------------------|
| ≤65 km | Type 1 registration | ASV |
| >65 km | Type 2 (5-year) | ASV |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver Certificate of Competence (CoC) for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for journeys >8 hours
- Veterinary health certificate
- TRACES documentation for cross-border movements

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005. Luxembourg fully applies EU standards.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (EUR) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | Up to €5,000 | Luxembourg animal welfare law |
| Welfare violations | Up to €5,000 | Luxembourg law |
| Failure to maintain journey log | Up to €2,500 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
