## Overview

Luxembourg participates in the **Eurovignette** scheme. Heavy goods vehicles >12t must purchase a Eurovignette (time-based vignette) for use on Luxembourg's motorways. The Eurovignette is shared with the Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, and Sweden.

---

## Which Vehicles Must Pay

Vehicles with GVW **exceeding 12 tonnes** on the Luxembourg motorway network.

---

## Eurovignette

A single Eurovignette covers motorway usage in all participating countries:
- Luxembourg, Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Sweden

Purchase at: https://www.eurovignettes.eu/

---

## Rates

Rates vary by vehicle class (axle count) and EURO emission class. Valid periods: day, week, month, year.
