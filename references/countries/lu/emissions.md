## Overview

Luxembourg applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The Administration des Transports handles transport emission policy. Luxembourg has no national Low Emission Zone programme and does not operate a national ZEZ system for HGVs. As a small country with a large transit HGV flow (primarily on A1/A3/A6 motorways), Luxembourg focuses on EU-level measures.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Luxembourg national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**
- All new registrations: **1 July 2027**

CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law.

---

## National LEZ Programme

**Luxembourg has no national Low Emission Zone or Zero Emission Zone programme as of 2026.** The country's urban density and road network do not support widespread LEZ implementation comparable to neighbouring Belgium or Germany.

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- Luxembourg offers favourable **company car tax treatment** for ZEV vehicles
- **AIDE** (Agence de l'Environnement) administers grants for environmental investment including fleet electrification
- Luxembourg has below-EU-average diesel fuel taxes, making ZEV TCO arguments less compelling than in neighbouring countries
