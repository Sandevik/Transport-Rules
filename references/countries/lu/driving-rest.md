## Overview

Luxembourg applies EU Regulation 561/2006. The **Administration des transports** oversees compliance. Luxembourg is a small but strategically located transit/logistics hub (Kirchberg plateau hosts major logistics operations). No significant national deviations from EU rules.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.

**Smart Tachograph note:** From July 1, 2026, Smart Tachograph Type 2 is required for light commercial vehicles (2.5–3.5t) in international operations — Luxembourg applies this EU-wide deadline.
