## Overview

Luxembourg implements Directive 92/106/EEC as EU law. Luxembourg's primary combined transport asset is the **CFL Multimodal terminal at Bettembourg**, one of Europe's major intermodal hubs connecting to ports of Rotterdam, Antwerp, Hamburg, and Mediterranean routes. Luxembourg has no general HGV driving bans, so the driving ban exemption is less operationally critical here than in neighbouring Germany or France.

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle vehicles operating as part of qualifying combined transport journeys: **44 tonnes** (vs. standard 40t).

---

## Key Intermodal Terminals

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Bettembourg (CFL Multimodal) | Bettembourg | Rotterdam, Antwerp, Hamburg, Lyon, Spain |
| Dudelange logistics zone | Dudelange | Rail and road integration |

**Bettembourg** is strategically located at the convergence of the Rhine-Alpine and North Sea–Mediterranean rail freight corridors, making it a key gateway for Luxembourg-based and transit freight.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail booking confirmation from CFL Multimodal or equivalent terminal
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
