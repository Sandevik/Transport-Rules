## Overview

The Czech Republic applies EU Regulation 561/2006 without national deviations. Enforcement is by **INSID** (Inspekce Silniční Dopravy — Road Transport Inspection) and the police. The Czech Republic is a major transit country on the Central European freight corridor and enforcement is active, particularly on the D1 motorway.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No deviations from EU 561/2006 core rules.
