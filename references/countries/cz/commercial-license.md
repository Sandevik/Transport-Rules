## Overview

The Czech Republic issues Community Licences through the Krajský úřad (Regional Office) competent for the operator's registered address, under the authority of the Ministry of Transport. Operators must hold a Koncesní listina (concession document) as well as the Community Licence. The CPC exam (Osvědčení o odborné způsobilosti) is administered by approved examination centres.

---

## Licensing Requirements

| Requirement | Standard | Czech specifics |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| Establishment | Genuine Czech place of business | IČO registration; established place of business |
| Good repute | No serious offences | Criminal extract; transport register check |
| Financial standing | €9,000 (1st vehicle) + €5,000 each additional | Annual accounts or bank guarantee |
| Professional competence | Osvědčení o odborné způsobilosti | Via approved examination centres |

---

## National Licensing Authority

**Krajský úřad** (Regional Office): issues Community Licences and domestic transport licences in their respective region.

---

## Cabotage Rules

- Max **3 cabotage operations** in Czech Republic within 7 days after international delivery
- **4-day cooling-off** period
- MPSV posting notification required (see posting-workers.md)

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU 1071/2009.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CZK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Operating without Community Licence | CZK 100,000–CZK 500,000 | Zákon o silniční dopravě |
