# Czech Republic — Vehicle Dimensions and Weights

**EU Baseline:** [references/eu/weights-dimensions.md — Directive 96/53/EC (as amended by Directive 2015/719/EU)]

**Sources:**
- https://www.mdcr.cz/ — Ministry of Transport
- https://www.rsd.cz/ — ŘSD: Road Directorate

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

The Czech Republic applies EU standard weights and dimensions under Zákon č. 56/2001 Sb. (Road Traffic Act) and implementing regulations. No special large vehicle programme. The MYTO CZ electronic tolling system applies to vehicles >3.5t on motorways and selected 1st-class roads.

---

## Standard Limits (EU Baseline Applies)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Max GVW (general) | **40 tonnes** |
| Max GVW (combined transport, 5+ axles) | **44 tonnes** |
| Max width | **2.55 m** (2.60 m refrigerated) |
| Max height | **4.00 m** |
| Max length (articulated) | **16.50 m** |
| Max length (road train) | **18.75 m** |

---

## Exceptional Transport

Permits (mimořádné povolení) issued by ŘSD (Ředitelství silnic a dálnic) or regional road authorities for loads exceeding standard limits.

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from EU Directive 96/53/EC.

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CZK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Exceeding max GVW | CZK 5,000–CZK 200,000 | Zákon č. 56/2001 Sb. |
| Operating oversize without permit | CZK 5,000–CZK 100,000 | Zákon č. 56/2001 Sb. |
