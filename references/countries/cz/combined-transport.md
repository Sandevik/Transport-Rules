## Overview

The Czech Republic implements Directive 92/106/EEC through national transport law. The Czech Republic's central European location — landlocked but bordering Germany, Austria, Poland, and Slovakia — makes it an important combined transport transit country. ČD Cargo operates the main rail freight network, with intermodal terminals at Prague, Brno, and Lovosice (river port on the Elbe/Labe).

---

## Weight Allowance

5+ axle combinations on qualifying combined transport road legs: **44 tonnes**.

---

## Key Intermodal Terminals

| Terminal | Location | Connections |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Praha (ÚKD Uhříněves) | Prague | Rail intermodal hub |
| Brno | Brno | Central Czech logistics |
| Lovosice | North Bohemia | Elbe/Labe river port; rail connection to Germany |
| Ostrava | Ostrava | Industrial northeast; Poland border |

**Lovosice**: Inland port on the Elbe/Labe — river barges to Hamburg qualify as the non-road leg for combined transport purposes.

---

## Documentation Required

- Rail/river terminal booking/confirmation
- CMR waybill for road leg
- Vehicle registration confirming ≥5 axles (for 44t)
