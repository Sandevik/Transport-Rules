## Overview

The Czech Republic implements Directive 2020/1057/EU through the zákon o vysílání pracovníků (Act on Posting Workers). Foreign carriers must notify via the MPSV (Ministerstvo práce a sociálních věcí / Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs) online system before deploying posted drivers for cabotage or cross-trade. Enforcement is by SÚIP (Státní úřad inspekce práce / State Labour Inspection Authority).

---

## When Posting Rules Apply

| Operation | Notification required | Min. pay applicable |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Cabotage in Czech Republic | YES | YES |
| Cross-trade (load/unload in CZ + third country) | YES | YES |
| Bilateral (home country ↔ Czech Republic only) | NO | NO |
| Transit | NO | NO |

---

## Notification Portal

- Portal: https://www.mpsv.cz/ → section "Vysílání pracovníků"
- Must notify before work commences

---

## Minimum Pay

- **Minimální mzda (minimum wage)**: approximately CZK 18,900/month (2025; updated annually)
- **Sector collective agreements**: road transport sector collective agreements may set higher rates
- Posted drivers must receive at least the Czech minimum wage for hours worked in Czech Republic

---

## Driver Documentation

- MPSV notification confirmation
- Employment contract showing Czech-level pay
- A1 social security certificate
- CMR waybill

---

## Enforcement

| Authority | Full name | Website |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| SÚIP | Státní úřad inspekce práce | https://www.suip.cz/ |
| MPSV | Ministry of Labour | https://www.mpsv.cz/ |

**Advance payment at roadside:** YES — Czech authorities demand advance security deposits.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CZK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Failure to pay minimum wages | CZK 200,000–CZK 5,000,000 | Zákon o inspekci práce |
| Failure to notify posting | CZK 100,000–CZK 1,000,000 | Zákon o vysílání pracovníků |
