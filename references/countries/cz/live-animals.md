## Overview

The Czech Republic applies Regulation (EC) 1/2005 as uniform EU law. The competent authority is the SVS (Státní veterinární správa / State Veterinary Administration). Enforcement is by Krajské veterinární správy (Regional Veterinary Administrations). The Czech Republic is a significant pig and cattle producer and a transit country for livestock movements.

---

## Authorisation

| Journey type | Issuing authority |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Type 1 (≤65 km) | Krajská veterinární správa |
| Type 2 (>65 km, 5 years) | Krajská veterinární správa |

---

## Documentation

- Type 2 authorisation copy
- Driver CoC for type 2 journeys
- Journey log for >8 hours
- Veterinární osvědčení (veterinary health certificate)
- TRACES movement document

---

## Deviations from EU Baseline

No significant deviations from Regulation (EC) 1/2005.

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Penalty (CZK) | Legal basis |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Transport without authorisation | CZK 50,000–CZK 500,000 | Zákon o veterinární péči |
| Welfare violations | CZK 50,000–CZK 500,000 | Zákon na ochranu zvířat |
| Failure to maintain journey log | CZK 10,000–CZK 50,000 | VO (EG) 1/2005 |
