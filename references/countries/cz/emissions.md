## Overview

The Czech Republic applies EU emission standards as direct EU law. The Ministry of Transport (Ministerstvo dopravy) coordinates transport emission policy. No national LEZ system for HGVs exists as of 2026, though Prague and Brno are evaluating clean transport zones.

---

## Euro VII Application

No Czech national deviations from EU Euro VII timeline:
- New type approvals: **1 July 2026**; all new registrations: **1 July 2027**
- CO2 manufacturer targets apply as EU law

---

## National ZEV Incentives

- **MPO/SFŽP** (Czech Environmental Fund): grants for ZEV commercial vehicles
- EU RRF-funded programmes via IROP/OPD3 transport funding
- Reduced road charges (mýto) for ZEV vehicles
