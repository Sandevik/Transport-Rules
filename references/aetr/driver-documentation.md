# AETR — Driver Documentation and Record Book

**Framework:** AETR Agreement, Article 12 and Annex 1B. Covers the driver record book used when tachograph equipment is unavailable and manual entry obligations.

**Sources:**
- https://unece.org/transport/road-transport/aetr-agreement-and-status — UNECE: AETR Agreement text
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:22010A0317%2801%29 — EUR-Lex: AETR consolidated text

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

AETR Article 12 and Annex 1B require drivers to maintain records of their driving and rest activity. The primary recording mechanism is the **digital tachograph** (Annex 1B). When the tachograph is unavailable or malfunctioning, the driver must use a **record book** or complete **manual entries** on printouts.

---

## Digital Tachograph (Primary Method)

Vehicles in scope must be equipped with a tachograph conforming to **AETR Annex 1B**. The tachograph automatically records:
- Driving time
- Periods of other work
- Periods of availability
- Breaks and rest periods
- Speed
- Distance

See `references/aetr/tachograph.md` for full tachograph requirements.

---

## Driver Record Book (Fallback)

If a vehicle is **exempt from tachograph equipment** (e.g., vehicle was manufactured before tachograph obligations applied, or operating under a recognized exemption) or if the tachograph is **temporarily malfunctioning**, drivers must use a **driver record book**.

### Record Book Requirements

Each page of the record book must show:
- Driver's surname and first name
- Date
- Place where the journey starts and ends
- Registration number of the vehicle(s) used
- Odometer readings at the start and end of the day
- Time of departure and arrival
- Driving time (all driving periods)
- Other work (non-driving work)
- Rest periods
- Total daily driving time

### Format

The record book must conform to the model in AETR Annex 1B (same model as AETR tachograph chart replacement). Individual contracting parties may prescribe additional national models, provided the minimum information is captured.

---

## Tachograph Malfunction — Manual Entries

When a **digital tachograph malfunctions** during a journey:

1. The driver must **make manual entries** on tachograph printouts or on separate sheets
2. Manual entries must cover all activity periods (driving, other work, availability, rest) from the point of malfunction
3. The vehicle must be **repaired within 7 days** of returning to base, or **before leaving the current location** if the repair can be done there
4. If the vehicle cannot continue (tachograph is a legal requirement), the driver must not drive further until repaired

### Printout Annotations

When manually annotating a printout, the driver must write:
- Name and driving licence number
- Date
- Activity categories (using standard symbols: steering wheel = driving; briefcase = other work; horizontal bars = availability; bed = rest)
- Times for each activity

---

## 28-Day Carry Requirement

Drivers must carry and present on request:
- Current day's records (from midnight)
- Records for the **preceding 28 calendar days**

This applies whether records are on the driver card, tachograph printouts, or record book pages.

---

## Record Retention (Undertaking)

The transport undertaking must retain all records for a minimum of **12 months** after the relevant period. This includes:
- Downloaded tachograph data (vehicle unit and driver cards)
- Printouts and manual entries
- Record book pages (if used)
