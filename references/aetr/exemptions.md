# AETR — Exemptions

**Framework:** AETR Agreement, Article 13. Lists vehicle types and operations that are exempt from AETR driving and rest time requirements.

**Sources:**
- https://unece.org/transport/road-transport/aetr-agreement-and-status — UNECE: AETR Agreement text
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:22010A0317%2801%29 — EUR-Lex: AETR consolidated text

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

---

## Overview

Article 13 AETR provides mandatory exemptions from the driving and rest time rules. These mirror — but are not identical to — the exemptions in EU Regulation 561/2006 Article 13. AETR exemptions apply for the entire AETR-regulated journey; national authorities cannot unilaterally narrow or expand them on international routes.

---

## Mandatory AETR Exemptions (Article 13)

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Low-weight goods vehicles** | Vehicles used for the carriage of goods where the **permissible maximum weight (including trailer/semi-trailer) does not exceed 3.5 tonnes** |
| **Low-speed vehicles** | Vehicles with a **maximum authorized speed not exceeding 40 km/h** |
| **Armed forces** | Vehicles owned or hired by the armed services, civil defense, fire services, and forces responsible for maintaining public order, when the carriage is undertaken as a consequence of their tasks |
| **Essential services** | Vehicles used in connection with: sewerage, flood protection, water, gas and electricity maintenance services, highway maintenance and control, refuse collection and disposal, telegraph and telephone services, carriage of postal articles, broadcasting, and the detection of radio or television transmitters or receivers |
| **Emergency and rescue** | Vehicles used in emergencies or rescue operations |
| **Medical** | Specialized vehicles used for medical purposes |
| **Breakdown recovery** | Specialized breakdown/recovery vehicles operating within 100 km of their base |
| **Circus and funfair** | Vehicles transporting circus and funfair equipment |
| **Road testing** | Vehicles undergoing road tests for technical development, repair or maintenance, and new or rebuilt vehicles not yet put into service |
| **Non-commercial personal use** | Vehicles used for non-commercial carriage of goods for personal use |
| **Agricultural and forestry tractors** | Tractors used for agricultural and forestry purposes |

---

## Key Differences from EU 561/2006 Exemptions

AETR Article 13 and EU 561/2006 Article 13 are structurally similar but differ in some details:

| Area | EU 561/2006 | AETR |
|------|------------|------|
| Scope to set additional exemptions | Member states may grant additional exemptions for domestic operations only | Contracting parties may not add exemptions on AETR international operations |
| Agricultural/forestry radius | Within 100 km of base | Not radius-limited in AETR text |
| Breakdown recovery radius | 100 km | 100 km |
| National derogations | Art. 13(1) allows member states to grant national-only derogations | Not available for AETR international operations |

---

## Practical Notes

- An exemption under AETR applies for the entire journey on that vehicle/operation type
- Exempt vehicles crossing AETR countries are not required to carry tachograph equipment or driver cards under AETR, but may still be subject to national vehicle inspection rules
- Mixed operations (partly exempt, partly not) on the same vehicle are generally treated as non-exempt for the whole journey
