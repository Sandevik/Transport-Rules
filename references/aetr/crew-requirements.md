# AETR — Crew Requirements

**Framework:** AETR Agreement, Article 5. Covers minimum driver age, crew composition, and multi-manning rules.

## Minimum Driver Age (Article 5)

| Vehicle type | Minimum age | Condition |
|-------------|------------|-----------|
| Goods vehicles (any weight in scope) | **21 years** | Default |
| Goods vehicles (any weight in scope) | **18 years** | If driver holds a certificate of professional competence recognized under national law of the contracting party |
| Passenger vehicles (coaches/buses >9 seats) | **21 years** | Default |
| Passenger vehicles on regular services ≤50 km radius | **21 years** | National rules may vary |

> **EU/EEA context:** EU drivers holding a Category C licence with Driver CPC (Directive 2003/59/EC) satisfy the AETR age-18 professional competence condition, provided the licence was issued from age 18. The CPC qualification is the recognized certificate for this purpose within the EU.

## Solo vs Multi-Manning (Double-Manning)

### Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Single-crew** | One driver per vehicle; standard operation |
| **Double-crew (multi-manning)** | Two or more drivers in the vehicle simultaneously during driving periods |

Multi-manning is not mandated by AETR but is an option that affects how rest rules apply. Operators typically choose double-manning for long international runs to extend vehicle operating time.

### Multi-Manning Rest Rules

When a vehicle is double-manned, the **passenger-seat rest** provision applies:

- A driver who is **not driving** and is seated in the cab (passenger seat or bunk) may count that time as **rest**, provided the vehicle is moving and the driver is not performing other work
- The minimum **daily rest** for each driver in a multi-manned vehicle is **9 consecutive hours** within a 30-hour window from the end of the last rest period (instead of the standard 11 hours)
- The 30-hour window (vs 24 hours for solo) gives the double crew flexibility to alternate driving without stopping the vehicle

| Rule | Solo | Multi-manned |
|------|------|-------------|
| Daily rest minimum | 11 h (or 9 h reduced) | 9 h within 30-hour period |
| Rest counting in passenger seat | Not applicable | Counts as rest if not working |
| Weekly rest | Same rules apply | Same rules apply — each driver individually |

### Practical Operation

1. Driver A drives while Driver B rests in the cab (counts as rest)
2. After 4h30min driving, Driver A takes a break — Driver B takes over
3. Each driver accumulates rest independently; the vehicle can continue moving
4. Weekly rest must still be taken outside the vehicle cab (Mobility Package requirement in EU; not explicitly in AETR text — see `references/aetr/driving-rest-time.md`)

## Crew Documentation

Each crew member must carry and be able to present during roadside inspections:
- Valid driving licence for the vehicle category
- Driver card (for digital tachograph operations)
- Manual entries or printouts covering the current day and preceding 28 days
- Any relevant professional competence certificate (where relied upon for the age-18 provision)
