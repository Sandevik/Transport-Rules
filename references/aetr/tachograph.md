# AETR — Tachograph Requirements

**Framework:** AETR Agreement, Annex 1B. This file is the baseline for **Tier 2 countries** (non-EU/EEA AETR signatories) regarding tachograph obligations during AETR operations.

**Sources:**
- https://unece.org/transport/road-transport/aetr-agreement-and-status — UNECE: AETR Agreement text including Annex 1B
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:22010A0317%2801%29 — EUR-Lex: AETR consolidated text

**Last verified: 2026-03-19**

---

## Overview

AETR Annex 1B specifies the technical requirements for recording equipment (tachographs) in vehicles performing international transport under the AETR Agreement. Requirements largely align with EU digital tachograph standards but have their own update cycle and enforcement mechanism separate from EU Regulation 165/2014.

---

## Tachograph Requirement

Vehicles subject to AETR (goods vehicles >3.5t and coaches in international transport crossing at least one non-EU AETR country) must be equipped with a **digital tachograph** conforming to AETR Annex 1B.

---

## Cards Required

| Card type | Holder | Validity |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Driver card | Individual driver | 5 years |
| Workshop card | Approved calibration workshop | 1 year |
| Company card | Transport company | 5 years |
| Control card | Enforcement authority | 5 years |

Driver cards are issued by the national authority in the driver's country of residence. EU-issued driver cards are valid for AETR operations. Non-EU AETR country cards are valid for AETR operations but may not be accepted for EU 561/2006 enforcement.

---

## Data Download Requirements

| Unit | Minimum download frequency |
|------|--------------------------|
| Vehicle unit | Every **90 days** |
| Driver card | Every **28 days** |
| Data retention | **12 months** |

---

## Calibration

- Calibration required every **2 years** or after any repair/modification affecting accuracy
- Only approved (accredited) workshops may calibrate

---

## Key Difference from EU Smart Tachograph

- EU Regulation 2020/1054 (Mobility Package) imposed retrofit deadlines for Smart v2 tachographs in EU/EEA operations (see `references/eu/smart-tachograph.md`)
- AETR Annex 1B has **not adopted** Smart v2 requirements; digital tachograph (Annex 1B) remains the standard for AETR-only operations
- Vehicles retrofitted to Smart v2 for EU operations also satisfy AETR Annex 1B requirements (backwards compatible)
