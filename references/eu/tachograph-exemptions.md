# EU — Tachograph and Driving Hours Exemptions

**Sources:**
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006R0561 — EU Regulation 561/2006 — Articles 3, 13, 14

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

EU Regulation 561/2006 on driving and rest times does not apply universally. Articles 3, 13, and 14 define categories of vehicles and operations that are exempt. Member states may also grant additional national exemptions under Art. 14. Understanding exemptions is critical for compliance — operating a vehicle outside the scope of 561/2006 does not necessarily mean tachograph equipment is not required (see Regulation 165/2014 scope separately).

---

## Article 3 — Vehicles Excluded from Scope (EU-wide, automatic)

These vehicles are excluded from Regulation 561/2006 by its own terms. No permit or national decision required:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Vehicles ≤ 40 km/h max speed | Vehicles whose maximum speed does not exceed 40 km/h |
| Military vehicles | Vehicles used by armed forces, civil defence, fire services, and forces responsible for maintaining public order |
| Emergency services | Vehicles used in connection with sewerage, flood protection, water, gas and electricity maintenance, highway maintenance and control, refuse collection, telegraph services, carriage of postal articles, broadcasting services, and detection of radio or television transmitters or receivers |
| Agricultural/forestry | Specialised vehicles used for agricultural and forestry activities |
| Specialised breakdown vehicles | Only operating within 100 km of their base |
| Vehicles being tested | Vehicles undergoing road tests for technical development, repair or maintenance purposes, and new or rebuilt vehicles which have not yet been put into service |
| Non-commercial carriage | Vehicles used for non-commercial carriage of goods |

---

## Article 13 — National Exemptions (Member States May Exempt)

Member states may, within their own territory, grant exemptions from 561/2006 for the following categories. These exemptions **do not apply on international journeys** — only domestic operations:

| Category | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicles ≤ 7.5t GVW for non-commercial carriage | Carriage of materials, equipment or machinery for the driver's use in the course of his work, within 100 km radius of base | Driver must not make transport their principal activity |
| Regular passenger services ≤ 50 km | Vehicles used on regular passenger services where the route covered by the service in question does not exceed 50 km | Scheduled bus/coach on short urban routes |
| Max speed ≤ 40 km/h | Vehicles with a maximum authorised speed not exceeding 40 km/h | Same as Art. 3 — automatic |
| Military + emergency | Vehicles used by the following public services: armed forces, civil defence, fire services, forces responsible for maintaining public order, ambulance services | Same as Art. 3 — automatic |
| Agriculture, horticulture, forestry, farming, fishery | Vehicles used in connection with agricultural, horticultural, forestry, farming or fishery undertakings, only within a 100 km radius of the base of the undertaking | Tractors with trailers etc. |
| Agricultural tractors | Tractors used for agricultural or forestry purposes | |
| Construction vehicles | Vehicles used by universal service providers for the carriage of items as part of universal service, within 150 km | |
| Construction (100 km) | Specialised vehicles used for medical purposes within 100 km of base | |
| Vehicles used exclusively on islands ≤ 2,300 km² | Only applies to islands not connected to mainland by bridge or tunnel | |
| GVW ≤ 7.5t natural gas / electric | Vehicles propelled by natural gas or electricity, GVW ≤ 7.5t, within 100 km of base | To encourage zero-emission short-haul |
| Driving schools and examination vehicles | Vehicles used for driving instruction/examination for the purpose of obtaining a driving licence | If not used for commercial carriage |
| Vehicles used in connection with sewerage/water/gas etc. | Specialised vehicles used within 100 km of base | Infrastructure maintenance |
| Vehicles carrying fairground equipment | Specialised vehicles that carry and use circus or funfair equipment | |
| Mobile cranes | Specialised vehicles | |
| Vehicles used for collecting milk/returning milk containers | Within 100 km of farm base | |
| Carriage of money and valuables | Specialised vehicles within a member state | |
| Medical waste | Vehicles transporting waste that do not constitute a risk | |
| Vehicles used exclusively for carrying livestock | Combined short-haul livestock transport | |
| Vintage/historic vehicles | Vehicles manufactured before 1 January 1947 | Historic vehicle shows, etc. |

**Important:** Even when a vehicle is exempt from 561/2006, it may still need a tachograph under Regulation 165/2014 if it is used for hire and reward carriage. Check both regulations separately.

---

## Article 14 — National Exemptions (By Individual Member State Decision)

In exceptional circumstances, member states may grant a **temporary exemption for up to 30 days** for specific operations, and must notify the European Commission. These are ad hoc and country-specific — check national transport authority websites.

Common examples used by member states:
- Seasonal harvest operations beyond 100 km radius
- Emergency relief operations
- Military exercises
- Major infrastructure projects

---

## AETR Equivalent Exemptions

The AETR Agreement (for non-EU operations) has broadly equivalent exemptions under Article 2. Key difference: AETR exemptions apply on AETR-covered international journeys outside the EU.

---

## Tachograph Requirement vs. 561/2006 Scope

**Critical distinction:**
- **561/2006** governs driving/rest times → can be exempt
- **Regulation 165/2014** governs tachograph installation → different scope rules

A vehicle may be exempt from 561/2006 driving time rules but still require a tachograph under 165/2014 (or vice versa). Check both separately for each vehicle category.

---

## Penalties

Member states set penalties for incorrect use of exemptions. Claiming an exemption that does not apply results in the full driving/rest time penalties applying retroactively.

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Falsely claiming exemption | Full 561/2006 penalties apply + possible fraud charges |
| No tachograph despite requirement | Prohibition from operating + national fine |
| Incorrect documentation of exempt status | Fine (varies by member state) |
