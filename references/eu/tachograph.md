# EU — Tachograph

**Sources:**
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32014R0165 — EU Regulation 165/2014 — tachographs in road transport

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

EU Regulation 165/2014 requires tachographs in all vehicles used for road transport subject to EU Regulation 561/2006 (driving and rest times). Digital tachographs record driving time, rest periods, and other activities. Smart tachograph v2 (GNSS + ITS-G5 DSRC communication) is being progressively introduced for all new vehicles. See `references/eu/smart-tachograph.md` for full smart tachograph v2 technical details and retrofit timelines.

## Scope

Tachographs are mandatory in vehicles used for commercial road transport with:
- GVW > 3.5 tonnes (goods vehicles), OR
- More than 9 seats including driver (passenger vehicles)

Exemptions exist for vehicles in national or international transport by derogation — see Regulation 561/2006 Art. 13 exemptions.

## Calibration Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration interval | Every 2 years | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| Seal check | Every 6 years | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| After repair/adjustment | Immediate recalibration required | Art. 23 Regulation 165/2014 |
| Workshop type | Approved tachograph workshop only | Art. 24 Regulation 165/2014 |

---

## Data Download Obligations

| Data | Download interval |
|---|---|
| Vehicle unit (VU) data | At least every 90 days |
| Driver card data | At least every 28 days |
| Storage minimum | 12 months (operator) |

## Enforcement

Enforcement is by member state competent authorities. Roadside tachograph checks are mandatory — member states must check at least 3% of all working days of mobile workers (Directive 2006/22/EC).

---

## Penalties

Member states set national penalties. EU framework requires penalties to be "effective, proportionate and dissuasive." See individual country files for national penalty schedules.

| Violation type | EU minimum principle |
|---|---|
| Tachograph not installed/calibrated | Prohibition from use until compliant |
| Manipulation/tampering | Criminal prosecution |
| Records not kept | Proportionate fine |
