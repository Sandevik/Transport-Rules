## Overview

EU Regulation 540/2014 on the sound level of motor vehicles (replacing Directive 70/157/EEC) sets mandatory noise limits for new motor vehicles sold in the EU. Limits apply to type approval — not to in-use vehicles retroactively, except via Low Emission Zone (LEZ) and urban access restrictions. Beyond type approval noise limits, many EU cities impose **time-based delivery windows** to reduce urban noise from trucks, and some grant exemptions to electric/low-noise vehicles.

---

## EU Noise Limits (Regulation 540/2014)

Limits in **dB(A)** — measured under drive-by test procedures per UN-ECE Regulation 51.03:

### Heavy Goods Vehicles (N2, N3)

| Category | Phase 1 (2016) | Phase 2 (2020) | Phase 3 (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| N2 ≤ 150 kW engine | 72 dB(A) | 71 dB(A) | 70 dB(A) |
| N2 > 150 kW engine | 73 dB(A) | 72 dB(A) | 71 dB(A) |
| N3 ≤ 150 kW | 74 dB(A) | 73 dB(A) | 72 dB(A) |
| N3 > 150 kW | 75 dB(A) | 74 dB(A) | 73 dB(A) |

### Buses and Coaches (M2, M3)

| Category | Phase 1 (2016) | Phase 2 (2020) | Phase 3 (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| M2 ≤ 150 kW | 71 dB(A) | 70 dB(A) | 69 dB(A) |
| M2 > 150 kW | 72 dB(A) | 71 dB(A) | 70 dB(A) |
| M3 ≤ 150 kW | 72 dB(A) | 71 dB(A) | 70 dB(A) |
| M3 > 150 kW | 74 dB(A) | 73 dB(A) | 72 dB(A) |

## Urban Delivery Restrictions (City-Specific)

Beyond type approval limits, many EU cities restrict HGV deliveries during night hours and sometimes early morning to protect residents from noise. These are **city/municipality rules**, not EU law:

| City | HGV delivery restriction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paris (FR) | 22:00–07:00 HGV ban (varies by arrondissement) | Exceptions for LEZ-approved quiet vehicles |
| Amsterdam (NL) | Night access restrictions, LEZ applies | Electric delivery vehicles exempt |
| Stockholm (SE) | Night delivery restrictions in some areas | Quiet electric exemptions in city centre |
| Berlin (DE) | 22:00–06:00 night driving ban area rules | Environmental zone restrictions |
| Vienna (AT) | Night delivery restrictions in inner districts | |
| London (GB) | LLCS (London Lorry Control Scheme) — most areas 23:00–07:00 (Mon–Sat), 23:00–07:00 Sun | Heavy lorries > 18t |

*Always check local municipality rules — restrictions change frequently.*

## Low-Noise Vehicle Exemptions

Some cities grant **time window exemptions** to electric, hydrogen, or otherwise certified low-noise vehicles for off-hours delivery:
- Paris: "Livraison propre" scheme — certified quiet electric vans may deliver 22:00–07:00
- Stockholm: "Stadslogistik" quiet delivery windows for zero-emission vehicles
- Amsterdam: Zero-emission zones (ZEZ) — only electric vehicles from 2025 in city centre

## Noise Measurement and UN-ECE Regulation 51

The current drive-by noise test method (UN-ECE Regulation 51.03, replaces 51.02) uses a modified acceleration test that better reflects real-world urban driving conditions:
- **ASEP** (Additional Sound Emission Provisions) — ensures vehicles don't emit more noise in real driving than in test
- Tests measure combined contribution of engine, intake, exhaust, tyres, and vehicle body
- Electric vehicles subject to **AVAS** (Acoustic Vehicle Alerting System) requirements — must emit artificial sound at low speeds (<20 km/h)

---

## Tyre Noise (Separate Regulation)

EU Regulation 661/2009 (and Regulation 1222/2009 on tyre labelling) sets **tyre rolling noise limits** separately from vehicle noise limits. Tyres sold in the EU must carry a noise label (A/B/C classes). Tyre noise is a major contributor to HGV road noise at higher speeds.

## Enforcement

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| Type approval authorities | Issue vehicle type approval (national competent authorities e.g. KBA in DE, RDW in NL) |
| Municipal traffic police | Enforce city noise delivery restrictions |
| Technical inspection bodies | Periodic roadworthiness check may include exhaust noise |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Exceeding urban delivery time restrictions | Municipal fine (varies by city) |
| Selling vehicle without EU type approval | Cannot be registered in EU |
| Modified exhaust system increasing noise | Vehicle inspection failure + fine |
