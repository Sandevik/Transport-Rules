# Windshield Defects and Mandatory Vehicle Stickers

**EU Baseline:** Directive 2014/47/EU — roadside technical inspection of commercial vehicles (Annex II, Category 1.1 field of vision); Directive 2014/45/EU — periodic roadworthiness testing.

**Sources:**
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32014L0047 — Directive 2014/47/EU (Annex II item 1.1.1: windscreen condition)
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32014L0045 — Directive 2014/45/EU (Annex II: items to be tested)
- Country-specific law references listed per country below

**Last verified: 2026-06-02**

> **Important:** Fine amounts below are sourced from training data (knowledge cutoff August 2025). Verify current amounts via the official national authority before relying on them for compliance purposes.

## EU Baseline — Windshield Defect Classification

Under Directive 2014/47/EU Annex II, **item 1.1.1 — Windscreen condition** falls in Category 1 (Field of Vision). The EU-standardised defect classification applies:

| Defect category | Windshield trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor** | Small chip or crack outside the driver's critical sight zone; no vision impairment | Pass with advisory; repair before next test |
| **Major (Dangerous)** | Crack or damage in the driver's critical sight zone that impairs vision | Immediate prohibition from driving; vehicle may not continue |

Every EU member state must apply at minimum this classification at roadside technical inspections under Directive 2014/47/EU. In practice most countries apply stricter or more detailed zone-based rules.

---

## Windshield Zones — How Countries Define the Critical Area

Most EU member states and several non-EU countries define visibility zones based on the wiper sweep area and the driver's direct line of sight. The terminology and exact dimensions differ but the principle is consistent:

| Zone | Description | Typical threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical / Zone A** | The area directly in the driver's line of sight, within the wiper sweep zone — often defined as a band of ~100–300 mm in front of the driver's eyes | Any crack in this zone is a dangerous defect; no minimum size |
| **Secondary / Zone B** | Remaining area swept by wipers outside Zone A | Significant crack (typically >2–5 cm) is a major defect |
| **Outer / Zone C** | Area outside wiper sweep | Minor or no defect unless extremely large |

## Germany

**Legal basis:** StVZO §35b Abs. 2 — *"An adequate field of vision for the driver must be ensured under all operating and weather conditions."* HU-Richtlinien (Hauptuntersuchung guidelines) define the defect zones.

### Windshield cracks

| Zone | Definition | HU classification | Roadside consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zone A (Fahrsichtbereich)** | The critical wiper-swept zone directly in front of the driver's eyes, approx. 29 cm x 25 cm centered on the driver's line of sight | **Gefährlicher Mangel** (dangerous defect) for any crack/chip significantly impairing vision — vehicle must not be driven; red HU sticker | Prohibition from driving (Fahrtunterbrechung) until repaired; vehicle cannot continue under its own power |
| **Zone B** | Remaining area swept by wipers | **Erheblicher Mangel** (significant defect) for large/structural cracks | HU failed (orange sticker); repair and retest required within 4 weeks |
| **Zone C** | Outside wiper sweep area | Minor or no defect for small chips; structural cracks = erheblicher Mangel | Advisory; no immediate prohibition |

**Fine for driving with impaired visibility (police roadside check):**
EUR 60 fine — OWiG §49a in conjunction with StVZO §35b and Bußgeldkatalogverordnung (BKatV).

**At BALM fliegende Kontrolle (flying inspection):** BALM officers apply the same HU-Richtlinien classification. A Zone A crack triggers an immediate prohibition notice (Untersagung der Weiterfahrt). The vehicle must be towed or repaired on the spot before continuing.

### Mandatory stickers — Germany

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing/expired | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HU-Plakette** | Round coloured sticker on rear licence plate — colour encodes the year, position on the clock face encodes the month of next due HU | All vehicles | 2–4 months overdue: EUR 25; 4–8 months: EUR 60 + 1 Punkt; >8 months: EUR 75 + 1 Punkt | §29 StVZO; BKatV lfd. Nr. 261 |
| **SP-Plakette (Sicherheitsprüfung)** | Oval sticker for annual safety check between HU inspections | N2/N3 commercial vehicles >7.5t GVW | Similar fine schedule to HU-Plakette; check current BKatV entry | §29 StVZO; §57a StVZO |
| **Umweltplakette** | Colour-coded environmental sticker (green/yellow/red by emission class) | All vehicles entering designated Umweltzonen (environmental zones) | EUR 25 fine for entering zone without valid sticker | BKatV; BImSchV |
| **ADR orange plate** | Rectangular orange hazard identification plate, mandatory when carrying dangerous goods above thresholds | Vehicles carrying ADR dangerous goods | Serious infringement; see `eu/dangerous-goods.md` | ADR 5.3.2 |

**HU-Plakette colour cycle (approximate — verify current cycle via ADAC or TÜV):**
The sticker colour rotates on a 6-year cycle: the number on the sticker face shows the last digit of the year; the position on the clock face shows the month. Officers check both colour and position at a glance.

## Austria

**Legal basis:** §102 Abs. 5 KFG (Kraftfahrgesetz) — prohibition from driving a vehicle not in roadworthy condition; §57a KFG — periodic roadworthiness test (Begutachtung).

### Windshield cracks

A crack in the critical zone (broadly equivalent to Zone A/Zone B) results in **§57a Begutachtung (Pickerl) failure** — the vehicle may not be driven until repaired and re-examined.

| Zone | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Critical zone (direct driver sight line, wiper sweep) | Pickerl failure; prohibition from driving |
| Outside critical zone but within glass | Major defect; must repair |

**Fine for driving with defective windshield (KFG §134):** minimum EUR 72, up to EUR 5,000 for serious violations.

### Mandatory stickers — Austria

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **§57a Pickerl** | Oval sticker on windscreen showing date of last periodic roadworthiness test | All motor vehicles; N2/N3 commercial vehicles annually | EUR 72–726 (§134 KFG) | §57a KFG |
| **Autobahnvignette** | Since 2022: digital registration only (no physical sticker). Previously a round windscreen sticker. Registering vehicle via eVignette or GO Box required for motorway use. | All vehicles on Austrian motorways and expressways | Kontrollgebühr EUR 220 + potential additional fine | BStMG §20 |

## France

**Legal basis:** Code de la Route Article R316-4 — windscreen must not impair the driver's field of vision; Code de la Route Article R313-1 et seq. — vehicle lighting and equipment.

### Windshield cracks

A crack in the driver's critical sight zone at Controle Technique Poids Lourds (CTPL — heavy vehicle periodic inspection): **defaut critique** — vehicle rejected, prohibition from using on public roads until repaired.

**Fine at roadside for driving with impaired windshield visibility:**
EUR 135 (forfait majoré — R316-4 Code de la Route via the standard minor infringement tariff; verify current rate).

### Mandatory stickers — France

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vignette Crit'Air** | Coloured circular sticker indicating the vehicle's emission class (Classes 0–5). Required to enter Zones a Faibles Emissions (ZFE-m) in Paris, Lyon, Grenoble, Bordeaux, Marseille, Strasbourg, Rouen, Montpellier and others. | All vehicles entering a ZFE-m. Commercial vehicles (heavy trucks) also require it where city-centre access depends on Euro standard. | Cars/light vehicles: EUR 68–135; heavy vehicles (N2/N3): EUR 450–750 | Arrêté du 21 juin 2016; Code de la Route R318-2 |

**Note:** The number of French cities requiring Crit'Air has expanded. Verify whether Crit'Air is required for the specific city on your route at certificat-air.gouv.fr.

## Netherlands

**Legal basis:** Wegenverkeerswet 1994, Article 5 — vehicle may not endanger road safety; RDW APK (Algemene Periodieke Keuring) inspection standards.

### Windshield cracks

APK failure if crack is in the driver's swept wiper area and impairs vision. ILT (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport) can prohibit a vehicle at roadside for dangerous windshield condition.

**Fine at roadside:** EUR 140–310 range for operating an unsafe vehicle (Wet Administratiefrechtelijke Handhaving Verkeersvoorschriften — WAHV fines schedule).

### Mandatory stickers — Netherlands

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| APK sticker | No separate windscreen sticker — APK is registered electronically by the RDW. Officers verify via number plate lookup. | All vehicles | N/A (no sticker; fine for driving with expired APK per WAHV) |
| **LEZ / ZE zone sticker** | No physical sticker — Dutch zero-emission zones (ZE-zones) use number plate recognition. | All vehicles in ZE-zones in Rotterdam, Amsterdam, Utrecht and others | Fine based on number plate scan; EUR 100–350 |

## Belgium

**Legal basis:** Code de la Route / Wegverkeersreglement, Article 23 — vehicle condition; GOCA (Groupement des Organismes Agréés pour le Contrôle des Automobiles) inspection standards.

### Windshield cracks

Controle Technique (CT/Keuring) failure if crack is in the critical driver sight zone. A "Contre-Visite" (re-inspection) is required within 15 days. Driving with a red/refusal result can lead to vehicle prohibition.

**Fine at roadside:** EUR 50–200 depending on severity (police discretion under Code de la Route Art. 23).

### Mandatory stickers — Belgium

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CT Sticker (Keuring)** | No separate sticker for HGVs — inspection certificate must be in vehicle. Police check document. | All N2/N3 vehicles | Fine for missing/expired controle technique document |
| **Viapass / BEnelux e-vignette** | Electronic (no physical sticker) — toll for vehicles >3.5t on Belgian motorways via OBU. | N2/N3 vehicles on Belgian motorways | Fine for missing OBU; see `references/countries/be/tolls.md` |
| **LEZ sticker (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent)** | Physical or digital — LEZ access requires registration; some cities use vignette stickers for foreign vehicles | Vehicles entering LEZ areas | EUR 350 per infraction in Brussels; varies by city |

## Sweden

**Legal basis:** Fordonslagen (SFS 2002:574) §5 — vehicles must be roadworthy; TSFS (Transportstyrelens föreskrifter) on inspection standards.

### Windshield cracks

At kontrollbesiktning: a crack through the driver's direct field of vision (the zone directly in front of the driver, within the wiper sweep) is a **kassaklass 3 anmärkning (immediate fail / kassation)**. The vehicle receives körförbud and may not be driven until repaired and re-inspected.

At vägkontroll (roadside check): Transportstyrelsen or police inspector can issue körförbud for a windshield crack that clearly impairs the driver's visibility.

**Fine:** Sanktionsavgift for driving with körförbud; see `references/countries/se/penalties.md` for current SEK amounts.

### Mandatory stickers — Sweden

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Besiktningsdekalen** | No longer a physical windscreen sticker in all cases — inspection result is registered in Transportstyrelens system. Verification by numberplate. Some vehicles may still have adhesive labels. | All vehicles | Sanktionsavgift for driving with expired besiktning; see `se/penalties.md` |
| **Miljöklassmärkning** | Classification sticker for older vehicles in Miljözoner; modern clean vehicles are registered electronically | Vehicles not registered in the electronic system and entering Miljözoner | SEK fine; see `se/environment-law.md` |

## United Kingdom

**Legal basis:** Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986 (SI 1986/1078), Regulation 30 — windscreen must not impair the driver's vision; Road Traffic Act 1988.

### Windshield cracks

The DVSA's vehicle defect classification for HGVs at roadside inspection:

| Zone | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Driver's swept area** (the area swept by wipers directly in front of the driver) | Any crack, chip, or damage that significantly impairs vision = **Category S — immediate prohibition** (vehicle may not move until repaired) |
| Outside swept area | Major defect or advisory depending on size and nature |

**Fine for driving with impaired windshield visibility:** up to GBP 2,500 + 3 penalty points under the Road Traffic Act 1988. For commercial vehicles/operators: prosecution under Construction and Use Regulations 1986 Reg. 30; unlimited fine for HGV operator.

**DVSA roadside prohibition notice (PG9):** issued when a dangerous defect is found; vehicle cannot move until defect is rectified and DVSA confirms.

### Mandatory stickers — UK

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Operator's licence disc** | Disc displayed in the front windscreen of commercial vehicles showing the operator's licence number, traffic area, and expiry date. Must be visible and current. | All N2/N3 vehicles under an operator's licence | Prosecution; operator's licence review; up to GBP 2,500 fine |
| **MOT/Annual test** | No windscreen sticker — MOT result is registered electronically on the DVLA system. Officers verify by number plate. | All commercial vehicles — annually from first registration for N2/N3 | Fine + possible prosecution for using unlicensed vehicle |
| **International (GB oval / UK plate)** | Rear identification plate required for international travel | Vehicles on international routes | Roadside fine; varies by country |

## Switzerland (AETR)

**Legal basis:** VTS (Verordnung über die technischen Anforderungen an Strassenfahrzeuge) Art. 70 — field of vision requirements; VZV (Verkehrszulassungsverordnung) — periodic inspection (MFK).

### Windshield cracks

At MFK (Motorfahrzeugkontrolle — periodic inspection): crack in the critical driver sight zone = inspection failure; prohibition from driving until repaired and re-inspected.

**Fine at roadside (Polizei or Zoll):** CHF 100–300 for operating a vehicle with dangerous windshield condition.

### Mandatory stickers — Switzerland

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Autobahnvignette** | Round windscreen sticker — mandatory for all vehicles on Swiss motorways. Annual (calendar year). 2025 price: CHF 40. | All vehicles on Swiss motorways | CHF 200 fine for missing vignette; officers check at border crossings and roadside |
| **MFK sticker** | No separate windscreen sticker for HGVs — inspection validity is in registration documents and on licence plate. | All vehicles | Fine + prohibition for expired inspection |

## Poland

**Legal basis:** Prawo o ruchu drogowym (Road Traffic Law), Art. 66 — vehicle must be roadworthy; Rozporządzenie (Regulation) on vehicle inspection standards.

### Windshield cracks

At Stacja Kontroli Pojazdów (SKP — periodic vehicle inspection): crack in the driver's critical zone = inspection failure.

**Fine at roadside:** PLN 200–500 for driving with a defective windshield impairing vision (Kodeks wykroczen Art. 97 + police discretion).

### Mandatory stickers — Poland

| Sticker | What it is | Who needs it | Fine if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Naklejka kontrolna** | Inspection sticker on windscreen showing validity of periodic technical inspection | All vehicles | PLN 200–500 fine; vehicle can be immobilised |
| **e-TOLL / SENT** | Electronic — no physical sticker. OBU (On-Board Unit) required for N2/N3 vehicles on tolled roads. | N2/N3 vehicles on tolled roads in Poland | Fine for missing OBU; see `references/countries/pl/tolls.md` |
