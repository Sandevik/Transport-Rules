## Overview

EU Council Directive 92/6/EEC (as amended by Directive 2002/85/EC) requires the installation and use of speed limitation devices (speed limiters / governed speed devices) in heavy motor vehicles used for the carriage of goods or passengers. The purpose is road safety — preventing vehicles from exceeding safe operating speeds. Speed limiters must be set and sealed by an approved workshop and may not be tampered with.

---

## Scope — Which Vehicles Require a Speed Limiter

| Vehicle Category | UN-ECE Category | GVW | Required Speed | In Force |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Goods vehicles | N3 | > 12 tonnes | **90 km/h** | Since 1 January 1994 (new), 1 January 1995 (existing) |
| Goods vehicles | N2 | > 3.5t ≤ 12t | **90 km/h** | Since 1 August 2001 (new), 1 January 2005 (existing) |
| Passenger vehicles | M3 | > 5 tonnes, > 8 seats | **100 km/h** | Since 1 January 1994 (new), 1 January 1995 (existing) |
| Passenger vehicles | M2 | ≤ 5 tonnes, > 8 seats | **100 km/h** | Since 1 August 2001 (new), 1 January 2005 (existing) |

**Note:** Where a country imposes a **lower national speed limit** for a vehicle category, the speed limiter must be set to that lower limit. Example: Some countries impose 80 km/h max for HGVs — in those countries, the limiter must be set to 80 km/h.

---

## Technical Requirements

Speed limiters must comply with **UN-ECE Regulation 89** (or equivalent technical standard). Key requirements:

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Set speed (trucks) | 90 km/h — vehicle cannot exceed this speed |
| Set speed (buses/coaches) | 100 km/h — vehicle cannot exceed this speed |
| Tolerance band | ± 4 km/h permissible in calibration |
| Installation | Approved workshop only (same as tachograph) |
| Sealing | Speed limiter unit must be sealed after calibration |
| After repair | Recalibration and resealing required |
| Tamper protection | Tampering is illegal under national road traffic law |
| Signage | Some countries require "90" or "100" plate on rear of vehicle |

---

## Exemptions

The following vehicle categories are **exempt** from speed limiter requirements under Directive 92/6/EEC:

- Vehicles that are used by armed forces, civil defence, fire services and forces responsible for maintaining public order
- Vehicles undergoing road tests for the purposes of technical development, repair or maintenance
- Vehicles used for non-commercial carriage of goods
- Vehicles constructed before 1 January 1988 (for Directive 92/6/EEC — check national rules for current date)

---

## National Speed Limits for HGVs

Speed limiters cap the maximum speed but national road speed limits also apply. Where the national limit is lower, that limit governs:

| Country | Motorway limit (HGV) | Rural road limit | Urban |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU general | 80–90 km/h (varies) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Germany (DE) | 80 km/h (>7.5t) | 60 km/h (>7.5t) | 50 km/h |
| France (FR) | 80 km/h (>3.5t) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Netherlands (NL) | 80 km/h (HGV) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Poland (PL) | 80 km/h | 70 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Sweden (SE) | 80 km/h (>3.5t on some roads, 90 km/h on motorway) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |
| Norway (NO) | 80 km/h (>5.5t) | 80 km/h | 50 km/h |

*Verify current national limits as these are set under national road traffic law and change periodically.*

---

## Enforcement

Member states set national penalties and enforcement procedures.

| Enforcement type | Detail |
|---|---|
| Roadside check | DVSA (GB), BALM (DE), Polizei check with diagnostic equipment |
| Tachograph data | Speed limiter compliance can be verified via tachograph data download |
| Plaque/certificate | Vehicles must carry calibration certificate |
| Workshop approval | Only approved workshops may set/seal speed limiters |

---

## Penalties

| Violation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Speed limiter not installed | Prohibition from use on public roads + national fine |
| Speed limiter sealed at wrong speed | Fine + re-calibration required |
| Tampered/disconnected limiter | Serious fine + possible criminal prosecution + vehicle prohibition |
| No calibration certificate | Fine |

Actual fine amounts are set by member states — see individual country files.
