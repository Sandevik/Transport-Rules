# EU — Driver Licensing for Commercial Vehicles

**Framework:** Directive 2006/126/EC. Establishes EU-wide driving licence categories, minimum age requirements, and validity rules for commercial vehicle drivers.

**Sources:**
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32006L0126 — Directive 2006/126/EC full text
- https://transport.ec.europa.eu/transport-modes/road/driving-licences_en — EC DG MOVE driving licences overview

**Last verified: 2026-05-31**

## Commercial Vehicle Licence Categories

| Category | Vehicles covered | Minimum age |
|----------|-----------------|------------|
| **C** | Motor vehicles for goods transport with GVW > 3.5 t (rigid) | **21 years** (or 18 with Driver CPC) |
| **CE** | Category C + trailer with GVW > 750 kg (articulated lorry / road train) | **21 years** (or 18 with Driver CPC) |
| **D** | Motor vehicles for passenger transport with > 8 seats (bus/coach, rigid) | **24 years** (or 21 with Driver CPC) |
| **DE** | Category D + trailer with GVW > 750 kg (coach with trailer) | **24 years** (or 21 with Driver CPC) |
| **C1** | Goods vehicles GVW 3.5–7.5 t | **18 years** |
| **C1E** | C1 + trailer GVW > 750 kg; combination ≤ 12 t | **18 years** |

> **Driver CPC reduces the minimum age** for C and D categories by 3 years (to 18 and 21 respectively), provided the driver holds a valid CPC certificate (Directive 2003/59/EC).

---

## Licence Validity

| Category | Validity period |
|----------|----------------|
| C, CE, D, DE (commercial) | **5 years** (medical check required at renewal) |
| C1, C1E (light commercial) | 5 years |

Medical fitness requirements apply for commercial licence renewal. Member states may impose shorter validity periods for older drivers.

---

## EU Mutual Recognition

EU driving licences are mutually recognised across all member states and EEA countries (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein). A driver holding a Swedish Category CE licence may drive an articulated lorry in Germany, France, Italy, etc. without additional licensing.

**Third-country drivers:** Licences from non-EU/EEA countries are not automatically recognised. Exchange arrangements depend on bilateral agreements. Some countries (e.g., Switzerland, Turkey) have bilateral recognition agreements with the EU for certain categories.

---

## Relationship to Driver CPC

Directive 2006/126/EC governs the **licence** (entitlement to drive). Directive 2003/59/EC governs the **Driver CPC** (professional competence qualification). Both are required for commercial transport:

- A driver needs a valid C/CE licence **and** a valid CPC card to legally drive commercially
- The CPC card must be carried alongside the driving licence
- See `references/eu/professional-driver.md` for CPC requirements
