## Overview

Commercial vehicles operating in international road transport must carry a defined set of documents at all times. Requirements vary by operation type (goods vs. passengers), route (EU/EEA vs. non-EU), cargo type (general, dangerous goods, perishable, live animals), and weight/dimension class. This checklist covers the full scope of documents that may be required; not every document applies to every journey — use the "When needed" column to determine applicability.

Enforcement officers (police, transport inspectorates, customs) across the EU are empowered to check documents at the roadside. Non-compliance typically results in administrative fines or operational bans. In some countries (PL, RO, BG, HU) checks are systematic; in others (DE, NL, SE) they are risk-based.

## Master Document Checklist

| # | Document | Required by | When needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vehicle registration certificate** (logbook / V5C / Fahrzeugschein / Vägtrafikregistret) | National road traffic law (all countries) | Always — every journey | Must show current registered keeper; trailer registration required separately in most countries |
| 2 | **Roadworthiness / MOT certificate** (periodic inspection certificate) | National law; Directive 2014/45/EU | Always for commercial vehicles; copy mandatory at roadside in **PL, CZ, HU, RO, BG** | In SE: Kontrollbesiktning certificate; in DE: HU/AU sticker on plate is sufficient; original or certified copy accepted |
| 3 | **MTPL insurance certificate / Green Card** | Directive 2009/103/EC as amended | Always — within EU/EEA: insurance certificate; entering TR, UA, BY, RS, ME, MK, MA, GE, etc.: **Green Card** with valid country codes | Digital insurance certificates accepted in some countries; Green Card must list all transit/destination countries |
| 4 | **EU Community Licence — certified copy** (operator transport licence) | EU Regulation 1072/2009 Art. 4 | Always for commercial goods transport in EU/EEA | One certified copy per vehicle; original kept at operator's premises; cabotage: driver must be able to produce copy |
| 5 | **Driver's national driving licence** | Directive 2006/126/EC | Always | Category C1/C/CE or D for HGV/coach; must be valid and not expired |
| 6 | **International Driving Permit (IDP)** | Vienna Convention 1968 / bilateral agreements | Required for **non-EU licence holders** in countries that do not recognise the national licence (e.g., some AETR non-EU states); not required within EU for EU licence holders | IDP 1968 format (multilingual annex); issued by national motoring authority; valid 3 years |
| 7 | **Driver CPC qualification card** (Digital Tachograph card or separate card) | Directive 2003/59/EC | Always for professional HGV/coach drivers in EU/EEA | Card shows 95-hour periodic training; must carry alongside driver card; combined with driver card in some countries |
| 8 | **Tachograph driver card** | EU Regulation 165/2014 Art. 27 | Always when driving a vehicle subject to tachograph rules | Inserted in tachograph during driving; PIN-protected; issued by national authority |
| 9 | **Tachograph records — current week + preceding 28 calendar days** | EU Regulation 561/2006 Art. 36 | Always for tachograph-equipped vehicles | Digital: printouts or card data; analogue: paper charts; must show all driving and rest periods |
| 10 | **CMR consignment note** (second copy — accompanies goods) | CMR Convention Art. 5 | All international goods transport between CMR contracting states | Three originals issued; second copy travels with goods; eCMR accepted where both countries have ratified the eCMR Protocol |
| 11 | **Posting declaration / A1 certificate** | Directive 2020/1057/EU; national posting-of-workers law | Required when performing **cabotage** or cross-border transport operations in FR, DE, AT, BE, NL, ES, IT, and others | Filed in advance via national IMI (Internal Market Information system) portal; driver carries printout or digital confirmation; FR: SIPSI system; DE: via Zoll.de |
| 12 | **ADR transport document** | ADR 2025 — 5.4.1 | When carrying **dangerous goods** in quantities above exemption thresholds | Shows UN number, proper shipping name, hazard class, packing group, quantity, consignor/consignee |
| 12a | **ADR written instructions (Tremcard)** | ADR 2025 — 5.4.3 | Always when carrying dangerous goods (no threshold) | One set per language of countries traversed; kept in cab accessible to crew |
| 12b | **ADR vehicle approval certificate** | ADR 2025 — 9.1.3 | For EX, FL, AT, MEMU vehicles; tank vehicles; vehicles carrying explosives | Issued by competent authority; shows vehicle type, approval, expiry |
| 12c | **Driver ADR certificate** | ADR 2025 — 8.2.1 | For all drivers carrying ADR goods in quantities requiring transport document | Category-specific (general, tank, Class 1, Class 7); valid 5 years with periodic training |
| 13 | **Bilateral transport permit or CEMT/ITF permit** | Bilateral transport agreements; ITF permit system | For transport operations between **non-EU countries** or where bilateral quota applies (e.g., UK post-Brexit, TR, MA, GE) | TIR Carnet may substitute for transit through third countries; CEMT/ITF permit for multilateral operations |
| 14 | **TIR Carnet** | TIR Convention 1975 | Transit through countries requiring TIR for customs transit (TR, UA, MA, GE, etc.) | Issued by national guaranteeing association (e.g., TOBB-TR, NLT-NL); one carnet per vehicle per journey |
| 15 | **ATP certificate** (number on CMR or separate document) | ATP Agreement (UNECE); Regulation EC 37/2005 | Transporting **temperature-controlled perishable foodstuffs** under ATP categories | Certificate number entered on CMR; vehicle must carry ATP plate/marking; certificate issued after inspection |
| 16 | **Phytosanitary certificate** | Regulation EU 2016/2031 — plant health | Transporting **plants, plant products, or other regulated articles** across borders | Issued by national plant protection organisation of exporting country; required at EU entry point |
| 17 | **Health certificate / TRACES document** | Regulation EU 2017/625 — official controls; Regulation EC 853/2004 | Transporting **animal products or live animals** across borders; required at EU BCP (Border Control Post) | TRACES NT (Trade Control and Expert System) — EU digital platform; certificate accompanies consignment |
| 18 | **CITES permit** | Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species (CITES); EU Regulation 338/97 | Transporting **protected species, wildlife products, or CITES-listed specimens** | Import/export permits issued by CITES Management Authority; must be presented at border |
| 19 | **Exceptional load / oversize permit** | National road transport law (all countries) | When vehicle + load exceeds standard dimension or weight limits | Each country of transit requires own permit; typically time-limited and route-specific; police escort may be required |
| 20 | **Overweight axle permit** | National law | When axle weight exceeds national BK class or statutory limit | In SE: dispensation from Transportstyrelsen; in DE: Ausnahmegenehmigung from Landesbehörde |
| 21 | **Speed limiter calibration certificate** | Directive 92/6/EEC as amended; national law | Some countries (IT, ES, GR) may check at roadside | Shows vehicle's speed limiter setting (90 km/h for HGV, 100 km/h for coaches); issued at periodic inspection |

## Country-Specific Additional Documents

| Country | Additional requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **SE** | Transportdispens (dispensation permit) | For overweight/oversize; issued by Transportstyrelsen or Trafikverket; required for BK2/BK3 weight exceptions |
| **CH** | LSVA OBU confirmation / LSVA declaration | Swiss heavy vehicle charge (Leistungsabhängige Schwerverkehrsabgabe); OBU fitted to vehicle; confirmation of payment/registration |
| **FR** | Crit'Air vignette on windscreen | Required in ZPA (Zones à Protection Accrue) and ZCR (Zones à Circulation Restreinte) in Paris, Lyon, Grenoble, Strasbourg, etc.; must be affixed before entering restricted zone |
| **DE** | Umweltzone sticker (Plakette) | Environmental zone (Umweltzone) sticker for vehicles entering city low-emission zones (Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, etc.); Euro standard must be certified on the sticker |
| **AT** | ASFINAG vignette or GO-Box | Motorway vignette for vehicles under 3.5t; GO-Box for HGV > 3.5t (distance-based toll) |
| **NO** | AutoPASS / toll payment confirmation | Automated toll collection; foreign vehicles must register or pay at toll stations |
| **PL** | e-TOLL transponder or registration | For trucks on national roads subject to e-toll; viaTOLL replaced by e-TOLL system |
| **HU** | HU-GO transponder or GO e-voucher | Electronic toll for trucks on Hungarian national roads |
| **TR** | Turkish compulsory insurance (Trafik Sigortası) | In addition to Green Card; mandatory local insurance purchased at border if not arranged in advance |
| **IT** | TelePass or cash payment | Motorway toll payment; no mandatory document, but transponder recommended |

## Document Retention and Record-Keeping

| Document | Minimum retention period | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Tachograph records (digital printouts / charts) | 1 year at operator's premises (EU Reg. 561/2006 Art. 36) | Operator + driver carries 28 days |
| CMR consignment notes | 5 years (commercial practice; VAT audit requirements) | Carrier's files |
| Community Licence | Duration of licence validity (5 years); original at premises | Certified copy in vehicle |
| ADR transport documents | Until journey complete; carrier retains 3 months | With goods during transport |
| Posting declarations | At least 2 years after posting ends | Operator records |

## Penalties

Failure to carry required documents is enforced under national law. Representative sanctions:

| Country | Sanction | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| SE | 10,000 SEK per missing document | Missing CMR, Community Licence copy, or CPC card (Yrkestrafikförordning 2012:237) |
| DE | €75–1,500 depending on document | BKatV (Bußgeldkatalog); tachograph records carry highest penalties |
| FR | €750–15,000 + vehicle immobilisation | Posting declaration missing: €2,000 per worker per day |
| PL | 500–10,000 PLN | GITD enforcement; roadworthiness certificate missing: 2,000 PLN |
| RO | 2,000–6,000 RON per infraction | Politia Rutiera / RAR checks |
| EU (tachograph records) | Infringement classification under Directive 2006/22/EC Annex I | Serious/very serious infringements trigger operator compliance investigation |
