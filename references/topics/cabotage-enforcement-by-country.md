## Overview

EU Regulation 1072/2009 sets a uniform rule across all EU member states: **a non-resident haulier may perform a maximum of 3 cabotage operations within 7 days following an international delivery into the host member state**. After that 7-day window expires, the vehicle must leave the host country and may not return to perform cabotage for a minimum of **4 days** (the "cooling off" period introduced by Mobility Package Regulation 2020/1055, effective 21 February 2022).

While the rule is uniform, **enforcement intensity varies dramatically** by country. This file documents practical enforcement realities.

---

## The Cabotage Rule — Precise Definition

### What Counts as Cabotage

A cabotage operation is a **domestic road transport operation performed on a temporary basis** in a host member state by a non-resident carrier. Key requirements:
- Both loading and unloading points must be within the host country
- The vehicle must be registered in a different EU member state
- The carrier must hold a valid EU Community Licence (issued in the vehicle's home state)

**Cross-border operations are NOT cabotage** — picking up in Germany and delivering in France is international transport, not cabotage, even if performed by a non-French carrier.

### The 3-in-7 Rule

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Maximum cabotage operations | 3 operations within 7 days |
| The 7-day window | Starts from the day of first unloading of the international inbound delivery |
| Operations must be completed | Within the 7-day window; no partial operations extending beyond day 7 |
| Vehicle must then leave | Before the 7-day window expires OR immediately after 3rd operation |
| Cooling-off period | Minimum 4 days before the same vehicle may return to perform cabotage in the same country |

**Example:** A Polish truck delivers goods to Munich (international delivery) on Monday. It may then perform up to 3 domestic German transport operations (e.g., Munich–Hamburg, Hamburg–Frankfurt, Frankfurt–Stuttgart) within the next 7 days (i.e., by the following Monday). After that, the truck must leave Germany and may not re-enter for cabotage for at least 4 days.

## Required Documentation for Each Cabotage Operation

Every cabotage operation must be covered by a **CMR consignment note** (or eCMR) that is available for inspection. Operators must carry:

| Document | Content required | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|-------|
| CMR — international inbound delivery | Proof that the vehicle entered the country following an international delivery | This is the "anchor" document proving the right to perform cabotage |
| CMR — cabotage operation 1 | Shipper, consignee, loading address, delivery address, date, nature and quantity of goods | Must be on board during the operation |
| CMR — cabotage operation 2 (if performed) | Same fields as above | |
| CMR — cabotage operation 3 (if performed) | Same fields as above | |
| EU Community Licence copy | Certified copy of the carrier's Community Licence | Must be in the vehicle at all times |
| Driver attestation | If driver is not a citizen of the vehicle's home member state | |

**Timing documentation:** For the 7-day rule, the dates on the CMR documents and tachograph records must be consistent. Inspectors cross-check CMR dates with tachograph data.

**eCMR:** Electronic CMR (additional protocol to CMR Convention, 2008) is accepted in most EU states. Ensure the eCMR is accessible on a device that can be presented at roadside.

## Enforcement by Country

### Germany — MOST ACTIVE Enforcer

**Authority:** BALM (Bundesamt für Logistik und Mobilität) + Zoll (Customs / Federal Customs Service)

Germany runs the most active cabotage enforcement program in Europe. BALM operates dedicated Kontrollgruppen (inspection teams) that conduct both roadside checks and undercover operations.

| Fine | Amount | Who is liable |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Illegal cabotage operation (per operation) | Up to **€30,000** | Operator (Unternehmer) |
| Driver fine for assisting illegal cabotage | Up to **€10,000** | Driver |
| Operating without Community Licence copy | Up to **€10,000** | Operator |
| Missing posting declaration (Mindestlohn) | Up to **€30,000** | Operator |

**Enforcement methods:**
- Roadside checks on motorways (Autobahn), particularly at border crossings and rest areas
- Document checks targeting foreign-registered vehicles, especially Polish, Romanian, Bulgarian plates
- Undercover operations by BALM inspectors posing as shippers
- Cross-referencing with BALM's digital records system

**Common findings:**
- Driver cannot produce CMR for the prior international delivery
- Day counter started incorrectly (operators count from entry date, not from inbound delivery unloading)
- 4th or subsequent cabotage operations on day 7 or 8
- Missing or incorrect posting declaration in FMS system

**Additional German requirement — posting declaration:**
Germany requires foreign carriers performing cabotage to register each driver via the **FMS portal (Fahrermeldestellen-Meldeportal / Mindestlohn)** before the driver starts work in Germany. This is separate from the EU Posting of Workers Directive declaration. Failure results in fines of up to €30,000.
- FMS portal: https://www.fms-portal.de/

See also references/countries/de/posting-declaration.md and references/countries/de/posting-workers.md.

### France — VERY ACTIVE Enforcer

**Authority:** DREAL (Directions Régionales de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement) — regional transport inspectorates

France has significantly increased cabotage enforcement since 2019 and is the second-most active enforcer in Europe.

| Fine | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Illegal cabotage operation | Up to **€15,000** per violation |
| Failure to have required documents | Up to **€1,500** per missing document |
| Missing posting declaration (SIPSI) | Up to **€10,000** |

**Enforcement methods:**
- Roadside checks by DREAL inspectors, often in coordination with Gendarmerie
- Checks at motorway rest areas, particularly in transit corridors (Lyon, Strasbourg, Lille areas)
- DIRECCTE (labour inspectorate) may also check posting compliance

**French-specific requirements:**
- **SIPSI declaration:** Foreign carriers performing cabotage must file a posting of workers declaration via the SIPSI portal before the driver begins work in France: https://www.sipsi.travail.gouv.fr/
- **Minimum wage compliance:** French minimum wage (SMIC) must be paid or matched for the duration the driver works in France
- **Pay slips:** Inspectors may demand to see pay records for drivers; French law allows retroactive wage claims

See references/countries/fr/posting-declaration.md and references/countries/fr/posting-workers.md.

### Netherlands — ACTIVE Enforcer

**Authority:** ILT (Inspectie Leefomgeving en Transport)

| Fine | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Illegal cabotage | Up to **€10,000** per violation |
| Missing documents | **€1,500–€4,000** |

**Enforcement methods:**
- Regular roadside checks at major logistics hubs (Venlo, Rotterdam, Eindhoven)
- Active data-sharing with BALM (Germany) and FOD Mobiliteit (Belgium)
- ILT participates in joint EU enforcement operations (EuroContrôle Route)

**Dutch posting requirement:** DUOS portal declaration required for posting of workers:
- https://www.duos.nl/

---

### Belgium — MODERATELY ACTIVE Enforcer

**Authority:** FOD Mobiliteit en Openbare Werken (FPS Mobility and Public Works)

| Fine | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Illegal cabotage | **€1,500–€5,000** (graduated) |
| Missing documents | **€500–€1,500** |

Belgium cooperates actively with both Germany and the Netherlands on enforcement. Cross-border intelligence sharing means violations detected in one country may be followed up in another.

**Belgian posting requirement:** LIMOSA declaration via https://www.limosa.be/

See references/countries/be/posting-declaration.md.

---

### Sweden — LESS ACTIVE Enforcer

**Authority:** Transportstyrelsen (administrative authority); Polismyndigheten (roadside enforcement)

| Fine | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Illegal cabotage (violation of EU 1072/2009) | **60,000 SEK** (company sanction) |
| Missing posting declaration during cabotage | **60,000 SEK** |

Sweden has relatively few dedicated cabotage enforcement operations but conducts spot checks, particularly on trucks arriving from continental Europe via ferry (Gothenburg, Malmö/Trelleborg).

**Note:** The 60,000 SEK cabotage fine is the highest-tier sanction in Sweden's transport sanctions framework and is imposed on companies (not drivers directly). Drivers are rarely fined separately.

See references/countries/se/penalties.md.

### United Kingdom — DIFFERENT RULE (post-Brexit)

**Note:** UK is no longer bound by EU Regulation 1072/2009. The Trade and Cooperation Agreement (TCA) 2021 sets different cabotage rules for UK operations.

**Authority:** DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency)

| Rule element | UK/TCA rule | EU 1072/2009 rule |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum cabotage operations | **2 operations** within 7 days | 3 operations within 7 days |
| Cooling off | Not specified in TCA; de facto restrictions apply | 4 days (Mobility Package) |
| Right to perform cabotage | EU carrier may perform in UK under TCA; UK carrier may perform in EU under TCA | Only EU carriers in EU territory |

| Fine | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Illegal cabotage (per operation) | Up to **£1,000 per driver** per violation |
| Operator (company) fine | Up to **£5,000** |

DVSA enforcement is active at major entry points: Dover, Folkestone, Harwich, and other port of entry checks.

## Common Violations and How to Avoid Them

| Violation | Root cause | Prevention |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **No CMR for inbound international delivery** | Driver did not retain the inbound delivery CMR | Establish SOP: driver always keeps inbound CMR; digital copy in TMS |
| **Day counter error** | Operator counts from entry date, not from first unloading | Count from the day of first unloading of the inbound delivery, not day of border crossing |
| **Operation on day 8** (thinking day 7 is OK) | Misunderstanding of "within 7 days" | Day of first unloading = day 1; operations must be completed by end of day 7 |
| **Cross-border pickup counted as cabotage** | Misclassification of international operation | Any load that starts or ends outside the host country = international transport, not cabotage |
| **Missing posting declaration** | Separate compliance system not integrated into dispatch | Integrate SIPSI/FMS/LIMOSA/DUOS filing into dispatch workflow; file before driver starts |
| **No CMR for each cabotage leg** | Single CMR used for multiple operations | Each load requires its own CMR |
| **Cooling-off violation** | Vehicle returned to same country within 4 days | Track by vehicle/registration plate; enforce at dispatch level |

CABOTAGE_MAX_OPERATIONS = 3           # within 7-day window (EU); 2 for UK
CABOTAGE_WINDOW_DAYS = 7             # starts from day of first unloading of international delivery
COOLING_OFF_DAYS = 4                  # Mobility Package 2020; applies per vehicle per host country
UK_CABOTAGE_MAX_OPERATIONS = 2        # TCA rule, not EU 1072/2009
DAY_COUNTER_START = "first_unloading_date"  # NOT border crossing date

# Posting declarations required BEFORE driver starts cabotage work:
POSTING_PORTALS = {
  "DE": "https://www.fms-portal.de/",

]
