# Cross-Border Scenarios — What Changes at Specific Borders

**Sources:**
- Directive 96/53/EC — EU weights and dimensions baseline
- EU Regulation 561/2006 and AETR — driving and rest time
- Swiss bilateral agreement on land transport (CH-EU 1999)
- TIR Convention (UNECE) — transit between non-EU states
- EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement (TCA, 2020) — post-Brexit transport
- EU-UA bilateral agreements (wartime emergency arrangements 2022–)
- AETR Convention (1970, as amended)
- Cabotage rules: EU Reg. 1072/2009

**Last verified: 2026-06-01**

---

## Overview

This file describes the **practical rule-change points** when a truck crosses key international borders. The format used throughout is: "At the border, X becomes Y" with the specific rule that changes and why.

Understanding these transitions is essential for transport management systems, TMS compliance modules, and driver briefings for international operations.

---

## SE → DE (Sweden to Germany)

This is the most operationally significant border transition for Swedish carriers.

### Dimensions and weight

| Rule | In Sweden (SE) | At German border → |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Max gross weight | 74 t (BK4/EMS) or 64 t (BK3) | **40 t** (§34 StVZO) |
| Max combination length | 34.5 m (LCV/EMS) | **18.75 m** (§32 StVZO) |
| Max width | 2.60 m standard | **2.55 m** (2.60 m reefer only) |
| Max height | 4.50 m | **4.00 m** (§32 StVZO) |
| Lang-Lkw (25.25 m) | Allowed on designated roads | Only on **Positivnetz** (specific designated network) |

**Consequence:** A Swedish EMS 34.5 m / 74 t combination **cannot legally enter Germany** without breaking down the combination or obtaining an exceptional load permit. Standard Swedish forest/paper industry combinations must either stop at the border and re-load into a German-legal configuration, or the Swedish shipper must arrange German-legal vehicles from the ferry/border onwards.

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | In Sweden | At German border → |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation 561/2006 | EU Regulation 561/2006 |
| Substance | **No change** — same EU rules apply | Same |
| Enforcement | Transportstyrelsen / Polis | BALM / Polizei |
| Tachograph records | EU-valid records accepted | Same |

### Cabotage

| Rule | Sweden | Germany |
|------|--------|---------|
| Cabotage operations | EU Reg. 1072/2009 (3 operations in 7 days) | Same EU rule; stricter German enforcement |
| Cooling-off period | 4-day cooling off after 7-day cabotage | Same (Mobility Package 2022) |

### Tolls

| Rule | Sweden | Germany |
|------|--------|---------|
| Toll system | No general HGV motorway toll (SE) | **LKW-Maut mandatory** from first kilometre on tolled roads (Toll Collect) |
| Registration | Not needed for SE roads | **OBU must be registered with Toll Collect** before entering Germany |
| Failure to pay | — | BALM: fines + BALM security deposit |

### Posting of workers

| Rule | Sweden → Germany |
|------|-----------------|
| Declaration required | **FMS-Portal** (German posting declaration) required for drivers employed by non-German companies operating in Germany |
| Cabotage operations | Always posting triggers |
| Transit | Pure transit (no loading/unloading in DE) is generally exempt |

## SE → NO (Sweden to Norway)

### Dimensions and weight

| Rule | In Sweden (SE) | In Norway (NO) |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Max gross weight | 74 t (EMS/BK4) | **60 t** (standard); up to 74 t on specifically designated Norwegian roads under bilateral SE–NO framework |
| Max length | 34.5 m (LCV) | **25.25 m** standard; bilateral agreement allows SE-registered EMS on some Norwegian roads |
| Max width | 2.60 m | 2.55 m standard |

**Nordic bilateral framework:** Sweden and Norway have a bilateral agreement allowing Swedish EMS-style combinations (up to 25.25 m / 60 t) on designated Norwegian road sections. The full 34.5 m combination is generally **not** permitted in Norway without a special Statens vegvesen permit. Check current Statens vegvesen dispensation list before planning.

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | Sweden | Norway |
|------|--------|--------|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation 561/2006 | EU Reg. 561/2006 via EEA Agreement |
| Substance | **No change** | Same |

### Tolls

Norway has extensive road tolls (Autopass system):
- Norwegian toll ring system: OBU or license plate billing via Autopass
- LSVA-equivalent: Norway has no Swiss-style LSVA but per-road tolls apply

### Cabotage

Norway is EEA but not EU. EU Regulation 1072/2009 applies via EEA Agreement — same 3-in-7 cabotage rule applies in Norway as in EU member states.

## EU → CH (EU member state to Switzerland)

### Dimensions and weight

| Rule | EU (standard) | Switzerland |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| Max gross weight | 40 t (road train) | **40 t** — same, but strictly enforced |
| Max length | 18.75 m | **18.75 m** — same |
| Nordic EMS (34.5 m) | Not allowed in EU either | Not allowed in CH |

No change in standard limits at the border, but **enforcement is significantly stricter** in Switzerland.

### Night and Sunday bans

| Rule | EU origin state (e.g. DE) | Switzerland |
|------|--------------------------|-------------|
| Sunday ban | Sundays banned for HGVs > 7.5 t (DE equivalent) | **Sunday ban applies all day** — no HGV > 3.5 t |
| Night ban | Night restrictions (varies by country) | **22:00–05:00 every night** — no HGV > 3.5 t |
| Public holidays | Varies | **Same as Sunday** (complete ban) |

**At the Swiss border:** If it is a Sunday, the driver cannot proceed. If it is evening (22:00 onwards), the driver cannot proceed. These bans apply from the moment of crossing into Switzerland.

### LSVA (Heavy Vehicle Tax)

| Rule | EU | Switzerland |
|------|-----|------------|
| Kilometre tax | None (or national tolls only) | **LSVA mandatory** from first km in Switzerland |
| OBU required | No | **Yes** — OBU must be fitted or manual declaration at border |
| Calculation base | — | Distance × weight × emission class |
| Sunday/night driving | — | LSVA still charged even if driving under exemption |

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | EU (561/2006) | Switzerland (AETR) |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Legal basis | EU Reg. 561/2006 | AETR Convention |
| Substance | Same rules | **Substantially same** — AETR aligns with 561/2006 |
| Effective difference | Minimal for most operations | Enforcement by Swiss Polizei |

### ADR dangerous goods

Switzerland has its own ADR implementing rules aligned with but not identical to EU Directive 2008/68/EC. Through the bilateral CH-EU land transport agreement, ADR applies in Switzerland. No change in substance at the border, but Swiss enforcement uses Swiss procedures.

## EU → TR (EU to Turkey)

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | EU | Turkey |
|------|-----|--------|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation 561/2006 | **AETR Convention** |
| Substance | EU 561/2006 rules | AETR rules (largely identical; see AETR reference) |
| Documentation | Digital tachograph records | **AETR tachograph records** — same hardware, different legal basis |

**At the Turkish border:** The legal basis for driving/rest shifts from EU Reg. 561/2006 to AETR. In practice the rules are nearly identical, but enforcement is under Turkish authorities and the AETR framework.

### International permits

| Rule | Within EU | Turkey |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Market access | EU Community Licence | **Bilateral permit required** (or ECMT/CEMT permit) |
| TIR carnet | Not needed within EU | **TIR carnet** required for transit through Turkey and beyond |
| Cabotage | Prohibited (non-Turkish carriers) | Prohibited — only Turkish flag for domestic operations |

### Weights and dimensions

Turkey applies its own limits (broadly similar to EU standard limits of 40 t / 18.75 m) but with national variations. Cross-check for specific dimensions.

## EU → GB (EU to United Kingdom, post-Brexit)

### Market access / cabotage

| Rule | EU (pre-2021) | UK (post-Brexit) |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| Community Licence | Allowed unlimited cabotage | **No longer valid in UK** |
| Cabotage | 3 operations in 7 days within EU | In UK: **2 cabotage operations in 7 days** for EU carriers; then must leave UK for 7 days |
| Market access | Open | Requires ECMT permit or bilateral UK-EU permit or under TCA provisions |

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | EU | United Kingdom |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Legal basis | EU Regulation 561/2006 | **UK assimilated law** (formerly EU Reg. 561/2006, retained post-Brexit) |
| Substance | EU 561/2006 | **Identical rules** — UK retained EU rules at exit; no substantive change |
| Enforcement | National enforcement | DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) |
| Tachograph | EU/AETR tachograph | EU tachograph records accepted in UK |

### Northern Ireland

Special situation under UK Internal Market Scheme (UKIMS) and the Windsor Framework (2023):
- Northern Ireland (NI) remains aligned with EU single market rules for goods
- EU carriers can operate in NI under same rules as in Republic of Ireland
- NI–Great Britain (GB) movements have additional customs documentation requirements

### Weights and dimensions

UK standard limits: 44 t (6-axle articulated), 18.75 m (road train). Broadly aligned with EU but UK uses its own national legislation (Road Vehicles (Construction and Use) Regulations 1986). No practical difference for standard operations.

### Tolls

UK has no EU EETS membership post-Brexit. UK-specific tolls (M6 Toll, Dartford Crossing, etc.) require separate UK payment methods.

## EU → UA (EU to Ukraine — wartime emergency arrangements, 2022–)

### Background

Following the full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, the EU and Ukraine entered into an emergency bilateral transport agreement (Multiple Bilateral Agreement, renewed annually) replacing the previous permit quota system.

### Market access

| Rule | Standard (pre-2022) | Emergency arrangement (2022–) |
|------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| Permits | Annual bilateral quota permits | **Permit-free access** for EU-UA bilateral transport |
| Cabotage | Prohibited | Remains prohibited |
| Transit (to third countries) | Permit required | Streamlined; check current status |

**Note:** The emergency arrangement is renewed annually. Status as of 2026-06-01: arrangement in effect; check current EU Council decisions for any modifications.

### Driving and rest time

| Rule | EU (into Ukraine) | Ukraine |
|------|------------------|---------|
| Legal basis | EU Reg. 561/2006 applies to EU-registered vehicle | **AETR Convention** applies in Ukraine |
| Substance | Near-identical to AETR | AETR rules |

### Special permit rules (remaining)

Even under the emergency arrangement, some movements to Ukraine require additional documentation:
- TIR carnet may be required for goods continuing beyond Ukraine
- Phytosanitary and veterinary certificates for relevant goods
- Ukrainian customs transit document (ТТН — transport document) for domestic Ukrainian movements

### Infrastructure

- Border crossing points: significantly congested (Medyka, Dorohusk, Chelm, Shehyni); plan for waiting times of hours to days
- Alternative: rail transfer at border (broader gauge in Ukraine); increasingly used for heavy/industrial goods

## General Principles for Cross-Border Planning

1. **Dimension check first:** Swedish EMS/BK4 combinations require restructuring before crossing into virtually every non-Nordic country
2. **Permit lead time:** Exceptional load permits must be applied for in each transit country separately; allow 3–6 weeks for complex multi-country loads
3. **Tachograph records carry over:** EU digital tachograph records are valid proof of compliance in all AETR signatories and most countries with bilateral agreements
4. **Night/Sunday bans differ:** Swiss bans are the strictest in Europe; plan staging points accordingly
5. **Tolls require advance preparation:** LSVA (CH), Toll Collect (DE), ASFINAG GO (AT) all require OBU registration before entry
6. **Posting declarations:** EU Directive 2020/1057 requires IMI/national portal declarations for any driver performing cabotage or loading/unloading operations in another EU state — even during transit if operations are performed
7. **ADR tunnel categories:** ADR tunnel codes change at border; check the category of each tunnel on the route in each country
