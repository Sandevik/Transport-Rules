## Overview

This reference lists mandatory safety equipment requirements for **motor vehicles** (including commercial vehicles) in each covered country. "Mandatory" means legally required to be carried in the vehicle — not merely recommended best practice. Where an item is strongly recommended but not legally required, it is marked as "recommended."

**Important notes:**
- Requirements for **commercial vehicles (trucks, buses)** may differ from passenger cars — this table covers general requirements applicable to commercial motor vehicles unless otherwise noted
- Requirements can change; always verify with the national authority before operating in a new country
- Winter equipment (snow chains, winter tyres) is covered separately in country-specific winter-requirements files
- Some countries enforce requirements more strictly at the border than in practice on the road

## Country-by-Country Details

### AT — Austria

**Legal basis:** §102 KFG (Kraftfahrgesetz) and StVO (Straßenverkehrsordnung)

| Item | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| Warning triangle | 1 — required. Must be placed at least 50m behind the vehicle on motorways; 100m on motorways at speed >100 km/h |
| High-visibility vest | Required; must be worn by the driver and any person exiting the vehicle on the road. Must be **kept in the passenger compartment** (not the boot — must be reachable without exiting first) |
| Fire extinguisher | Required for all vehicles. Minimum 1 kg powder or equivalent. |
| First aid kit | Required — must be in a yellow/orange box meeting Austrian standard (Verbandkasten) |
| Spare bulbs | Not required, but recommended |

**Note for trucks/buses:** Commercial vehicles have additional specific requirements under KFG.

### ES — Spain

**Legal basis:** Reglamento General de Circulación (RGC), Real Decreto 2822/1998 and updates

| Item | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| Warning triangle | **2 triangles** — required; one placed in front and one behind the vehicle |
| High-visibility vest | **One vest per person in the vehicle** (driver + each passenger) — required. Must be worn before exiting the vehicle onto the road. Must be stored inside the vehicle (not in the boot). |
| Fire extinguisher | Not required for passenger cars; required for trucks and buses |
| First aid kit | Not required (recommended) |
| Spare bulbs | Required — must carry a complete set of spare bulbs |

**Note (2021 update):** Spain modified triangle placement rules — triangles are no longer required to be placed on the carriageway if it would put the person in danger. Emergency lights (hazard warning) plus roadside stop is then sufficient. However, **carrying two triangles** remains mandatory.

## Commercial Vehicles — Additional Notes

### Trucks (>3.5 t GVW)

Most countries require additional equipment for trucks that is not required for passenger cars:
- **Fire extinguisher**: Required in most countries even where not required for passenger cars (AT, BE, BG, DE, ES, FR, GR, HR, LT, LV, PL, PT, RO, SK, TR, CY)
- **ADR dangerous goods vehicles**: Extensive additional equipment required regardless of country (fire extinguishers, PPE, absorbent material, chocks, emergency flashlights) — see `references/eu/dangerous-goods.md`
- **Buses**: Many countries require fire extinguisher and first aid kit in all passenger buses

### High-Visibility Vest Storage

A critical distinction across Europe is **where** the vest must be stored:

- **In cab / passenger compartment** (accessible before exiting): AT, CZ, ES, FR, IT
- **Any accessible location**: Most other countries
- The rationale for in-cab storage is that the driver must be able to put the vest on before opening the door and stepping out onto the road

### Warning Triangle Placement

Standard practice across Europe is to place the triangle **at least 30–50 m behind the vehicle** on ordinary roads, and **at least 100 m** on motorways/expressways. Specific distances vary by country law.

## Penalties

Penalties for missing mandatory equipment vary significantly by country and are set under national law. General guidance:

| Country | Typical fine for missing equipment |
|---------|-----------------------------------|
| AT | EUR 36–726 depending on severity |
| DE | EUR 5–30 per item typically |
| ES | EUR 200 per missing item |
| IT | EUR 87–345 |
| FR | EUR 11–135 |
| PL | PLN 50–500 |
| SE | SEK 1,000–3,000 (Ordningsbot for minor violations) |

Fines are subject to change. For current amounts, consult the national transport authority or police.
